# JOB DESCRIPTION — Head of Red Team Division

**Position:** Head of Red Team Division / Principal Red Team Lead  
**Division:** Red Team Division, Cyber Security Practice  
**Company:** Aras Integrasi Sdn Bhd  
**Reports to:** Director, Cyber Security Practice  
**Location:** Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (hybrid)  
**Employment type:** Full-time, permanent  
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Position Summary

The Head of Red Team Division leads a newly established national capability division within Aras Integrasi's Cyber Security Practice. This is a leadership role combining deep technical red team expertise with national-level stakeholder engagement and P&L ownership. The division operates a sovereign, open-source offensive security stack (VORON-C2) and provides red team, dark web intelligence, and detection engineering services to government, defence, critical infrastructure, and commercial clients.

This is not a standard red team lead role. It requires someone who can brief NACSA directors, design national cyber exercises, manage a 5-person division, and still understand a C2 beacon config when needed.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Division Leadership & Operations (30%)

- Own division P&L, revenue target achievement, and capacity planning
- Define and maintain division operating model — engagement lifecycle, quality standards, escalation paths
- Conduct quarterly performance reviews, skills assessments, and career development for all division staff
- Manage division budget (infrastructure, tooling, training, conference attendance)
- Chair weekly division operations meeting — engagement status, pipeline review, blocker resolution
- Report monthly to Director, Cyber Security Practice on division performance, revenue, and strategic milestones
- Lead annual division strategy review and roadmap update

### Engagement Management & Delivery (25%)

- Scope all red team engagements — define objectives, rules of engagement (ROE), timelines, deliverables, and pricing
- Author or co-author all engagement authorization documents (letters of authorisation, scope documents, legal frameworks)
- Serve as primary client contact for engagement scoping, delivery updates, and executive debriefs
- Quality-assure all engagement deliverables — technical reports, executive briefings, remediation recommendations
- Approve all engagement go/no-go decisions (with Director co-sign for government engagements)
- Manage engagement risk — operational, legal, reputational
- Conduct post-engagement reviews and drive continuous improvement

### National Capability & Stakeholder Management (25%)

- Build and maintain relationships with national cybersecurity stakeholders: NACSA, JDN/JDM, CNII sector leads, relevant ministries
- Design and pitch national-level cyber exercises (red vs blue) for government and critical infrastructure
- Represent Aras Integrasi at national cybersecurity forums, working groups, and industry events
- Contribute to national cybersecurity policy discussions through position papers, advisory inputs, and thought leadership
- Maintain awareness of Malaysian Cyber Security Act 2024 and related regulatory developments
- Develop and maintain engagement frameworks for government and defence sector clients

### Capability Development & Innovation (10%)

- Maintain personal technical currency in offensive security, C2 frameworks, and threat landscape
- Oversee VORON-C2 infrastructure strategy and evolution
- Drive adoption of AI-augmented offensive tools (CyberStrike integration, local LLM-assisted operations)
- Review and approve division research outputs (vulnerabilities, tools, publications)
- Maintain division's competitive intelligence — monitor global red team tooling, techniques, and service offerings

### Talent Pipeline & Development (10%)

- Oversee the VORON-C2 Skunkworks intern programme — approval process, mentorship, cohort selection
- Identify high-potential interns for conversion to full-time roles
- Maintain division skills matrix and individual development plans
- Build external talent pipeline through university relationships, CTF sponsorship, and community engagement
- Allocate research time and training budget across team

## Requirements

### Essential Qualifications

- Malaysian citizen (national capability requirement — government engagements require security clearance)
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, Cybersecurity, or equivalent demonstrated experience
- 8–12 years cybersecurity industry experience
- 4+ years in red team leadership, offensive security, or adversarial emulation roles
- Demonstrated experience managing technical teams (3+ direct reports minimum)
- Demonstrated experience engaging government or national security stakeholders
- Strong written and verbal communication — capable of briefing C-suite and ministerial-level audiences

### Essential Technical Knowledge

- Deep understanding of MITRE ATT&CK framework and adversary emulation methodologies
- Hands-on experience with C2 frameworks (Cobalt Strike, Sliver, Mythic, Havoc, or AdaptixC2)
- Understanding of full attack chain: initial access, persistence, privilege escalation, lateral movement, impact
- Knowledge of detection engineering and SIEM concepts (Wazuh, Splunk, ELK, or equivalent)
- Familiarity with Malaysian threat landscape and regional cybercrime ecosystem
- Understanding of Cyber Security Act 2024 and legal frameworks for offensive security operations

### Essential Certifications (one or more)

- CRTO (Certified Red Team Operator) or CRTO-II
- OSEP (Offensive Security Experienced Penetration Tester)
- OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional) + demonstrated leadership experience
- GREM (GIAC Reverse Engineering Malware) or equivalent advanced certification
- Or equivalent demonstrated capability through portfolio, published research, or operational track record

### Preferred

- Experience building or leading a red team function from scratch
- Experience with national cyber exercises or government-sector red teaming
- Published security research (CVEs, conference talks, blog posts, white papers)
- Active in Malaysian cybersecurity community (TalkSec, SecOps, local CTFs)
- Postgraduate degree in cybersecurity or related field

### Language

- Bahasa Malaysia (fluent — required for government engagement)
- English (fluent — required for technical work and international stakeholders)
