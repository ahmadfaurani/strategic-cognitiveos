## Position Summary

The Dark Web Intelligence Analyst is the division's eyes into the underground. You monitor dark web forums, marketplaces, Telegram channels, and underground communities to build threat actor profiles, detect leaked credentials, track emerging tooling, and produce pre-engagement intelligence packages that make red team operations threat-driven rather than generic.

This role sits inside the Red Team Division — not as a separate intelligence function — because dark web intelligence serves three direct purposes: (1) pre-engagement intelligence for red team operations, (2) standalone monitoring service for government and CNII clients, (3) threat intelligence feed into GovSec TIP. You are the bridge between what's happening underground and what the division does operationally.

This is a hands-on intelligence collection and analysis role. You will navigate Tor hidden services, monitor underground forums, track ransomware leak sites, analyze credential dumps, and produce structured intelligence products. You will also maintain the operational security of the division's dark web access infrastructure.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Dark Web Monitoring & Collection (35%)

- Monitor underground forums, marketplaces, and communities:
  - **Ransomware leak sites** — Akira, Fog, and emerging groups; track victims, especially Malaysian/SEA entities
  - **Underground forums** — carding markets, exploit marketplaces, initial access broker listings
  - **Telegram channels** — cybercrime channels, IAB channels, Malaysian/SEA-focused threat groups
  - **IRC/Discord communities** — tool sharing, TTP discussions, adversary communications
  - **Code repositories** — GitHub/GitLab for leaked credentials, exposed API keys, internal code
- Maintain safe collection infrastructure:
  - Tor, I2P, VPN configurations for secure dark web access
  - Monitored accounts with operational cover (legend development, persona management)
  - Collection automation — scraping pipelines, alert systems, keyword monitoring
  - OPSEC protocols for dark web navigation — no cross-contamination with personal identity
- Develop and maintain source network:
  - Identify reliable sources across forum tiers (public, registered, VIP/invite-only)
  - Build collection cadence — daily sweeps, weekly deep dives, monthly comprehensive reviews
  - Track source reliability and access continuity

### Threat Actor Profiling & TTP Analysis (20%)

- Build and maintain threat actor profile library:
  - Group affiliations, target sectors, geographic focus, tool preferences
  - TTP mapping to MITRE ATT&CK framework
  - Tool acquisition patterns (what they buy vs develop, where they source)
  - Affiliate program participation (ransomware-as-a-service relationships)
- Track emerging threats relevant to Malaysian landscape:
  - New ransomware groups targeting SEA
  - Initial access brokers selling access to Malaysian organisations
  - AI-generated attack tooling and its adoption by threat actors
  - Supply chain attack trends affecting Malaysian vendor ecosystem
- Produce actor-specific threat briefs for use in red team engagement planning

### Credential Leak Monitoring & Alerting (15%)

- Monitor credential dumps and breach databases for:
  - Malaysian government domain credentials (.gov.my)
  - CNII sector credentials (energy, finance, telecoms, healthcare)
  - Client-specific credential monitoring (per subscription contract)
  - Aras Integrasi corporate credentials (internal security)
- Develop and maintain credential monitoring pipeline:
  - Automated ingestion from breach databases (HaveIBeenPwned API, DeHashed, leak sites)
  - Domain-specific monitoring (government, financial, telecoms)
  - Real-time alerting to affected clients with structured remediation guidance
- Produce credential leak reports with severity scoring, exposure assessment, and remediation recommendations

### Pre-Engagement Intelligence Packages (10%)

- Before each red team engagement, produce a structured intelligence package:
  - **Threat landscape** — who targets this sector, what TTPs are current
  - **Target-specific intelligence** — public exposure, leaked credentials, infrastructure footprint
  - **Actor selection** — which threat actor(s) to emulate based on relevance
  - **TTP recommendations** — specific techniques to focus on based on current threat data
  - **OSINT summary** — surface web exposure, social media, job postings, tech stack identification
- Coordinate with Senior Red Team Operator to integrate intelligence into engagement planning
- Maintain library of pre-engagement intelligence templates by sector

### Intelligence Reporting & Delivery (10%)

- Produce structured intelligence products:
  - **Monthly Dark Web Monitoring Report** — for subscription clients (government/commercial)
  - **Credential Leak Alerts** — real-time, event-driven, per client
  - **Threat Actor Profiles** — quarterly, standalone or as part of retainer
  - **Dark Web Trend Briefs** — quarterly, executive-level, strategic trends
  - **IOC Feeds** — continuous, machine-readable (STIX/TAXII), feeds into GovSec TIP
- Maintain client delivery calendar and ensure timely report production
- Tailor reporting depth and format to audience (technical vs executive)

### Cross-Functional Intelligence Sharing (10%)

- Feed intelligence into broader Cyber Security Practice:
  - Threat Intelligence workstream — share IOCs, actor profiles, trend data
  - GovSec TIP — structured IOC feeds, threat bulletins
  - Blockchain Intelligence — dark web marketplaces using crypto (cross-pollination)
  - Professional Services — threat context for client advisory engagements
- Participate in weekly division operations meeting — intelligence brief segment
- Maintain intelligence sharing SOPs with information classification and handling markings

### Intelligence Collection Workflow

```
1. Source Identification — identify relevant forums, channels, marketplaces
2. Access Acquisition — register/monitoring accounts, maintain access
3. Collection — daily sweeps, automated scrapers, keyword alerts
4. Processing — de-duplicate, structure, enrich with context
5. Analysis — identify patterns, assess relevance, map to ATT&CK
6. Production — write intelligence product (report, alert, IOC feed)
7. Delivery — distribute to client, division, or GovSec TIP
8. Feedback — track accuracy, refine collection priorities
```

### Pre-Engagement Intelligence Package Structure

```
1. Executive Summary — key threats to this sector/organisation
2. Threat Actor Assessment — relevant actors, their TTPs, tooling
3. Credential Exposure — any leaked credentials for target domains
4. Infrastructure Exposure — Shodan/Censys findings, exposed services
5. OSINT Summary — surface web footprint, social media, tech stack
6. Actor Recommendation — which actor to emulate and why
7. TTP Recommendations — specific techniques to focus on
8. Confidence Assessment — source reliability and analytical confidence
```

## Why This Role

Dark web intelligence analysts typically sit in Security Operations Centres, monitoring for indicators and feeding alerts to blue teams. This role is different. You're embedded in a red team division — your intelligence directly shapes what the operators emulate, which TTPs they use, and which threat actors they replicate. You're not producing intelligence for a dashboard; you're producing intelligence that becomes an attack plan.

Additionally, your dark web monitoring becomes a standalone commercial service from Month 3 — providing immediate revenue while the red team capability matures. You're not waiting for the division to scale; you're the first revenue stream.
