## 9. CONCLUSIONS

### 9.1 Bottom Line
MD Ysahrudin is a **structurally competitive candidate** for Bukit Naning but faces **significant persuasion gaps** that could cost the seat if unaddressed. His profile fits the **Malay rural majority (61%)** well but leaves the **Chinese minority (37.64%)** unengaged—a dangerous imbalance in a marginal seat.

### 9.2 Critical Path to Victory
1. **Protect the base:** Deepen rural Malay engagement through agricultural smallholder listening tour
2. **Persuade the persuadables:** Chinese district town halls + SME/education policy pledges
3. **Mobilize the missing:** Youth digital campaign + first-time voter registration drive
4. **Close the credibility gap:** Publish 5-7 measurable constituency pledges within 2 weeks

### 9.3 Win Condition
**PH wins if:**
- Malay vote holds at 55%+ (some fragmentation acceptable)
- Chinese vote achieves 35%+ support (not unified against PH)

**PH loses if:**

- Malay vote splits 3 ways with PH <40%
