**Total Public Expenditure 2026: RM 470 billion**

- Federal Operating Expenditure (OE): RM 338.2 billion

- 2025 Total: RM 452 billion

## 📎 Appendix A: Full Budget Speech Excerpts (Cybersecurity & AI)

### Excerpt 1: National Security (Cyber Crime Package)

> "To tackle scams and cybercrime, a new Cybercrime Bill will be introduced to address rising online threats. The National Cyber Security Agency (NACSA) will establish a Cybersecurity and Cryptology Development Centre to strengthen national digital sovereignty."
> 
> "The National Scam Response Centre (NSRC), now directly managed by PDRM, will receive RM12 million to improve response time in financial fraud cases."
> 
> "In addition, PDRM's D11 Division will get RM20 million to modernise digital forensic systems and form a Behavioural Science Unit focused on analysing criminal behaviour and patterns."

### Excerpt 2: Enforcement Enhancement

> "For 2026, the enforcement capacity of key agencies, namely the MACC, RMP, KPDN, RMCD and MyCC, will be further strengthened through enhanced training programmes and acquisition of new assets, with an allocation of RM700 million."
> — Budget Speech Para 24

### Excerpt 3: Digital Government

> "MyDigital ID initiative aims to reach 15 million users by the end of 2025. The initiative will be extended to key sectors, including finance, telecommunications, e-commerce and healthcare."
> — Budget Speech Para 31

> "Leveraging internal expertise, the GovTech Malaysia Unit is being empowered to develop more government digital applications to enhance the efficiency of public service delivery."
> — Budget Speech Para 32
