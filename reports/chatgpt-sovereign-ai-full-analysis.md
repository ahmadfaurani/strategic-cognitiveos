## ✅ Validated Strengths (What We Got Right)

### 1. Core Strategic Thesis — VALIDATED
> "The proposal correctly positions sovereign AI as a national risk-control mechanism, not merely a technology procurement exercise."

**Key Validation:** Processing sovereignty > Hosting sovereignty
- Data centre investment alone = limited strategic control
- Real leverage: AI inference serving, fine-tuning platform, application harnesses, AI operations/governance, secure workflow integration, domestic model adaptation, government adoption layer

### 2. Utilization as Key Failure Risk — VALIDATED
> "The mandate funds the build. The unsolved problem is utilization."

**Recommended Reframe:**
> "Malaysia does not only need sovereign AI infrastructure. Malaysia needs a sovereign AI **operating layer** that controls sensitive processing, embeds adoption into daily government workflows, and converts national AI investment into measurable public-sector capability."

### 3. Value Chain Positioning — VALIDATED
> "The proposal correctly recommends that Malaysia move up the AI value chain instead of remaining a passive host for foreign platforms."

### 4. PPI Model — VALIDATED
> "Commercially sensible. Shifts government exposure from asset ownership to service consumption."

### 5. Customer-Managed Keys — VALIDATED
> "One of the proposal's most important technical claims. Addresses operator insider risk, foreign jurisdiction risk, and portability risk."

### 6. Bahasa Melayu LLM — VALIDATED (with caveat)
> "Strong national differentiation **if benchmark claims are independently verified**."

### 7. Google Workspace Agent — VALIDATED
> "Practical because it meets users where they already work."
