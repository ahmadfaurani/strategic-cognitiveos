## Executive Summary

Two renowned political scientists, **Prof James Chin** and **Dr Ong Kian Ming**, shared a pessimistic outlook for the ruling **Pakatan Harapan (PH)** coalition in the Negeri Sembilan state election. Both analysts predicted a strong "Blue Wave" favoring **Barisan Nasional (BN)**, potentially resulting in the loss of the PH state government and triggering political fallout in Melaka.

---

## Key Predictions & Projections [CRITICAL FOR PIR-PM-03]

### Dr Ong Kian Ming (Taylor's University / Former Bangi MP)
*   **Projection Date:** July 27
*   **Seat Count Forecast:**
    *   **BN:** 23 – 25 state seats
    *   **PH:** 11 – 13 state seats (PKR likely 1 seat; rest DAP)
*   **Chennah Seat (Anthony Loke):**
    *   Status: "Too close to call."
    *   Challenge: Anti-DAP/PH sentiment among the **48% Malay** constituency demographic.
*   **Orang Asli Vote:**
    *   Comprises **7%** of voters.
    *   Trend: Many view BN as their "protector."
    *   Impact: Swing sufficient to give BN a small margin of victory.

### Prof James Chin (Asia Institute, University of Tasmania / UTAR)
*   **DAP Chinese Seats:** Foresees **50/50 chances** of retaining the core seven seats.
*   **Chennah Seat:**
    *   Incumbent: **Anthony Loke** (DAP Sec-Gen & Transport Minister).
    *   History: Won in 2013, 2018, and 2023.
    *   Opponent: MCA's Siow Kong Choon (Straight fight).
    *   Assessment: "Even Chennah is 50/50 but Loke is fighting hard."
*   **Malay Seats:**
    *   **UMNO machinery** working effectively with **PAS ("Moon") machinery** at grassroots.
    *   Prediction: Majority of Malay seats to UMNO; a few to PAS.
*   **Menteri Besar:** Datuk Seri Aminuddin Harun expected to lose his seat.

---

## Critical Factors Influencing the Election

### 1. Grassroots Machinery
> "BN (Barisan Nasional) will sweep the Malay areas. The MB (Menteri Besar Datuk Seri Aminuddin Harun) will lose his seat. There will be no PH government on Sunday (Aug 2) morning."

### 2. Money Politics & Vote Buying
Chin noted significant cash distribution in Chinese areas, indicating PH desperation.

> "Lots of money is being distributed in Chinese areas. My goodness, it's a lot... if I were living in those areas, especially around PD (Port Dickson), my *Kopi-O money* would last for a whole month – or more."

*Context:* References a police report lodged over alleged vote-buying during Chuah campaign event.

### 3. Indian Community Sentiment
Chin identified a strategic error by Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim (DSAI).

> "(Moreover,) DSAI (Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim) made a strategic mistake by saying "sori" (apologising) to the Indian community. Now they're even more angry at PH."

### 4. Backlash on Projections
*   Ong Kian Ming faced backlash from DAP loyalists for his BN-favoring projection.
*   Ong clarified: "my prediction is based on projection of the likely Malay, Chinese, Indian and Others support for BN, PN and PH as opposed to who I'm supporting or not supporting."

---

## Potential Consequences

*   **Federal Impact:** Loss of N9 could trigger instability in neighboring states.
    > "If N9 falls, Melaka will dissolve this coming week."
*   **Government Status:** PH risks losing control of the N9 state government entirely on Sunday morning (Aug 2).
