## Background & Political Crisis

### 2026 Royal Crisis
*   **17 April 2026:** Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun announced the removal of **Datuk Mubarak Dohak** as Undang of Sungei Ujong for 33 alleged offences.
*   **19 April 2026:** Mubarak and three other Undangs declared the removal of **Tuanku Muhriz** (Yang di-Pertuan Besar) and named **Tunku Nadzaruddin** as the 12th ruler.
*   **20 April 2026:** Aminuddin rejected the declaration, citing Mubarak lacked authority. The Undangs claimed Aminuddin was unfit to lead.
*   **Impact:** State Legislative Assembly opening postponed indefinitely due to Undang boycott.

### Collapse of Coalition
*   **27 April 2026:** 14 UMNO assemblymen withdrew support for Aminuddin, accusing him of mishandling the royal crisis.
*   **30 April 2026:** Federal UMNO President Ahmad Zahid Hamidi announced UMNO would **remain in the unity government** with PH.
*   **24 May 2026:** DAP Secretary-General Anthony Loke called for dissolution due to "trust issues" with UMNO.
*   **4 June 2026:** Aminuddin announced snap election; Assembly dissolved on **5 June 2026** with Tuanku Muhriz's consent.

### BN-PN Alliance
*   BN entered an **informal election pact** with PN.
*   Observers attributed the result to a **strong Malay vote swing** and consolidation of Malay-Muslim support towards BN-PN.
*   PH suffered setbacks following defeats in 2025 Sabah and 2026 Johor state elections.

### High-Profile Defeats
