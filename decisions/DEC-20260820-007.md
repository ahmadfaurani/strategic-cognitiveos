# Context

Organizational structure was ambiguous during VoronCitadel POC Mode planning. Clarification needed to determine infrastructure ownership, product development authority, and hiring chain of command.

# Decision

```
CTO (Farul — STK-20260803-006)
├── Teras — Infrastructure Layer
│   ├── GPU Compute (NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell)
│   ├── AI Services (LLM, RAG, Vision, Speech, OCR)
│   ├── Governed AI Platform (Identity, API Gateway, Guardrails, Audit)
│   └── Applications (Akal Suite, Developer APIs)
│
└── CyberSecurity Practice (Sovereign Org, aligned with CTO)
    ├── Practice CTO: Fuad (STK-20260804-003)
    ├── VoronCitadel — GRC & Digital Risk Quantification
    ├── GovSec-TIP — Threat Intelligence Platform
    └── ChainSentry — Blockchain Forensics
```

# Rationale

1. **Teras as infrastructure** — GPU compute, deployment isolation, governance, and monitoring handled at platform level, not product level. Eliminates duplicate infrastructure builds per product.
2. **CyberSecurity Practice sovereignty** — Independent product roadmap and commercial authority. Products are not subordinate to Teras; they deploy ON Teras.
3. **Fuad as Practice CTO** — Practice-level technical authority for all 3 cybersecurity products. Not org CTO (Farul). ~0.3 FTE allocable to VoronCitadel specifically.
4. **Alignment with CTO** — Practice operates under Farul's technical oversight but maintains product independence.

# Alternatives Considered

- **Practice as subordinate to Teras:** Rejected — would make product decisions subordinate to infrastructure decisions, slowing POC velocity.
- **Practice as separate from CTO:** Rejected — needs technical alignment with infrastructure layer for integration.

Confirmed by DAF via Telegram, 2026-08-20.
