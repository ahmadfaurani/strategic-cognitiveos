# Decision

## Context

DAF reviewed the verified chain:SENTRY doc suite (Sep 7 delivery) and the Weekly Strategic Brief decision list. The structural question: Op Plan v1.1 requires 5–7 FTE (7 recommended, 5 = constrained floor) while the Technical Execution Unit is 2 FTE (Fuad + Syahir) under a no-hire-before-January-2027 directive.

## Decision (DAF, 2026-09-08 03:18 UTC)

1. **Follow Operational Plan v1.1 moving forward** — the 7-FTE capacity model, phase gates, and operational controls become the governing operating model for chain:SENTRY.
2. **Hiring JD review for each FTE** in the coverage model.
3. **Hadri delivers the Overall JD pack by 16 September 2026** (ACT-20260908-002).
