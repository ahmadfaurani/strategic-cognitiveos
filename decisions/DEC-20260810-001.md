# Context

Following the GovSec × CSM SiberSUITE integration session on 10 August 2026 at Menara CyberAxis, DAF identified three immediate collaboration areas where GovSec can complement and integrate with CSM's existing SiberSUITE capabilities without duplicating established functions. The session provided clarity on SiberSUITE's technical functions, feature set, and operational perimeter.

The original TI integration initiative (INIT-20260804-003) was scoped around threat intelligence data feeds and taxonomy. The session revealed that the integration opportunity is broader — encompassing telemetry, scoring, and AI-enabled component analysis.

# Decision

Three immediate collaboration areas are formally identified and prioritised:

1. **Telemetry Integration** — Identify relevant SiberSUITE telemetry, threat intelligence feeds, and integration points that can be consumed, correlated, and analysed through GovSec.

2. **Cybersecurity Score Card Framework** — Jointly explore an evidence-driven framework to provide operational and management visibility into cybersecurity posture, risk, exposure, and remediation progress. Focus: CNII Sector Scoring.

3. **CBOM Agent** — Assess the potential for an AI-enabled agent to continuously analyse cyber components, dependencies, vulnerabilities, and associated threat intelligence, with findings feeding into the wider GovSec analytics and scorecard framework.

Technical follow-up coordination assigned to Hadri and Fuad.

The broader integration architecture is articulated as: SiberSUITE → Telemetry → GovSec Analytics → CBOM Agent → Cybersecurity Score Card.

# Rationale

- **Complementarity over competition:** The session confirmed GovSec should complement SiberSUITE rather than duplicate it. The three areas leverage GovSec's analytics and AI capabilities on top of SiberSUITE's telemetry and operational data.
- **CNII sector need:** A cybersecurity score card for CNII sectors addresses a documented national requirement for posture visibility — no existing product currently serves this niche comprehensively.
- **AI differentiation:** The CBOM Agent introduces an AI-native capability that differentiates the joint offering and connects to Aras's broader AI agent architecture.
- **CyberDSA readiness:** All three areas contribute to a demonstrable joint product for the CyberDSA October 2026 launch milestone.

# Alternatives Considered

- **Single-track TI integration only (original scope):** Rejected — the session revealed broader integration opportunity that would be artificially constrained by limiting to TI feeds alone.
- **Sequential rollout (Telemetry → Score Card → CBOM):** Not explicitly rejected but the email framing treats all three as parallel immediate areas, suggesting concurrent exploration.
- **Score Card as standalone product (without CSM partnership):** Not discussed — the joint exploration framing indicates CSM's involvement is essential for CNII sector legitimacy.

# Assumptions

- CSM is willing to expose SiberSUITE telemetry data for GovSec consumption
- CNII sector scoring framework is a shared priority for both CSM and Aras
- The CBOM Agent concept is new (not an existing CSM or Aras product)
- Hadri and Fuad have capacity to coordinate technical follow-up across three sub-workstreams
- The CyberDSA October milestone remains the delivery target
