# DEC-20260819-006 — CyberDSA 2026 Market Positioning Narrative Adoption

## Decision

**APPROVED:** Adopt "National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment" as the definitive CyberDSA 2026 market positioning narrative.

## Decided By

DAF (Faurani Jaafar), Director — Cyber Security Practice, Aras Integrasi

## Date

19 August 2026, 12:36 MYT

## Context

Rashid Bin Ramli (WIG) requested topline messaging and narrative direction for CyberDSA 2026 communications, proposing "National Grid of Sovereign AI Full Stack for Malaysia" as a possible anchor. Given T-49 timeline pressure and Amelia Nadia being out of office, DAF provided the definitive narrative response.

## Decision Detail

### Approved Headline
**National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment**

### 6-Layer Narrative Framework
1. National Story — Malaysia building its own cybersecurity technology ecosystem
2. Aras Story — Aras as Malaysian sovereign technology integrator
3. Technology Story — Integrated capability across governance, intelligence, operations, emerging tech
4. Partnership Story — Collaboration with CSM and institutional expertise
5. CyberDSA Story — Platform to demonstrate cohesive cybersecurity stack
6. Market Story — Government/CNII/GLC engage around capability deployment, not individual products

### Key Reframe
- "National Grid of Sovereign AI Full Stack for Malaysia" → "National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment"
- "AI" → "Technology" (broader scope)
- "Grid" → "Grade" (quality, not topology)
- "for Malaysia" → "Deployment" (operational, not aspirational)

### Application
Anchor for: booth, product messaging, social media, media engagement, Flash Talk. Branding team retains creative flexibility in translation.

### CSM Partnership
CyberSecurity Malaysia explicitly named as strategic ecosystem partner in the narrative.

## Rationale

1. **Strategic alignment** — Mirrors NACSA's own "sovereign cyber security resilience" language (MCSS 2025-2030). Positions Aras as implementing existing national direction.
2. **Dr. Megat engagement** — Aligns with INT-20260819-001 recommended narrative: "Malaysia is reaching the point where sovereign cybersecurity capability can move from policy ambition into operational deployment."
3. **Scope correction** — "Technology" is broader than "AI" — encompasses full cybersecurity lifecycle (governance, intelligence, operations, emerging tech), not just AI-enabled components.
4. **Positioning** — "National-Grade" establishes quality benchmark. "Deployment" signals operational readiness, not concept-stage.

## Implications

- All CyberDSA 2026 external communications derive from this 6-layer framework
- Branding team (Azzatullina, Norshaza, Rashid) to develop creative implementation
- Product messaging positioned as enabling components within national capability architecture
- CSM partnership publicly articulated
- DAF to synch with Azzatullina (Aug 20-21) to initiate creative development
