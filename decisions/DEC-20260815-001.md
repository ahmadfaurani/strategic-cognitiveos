# Decision Record

## Decision

The R.I.S.I.K AI Platform Development & Operationalisation Programme cost structure is formalised at **RM5,000,000** over **12 months**, structured across 9 cost components:

1. R.I.S.I.K Framework & IP Development — RM750,000 (15%)
2. AI Engineering & Intelligence Capability Development — RM900,000 (18%)
3. Dedicated 7-Person Development Team — RM1,800,000 (36%)
4. AI Infrastructure, Tooling & Development Environment — RM550,000 (11%)
5. Data, Integration & Production Deployment — RM450,000 (9%)
6. Cybersecurity, AI Assurance & Validation — RM200,000 (4%)
7. Training, Knowledge Transfer & Documentation — RM100,000 (2%)
8. Programme Governance, IP, Legal & Administration — RM100,000 (2%)
9. Technical / Programme Contingency — RM150,000 (3%)

## Rationale

- **Asset creation focus:** 69% of programme value in direct IP creation, AI engineering, and dedicated human capital
- **Production orientation:** Funding includes data integration, deployment, security assurance, UAT, documentation, and knowledge transfer — not just MVP
- **AI-specific costing:** GPU capacity, model consumption, vector/database services, MLOps recognised as first-class cost drivers
- **Assurance and governance:** Cybersecurity, AI-output validation, auditability explicitly funded (4%)
- **Controlled contingency:** Only 3% reserved — preserves cost discipline
- **MCMC as target:** MCMC (Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission) identified as the proposed buyer/funder

## Strategic Implications

1. **INIT-20260803-002 advancement:** R.I.S.I.K moves from "coordination" stage to "cost-structured, fundable programme" — the framework now has a concrete commercial pathway
2. **MCMC engagement:** This creates a formal funding/selling pathway with MCMC, expanding the stakeholder ecosystem beyond UiTM + PMO
3. **UiTM collaboration impact:** The cost structure provides the commercial backbone that the UiTM academic collaboration (DEC-20260807-001) needs to transition from research to operational capability
4. **Stage-gated delivery:** 5 formal stage gates (G1-G5) provide structured acceptance milestones for MCMC oversight
5. **Portfolio impact:** R.I.S.I.K transitions from Tier 2 (Incubation) toward Tier 1 (Flagship) contingent on MCMC engagement

## Alternatives Considered

- **Lower cost positioning:** Rejected — the document explicitly argues against evaluating RM5M against developer headcount alone; the programme creates durable IP, operational capability, and reusable strategic assets
- **Phased funding (smaller initial commitment):** The 5 stage-gate model allows MCMC to evaluate at G1/G2 before full commitment, but the cost structure is presented as a single 12-month programme
- **Different funder:** PMO was CC'd on original UiTM collaboration (Aug 3); MCMC as communications regulator is a more natural funder for an information warfare / strategic intelligence platform

## Conditions

- MCMC must be engaged with the proposal
- Cost structure is an "analytical planning model rather than a contractual billing schedule"
- Stage gates provide acceptance checkpoints but require MCMC agreement on criteria
