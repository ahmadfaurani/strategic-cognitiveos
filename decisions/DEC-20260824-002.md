# Context

The Aug 24 Bursa POC meeting discussed both the immediate Phase 0 scope and the broader 24-entity federation vision. The 24-entity model was introduced in the earlier Aug 24 meeting (referenced in CONV-20260822-002). Without formal designation as a hypothesis, there is risk that stakeholders treat the federation as committed architecture.

# Decision

1. The ~24-entity federation is formally a **target operating-model hypothesis**
2. It is **NOT a committed scope** for Phase 0
3. The **Validation Phase** must complete before any scale decision:
   - URD/SRS
   - Architecture & Capacity Validation
   - Bursa Acceptance
   - Scale Decision
4. The **shared-vendor model** must be validated against actual participant vendor inventories before being treated as an architectural or commercial assumption

# Rationale

- Prevents architectural overreach within Bursa's 4-month timeline
- Ensures the POC is evaluated on Phase 0 TPRM value delivery
- Provides a structured validation gate before scaling
- Protects against unvalidated assumptions being baked into commercial or architectural commitments
