# Decision Record

## Finding

PRISM is an Aras Integrasi product — an AI-powered campaign intelligence platform with 4-layer architecture (Command/Operations/Intelligence/Administration), 6 autonomous AI agents, multi-database backend, and continuous loop design.

**PRISM 2.0 = PRISM 1.0 (electoral) + R.I.S.I.K doctrine (information warfare) = KKOM system**

The integration is internal product evolution. Not connecting to an external PMO system — evolving Aras's own platform.

## What This Changes

1. Build effort revised from ~58 person-days (16 functions) to ~45 person-days (18 functions) — 22% reduction
2. Stages 1-2 of Aliran Kerja build sequence are partially complete via existing PRISM infrastructure
3. "Developer" question from Aliran Kerja answered: Aras/Farul's team
4. "Technical companion" maps to Hadri/Fuad
5. MCMC proposal narrative shifts from "building from scratch" to "enhancing operational platform"
6. UiTM role shifts from co-builder to validator of R.I.S.I.K doctrine implementation ON PRISM
