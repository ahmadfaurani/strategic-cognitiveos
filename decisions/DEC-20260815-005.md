# Decision

<!-- See frontmatter decision field -->

2. Confirmation of availability, capacity, and reporting line
3. Definition of authority scope and written mandate

# Rationale

<!-- See frontmatter rationale field -->

1. **Already functioning in role:** Amelia is already coordinating CSM-Aras communication, managing event logistics, and serving as the communication bridge between Aras and CSM (via Aisha). She is not a new entrant — she is already doing parts of the job.

2. **DAF trust established:** DAF's Aug 15 reply explicitly praised her diligence, responsiveness, and collaboration. The trust relationship is already active.

3. **Cross-organisational positioning:** As a WIG team member (not Aras direct staff), Amelia occupies a neutral coordination position that works across Aras, CSM, and WIG stakeholder groups. This is structurally advantageous for a coordinator role.

4. **SPOF reduction:** Appointing Amelia as Programme Coordinator would reduce DAF from 21 to ~14 criteria (33% reduction), create a coordination layer for all 6 execution dimensions, and enable transfer of tracker ownership (Metric 7) and weekly review facilitation (Action #7).

5. **Compounding effect:** One appointment unblocks:
   - Metric 8 (SPOF #1 and #4)
   - Metric 7 (tracker ownership shifts from Hadri to PC)
   - Action #7 (weekly review facilitation)
   - Action #4 (stakeholder coverage tracker creation)
   - Gap 1 from INT-003 (coordination layer)

# Alternatives Considered

1. **Internal Aras hire (new candidate):** Slower — would require recruitment, onboarding, and context-building. No existing trust relationship with DAF or CSM. Time cost: 2-4 weeks minimum. **Rejected** — CyberDSA is in 7 weeks.

2. **Assign to Hadri:** Hadri already carries 9 CyberDSA criteria and is primary on 4 CSM stakeholders. Adding coordinator role would create a new SPOF on Hadri. **Rejected** — trades one bottleneck for another.

3. **Assign to Shuhada:** Shuhada is solo on 8 commercial pipeline criteria. Adding coordinator role would worsen her existing SPOF. **Rejected** — same reason as Hadri.

4. **DAF retains role with deputy support:** Partial mitigation but doesn't address the structural gap. DAF remains the bottleneck. **Rejected** — incremental, not structural.

5. **External consultant:** Fast onboarding but lacks organisational context, stakeholder relationships, and trust capital. **Rejected** — context cost too high for a 7-week sprint.
