# DEC-20260908-003 — R.I.S.I.K Cost Structure Revised to RM3.8M (V2)

## Decision

The operative R.I.S.I.K AI Platform Development & Operationalisation Programme figure is **RM3,800,000 (V2)**, effective immediately and applying to the MCMC evaluation session on Thu 10 Sep 2026, 15:00-16:00 MYT. The RM5.0M figure of DEC-20260815-001 is superseded. All MCMC-facing materials, prep documents, and narrative assets cite RM3.8M from this decision forward.
