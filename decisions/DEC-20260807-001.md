# Context

DAF sent a formal collaboration proposal to Prof. Madya Dr. Suhaimee (Director, UiTM Centre for Media and Information Warfare Studies) on 3 August 2026, proposing strategic collaboration on AI Enablement of the R.I.S.I.K Framework. The proposal outlined joint AI-driven framework development, research, datasets, simulations, capability-building, and proof of concept development. The initiative was registered as INIT-20260803-002 at Draft/Framed stage. Two PMO officials were CC'd on the original email, signaling PMO-level awareness.

# Decision

UiTM, through Prof. Madya Dr. Suhaimee as Director of the Centre for Media and Information Warfare Studies, formally expresses **agreement in principle** to explore the collaboration further. The decision includes:

1. Acceptance that the proposed initiative presents "significant potential in advancing research and practical capabilities in strategic communication, media and information warfare, and AI-enabled decision support"
2. Designation of 5 team members for coordination, including the Director himself
3. Commitment to coordinate on next working session, project planning, and subsequent activities

# Rationale

UiTM's response references "previous meeting" discussions, confirming pre-existing relationship and contextual understanding. The response is warm, prompt (4 days), and designates institutional resources (5 named staff with contact details) — all indicators of genuine academic intent rather than diplomatic deferral.

# Alternatives Considered

- **Decline collaboration** — not chosen; UiTM explicitly agreed
- **Request modifications to scope** — not raised; UiTM accepted the proposed scope without amendments
- **Defer response** — not chosen; response came within 4 days, well within DAF's 14-day success metric

# Assumptions

- "Agreement in principle" means UiTM is prepared to proceed with working sessions and scoping discussions, but formal binding commitments (MoU, funding, IP) are not yet made
- The 5 designated team members have been consulted and are available for coordination
- Prof. Suhaimee's signature as Director carries institutional authority for this level of agreement
