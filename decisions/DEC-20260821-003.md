**Hadri:** Consumed by CSM/customer-success responsibilities — not available product engineering capacity.

**Fuad:** Practice CTO/architecture/vision — not a daily production engineer.

**Hiring Model (Athena #23):** Head of Engineering + Customer Success Engineer + Junior Backend = RM39,656/month / RM475,968/year.

**Decision needed:** Convert hiring model into management resource request/operationalisation plan. This is a commercial decision (budget approval), not a technical one.

**Impact if delayed:** Bursa POC, CyberDSA demos, and any additional POCs from CSM stakeholder activation all bottleneck through 2 people who are already at capacity. The phase transition from product creation to activation will stall.
