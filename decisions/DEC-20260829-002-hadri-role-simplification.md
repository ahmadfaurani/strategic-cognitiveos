# Decision: Hadri Role Simplification — Lead Architect Removed

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Authority:** DAF
**Status:** DECIDED

## Decision

Remove "Lead Architect" title from Hadri's role portfolio effective immediately. Hadri's role going forward is **Cybersecurity Practice COO** — focused on operational coordination, stakeholder management, roadmap ownership, and practice governance.

## Rationale

Hadri has been carrying three hats (COO + Lead Architect + Blockchain Lead) with ~0 FTE for VoronCitadel and SPOF status across 4 CSM tracks. The Lead Architect role was absorbing technical delivery capacity that should be distributed across the team. Removing it concentrates Hadri on the COO function where his relational and operational strengths are most valuable.

## Impact

- **chain:SENTRY engineering:** Moves to Syahir (DEC-20260829-004). Hadri retains roadmap ownership only.
- **Teras validation:** Hadri's technical validation role for the infrastructure layer (DEC-20260820-009) transitions to engineering team over time.
- **CSM tracks:** Hadri retains CSM stakeholder ownership (5 primary stakeholders) — this is COO-level relationship management, not architecture.
- **TBH-001 reporting:** TBH-001 (PM) continues to report to Hadri as Practice COO. Unchanged.
- **Risks closed:** RSK-20260820-004 (HoE dual-role risk) — partially mitigated. Hadri is NOT being considered for HoE. HoE will be a separate decision post-Fuad career conversation.

## Previous Role

COO / Lead Architect / Blockchain Lead (three hats)

## New Role

Cybersecurity Practice COO (single focus)
