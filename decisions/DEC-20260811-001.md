# Context

On Aug 10, DAF declared a development freeze on GovSec TIP specifically (DEC-20260810-002), pivoting to CyberDSA launch readiness. On Aug 11, DAF escalated this significantly: the freeze now applies to ALL THREE Cybersecurity Flagship Products — VoronCitadel, GovSec TIP, and ChainSentry. The strategic rationale is that sufficient MVP capabilities exist across all three products, and the focus must shift from continuous development to productisation, commercial readiness, and GTM execution.

The email was sent to Ahmad Fuad (Product Owner) with Hadri, Farul, Syahir, and Kenny Kok cc'd — indicating this is a formal, broad-circulation directive, not a private conversation.

DAF references "my earlier email" suggesting this freeze was communicated informally before this formal written directive.

# Decision

**1. Expanded Development Freeze:** Effective immediately, all feature development across all three Cybersecurity Flagship Products (VoronCitadel, GovSec TIP, ChainSentry) is paused. Development is restricted to:
- Critical bug fixes and platform stability
- Security remediation
- Issues that materially affect demos, POCs, or customer engagements
- Previously committed integration requirements necessary for commercialisation

**2. DevSecOps Intern Notification:** Fuad to pass the freeze directive to the devsecops intern, with Fuad and Hadri as overseers.

**3. Centralised Product Repository:** All product documentation to be consolidated into a single centralised repository with a proper product baseline.

**4. Six-Category Productisation Framework:** For each of the three products, the following must be compiled/developed:

| Category | Status | Contents |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Current MVP Specification | ✅ Exists for all 3 | Functions, features, architecture, deployment requirements, limitations, gaps |
| Product Roadmap | ❌ Pending | MVP baseline, near-term requirements, future capabilities, Must/Should/Future prioritisation |
| Product Backlog | ❌ Pending | Outstanding tech items, defects, integration requirements, enhancement requests |
| Commercialisation Readiness | Varies | Target customers, deployment model, licensing/packaging, POC requirements, production dependencies |
| Sales & GTM Materials | Varies | One-pager, sales deck, demo flow, tech FAQ, sales kit |
| Product Governance | ❌ Pending | Product owner, technical owner, version baseline, documentation owner, change/release management |

**Commercialisation & Sales readiness by product:**
- VoronCitadel: Ready
- GovSec TIP: Partial
- ChainSentry: Partial

**5. Current vs Future Separation:** All documentation must clearly separate what exists today from what is intended to be built in the future.

# Rationale

- **Feature maturity reached across products:** DAF assesses sufficient tangible MVP capabilities exist across all three products to shift from development to productisation
- **Productisation gap:** Products exist technically but lack the structured documentation, governance, and commercial readiness needed for GTM execution
- **CyberDSA October milestone:** GovSec TIP must be demo-ready; the other products need proper baselines for commercial conversations
- **Governance deficit:** No formal product governance (owners, version baselines, change management) exists for any product
- **Commercial readiness variance:** VoronCitadel is assessed as "Ready" while GovSec and ChainSentry are "Partial" — the framework ensures each product has a clear path to commercial readiness

# Alternatives Considered

- **Product-by-product freeze (GovSec only):** Rejected — DAF explicitly extended beyond GovSec to all three products, indicating the productisation challenge is portfolio-wide
- **Continue selective feature development:** Rejected — DAF's language is definitive: "formally pause all further feature development"
- **External product management hire first:** Not discussed — the directive assigns execution to existing team (Fuad, Hadri) with governance from DAF
