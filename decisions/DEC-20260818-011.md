# Context

The CyberDSA 2026 positioning statement (DEC-20260816-002) established the headline: "National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment." This decision provides the complete media and brand narrative framework that operationalises that positioning for the branding team.

# Decision

1. **Adopt** DOC-20260818-002 (CyberDSA 2026 Key Media & Brand Narrative) as the authoritative brand framework
2. **Direct** the branding team to frame all media, PR, and communications around sovereign capability — not individual products
3. **Establish** the branding hierarchy: Sovereign Capability → Integrated Stack → Technology Pillars → Individual Features
4. **Adopt** campaign line: "Built in Malaysia. Integrated for Malaysia. Engineered for Sovereignty."
5. **Adopt** corporate positioning: "Aras Integrasi — Malaysian Sovereign Technology Integrator"
6. **Enforce** messaging guardrails (§9 of DOC-20260818-002) — avoid product-vendor language, unsubstantiated claims, and endorsement implications
7. **Direct** visual brand narrative to present three products as components of one architecture

# Rationale

- Product-level positioning reduces Aras to "another cybersecurity vendor" — the strategic differentiator is integration and capability ownership
- The sovereign capability framing encompasses ecosystem collaboration (CSM), infrastructure sovereignty, and engineering capability — none of which are captured at product level
- Messaging guardrails directly address RSK-20260816-003 ("Malaysia's First" claim risk) by prohibiting unverified superlatives
- The narrative provides a complete framework for all downstream work: one-pagers, demo materials, social media, press releases, stakeholder briefings
