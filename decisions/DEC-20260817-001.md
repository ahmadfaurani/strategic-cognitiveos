## Decision Statement

**Establish a weekly Portfolio, GTM & Delivery Review meeting every Tuesday at 2:30 PM MYT.**

This meeting serves as the primary execution governance mechanism for:
1. Portfolio prioritization across all workstreams
2. Go-to-market alignment (product, commercial, marketing)
3. Delivery accountability and risk escalation
4. Cross-workstream dependency management

---

## Context & Rationale

### Problem Statement

DAF's profile (USER.md) identifies a core constraint:

> **Execution sustainability: 3/5** — Workload and cognitive switching risk

> **Principal Development Challenge:** "From being the primary driver of every strategic initiative to becoming the architect of an institution capable of driving those initiatives at scale."

Without structured governance, strategic initiatives fragment across:
- Multiple stakeholders (CSM, Aras, MTAI, WIG, PERJASA, JDN)
- Parallel workstreams (CyberDSA launch, Co-Design Lab, GTM activation, productisation)
- Competing priorities (urgent vs. important)
- Execution dependencies (Fuad's technical deliverables, Hadri's stakeholder coordination, DAF's strategic mobilisation)

### Why This Decision Now

1. **CyberDSA War-Room Mode (DEC-20260815-004)** — 7 weeks to Oct 5-7, 2026 launch milestone
2. **Multiple P0 deadlines converging** — Aug 17 alone has 2 P0 items due (CSM mobilisation plan, GovSec Roadmap)
3. **Portfolio complexity** — 6 workstream clusters (USER.md) requiring coordination
4. **Execution accountability gap** — Need for weekly checkpoint to catch slippage early

## Operating Model

### Meeting Structure

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Cadence** | Weekly, every Tuesday |
| **Time** | 2:30 PM MYT (14:30) |
| **Duration** | 60 minutes (standard); 90 minutes if deep-dive required |
| **Format** | In-person or hybrid (Menara TM Level 30 / virtual) |
| **Chair** | DAF |
| **Secretary** | Rotating (Hadri, Fuad, or delegated) |
| **First Meeting** | Tuesday, August 19, 2026, 2:30 PM MYT |

### Core Participants

| Role | Person | Mandatory |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Portfolio Owner | DAF | Yes |
| Solutions Architect / CSM Liaison | Hadri | Yes |
| Product Owner (VoronDRQ, GovSec) | Fuad | Yes |
| Sales Coordination | Shuhada | As needed |
| Marketing Lead | Azzatullina / Amelia | As needed |

### Agenda Template

1. **Portfolio Review (15 min)**
   - Initiative status updates (Red/Amber/Green)
   - Deadline tracking (what's due this week, what slipped)
   - Resource constraints

2. **GTM Alignment (20 min)**
   - Product readiness (Voron Citadel, GovSec TIP, ChainSentry)
   - Commercial pipeline (lighthouse accounts, stakeholder mapping)
   - Marketing activation (CyberDSA, media coverage, events)

3. **Delivery Accountability (15 min)**
   - Action item review from previous week
   - Dependency blockers
   - Escalation decisions

4. **Risk & Escalation (10 min)**
   - Execution risks identified
   - Mitigation actions assigned
   - DAF decisions required
