# Context

The GovSec programme moved from mobilisation → product alignment → formal technical integration. The 4 Sep CSM digital signature on the CMERP × GovSec Technical Engineering Paper provided the endorsement trigger. A formal phase-transition decision was needed for management noting and downstream governance.

# Decision

The signed Technical Engineering Paper is the transition point from planning into controlled implementation and validation, with strict scope governance over all proposed future-state capabilities.

# Rationale

External technical endorsement is the strongest available anchor for a phase gate. The freeze (DEC-20260810-002) already concentrates effort on stability, hardening, documentation, handover and launch readiness — the decision aligns governance with the operating reality and the CyberDSA runway.

# Alternatives Considered

- Internal sign-off as transition anchor — insufficient external validation weight
- Defer transition to post-acceptance-testing — loses momentum and management noting value
