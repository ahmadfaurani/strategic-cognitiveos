# Context

CyberDSA 2026 requires coordinated stakeholder activation across CSM and NACSA. Prior engagement has been ad-hoc and personality-driven. The framework (DOC-20260819-001) establishes a deliberate sequence with 6 stakeholders, 6 decision gates, 6 waves, and 8 immediate action items.

# Decision

Adopt the CyberDSA 2026 Executive Stakeholder Engagement & Activation Framework as the authoritative operational playbook:

1. **6-stakeholder dependency chain:** Azrul → Zulfeka → Bala → Wan Roshaimi → Zaharudin → Dr. Megat
2. **6 decision gates:** Each gate has a control point blocking progression until closed
3. **6-wave activation plan:** 19 Aug through CyberDSA, with dependency order preserved
4. **Activation doctrine (6 principles):**
   - Align before asking
   - One stakeholder, one primary ask
   - Build internal coherence before external validation (CSM before NACSA)
   - Make participation valuable
   - Convert verbal support into operating behaviour
   - Keep the narrative integrated
5. **Aras owns POC delivery** — CSM technical leadership validates strategy/governance but is NOT asked to provide POC delivery resources
6. **NACSA engagement only after CSM alignment gates substantially complete** — approach with evidence, not as unformed endorsement request

# Rationale

- Prevents fragmented CSM buy-in (one narrative spine across all engagements)
- Prevents premature NACSA approach (Gate 6 requires evidence of prior CSM alignment)
- Prevents personality-dependency (institutional pathway, not individual relationships)
- Prevents verbal-only support (every meeting closes with owner, due date, written recap)
- Prevents parallel conversation conflicts (single owner, master tracker, sequenced engagements)
- Creates clear accountability chain with one primary ask per stakeholder
- Dependency chain ensures each stakeholder receives proposition at right maturity level

# Alternatives Considered

1. **Continue ad-hoc engagement** — rejected: produces fragmented messaging, unaccountable commitments, personality-dependency
2. **Engage all stakeholders simultaneously** — rejected: violates dependency chain logic; CSM alignment must precede NACSA; each stakeholder needs the proposition at a different maturity level
3. **Skip NACSA alignment** — rejected: national strategic alignment is required for sovereign capability positioning
