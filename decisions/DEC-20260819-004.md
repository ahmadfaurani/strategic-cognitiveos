# Context

Azrul Nazim Abdul Aziz has been an operational counterpart for the CSM × Aras relationship since at least October 2025. His previous classification as "Industry Engagement" (confirmed Aug 14, 2026) understated his strategic function. Accumulated discussions establish his primary value as:

- Partnership alignment and orchestration
- Institutional navigation (which CSM org owns what, what pathway)
- Stakeholder mobilisation (who participates, who sponsors)
- Collaboration continuity (preventing fragmentation)

DAF's correction (Aug 19, 2026): Azrul's value is NOT technical execution. He should NOT be positioned as technical POC owner. His value is much more important at the institutional alignment, partnership orchestration, and internal mobilisation layer.

The CyberDSA 2026 activation requires a CSM Activation Triangle (Fahdzli/Zulfeka/Azrul) to be aligned before broader CSM machinery engagement.

# Decision

## Azrul — Partnership & Alliance Anchor

**Primary Mission:** Ensure that the CSM × Aras partnership is institutionally aligned and that the appropriate CSM stakeholders are mobilised around agreed strategic initiatives.

**What Azrul SHOULD do:**
1. Align on the joint CSM × Aras strategic narrative
2. Validate CyberDSA as an appropriate partnership and market-activation milestone
3. Help identify and activate the correct CSM stakeholders
4. Facilitate cross-functional coordination where required
5. Maintain continuity between existing CSM–Aras collaboration and CyberDSA activation
6. Help position subsequent commercial, technical and strategic engagements through correct institutional channels

**What Azrul should NOT do:**
1. Technical POC support
2. Product demonstration support
3. Deployment owner
4. Engineering coordinator
5. Sales execution owner
6. Operational project manager for every CSM workstream

**Additional constraint:** Do NOT automatically align Wan Roshaimi (STK-20260812-001) or Zaharudin (STK-20260804-011) as POC-support resources merely because they are established CSM contacts. Technical participation should be activated only after the requirement has been defined and the appropriate CSM owner identified. This protects the institutional relationship from becoming personality-dependent.

## Key Distinction

Azrul should help OPEN and ORGANISE the institutional pathway. He should NOT be made responsible for EXECUTING every activity travelling through that pathway.

# Rationale

- Azrul's value is upstream — he sits at the beginning of the stakeholder activation sequence, not inside the POC execution layer
- His alignment enables: partnership legitimacy → correct stakeholder identification → internal CSM mobilisation → commercial/technical/communication workstreams → CyberDSA activation → post-CyberDSA market activation
- Misallocating him to delivery roles would waste his institutional multiplier function
- The constraint on auto-aligning Wan Roshaimi/Zaharudin protects against personality-dependency
- The engagement framing (joint design, not resource request) preserves his institutional standing and gives him agency

# Alternatives Considered

1. **Keep 'Industry Engagement' label** — rejected: understates his strategic function; does not reflect partnership orchestration role
2. **Position Azrul as technical POC with partnership overlay** — rejected: DAF explicitly corrected this — his value is NOT technical execution
3. **No formal mandate boundary** — rejected: risks misallocation to delivery roles and personality-dependency in the CSM relationship

# Confirmation

Confirmed by DAF via Telegram direct, 2026-08-19. Comprehensive working stakeholder profile provided with explicit mandate boundaries, engagement approach, and activation triangle positioning.
