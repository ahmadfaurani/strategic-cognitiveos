# Decision

Proceed with internal mobilisation across three parallel CSM workstreams to achieve product-market readiness for the **joint product launch with CSM at CyberDSA in early October 2026**.

# Scope of Decision

## Mobilisation Components

1. **Ownership assignment** — Clear ownership and supporting roles for each workstream (WS1: VoronDRQ GTM, WS2: CMERP Integration, WS3: TI Integration)
2. **Consolidated calendar** — Single engagement calendar and deliverables register across all 3 workstreams
3. **Integrated delivery plan** — Technical delivery plan for WS2 + WS3 aligned to CyberDSA milestone
4. **Interim intern deployment** — Newly hired intern(s) deployed for research, documentation, meeting preparation, and action tracking (strictly interim)
5. **Risk escalation** — Early escalation of technical dependencies and delivery risks
6. **Senior GovSec hiring escalation** — Hiring recategorised from manpower consideration to delivery-readiness requirement

## Delivery Milestone

- **Event:** CyberDSA 2026
- **Timing:** Early October 2026
- **Deliverable:** Joint product launch with CSM
- **Affected workstreams:** WS2 (CMERP) and WS3 (TI) are essential; WS1 (VoronDRQ GTM) is parallel commercial track

# Rationale

- Three CSM workstreams initiated in a single day (Aug 4) with compressed and overlapping timelines
- Workstreams 2 and 3 are technical prerequisites for GovSec product-market readiness
- Current delivery capacity is strained — single delivery owner (Hadri) across WS2 and WS3, single strategic owner (DAF) across all three
- Senior GovSec resource still pending — scope, stakeholder intensity, and technical complexity exceed intern-level capacity
- August is the critical window: any delay in technical scoping, validation, or dependency resolution directly affects CyberDSA readiness
- Intern deployment is explicitly a stopgap — not a substitute for the Senior GovSec resource
