# DEC-20260819-002 — HOI-Intelligence-Operations Repo Decommissioned from CognitiveOS Canonical Build

## Decision

HOI-Intelligence-Operations repository is decommissioned and no longer part of the CognitiveOS canonical build. `strategic-cognitiveos` is the sole canonical source for Honcho integration.

## Rationale

- HOI-Intel-Workspace was 6 commits behind remote and not actively maintained
- CognitiveOS has consolidated around `strategic-cognitiveos` as the single source of truth
- Reduces complexity and eliminates redundant data paths for Honcho ingestion
- Aligns with the design principle: "GitHub is the book. Honcho is the index."

## Impact

- Phase 2 batch ingestion sources exclusively from `strategic-cognitiveos`
- No HOI records will be ingested into Honcho
- HOI repo may be archived at DAF's discretion
