## Positioning Statement

**Aras Integrasi Sdn Bhd and its ecosystem partners demonstrate the capability to design, integrate, and operationalise sovereign cybersecurity technology end-to-end — delivering national-grade, full-stack protection for Malaysia's critical digital infrastructure.**

---

## Headline

**National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment**

---

## The Full Story

Aras Integrasi is not exhibiting individual products at CyberDSA 2026. Aras is demonstrating that a Malaysian technology organisation, working with its ecosystem partners including CyberSecurity Malaysia, can architect, integrate, and operationalise a complete sovereign cybersecurity capability stack — from governance, risk and compliance through threat intelligence and blockchain security, backed by operational deployment and institutional collaboration.

This is a capability statement, not a product catalogue. The differentiator is not any single product. It is the integration — the ability to deliver sovereign technology as a cohesive, operational deployment rather than a collection of point solutions.

---

## Three Product Pillars

### 1. VoronCitadel / VoronDRQ — Governance, Risk & Compliance

Malaysian-built GRC and compliance platform. Supports the 193-organisation market database and joint GTM activities with CyberSecurity Malaysia. Represents the governance and compliance layer of the sovereign stack — the foundation that ensures capabilities are deployed within a structured risk and compliance framework.

**Demo focus:** Live GRC workflows, compliance dashboards, CSM collaboration showcase, account-level engagement intelligence.

### 2. GovSec TIP v3.0 — Sovereign Threat Intelligence Platform

National-grade threat intelligence platform incorporating threat visualisation, executive intelligence briefings, AI-assisted analysis, threat hunting capabilities, and Malaysian-focused threat reporting. Represents the intelligence and detection layer — the capability to see, understand, and respond to threats within the Malaysian threat landscape.

**Demo focus:** Live threat feeds, executive briefing dashboards, AI-assisted analysis workflow, Malaysian threat landscape visualisation.

### 3. ChainSentry — Blockchain Security

Blockchain security capability providing an additional technology dimension within the broader CyberDSA story. Represents the emerging-threat layer — the ability to secure next-generation digital infrastructure beyond traditional cyber boundaries.

**Demo focus:** Smart contract analysis, blockchain transaction monitoring, vulnerability assessment workflow.

## Differentiators

1. **Malaysian-built, sovereign by design** — Not a foreign product rebranded. Built in Malaysia, for Malaysian threat landscape and regulatory context.

2. **Full-stack capability** — GRC + Threat Intelligence + Blockchain Security + CSM operational backing. Not point solutions — an integrated stack.

3. **Ecosystem-integrated** — Partnership with CyberSecurity Malaysia provides institutional credibility and operational deployment pathway. Not vendor-to-customer — institution-to-institution.

4. **Operational, not theoretical** — Products are deployed, being deployed, or in active commercial pipeline. Not prototypes — operational capabilities.

5. **End-to-end** — From policy and compliance (VoronCitadel) through threat detection and response (GovSec TIP) to emerging infrastructure security (ChainSentry). Covers the full cybersecurity lifecycle.

---

## CyberDSA 2026 Narrative Arc

**The setup:** Malaysia's digital infrastructure faces increasing, sophisticated threats. Foreign solutions exist but lack local context, local regulatory alignment, and local operational integration.

**The demonstration:** Aras Integrasi, working with CSM and ecosystem partners, has built and is operationalising a complete sovereign cybersecurity capability stack — designed for the Malaysian threat landscape, aligned with Malaysian regulatory requirements, and backed by institutional partnership.

**The ask:** Engage with Aras Integrasi as a sovereign technology partner — not a product vendor. Explore how the full-stack capability can be deployed within your organisation's cybersecurity framework.
