# Context

T-40 days to CyberDSA 2026 (Aug 26). The GovSec × CMERP engineering document closure timeline was previously expressed in calendar dates (Hadri's Aug 13 email targeted Aug 28). With 40 days remaining to CyberDSA, DAF requires T-minus anchoring to maintain countdown discipline across all stakeholders and make slippage immediately visible.

T-minus reference:
- T-40 = Aug 26
- T-38 = Aug 28
- T-35 = Aug 31
- T-30 = Sep 5
- T-25 = Sep 10
- T-20 = Sep 15

# Decision

All engineering closure commitments for GovSec × CMERP integration must be expressed in T-minus format relative to CyberDSA 2026. Hadri and Fuad must align and provide a single consolidated T-minus ETA for:
- Final engineering document closure
- Internal technical sign-off (Fattah Hafiz)
- CSM technical validation (Zaharudin)
- Engineering baseline confirmation

# Rationale

T-minus framing:
- Creates shared timeline discipline across Hadri, Fuad, and CSM stakeholders
- Makes slippage immediately visible (T-35 → T-33 is obvious; Aug 31 → Sep 2 is not)
- Generates urgency without micromanagement
- Aligns with the war-room operational tempo (INIT-20260813-006)
- Becomes the execution commitment tracked against CyberDSA

# Alternatives Considered

1. Calendar-date-only commitments — rejected (insufficient urgency signalling, harder to track against CyberDSA)
2. Separate internal vs CSM timelines — rejected (DAF explicitly requires single consolidated commitment)

# Confirmation

Confirmed by DAF in the T-40 CyberDSA email (Aug 26, 10:59 AM MYT). Authority: Director, Cyber Security Practice.
