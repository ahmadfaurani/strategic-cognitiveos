# Context

Hadri announced the rebrand in his August 14 email delivering v3.0 of the spec. The rebrand establishes a product family convention as he is creating chain:HARVEST — a Digital Asset Tracing MCP — as a second product.

# Decision

1. ChainSentry → chain:SENTRY (brand change)
2. New product: chain:HARVEST (Digital Asset Tracing MCP)
3. Product family naming convention: "chain:" prefix

# Rationale

A second product in the same domain requires a family naming convention. The "chain:" prefix signals a unified blockchain investigative toolset covering forensics (SENTRY) and tracing (HARVEST).

# Alternatives Considered

None documented. Hadri announced this as a decision rather than a proposal.

# Confirmation

DAF noted the email (Aug 14, Email 3) without objection. "Noted. Lets proceed with the documentation drive." — implicit acceptance.
