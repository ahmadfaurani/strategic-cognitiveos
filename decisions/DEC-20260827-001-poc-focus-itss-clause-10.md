# Decision

Bursa POC document focuses on ITSS §10.0 Supplier Management as the primary compliance hook.

## Context

After intake of both the RSWG Recommendation Paper (DOC-20260827-001) and the ITSS Directive 5.05-001 (DOC-20260827-002), the intelligence assessment (INT-20260827-002) identified that ITSS §10 Supplier Management is the existing binding TPRM precursor — already mandatory for all 30 POs. The RSWG §2.6 TSP Oversight is the enhanced version, but it's a recommendation, not yet binding.

## Rationale

ITSS §10 is already law. RSWG §2.6 is a recommendation. The POC must anchor on what brokers must do today, not what they might need to do tomorrow. This:
- Eliminates the "we'll wait for RSWG formalization" deferral objection
- Creates immediate commercial urgency (existing compliance gap)
- Positions VoronCitadel as the answer to a current obligation
- Still references RSWG §2.6 as the forward path (future-ready)

## What This Means for the POC Document

The POC document (INIT-20260824-001, currently 20-section draft v0.1) should:
1. Lead with ITSS §10.0 Supplier Management compliance — this is the existing binding requirement
2. Map VoronCitadel's TPRM module to §10's specific sub-clauses (10.1, 10.2, 10.3, 10.4)
3. Reference RSWG §2.6 as the enhancement direction — "where the standard is heading"
4. Position VoronCitadel as "ITSS §10 compliance today + RSWG §2.6 readiness tomorrow"

## ITSS §10 Sub-Clauses (from DOC-20260827-002)

| Clause | Requirement | VoronCitadel TPRM Coverage |
|--------|-------------|---------------------------|
| §10.1 Information Security in Supplier Management | Outsourcing risk policy (data centre, network, DR, app hosting, cloud); supplier types; access controls; recovery arrangements | Core TPRM capability |
| §10.2 Engagement of Suppliers | Background verification; ITSS compliance; confidentiality statements | TPRM onboarding workflow |
| §10.3 Supplier Agreements | Legal/regulatory obligations; controls; incident management; audit rights; escrow; testing evidence | TPRM contract management |
| §10.4 Management of Supplier Service Delivery | Performance monitoring; service reports; service continuity plans | TPRM continuous monitoring |
