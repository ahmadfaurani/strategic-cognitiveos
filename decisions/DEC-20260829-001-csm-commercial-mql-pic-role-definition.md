# DEC-20260829-001 — CSM Commercial MQL PIC Role Definition Established

## Decision

DAF authored and established the CSM Commercial MQL PIC role definition as a working document for CSM review and adoption.

## Key Elements

1. **Role Title:** CSM Commercial MQL PIC — Commercial Department Point-of-Contact for Qualified MQL Intake, Internal Processing and Sales Coordination
2. **Aishah:** Confirmed as initial MQL Receiver in CSM (resolves A2 from overdue)
3. **13-Section Framework:** Role title, purpose, primary objective, core responsibilities (5 areas), MQL disposition authority (11 statuses), minimum information requirements, SLA expectations, PIC authority boundaries, Account Owner relationship, accountability, KPIs, role statement, operating principle
4. **11-Status Disposition System:** Received → Under Review → Accepted → Assigned → Engagement Scheduled → Opportunity → POC/Evaluation → Nurture → Existing Opportunity → Rejected/Disqualified → Closed
5. **SLA Targets:** 1 business day acknowledgement, 2 days initial review, 3 days owner assignment, 5 days customer engagement initiation
6. **Authority Boundary:** PIC owns intake/routing/coordination/tracking/escalation. Account Owner owns qualification/customer strategy/opportunity/proposal/negotiation/closure.
7. **Operating Principle:** Every Qualified MQL must have a disposition, an owner and a next action.

## Resolves

- **A2 (Aisha PIC)** — overdue since Aug 28. Now resolved: Aishah confirmed as initial MQL Receiver, job scope defined via ART-20260829-001.
