# Decision

**Amelia Nadia Azharul Nahar is the Cybersecurity Practice Strategic Stakeholder Engagement Lead and principal operational owner for stakeholder engagement across the board.**

## Role Definition

**Title:** Cybersecurity Practice Strategic Stakeholder Engagement Lead  
**Incumbent:** Amelia Nadia Azharul Nahar (STK-20260813-014)  
**Reporting to:** DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice, Aras Integrasi)  
**Effective:** August 20, 2026  
**Operating clarification:** August 25, 2026

## Scope

This is a **practice-wide** role, not limited to CyberDSA 2026:

| Domain | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| CSM Partnership | Operational coordination of CSM-Aras stakeholder engagement, working-group follow-through and activation sequencing |
| Product GTM | Stakeholder engagement for VoronCitadel, GovSec TIP and chain:SENTRY opportunities and POCs |
| Event Activation | CyberDSA 2026 and future practice events — stakeholder scheduling, activation tracking and follow-through |
| Institutional Relationships | University, government and ecosystem stakeholder coordination as delegated |
| Media & Communications | External engagement coordination with DAF approval on strategic claims/positioning |

## 25 Aug 2026 Operating Model Clarification

| Lane | Accountable Role |
|---|---|
| Stakeholder engagement operations | **Amelia Nadia** |
| Executive sponsorship / strategic authority | **DAF** |
| Technical stakeholder support | **Ahmad Fuad** |
| Sales / commercial stakeholder support | **Hadri** |

This operating model applies to CyberDSA stakeholder engagement and serves as the default practice-wide engagement pattern unless DAF explicitly assigns a different owner.

## Authority

- **Can do autonomously:** Schedule meetings, coordinate logistics, maintain stakeholder contact, draft routine communications, maintain engagement matrix, track follow-ups and coordinate between Aras/CSM/WIG.
- **Requires DAF approval:** External strategic claims, commercial commitments, sensitive positioning, new senior-level relationship commitments.
- **Escalate to DAF:** Conflicting priorities, ambiguous authority, sensitive stakeholder issues or cross-workstream trade-offs.

## Supersession

This decision **supersedes DEC-20260815-005** (Internal Programme Coordinator nomination for CyberDSA 2026). The SSE Lead role absorbs and exceeds the CyberDSA-only coordinator scope.

## Execution Linkage

ACT-20260825-005 operationalises this mandate through a maintained CyberDSA stakeholder engagement matrix covering activation stage, owner/support lane, next action, deadline, blocker and evidence/status.
