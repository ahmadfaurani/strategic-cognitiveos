# Decision

**Amelia Nadia Azharul Nahar is formally designated as Cybersecurity Practice Strategic Stakeholder Engagement Lead, effective immediately.**

## Role Definition

**Title:** Cybersecurity Practice Strategic Stakeholder Engagement Lead
**Incumbent:** Amelia Nadia Azharul Nahar (STK-20260813-014)
**Organisation:** Wong International Group (WIG)
**Reporting to:** DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice, Aras Integrasi)
**Effective:** August 20, 2026

## Scope

This is a **practice-wide** role, not limited to CyberDSA 2026:

| Domain | Responsibilities |
|--------|-----------------|
| CSM Partnership | Operational coordination of CSM-Aras working group; Amelia-Aisha channel; Zulfeka sync-ups |
| Product GTM | Stakeholder engagement for VoronCitadel, GovSec-TIP, ChainSentry POCs and rollouts |
| Event Activation | CyberDSA 2026 and future practice events — booth, sponsorship, logistics, speaking slots |
| Institutional Relationships | University partnerships, government stakeholder coordination (as delegated) |
| Media & Communications | Media contact assessment, external messaging coordination (with DAF approval on claims) |

## Authority

- **Can do autonomously:** Schedule meetings, coordinate logistics, draft communications, maintain stakeholder contact, send meeting summaries, coordinate between Aras/CSM/WIG
- **Requires DAF approval:** External claims/messaging (e.g., "Malaysia's First"), strategic positioning statements, commercial commitments, new stakeholder relationships above working level
- **Escalate to DAF:** Ambiguous authority situations, conflicting priorities, capacity conflicts, sensitive stakeholder issues

## Supersession

This decision **supersedes DEC-20260815-005** (Internal Programme Coordinator nomination for CyberDSA 2026).

The SSE Lead role absorbs and exceeds the PC scope:
- PC was CyberDSA-only → SSE Lead is practice-wide
- PC was PROPOSED → SSE Lead is ACTIVE
- PC required WIG confirmation → SSE Lead is DAF-directed (WIG alignment implicit)
- PC focused on coordination → SSE Lead includes strategic engagement
