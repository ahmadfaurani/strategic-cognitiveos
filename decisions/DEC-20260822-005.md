# DEC-20260822-005 — Cost-per-MQL

## The Math

| Budget | MQLs (meeting requests) | Cost per MQL |
|--------|------------------------|-------------|
| RM 175,000 | 78 | **RM 2,244** |
| RM 295,000 | 78 | **RM 3,782** |
| RM 175,000 | 40 (fallback) | **RM 4,375** |
| RM 295,000 | 40 (fallback) | **RM 7,375** |

## Unit Economics

- Average deal size: RM 168,000 (early adopter pricing)
- 1 sale covers: 75 MQLs at RM 2,244/MQL
- Revenue target: 7 sales × RM 168,000 = RM 1,176,000
- Marketing spend as % of revenue: 14.9% (RM 175k) to 25.1% (RM 295k)

## Benchmark Context

- RM 2,000-4,000 per MQL: defensible for targeted ABM in niche government/enterprise market
- Above RM 5,000: requires higher deal sizes to justify
- At RM 168k average deal, the math works within the defensible range

## The Tuesday Ask

> "For RM 175-295k, how many MQLs will marketing commit to delivering, and at what cost-per-MQL?"

One number. One cost. One owner. No vanity dashboard.

## Fallback Scenario

If marketing commits to fewer than 78 MQLs:
- 40 MQLs → ~12 meetings → ~3.5 POCs → ~1 sale
- Revenue drops from RM 1.176M to ~RM 168,000
- This math must be ready for Tuesday
