# DEC-20260822-002 — DAF Initial Feedback on GTM Strategy

## Context

WIG Marketing (Said Farid) delivered the VoronCitadel GTM Strategy Final Draft on Aug 21. DAF reviewed and provided formal written feedback via email the same day at 4:41 PM.

## Decision

DAF provided formal initial feedback on the VoronCitadel GTM Strategy Final Draft from WIG Marketing.

## Positions

**Layer 1 (Credibility & Outreach, RM 20-35k): ALIGNED**
- Supports RM 35k expenditure
- Wants objective outcome definitions: MQL → Sales Meeting → Technical Meeting → POC
- Ecosystem partner build is a "force multiplier" — supports it
- Flagged: needs dedicated FTE (positioned for joint approval push)

**Layer 2 (Content/Media/PR, RM 75-140k): CONTESTED**
- LinkedIn thought leadership (RM 30-50k): questions ROI — DAF has 11k+ followers, Fuad 5k+
- Cannot see tangible way to calculate ROI
- Asked: "Is this going to be parked under my departmental cost?"
- GRC in 60 Seconds: open, needs tangible outcome measurement
- BFM Radio: "Yes/Maybe, let's talk about it"
- Bottom line: likes the exposure, needs tangible outcome from departmental perspective

**Layer 3 (Events & Conversion, RM 80-120k): FULL SUPPORT**
- All in on 07 (CYBERDSA), 08 (Roundtable), 09 (Workshops)
- Wants: quarterly flagship events with media coverage embedded
- Condition: coordinated, dedicated efficiency for engagement

## DAF's Own Conversion Model

Proposed as replacement/alternative to deck's flat targets:
- 193 orgs → 1,300 stakeholders (existing database)
- 260 engaged (20%)
- 78 meetings (30%)
- 23 POCs (30%)
- 7 sales (30%)
- Revenue: 7 × RM 168,000 = RM 1,176,000 (early adopter pricing)
- Retail pricing: RM 368,000

## Key Statement

> "Again, only achievable via a coordinated, concentrated effort by all involved. This is just 1 stream. I know media coverage can amplify. We need to do the math."

## Status

Proposed — to be discussed at Tuesday Aug 25, 2:30 PM alignment meeting. Not yet approved by all parties.
