# Context

The media narrative alignment review (Aug 21, Ember) identified co-branding as the #1 misalignment between DOC-20260818-002 (media narrative, Aug 18) and DOC-20260819-001 (activation framework, Aug 19). BRIEF-20260817-001 explicitly stated "three CSM co-branded, national-grade, full-stack sovereign technology offerings to launch." The media narrative used conservative "working with ecosystem partners including CSM" language.

DAF's decision (Aug 21): co-branding is confirmed. It is also the engagement proposition — the concrete ask brought to Wan Roshaimi and Roshdi.

# Decision

## 1. Co-Branding Confirmed

The three CyberDSA 2026 products are **CSM × Aras co-branded offerings**:
- VoronDRQ / VoronCitadel — Governance & Compliance
- GovSec TIP v3.0 — Threat Intelligence & AI
- ChainSentry — Emerging Technology Security

## 2. Co-Branding as Engagement Proposition

### For Wan Roshaimi (Gate 4 — Technical Confidence)
**Proposition:** "We are co-branding these products CSM × Aras for CyberDSA 2026. We need your technical validation of the co-branded stack — architecture posture, integration boundaries, and agreement on technical representation at CyberDSA."

This upgrades the ask from "validate our technical narrative" to "validate the technical basis for co-branding CSM's name on these products." Stronger, more concrete, and gives Wan Roshaimi a clear institutional stake.

### For Roshdi (Gate 0 — Executive Authorization)
**Proposition:** "We are proposing to co-brand three sovereign cybersecurity products CSM × Aras for launch at CyberDSA 2026. This positions CSM as co-developer and co-owner of national-grade sovereign technology. We seek your executive authorization as Acting CEO to proceed with co-branding."

This is the executive ask that makes every downstream gate legitimate. Without Roshdi's authorization, co-branding claims are unsupported.

## 3. Framework Amendment — Gate 0 Added

**Updated 7-stakeholder dependency chain:**

| Gate | Stakeholder | Role Lens | Primary Ask | Control Point |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Gate 0 — Executive Authorization** | **Roshdi (STK-20260815-010)** | **Acting CEO — Executive Authority** | **Authorize CSM × Aras co-branding for CyberDSA 2026** | **No co-branded materials or public claims until authorized** |
| Gate 1 — Partnership Alignment | Azrul | Partnership & Alliance | Lock narrative + sponsorship pathway | Do not activate broader CSM asks until narrative stable |
| Gate 2 — Commercial Ownership | Zulfeka | Commercial | Agree commercial model + targets | No event activation without conversion path |
| Gate 3 — Market & Media | Bala | Marketing & Media | Joint messaging + spokesperson coordination | No fragmented messaging |
| Gate 4 — Technical Confidence | Wan Roshaimi | CTO / Technology | Validate co-branded stack technical basis | Technical leadership validates; Aras owns POC delivery |
| Gate 5 — Operational Coordination | Zaharudin | Operational / Technical | Agree coordination + access + escalation | Keep light, practical, owner-based |
| Gate 6 — National Alignment | Dr. Megat | NACSA Strategic | Present mature co-branded proposition | Approach with evidence of CSM alignment |

**Gate 0 timing:** Can run in parallel with Gate 1. Roshdi provides executive authority while Azrul organizes the institutional pathway. Both must close before Gate 2+ activates with co-branding claims.
