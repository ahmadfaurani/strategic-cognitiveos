# Decision

DAF has established an NDA Framework comprising 11 key principles for the CSM × Aras Integrasi engagement relating to the Bursa Malaysia OMS × VoronCitadel GRC Platform POC.

## 11 Principles

1. Confidentiality and Restricted Information
2. No Marketing or Publicity
3. PDPA-Compliant Data Handling
4. Foreground IP — Bursa Ownership
5. Background IP — Aras Ownership
6. CSM Usage and Bursa Sublicensing Rights (perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence + sublicense to Bursa)
7. Mandatory Background IP Disclosure (before incorporation into POC)
8. Restriction on Reuse of Bursa/CSM-Derived Information
9. IP Warranty and Indemnity (Aras warrants non-infringement, indemnifies CSM)
10. Subsequent Service Agreement (NDA = initial framework; Service Agreement for implementation/production)
11. Working-Level Alignment Before Legal Review (align on principles, then route to legal teams)

## 4 Provisions Flagged for CSM Review

1. Foreground IP ownership (Bursa)
2. Background IP licensing (Aras → CSM)
3. Bursa sublicensing rights (CSM → Bursa)
4. Non-reuse provisions (Aras restriction)

## Rationale

The NDA Framework is necessary before progressing into deeper technical discovery, architecture review, assessment activities, or access to Bursa-specific information. It establishes clear confidentiality, data protection, and intellectual property boundaries for all three parties (Aras, CSM, Bursa Malaysia).

## Status

**Proposed** — Sent to Azrul (CSM) for working-level alignment. Requires CSM review and agreement on 4 critical provisions before routing to legal teams for formal agreement drafting.

## Approval Required

- Working-level: Azrul Nazim (CSM)
- Formal: Respective legal teams (CSM and Aras)
