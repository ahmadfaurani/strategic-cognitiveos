# Decision

## Directive 1: RADAR Cronjob Governance

RADAR (Data Research Acquisition & Development) assumes oversight of all cronjobs effective 2026-08-20.

### Active Cronjobs Under RADAR (5)

| Job Name | Schedule | Status | Function |
|----------|----------|--------|----------|
| tei-health-check | Every 20 min | OK | TEI embedding service health monitoring |
| Deriver Health Check | Every 15 min | OK | Honcho deriver pipeline health |
| Memory Dreaming Promotion | Daily 03:15 UTC | Error (Apply Patch) | REM-phase memory validation/promotion |
| TEI Alternative Review Reminder | One-shot (Aug 22) | Pending | TEI alternative review trigger |
| Monthly Model Stack Review | Monthly (1st, 09:00 UTC+8) | Pending | Model stack monthly review |

### Governance Transfer

- **From:** Ad-hoc (created during various sessions, no unified authority)
- **To:** RADAR (Data Research Acquisition & Development)
- **Scope:** All existing and future cronjobs

## Directive 2: RMR Marking Mandatory

All future intake events must:
1. Follow standard taxonomy template compliance (existing SOP requirement — now explicitly enforced)
2. Receive an RMR mark in the Radar Mark Registry (DOC-20260820-002)

**Rule: "You are marked if you are on the RADAR"** — no intake proceeds without an RMR mark.

### Updated Intake SOP (Effective Immediately)

Step 3 (Create Records) now includes:
- Assign RMR mark (sequential: RMR-001, RMR-002, ...)
- Log RMR entry in DOC-20260820-002 (RMR Registry Log)
- RMR entry links all records produced from the intake
