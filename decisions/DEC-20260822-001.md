# DEC-20260822-001 — Gate 4 Governing Principle

## Decision

1. **Governing Principle established:** "Do not make the evidence fit the co-branding narrative. Make the co-branding narrative precisely match the evidence."

2. **Reclassification:** SiberSUITE, CBOM Agent, and Cybersecurity Score Card are classified as **Exploratory Future-State Technical Collaboration** — not present-state GovSec capabilities, not committed integrations, not approved roadmap items.

3. **Correction of ASSESS-20260822-001:** The earlier assessment's statement that "CSM assets (LebahNet, CMERP, SiberSUITE) are confirmed in GovSec stack" is corrected. Only LebahNet and CMERP have potential present-state relevance (pending validation). SiberSUITE, CBOM Agent, and Cybersecurity Score Card are future-state candidates only.

4. **Binding constraint:** The evidence chain must be complete: Architecture → Interface → Execution → Evidence. Without it, technical co-branding remains vulnerable.

5. **Gate 4 Evidence Classification adopted:** 9 controlled maturity states (Implemented, Tested, Available, Validated, Designed, Proposed, Future-State Candidate, Pending, Unresolved) for all capability claims.

6. **Future-State Roadmap (8-stage):** Exploratory Discussion → Technical Feasibility Assessment → CSM × Aras Prioritisation → Architecture Definition → Governance Approval → Official Roadmap Integration → Implementation → Technical Validation. Only after this sequence should items contribute to co-branding substantiation.

7. **Secondary product positioning confirmed:**
   - GovSec: Primary technical co-branding candidate
   - VoronCitadel: Commercial/GTM collaboration candidate (not technical integration)
   - chain:SENTRY: Emerging technology/future collaboration candidate (conditional on later substantiation)

## Rationale

Wan Roshaimi's vendor-detection instinct (BAE Detica/PwC career) will test whether 'sovereign' means sovereign or vendor substitution. The deliberate exclusion of future-state items from present-state claims strengthens the case by demonstrating that evidence is being separated from ambition.
