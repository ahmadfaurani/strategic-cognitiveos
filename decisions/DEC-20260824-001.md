# Context

The Bursa Malaysia VoronCitadel POC was initiated by Azrul on Aug 20 (DEC-20260820-003). The Aug 24 meeting at Menara CyberAxis discussed the technical approach. The broader 24-entity federation vision, while compelling, carries significant architectural and commercial assumptions that cannot be validated in the initial phase.

# Decision

Phase 0 of the Bursa POC will be structured around **Third Party Risk Management (TPRM)**:

1. **Primary anchor:** Current VoronCitadel capability
2. **Scope:** 3–4 participating organisations (not 24-entity model)
3. **TPRM workflow:** Vendor onboarding → tiering → assessment → remediation → integration of third-party risk into each organisation's enterprise-risk view
4. **Progression:** TPRM Deployment → Shared Vendor Validation → Federated Compliance Document Check

# Rationale

- Grounds Phase 0 in **proven capability** rather than architectural hypothesis
- Creates **immediate demonstrable TPRM value** for Bursa
- Scope of 3–4 organisations is **manageable within Bursa's proposed 4-month timeline**
- Progressively validates requirements and architecture for the broader federation
- Avoids the risk of over-committing to unvalidated federated architecture
