# Context

Following the UiTM agreement in principle (DEC-20260807-001, Aug 7) and the RM5M cost structure formalisation for MCMC (DEC-20260815-001, Aug 15), DAF and Prof. Suhaimee met over the weekend (Aug 15-16). DAF proposed a structured four-pillar collaboration operating model to elevate the partnership from agreement-in-principle to a defined operating framework. Prof. Suhaimee confirmed full agreement and shared three R.I.S.I.K working documents (draft stage) for internal review.

# Decision

Four-pillar collaboration operating model formally agreed:

1. **R.I.S.I.K Framework — Core IP:** Further development, validation and institutionalisation of R.I.S.I.K as foundational methodology and core IP
2. **Applied AI Co-Development — Derivative IP:** Joint development of AI-enabled capabilities, tools and operational workflows that extend and operationalise R.I.S.I.K
3. **Joint Research & IP Development:** Structured pathway for collaborative research, publications, methodologies and new IP
4. **Structured Market & Government Activation:** Joint positioning and activation across government, institutional and strategic market stakeholders

Additional elements acknowledged:
- R.I.S.I.K Cohort Programme — practitioner/SME development platform
- R.I.S.I.K Centre of Excellence — long-term ambition, subject to successful development, validation and institutionalisation

# Rationale

- Core IP vs Derivative IP distinction critical for IP governance (Cohort Joint IP Framework RISIK model: academic+technology)
- PMO named as explicit end-state — connects R.I.S.I.K to Workstream A (Sovereign AI & Government AI Adoption)
- Strengthens MCMC RM5M funding case — now backed by formal collaboration framework
- Pathway: Framework → Capability → Validation → Practitioner Development → Government Applied Use Case (PMO)

# Three Working Documents Received (Draft Stage)

1. R.I.S.I.K – Panduan Rujukan Ringkas
2. R.I.S.I.K – Jilid Draf (Panduan + Kajian Kes)
3. Aliran Kerja – Modul R.I.S.I.K ke Sistem Penuh

# Operational Development Alignment Session (Proposed)

Focus areas:
1. Framework Decomposition — map R.I.S.I.K methodology, workflow, intended outputs
2. Applied AI Capability Mapping — identify AI-assisted analysis, automation, decision support, human validation areas
3. Initial Use Case Selection — agree 1-2 priority use cases for early development and validation
