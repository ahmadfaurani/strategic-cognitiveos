# Context

Azrul proposed Monday 10am deep dive project brief for Bursa Malaysia VoronCitadel POC (Aug 20, 7:30 AM MYT). DAF responded same morning (10:50 AM MYT) by reframing the session and committing to a more rigorous preparation approach.

# Decision

1. **Reframe Monday session** as "pre-flight alignment and readiness check" — not just a briefing
2. **Prepare 8-section consolidated POC document** as single working reference:
   - Section 1: POC Project Brief (executive overview, objectives, engagement model)
   - Section 2: POC Scope & Use Cases (Bursa use cases, in-scope capabilities, exclusions)
   - Section 3: POC Implementation Plan (phases, activities, dependencies, timeline, responsibilities)
   - Section 4: Success Criteria & Acceptance Framework (measurable outcomes, validation criteria)
   - Section 5: Testing & Validation Plan (functional testing, user validation, evidence)
   - Section 6: POC Roles & RACI (responsibilities across Aras, CSM, Bursa)
   - Section 7: Risks, Assumptions & Dependencies (prerequisites, constraints, alignment needs)
   - Section 8: Post-POC Pathway (pilot → production → commercial engagement)
3. **Ask CSM for Bursa stakeholder mapping** — operational, cybersecurity, compliance, technology, decision-making stakeholders
4. **Expand CC list** — adds Fuad (VoronCitadel Product Owner) and Farul (CTO, MTAI)

# Rationale

- Comprehensive document ensures Monday is a substantive working session, not just a chat
- 8-section structure covers full POC lifecycle — from brief through post-POC commercial pathway
- Section 6 (RACI) and Section 8 (Post-POC Pathway) directly support Gate 2 close requirements
- Adding Fuad brings product ownership into the engagement; adding Farul brings technical authority
- Pre-flight framing positions Aras as methodical and prepared

# Alternatives Considered

- **Simple briefing deck for Monday** — Rejected; insufficient rigor for Tier-1 financial CNII target
- **Delay document preparation until after Monday** — Rejected; misses opportunity to use Monday as substantive working session

# Confirmation

Confirmed by DAF via email to Azrul on Aug 20, 2026 at 10:50 AM MYT.
