# Context

On August 4, 2026, Hadri (Aras Solutions Architect) sent a formal invitation to four CyberSecurity Malaysia personnel to participate in the AI Systems Co-Design Lab (Cohort 01) — a governed environment providing GPU compute access, notebook environments, model endpoints, RAG pipelines, and API tooling for applied AI development. The invitation requested CSM to provide a brief description of their intended use case, problem, and expected output.

Eight days later, on August 12, 2026, Fathi Kamil bin Mohad Zainuddin (Head, MyCERT Department, CSM) responded formally accepting the invitation on behalf of the MyCERT department. He confirmed that MyCERT is currently building several local research products integrating with Generative AI and finds Aras's offering suitable for their requirements. A personnel list for the pilot phase was submitted as an attachment.

# Decision

CSM/MyCERT Department formally accepts the AI Systems Co-Design Lab (Cohort 01) invitation and submits personnel for the pilot phase.

# Rationale

Per Fathi Kamil's email:
- MyCERT is currently building local research products integrating with Generative AI
- Aras's Co-Design Lab offering is assessed as suitable for MyCERT's requirements
- MyCERT has personnel ready to explore Aras-provided models as part of a pilot phase

# Alternatives Considered

- **Decline or defer** — MyCERT could have declined or requested more information before committing. Instead, they accepted with a personnel list, indicating readiness to proceed.
- **Individual rather than department response** — Individual technologists could have responded separately. Fathi Kamil's department-level response signals institutional commitment.

# Assumptions

- The attached personnel list contains the specific MyCERT team members who will participate in the pilot
- MyCERT's "several local research products integrating with Generative AI" are genuine active projects (not aspirational)
- The pilot phase has an implied timeline (not yet specified)
- Aras will review and accept the submitted personnel for Cohort 01
