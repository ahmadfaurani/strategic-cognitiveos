## 3. STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT

**Overall Seat Dynamics:**

N07 Bukit Kepong is a **HIGH-PRIORITY TARGET** for PH. In 2022, it was a 3-way race with PN winning by only 710 votes over BN (44.27% vs 41.08%). PH (via MUDA) got only 13.79% — this is the baseline to improve from.

**2026 Dynamics:**
- Sahruddin Jamal (PN-BERSATU) is a high-profile candidate (former Johor MB)
- BN will be motivated to reclaim seat from PN
- 3-corner fight continues, splitting opposition
- PH has realistic path if they can consolidate anti-BN/PN vote

**Path to Victory:**

PH needs to:
1. Increase vote share from 13.79% to 35-40%
2. Benefit from BN-PN vote splitting
3. Consolidate Chinese (25.9%) + Indian (2.7%) + Orang Asli (1.1%) vote
4. Win 15-20% of Malay votes (69.7% majority)

This is **ACHIEVABLE** with strong candidate, intensive ground work, and BN-PN continued split.

**Key Voter Segments to Target:**
- Chinese voters (25.9%): Base, target 80%+ turnout
- Indian voters (2.7%): Strong PH lean, target 70%+ support
- Orang Asli (1.1%): Historically PH-leaning, protect
- Malay moderates (15-20% needed): Economic messaging, anti-corruption

**Key Voter Segments to Defend:**
- Previous MUDA/PH voters: Ensure they turn out
- First-time voters: Registration + education

**Opposition's Likely Strategy:**
- **PN (Sahruddin):** Incumbency, development record, high-profile leader
- **BN:** Attack PN's record, claim PH will split vote and let PN win

**Critical Success Factors:**
1. PH vote share >35%
2. Chinese turnout >80%
3. BN-PN vote split maintained (neither exceeds 40%)
4. Strong ground presence to counter Sahruddin's profile

**Failure Modes to Avoid:**
- PH vote share <20% (irrelevant kingmaker)
- BN-PN informal coordination (vote transfer)
- Low visibility against high-profile Sahruddin
- Poor ground organization
