## 3. STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT

**Overall Seat Dynamics:**

N07 Bukit Kepong is a **PN-held marginal seat** in the Labis parliament area. In 2022, Sahruddin Jamal (BERSATU) won with 44.27% in a 3-cornered fight against PH (41.09%) and BN (14.64%). The 710-vote margin (3.18%) makes this a **high-priority flip target** for PH.

This is a **Malay-majority rural/semi-rural seat** where PN's Islamic-conservative narrative has traction. However, the close margin shows PH can compete with the right candidate and strategy.

Key dynamics:
- 3-cornered fight (PH vs BN vs PN) — vote splitting dynamics critical
- Sahruddin Jamal is incumbent but vulnerable (narrow margin)
- BN's weak showing (14.64%) suggests room for PH to pick up moderate Malay votes
- Chinese + Indian voters (~20-25% combined) are PH base — must maximize turnout

**Path to Victory:**

1. **Hold PH Base:** Chinese + Indian voters >80% turnout
2. **Pick Up Moderate Malays:** Economic messaging over religious narrative
3. **Exploit BN-PN Competition:** Both compete for Malay votes — PH benefits from split
4. **Ground Game:** Out-organize PN in rural areas
5. **Candidate Quality:** Subramani Mani must connect with Malay majority

**Key Voter Segments to Target:**
- Malay moderates (25-35% of Malay voters): Economic issues, cost of living
- Chinese voters (15-20%): Core PH base, maximize turnout
- Indian voters (5-8%): Core PH base, maximize turnout
- Youth voters (18-35): Progressive message, jobs, education

**Key Voter Segments to Defend:**
- 2022 PH voters: Ensure they turn out (complacency risk)
- Malay voters who supported PH in 2022: Prevent drift to PN

**Opposition's Likely Strategy:**
- **PN (Sahruddin Jamal):** Incumbency advantage, Islamic-conservative narrative, rural Malay base
- **BN (Ahmad Syarie Yusof):** Traditional UMNO machinery, Malay unity narrative, may split PN vote

**Critical Success Factors:**
1. Chinese + Indian turnout >75%
2. Malay support for PH >30% (up from 2022)
3. PH vote share >43% (up from 41.09%)
4. BN vote share >15% (splits PN's Malay base)
5. Polling agent coverage 100%

**Failure Modes to Avoid:**
- Low Chinese/Indian turnout (<65%)
- Malay support drops below 25%
- BN collapses (<10%) — votes flow to PN instead of splitting
- Poor ground game in rural areas
- Candidate perceived as "outsider"
