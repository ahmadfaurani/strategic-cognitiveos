# Summary

The NDA Framework document establishes 11 key principles:

1. **Confidentiality and Restricted Information** — All Bursa, CSM, and Aras confidential information protected under defined confidentiality and access-control obligations. Bursa OMS information treated as restricted information category.

2. **No Marketing or Publicity** — Neither party may use Bursa Malaysia, the POC, technical findings, screenshots, results, branding, or associated information for marketing, publicity, case studies, social media, or external communications without prior written approval.

3. **PDPA-Compliant Data Handling** — Personal data handled per Malaysian PDPA requirements: purpose limitation, data minimisation, controlled access, retention, security, and breach-management.

4. **Foreground IP — Bursa Ownership** — All Bursa-specific outputs (PO documents, OMS-specific schemas, system architecture, integration designs, assessment outputs) remain property of Bursa Malaysia.

5. **Background IP — Aras Ownership** — Aras retains ownership of pre-existing VoronCitadel platform, tools, software, and other Background IP.

6. **CSM Usage and Bursa Sublicensing Rights** — CSM receives perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use applicable Aras Background IP, including right to sublicense required usage rights to Bursa Malaysia.

7. **Mandatory Background IP Disclosure** — Aras must disclose relevant technology, licensing basis, dependencies, third-party components, and associated restrictions before incorporating Background IP into POC.

8. **Restriction on Reuse of Bursa/CSM-Derived Information** — Aras cannot reuse, adapt, or commercialise anything derived from Bursa or CSM confidential information, assessments, PO documents, OMS schemas, architecture, or Bursa-specific materials for unrelated engagements.

9. **IP Warranty and Indemnity** — Aras warrants Background IP and licensing rights do not infringe third-party IP rights, with indemnification to CSM for such claims.

10. **Subsequent Service Agreement** — NDA is initial framework for POC. Implementation, production deployment, or managed services require proper Service Agreement (scope, commercial terms, SLA, support, licensing, liability, security, data processing).

11. **Working-Level Alignment Before Legal Review** — DAF proposes aligning on principles first before routing to respective legal teams for formal agreement drafting.

# 4 Critical Provisions Flagged for CSM Review

1. Foreground IP ownership (Bursa)
2. Background IP licensing (Aras → CSM)
3. Bursa sublicensing rights (CSM → Bursa)
4. Non-reuse provisions (Aras restriction)
