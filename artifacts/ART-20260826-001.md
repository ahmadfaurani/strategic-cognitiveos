# MCMC AI Capability Development Workshop Agenda

## Summary

Workshop agenda for the MCMC AI Capability Development Working-Level Session.

**Recommended Duration:** 2–2.5 hours
**Session Type:** Working-level fact finding
**Primary Focus:** AI capability development
**Strategic Direction:** Sovereign Monitoring

### 7-Session Structure

| # | Session | Time | Purpose |
|---|---------|------|---------|
| 1 | Opening & Alignment | 10 min | Confirm objectives, scope and expected outcomes |
| 2 | Current Capability Review | 25 min | Establish current-state tools, data, AI and workflows |
| 3 | Operational Requirements & Gaps | 30 min | Identify priority operational and technical gaps |
| 4 | AI Capability Development Opportunities | 30 min | Define high-value AI capability candidates |
| 5 | Integration & Sovereign Architecture | 25 min | Capture integration, data sovereignty and governance constraints |
| 6 | Prioritisation & Development Path | 20 min | Sequence discovery, POC, integration and advanced development |
| 7 | Next Steps | 10 min | Confirm owners, follow-up inputs and next working session |

### Target Workshop Outcomes

1. Validated baseline of MCMC's current monitoring, data, analytics and AI capabilities
2. Priority operational gaps, integration requirements and AI capability opportunities identified
3. Practical development path agreed (discovery → POC → integration → advanced)
4. Follow-up fact-finding process activated with clear owners and next actions

### 4-Phase Development Path

- **Phase 0** — Discovery & technical validation
- **Phase 1** — Quick-win / POC capabilities
- **Phase 2** — Platform and data integration
- **Phase 3** — Advanced sovereign AI capability development

### 6 Expected Post-Workshop Outputs

1. Current-State Capability Baseline
2. Capability Gap Register
3. AI Capability Opportunity List
4. Integration & Sovereignty Requirements
5. Initial Development Roadmap
6. Candidate Phase 0 / POC Scope

### Guiding Principle

> Enhance existing capability → Integrate where practical → Develop new capability where genuine gaps exist.
