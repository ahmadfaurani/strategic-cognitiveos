# VoronDRQ GTM Programme — Executive Summary for COO Approval

**Prepared by:** Faurani Jaafar, Director — Cyber Security Practice
**Date:** 8 August 2026
**Classification:** Confidential — Internal
**Decision Required:** Resource allocation and budget approval

---

## The Ask

Approve 4 hires, CRM tooling, and programme budget to sustain the VoronDRQ Go-To-Market outreach programme targeting Malaysian financial services institutions.

## Why Now

We have a complete GTM foundation: strategy validated, sales enablement kit delivered, 193-organisation prospect database built, CSM partnership established. What we don't have is the team to execute at sustained scale. The programme currently runs on two people — DAF (strategy, discovery, CSM liaison, coordination) and Fuad (product, demos, POC delivery, claims validation). This creates a single point of failure and caps us at 1 POC at a time.

## What We're Building

A sustained cycle: **Outreach → Discovery → Demonstration → POC → Commercial Conversion → Next Wave**

- **15 pilot accounts** (8 Lighthouse banks + 7 Conversion institutions)
- **3-week campaign cadence** per account (relevance → engagement → conversion)
- **7 stakeholder roles** per account, max 3 contacted initially
- **Target:** 10 engaged accounts, 5+ discovery sessions, 5–6 demos, 2–3 POC candidates
