# CSM Commercial MQL PIC — Role Definition & Job Scope

## 1. Role Title

**CSM Commercial MQL PIC**
Commercial Department Point-of-Contact for Qualified MQL Intake, Internal Processing and Sales Coordination

---

## 2. Role Purpose

The CSM Commercial MQL PIC serves as the designated operational owner within CyberSecurity Malaysia's Commercial Department for receiving Qualified Marketing Leads (MQLs) generated and handed over by Aras Integrasi.

The PIC is responsible for ensuring that every accepted MQL is properly registered, internally assessed, assigned to the appropriate CSM commercial owner, progressed through the relevant sales process, and coordinated with Aras where joint engagement is required.

The role is primarily a commercial intake, orchestration and accountability function.

The PIC is not necessarily the final Account Owner or Sales Lead for every opportunity.

---

## 3. Primary Objective

Ensure that every Qualified MQL handed over to CSM:

Receives clear ownership → enters the appropriate internal commercial process → is assigned to the correct sales resource → progresses toward customer engagement, opportunity qualification, POC or commercial closure.

The role exists to eliminate:

- Unowned leads
- Informal handovers
- Duplicate account engagement
- Internal coordination gaps
- Delayed customer follow-up
- Loss of MQL attribution
- Unclear Aras–CSM commercial accountability

---

## 4. Core Responsibilities

### A. MQL Intake

The PIC shall act as the single receiving point for Qualified MQLs submitted to the CSM Commercial Department.

Responsibilities include:

- Receive Qualified MQL handover from Aras.
- Confirm receipt of the MQL.
- Verify that minimum required information has been provided.
- Register the MQL within the appropriate CSM commercial tracking mechanism.
- Maintain the original MQL source and campaign attribution.

### B. Initial Commercial Review

Upon receipt, the PIC shall perform an initial internal review to determine:

- Whether the organisation already exists within the CSM sales pipeline.
- Whether there is an existing Account Owner.
- Whether another active CSM opportunity relates to the same requirement.
- Which CSM product, service or commercial portfolio is most relevant.
- Whether additional clarification is required from Aras.
- Whether the MQL should proceed to commercial qualification.

The PIC should not unnecessarily re-qualify an MQL that has already satisfied the agreed Aras–CSM MQL qualification criteria.

### C. Internal Assignment

The PIC shall identify and coordinate the appropriate internal CSM owner.

This may include assignment to:

- Account Manager
- Business Development representative
- Product / Solution Commercial Owner
- Technical Pre-Sales
- Relevant CSM business unit
- Strategic Account Owner
- Management stakeholder where escalation is required

The PIC retains coordination responsibility until a clear commercial owner has formally accepted the lead.

### D. Sales Coordination

The PIC shall facilitate progression of the MQL through CSM's internal sales process.

This includes:

- Coordinating the relevant Account Manager or sales personnel.
- Facilitating internal commercial alignment.
- Coordinating customer meeting scheduling where required.
- Ensuring relevant technical stakeholders are engaged.
- Coordinating joint Aras–CSM customer engagement where appropriate.
- Tracking agreed next actions.
- Identifying stalled opportunities requiring intervention.

### E. Aras–CSM Coordination

The PIC serves as the principal working-level commercial interface between Aras and CSM for handed-over MQLs.

Responsibilities include:

- Provide acknowledgement of MQL receipt.
- Communicate internal ownership.
- Coordinate joint customer engagement.
- Request additional information where necessary.
- Maintain visibility of MQL progression.
- Coordinate commercial and technical participation from CSM.
- Escalate material blockers affecting progression.

The PIC should provide sufficient visibility to Aras without exposing internal CSM information that is commercially confidential or unnecessary for joint execution.

---

## 5. MQL Disposition Authority

For every MQL received, the PIC should classify the MQL into one of the following statuses:

| Status | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| Received | MQL formally received by CSM Commercial |
| Under Review | Internal account / commercial assessment underway |
| Accepted | MQL accepted for commercial progression |
| Assigned | Named CSM sales/commercial owner appointed |
| Engagement Scheduled | Customer engagement has been arranged |
| Opportunity | Commercial opportunity formally identified |
| POC / Evaluation | Technical or commercial evaluation initiated |
| Nurture | Valid account but not immediately actionable |
| Existing Opportunity | Requirement already exists within CSM pipeline |
| Rejected / Disqualified | Does not meet agreed commercial criteria |
| Closed | Opportunity completed, won, lost or otherwise concluded |

A rejection should include a clear reason.

---

## 6. Minimum Information Required From Aras

The Qualified MQL handover should contain, where available:

### Account Information
- Organisation name
- Sector / industry
- Account classification or tier

### Stakeholder Information
- Stakeholder name
- Designation
- Department / function
- Contact details or engagement channel
- Stakeholder role within the potential decision process

### MQL Qualification
- Confirmation that a meeting or substantive engagement has been requested/agreed
- Customer area of interest
- Identified requirement / problem statement
- Relevant solution or capability
- Context leading to the engagement

### Commercial Context
- Potential opportunity area
- Known timeline
- Known budget information, where available
- Known incumbent or competing solution, where relevant
- Proposed immediate next action

### Attribution
- MQL source
- Campaign / event
- Date identified
- Aras originating owner
- Aras commercial / technical PIC

---

## 7. Service-Level Expectations

The following operating targets are recommended:

| Activity | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Acknowledge receipt | Within 1 business day |
| Initial internal review | Within 2 business days |
| Assign CSM commercial owner | Within 3 business days |
| Confirm disposition to Aras | Within 3 business days |
| Initiate / coordinate customer engagement | Target ≤5 business days |
| Escalate unassigned MQL | After 3 business days |
| Escalate stalled accepted MQL | After 5 business days without material action |

---

## 8. Authority of the PIC

The PIC should be authorised to:

- Receive Qualified MQLs on behalf of CSM Commercial.
- Access relevant internal commercial information required to identify account ownership.
- Coordinate with Account Managers and commercial teams.
- Request internal assignment of commercial ownership.
- Convene initial internal alignment where required.
- Coordinate Aras participation in relevant customer engagements.
- Request clarification or additional information from Aras.
- Escalate stalled MQLs to CSM Commercial management.

The PIC does not automatically have authority to:

- Approve commercial pricing.
- Commit CSM to contractual terms.
- Approve POCs involving significant resources.
- Override an established Account Owner.
- Make binding commitments to customers.
- Approve revenue recognition or commercial closure.

These remain subject to CSM's existing delegation of authority.

---

## 9. Relationship With the CSM Account Owner

The distinction between the two roles should be explicit.

**CSM Commercial MQL PIC** owns:
Intake → Routing → Coordination → Tracking → Escalation

**CSM Account / Sales Owner** owns:
Qualification → Customer Strategy → Opportunity → Proposal → Negotiation → Closure

Once an Account Owner has accepted responsibility, the PIC remains the coordination and governance point for the Aras-generated MQL where necessary, but should not duplicate the Account Owner's sales responsibilities.

---

## 10. Accountability

The PIC is accountable for ensuring that:

- No Qualified MQL remains without an identified disposition.
- Every accepted MQL has a named CSM owner.
- MQL source attribution is maintained.
- Customer engagement is coordinated within agreed timelines.
- Aras receives appropriate progression visibility.
- Internal commercial conflicts are identified early.
- Stalled MQLs are escalated.
- Qualified leads are not lost through administrative or ownership gaps.

---

## 11. Recommended Performance Indicators

The PIC should be measured primarily on processing effectiveness and commercial progression, rather than simply number of leads received.

Recommended KPIs:

- MQL acknowledgement rate
- MQL acceptance / disposition rate
- Average time to disposition
- Average time to commercial owner assignment
- Percentage of accepted MQLs assigned within SLA
- MQL-to-customer-meeting conversion
- MQL-to-opportunity conversion
- Number of stalled MQLs
- Average age of unassigned MQLs
- MQL-to-POC conversion
- MQL-to-sale conversion
- Revenue attributable to handed-over MQLs

---

## 12. Recommended Role Statement

«The CSM Commercial MQL PIC is the designated Commercial Department representative responsible for receiving Qualified MQLs from Aras Integrasi and ensuring their orderly transition into CSM's internal commercial and sales processes. The PIC owns the intake, internal routing, assignment coordination, progression tracking and escalation of each MQL until an appropriate CSM commercial owner is established and the lead enters the relevant customer engagement or opportunity-management process.»

---

## 13. Operating Principle

The role should follow one simple governance principle:

**Every Qualified MQL must have a disposition, an owner and a next action.**

No MQL should remain within an informal or undefined handover state.
