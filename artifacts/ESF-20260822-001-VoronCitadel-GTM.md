# ESF-20260822-001 — Engineered Success Framework: VoronCitadel GTM Programme

## §9 Doctrine Application

This framework engineers the probability of success for the VoronCitadel GTM Programme. It does not describe what we want to happen — it defines the conditions under which success is the most probable outcome, and the checkpoints that confirm we're on track.

---

## 1. Success Definition

**What success looks like by December 31, 2026:**

| Dimension | Target | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Pipeline** | 5-7 VoronCitadel POC conversations opened | POC charters signed or in active negotiation |
| **Revenue** | RM 840k-1.176M projected (5-7 sales × RM 168k early adopter) | Signed LOIs or commercial proposals in motion |
| **Category Recognition** | GRC as a recognised term among 50+ target org decision-makers | Discovery meeting transcripts referencing GRC unprompted |
| **CSM Credibility** | CSM LOI signed, 1+ co-hosted event executed | LOI document + event report with attendee list |
| **Operational Rhythm** | Weekly MQL review running for 8+ consecutive weeks | Cadence log, no missed weeks |
| **Partner Channel** | 3+ active referral partners referring leads | Signed partner agreements + referred leads in pipeline |

**What failure looks like:**
- 0 POC conversations by Nov 15 (6 weeks of silence = programme drift)
- Marketing cannot resource even Phase 1 scope (52 orgs)
- CSM LOI unsigned by Oct 1 (Roundtable invitations cannot reference CSM)
- No weekly cadence established by Sep 15 (activity without accountability)

## 9. Failure Modes and Recovery

### Mode 1: CSM Non-Response on LOI
- **Trigger:** No CSM acknowledgment by Sep 5
- **Impact:** Credibility story weakens, Roundtable can't reference CSM co-hosting
- **Recovery:** (1) Amelia escalates via SSE Lead channels. (2) If no response by Sep 15, proceed with "Aras Integrasi GRC Programme" branding — strong but less institutional. (3) CSM LOI becomes a nice-to-have, not a blocker. Continue Layer 2-3 without it.

### Mode 2: Marketing Cannot Execute Phase 1
- **Trigger:** Norshaza flags capacity at Tuesday or within 2 weeks of Sep 1
- **Impact:** Outreach scope reduced, MQL generation slows
- **Recovery:** (1) Reduce Phase 1 to 25 orgs (top priority within Tier 2). (2) Outsource email outreach to a specialist firm (RM 5-10k for 350 contacts). (3) DAF + Hadri handle follow-up calls personally for top 10 accounts.

### Mode 3: 0 POC Conversations by Nov 15
- **Trigger:** No discovery meetings converted to POC by Nov 15
- **Impact:** Revenue target at risk, programme credibility damaged
- **Recovery:** (1) Full pipeline review with Kenny. (2) Pivot to direct ABM on 5 lighthouse accounts with existing relationships. (3) Extend timeline to Jan 2027 for first POC. (4) Revise revenue target to RM 168k-336k (1-2 sales) for the 5-month window.

### Mode 4: Budget Not Approved
- **Trigger:** Management paper rejected or indefinitely delayed
- **Impact:** Paid activities (email outreach, CYBERDSA, Roundtable, content) cannot execute
- **Recovery:** (1) Submit minimum viable scope: Layer 3 only (RM 80-120k) — events are the highest-conversion layer. (2) Layer 1 runs on no-cost items (CSM LOI, partner referrals). (3) Layer 2 runs on DAF's organic LinkedIn only (no paid amplification). (4) Total cost: RM 80-120k instead of RM 175-295k.
