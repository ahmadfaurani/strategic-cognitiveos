# Job Description — GTM Delivery Owner

**Role Title:** GTM Programme Delivery Owner
**Department:** Cyber Security Practice
**Reports To:** Faurani Jaafar, Director — Cyber Security Practice
**Location:** Level 30, Menara TM, KL (hybrid)
**Employment Type:** Full-time
**Initiative:** INIT-20260808-002 / INIT-20260804-001

---

## Purpose

Own the day-to-day execution of the VoronDRQ Go-To-Market programme — coordinating across Sales, Marketing, Product, and the CSM partnership to ensure the campaign moves from strategy to measurable pipeline. This role exists to offload operational coordination from the Director so he can focus on strategic direction, stakeholder relationships, and CSM liaison.

## Key Responsibilities

### Programme Coordination
- Run the weekly Marketing-Sales review and fortnightly pipeline review
- Maintain the CRM campaign dashboard — account engagement, stakeholder movement, sessions booked, demos delivered, POCs in flight
- Track every active account against the 7-stage pipeline: Account Validation → Stakeholder Verification → Qualification → Discovery → Demonstration → POC Definition → Commercial Conversion
- Ensure every active account has a named owner, action, and dated next step (zero accounts without next action)
- Coordinate the monthly executive review with the COO and CSM counterpart

### Campaign Execution Support
- Manage the contact verification sprint before campaign launch (45–60 named stakeholders across 15 accounts)
- Maintain the account thesis library — ensure each institution has a current 1-page profile
- Coordinate content development timelines with Marketing and Practice (executive brief, diagnostic, roundtable deck)
- Track claims review status with Legal/Governance before any external distribution
- Manage the feedback loop — capture prospect objections, message themes, and product questions for the monthly Product Feedback session

### Cross-Functional Liaison
- Primary operational point of contact between Aras Sales, Aras Marketing, Aras Practice, and CSM joint account owners
- Schedule and coordinate discovery sessions, demonstrations, and POC kickoff meetings
- Maintain the joint account ownership roster (1 CSM + 1 Aras per account)
- Flag bottlenecks and resource conflicts to the Director before they become blockers

### Reporting
- Weekly campaign status report: accounts activated, stakeholders engaged, replies, objections, meetings booked, demos delivered
- Fortnightly pipeline forecast: stage movement, conversion rates, pipeline value by segment
- Monthly programme health dashboard for executive review

## Requirements

### Essential
- 3–5 years programme/project management experience in B2B technology, cybersecurity, or regulated industry
- Experience with CRM platforms (HubSpot, Salesforce, or equivalent) — campaign tracking, pipeline management, reporting
- Strong organisational discipline — can manage 15+ concurrent account tracks without losing detail
- Excellent written communication — able to produce clear status reports and meeting minutes
- Comfortable working across Sales, Marketing, and Technical teams as a coordinator (not a specialist in any one)
- Malaysian regulatory awareness — basic familiarity with RMiT, SC GTRM, PDPA, BNM guidelines (enough to understand context, not to advise)

### Desirable
- Experience in financial services sales support or account management
- PMP, PRINCE2, or equivalent programme management certification
- Experience with email automation platforms and LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Existing network in Malaysian cybersecurity or financial services compliance

## Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

- Campaign pilot launched on schedule (Week 1 by Sep 2)
- CRM dashboard live and reporting by Week 1
- 15 account theses completed and maintained
- Weekly, fortnightly, and monthly review cadences established and attended
- Zero active accounts without a named owner and dated next action
- Programme status report delivered on time every week
