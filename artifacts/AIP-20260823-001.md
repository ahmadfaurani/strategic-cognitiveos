### AIP-02: Reframe CSM Ask — Aras-Led, CSM-Visibility

**Leverage:** Lower friction = faster Gate 2 close. Zulfeka agreed to a "smaller working group" for focus. Asking him to commit CSM to standing cadences and account nominations exceeds that scope. Reframe so every ask is something he can say yes to in the meeting without checking capacity.

**Intelligence:**
The initial pitch asked Zulfeka to:
- Designate a CSM delegate for weekly MQL review (standing commitment)
- Nominate 2-3 ABM accounts from CSM's side (resource commitment)
- Review and sign LOI by Sep 15 (process commitment)

Each is a resource ask. Zulfeka is Head of Business Development — his capacity is constrained. The pattern from DOC-20260819-001 Risk #6: "stakeholder support remains verbal." The fix is reducing the commitment surface, not increasing it.

**Action:** Reframe each pillar:

| Pillar | Original Ask | Reframed Ask |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| MQL | CSM designates standing delegate for weekly review | Aras runs weekly review. CSM has visibility — no standing delegate required. CSM can attend when relevant. |
| ABM | CSM nominates 2-3 accounts | Aras proposes 5 accounts with rationale. CSM reviews and adds/adjusts. One decision, not a process. |
| LOI | CSM reviews and signs by Sep 15 | Aras drafts LOI. CSM reviews and signs — one document, one signature. |

**Why this works:** Zulfeka says yes to principles (MQL definition, ABM approach, co-branding narrative) not resource commitments. The cadence and depth of CSM involvement can deepen AFTER Gate 2 closes — but only if Gate 2 closes first.

**Owner:** DAF
**Deadline:** Before Wednesday Aug 26
**Success:** Pitch document (ART-20260823-001) updated or annotated with Aras-led framing
