### AIP-01: Funnel Reconciliation One-Pager — Pre-Read for Tuesday

**Leverage:** Makes Tuesday a decision meeting, not an exploration meeting.

**Intelligence:**
The deck proposes 300 contacts → 40 meetings → 15 assessments → 3 pilots. DAF's model: 193 orgs → 1,300 stakeholders → 260 engaged (20%) → 78 meetings (30%) → 23 POCs (30%) → 7 sales (30%) → RM 1.176M. The gap is the denominator — the deck doesn't use the stakeholder database.

**Action:** Prepare a one-page document reconciling both models into a single agreed funnel. Structure:

| Stage | Deck Target | DAF Model | Proposed Agreement |
|-------|------------|----------|-------------------|
| Total addressable | 300 contacts | 1,300 stakeholders (193 orgs) | 1,300 stakeholders (193 orgs) — existing asset |
| Engaged (20%) | — | 260 | 260 (Tier 2 first: 52 orgs × ~5 stakeholders = 260) |
| Meetings (30%) | 40 | 78 | 78 |
| Discovery/Assessment (30%) | 15 | 23 | 23 |
| POC (30%) | 3 | 7 | 5-7 (range) |
| Close (30%) | — | 7 | 5-7 (range) |
| Revenue | — | RM 1.176M | RM 840k-1.176M (range) |

**Key insight:** DAF's model isn't aggressive — it's a standard B2B funnel applied to real data. The deck's 300 is actually a subset (Layer 1+2 reach), not the full denominator. Both are correct at different scopes. The reconciliation is: deck = Layer 1-2 reach targets, DAF = full programme conversion targets. They're complementary, not competing.

**Owner:** DAF (Ember to draft by Monday Aug 24)
**Delivery:** Pre-read sent Monday evening to Said Farid, Norshaza, Hadri
**Success:** Both sides arrive Tuesday with a shared funnel document, not competing targets
