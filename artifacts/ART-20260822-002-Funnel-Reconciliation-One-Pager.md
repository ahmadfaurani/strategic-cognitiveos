# VoronCitadel GTM — Funnel Reconciliation One-Pager

**Pre-Read for Tuesday August 25, 2:30 PM Alignment Meeting**

**Purpose:** Align both teams on a single conversion funnel before the meeting. This document proposes a reconciled model that incorporates both the GTM deck targets and the Practice's conversion model. The meeting should confirm or adjust — not start from blank.

---

## The Two Models

**GTM Deck (Marketing):** 300 contacts → 40 meetings → 15 assessments → 3 pilots

**Practice Model (DAF):** 193 orgs → 1,300 stakeholders → 260 engaged (20%) → 78 meetings (30%) → 23 POCs (30%) → 7 sales (30%) → RM 1.176M

**The gap:** The deck's 300 is a reach number (Layer 1-2 channels). DAF's 1,300 is the total addressable stakeholder database. Both are correct — at different scopes. They're complementary, not competing.

## Why This Reconciliation Works

1. **Uses the real database as denominator** — DAF's 1,300 stakeholders is the actual addressable market, not an abstract number
2. **Phases the execution** — Phase 1 (52 orgs) is what Marketing can resource; Phase 2-3 expansion is contingent on Phase 1 results
3. **Applies standard B2B conversion rates** — 20% engagement, 30% per stage conversion. Not aggressive, not conservative
4. **Deck's 300 is the Layer 1-2 reach** — email outreach + LinkedIn + partner referrals combined. It's a subset of the 1,300, not a competing number
5. **Revenue target is conservative for Phase 1** — RM 168k-336k (1-2 sales) is achievable in 5 months for category creation. The RM 1.176M (7 sales) is the full programme target if all 3 phases activate

## Conversion Rate Benchmarks (for confidence)

| Rate | Proposed | B2B SaaS Benchmark | Assessment |
|------|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Engagement (MQL/Total) | 20% | 15-25% | Standard |
| Meeting/MQL | 30% | 25-35% | Standard |
| Assessment/Meeting | 30% | 25-40% | Standard |
| POC/Assessment | 30% | 20-40% | Standard |
| Close/POC | 30% | 25-50% (longer cycle) | Conservative — 5-month window is tight |

**The conservative assumption is Close/POC at 30%.** In enterprise GRC, POC-to-close can take 3-6 months. Our 5-month window means some POCs started in Nov won't close until Q1 2027. The RM 168k-336k Phase 1 target accounts for this — it's the pipeline opened, not fully closed, by Dec 31.
