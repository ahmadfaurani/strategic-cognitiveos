# ACT-20260819-003 — Engage Azrul on CyberDSA 2026 Joint Activation Framing

**Owner:** DAF
**Priority:** High
**Deadline:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** Pending

## Action

Engage Azrul Nazim on the CyberDSA 2026 joint activation, using the framing prescribed in DEC-20260819-004.

## Engagement Framing

**Use:** "We have reached the point where the CSM–Aras partnership can translate into a visible national market outcome. We would like to align with you on how CyberDSA can become that milestone and, together, identify the appropriate CSM stakeholders to bring into the initiative."

**Avoid:** "Azrul, these are the things we need CSM to do."

## Objectives

1. Align on the joint CSM × Aras strategic narrative for CyberDSA 2026
2. Validate CyberDSA as an appropriate partnership and market-activation milestone
3. Identify and activate the correct CSM stakeholders (through Azrul's institutional navigation)
4. Facilitate cross-functional coordination where required
5. Maintain continuity between existing CSM–Aras collaboration and CyberDSA activation

## Sequence Position

This is the FIRST step in the CSM Activation Triangle sequence:
1. **Azrul** (this action) — partnership alignment and internal mobilisation
2. Fahdzli — strategic legitimacy / senior institutional alignment
3. Zulfeka — business and market ownership

The triangle must be aligned before engaging the broader CSM machinery.

## Constraints

- Do NOT assign Azrul technical POC, product demo, deployment, engineering, sales execution, or project management roles (per DEC-20260819-004)
- Do NOT auto-align Wan Roshaimi or Zaharudin as POC-support — technical participation only after requirement defined and appropriate CSM owner identified
- Preserve Azrul's institutional standing — give him agency, not tasks
