# Action: Define CyberDSA 2026 Demo Preparation Workstream

**Assigned by:** Ember (Cognitive Loop Review, 2026-08-21)
**Owner:** DAF (with Fuad)
**Due:** August 28, 2026
**Priority:** Critical

## Context

Fuad's GovSec Product Roadmap (DOC-20260821-004) covers 12 engineering projects across Q3 2026 – Q2 2027. Q3 2026 is on track. However, the roadmap is entirely product engineering — no project addresses CyberDSA demo preparation.

CyberDSA (Oct 5-7, 2026) requires:
- **Demo script** — defined flows for each showcase scenario (threat intel feed, AI analyst query, executive dashboard, alert-to-case workflow)
- **VIP walkthrough** — executive narrative for booth visitors (Minister, CSM DG, NACSA, potential clients)
- **Booth environment spec** — hardware, network connectivity, seeded data, fallback states for live demo failures
- **Rehearsal schedule** — timed practice runs, demo operator training, contingency plans

## Rationale

This is the single largest gap from the Cognitive Loop review. The roadmap answers "is the product ready?" but not "can we show it convincingly?" A hardened platform with no rehearsed demo narrative = a platform that looks unfinished to VIP visitors.

## Deliverables

1. Demo script document (per-scenario flows)
2. VIP walkthrough narrative
3. Booth environment specification
4. Rehearsal schedule (target: complete by Sep 28, 1 week before CyberDSA)

## Dependencies

- Fuad's Q3 hardening progress (on track per DOC-20260821-004)
- Hadri's delivery documentation (ACT-20260810-005)
- ACT-20260819-010 (WIG messaging implementation — due Aug 24)
