# Action

Establish a dedicated CSM–Aras programme management mechanism for the GTM pipeline. Without it, Marketing Qualified Leads risk being generated faster than the joint team can qualify and progress them.

The mechanism must cover:
- Marketing → MQL → CSM qualification → discovery → product demonstration → POC → commercial conversion
- Joint account team assignment (1 Aras + 1 CSM per account)
- Pipeline tracking and reporting cadence
- POC governance and success criteria

# Context

Athena identifies this as a "material operating gap." The 193-organisation target universe is established, the operating model is proposed, but there is no dedicated programme management to drive the pipeline.

# Deliverable

Programme management mechanism document, including: pipeline stages, qualification criteria, ownership model, reporting cadence, POC governance.

# Priority

P0 — Immediate (13–21 August window)
