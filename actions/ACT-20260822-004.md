# ACT-20260822-004 — Define GTM Funnel Metrics

## Action

Establish a 5-stage funnel with conversion targets, replacing the deck's 4 flat numbers.

## Proposed Model (DAF's)

1. MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) — 260 (from 1,300 stakeholders, 20% engagement)
2. Sales Meeting — 78 (30% of MQL)
3. Discovery/Technical — 23 (30% of meetings)
4. POC — 7 (30% of discovery)
5. Close — 7 (assumed from POC, or apply close rate)

## Deck's Model (for comparison)

300 contacts → 40 meetings → 15 assessments → 3 pilots

## Gap

DAF's model uses the 1,300-stakeholder database as denominator. Deck doesn't. Need agreement on which model is the measurement framework.

## Success Criteria

- Funnel stages defined and agreed
- Conversion targets per stage agreed
- Measurement cadence (weekly MQL review proposed by DAF)
- CRM/tracking system identified
