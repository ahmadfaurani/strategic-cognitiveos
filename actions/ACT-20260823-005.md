# Action

Reframe the CSM ask in the Zulfeka pitch from "CSM commits to" → "Aras runs, CSM has visibility":

- **MQL:** Aras runs weekly review. CSM has visibility and input, not asked to allocate a standing delegate.
- **ABM:** Aras proposes 5 lighthouse accounts with rationale. CSM reviews and adds/adjusts — lighter than "nominate 2-3 from your side."
- **LOI:** Aras drafts. CSM reviews and signs — one decision, not a process.
