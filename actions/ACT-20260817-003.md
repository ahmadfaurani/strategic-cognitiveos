# Action: CSM to Identify Focal Points for CyberDSA 2026 Alignment

**Assigned by:** DAF (via email, Aug 17, 2026)
**Requested of:** CSM teams (via Fahdzli)
**Due:** Before alignment session

## Description

DAF's email requests: "Where convenient, perhaps the respective CSM teams could also begin identifying the appropriate focal points and priority stakeholders within their areas so that we can make the session as practical and action-oriented as possible."

CSM focal points needed for:
1. **Partnership** — Who owns the joint positioning narrative from CSM side?
2. **Marketing & Media** — Who coordinates joint communications and media coverage?
3. **Commercial** — Who identifies priority government, GLC and enterprise stakeholders?
4. **Post-Launch** — Who manages POC and commercial follow-up?

## Context

This aligns with the Stakeholder Coverage Plan (CONV-20260815-005) that already defines Aras-side coverage. CSM-side focal points are the missing piece.

## Success Criteria

- CSM identifies focal points for each of the four workstream areas
- Focal points confirmed before or during the alignment session
