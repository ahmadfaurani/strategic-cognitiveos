# Action

DAF to review how the AI Systems Co-Design Lab can strategically accelerate the three existing CSM-Aras workstreams and serve as a CyberDSA October 2026 demo asset. Determine whether to guide CSM's use case proposal toward existing workstream acceleration.

# Context

The Co-Design Lab is the 4th CSM track initiated on Aug 4. Unlike the other three (integration/GTM tracks), it positions Aras as a capability provider offering GPU compute infrastructure. This creates a strategic question: should the Co-Design Lab be:

1. **A cross-workstream accelerator** — guide CSM to use the compute to build AI features for CMERP (WS2), threat intelligence ML models for TI integration (WS3), or AI-assisted VoronDRQ components (WS1)?
2. **A standalone capability engagement** — let CSM propose an independent use case that may or may not align with existing tracks?
3. **A CyberDSA demo asset** — ensure the Co-Design Lab produces a tangible AI prototype that can be demonstrated at the joint product launch?

This review should inform:
- How Aras frames the "review and follow up" step when CSM responds
- Whether DAF should communicate strategic preferences to Hadri before CSM submits
- Whether the Co-Design Lab should be added to the CyberDSA mobilisation plan (DEC-20260804-001)

# Deliverable

Strategic positioning memo (internal) covering:
- Recommended use case direction for CSM (aligned with existing workstreams vs. open)
- CyberDSA demo potential assessment
- Impact on resource contention (4th parallel track)
- Guidance for Hadri's review of CSM's application
