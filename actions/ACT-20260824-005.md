# Action

Identify the 3–4 participating organisations for Bursa POC Phase 0. This is the **single largest gap** identified by the Cognitive Loop Review (ASSESS-20260824-001).

## Why This Is Critical

Every Phase 0 deliverable depends on knowing who the participants are:
- **Step 1 (TPRM Deployment):** Need organisations to onboard vendors
- **Step 2 (Shared Vendor Validation):** Need actual vendor inventories from participants
- **Step 3 (Federated Compliance Document Check):** Need participants maintaining environments for aggregation

Without this, Fuad's development plan (ACT-20260824-001, Sep 7) is generic, not participant-informed.

## What's Needed

- 3–4 named organisations within Bursa Malaysia's sectorial purview
- Confirmation of participation intent (formal or informal)
- Initial contact for technical engagement
- Understanding of each participant's vendor inventory availability

## Warning

Per ASSESS-20260824-001: If participants not identified by **Sep 14**, the 4-month timeline becomes mathematically impossible and Phase 0 should be reframed as a feasibility study.

## Deadline

Sep 7, 2026 — aligned with Fuad's development plan deadline so the plan can be participant-informed.
