# Action

Define Bursa's required aggregate visibility for the federated management view. The following elements must be specified:

1. **Data access** — What data Bursa can access at aggregate level vs. what remains participant-only
2. **Document access** — What compliance evidence/documents Bursa can evaluate or aggregate
3. **Tenant isolation** — How participant environments are isolated while enabling Bursa-level visibility
4. **Reporting hierarchy** — What reporting structure enables Bursa's sectorial oversight function

## Context

DAF's email (CONV-20260824-001) lists "Federated Management View" as one of the key technical areas requiring definition. This requirement flows from:
- Act 854 sector-level oversight requirements
- The federated compliance document check phase
- The broader 24-entity federation hypothesis (DEC-20260824-002)

## Deliverable

Requirements specification document covering the four elements above, suitable for inclusion in the URD/SRS during the Validation Phase.
