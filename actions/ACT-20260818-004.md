# Action

The branding team must:

1. **Adopt the narrative hierarchy** — all materials flow top-down: Sovereign Capability → Integrated Stack → Technology Pillars → Individual Features
2. **Apply the campaign line** — "Built in Malaysia. Integrated for Malaysia. Engineered for Sovereignty." as the primary campaign message
3. **Use the corporate positioning** — "Aras Integrasi — Malaysian Sovereign Technology Integrator" in all corporate/brand contexts
4. **Enforce messaging guardrails** (§9 of DOC-20260818-002):
   - Not "three new products" → "integrated sovereign cybersecurity stack"
   - Not "another cybersecurity vendor" → "sovereign technology integrator"
   - Not "AI-powered cybersecurity solutions" → use specific capability descriptions
   - Not "locally hosted foreign technology" → "Malaysian-built"
   - Not "Made in Malaysia" alone → explain capability ownership
   - Not "nationally deployed" → "designed for national-grade deployment"
   - Not implying government endorsement → "working with ecosystem partners including CyberSecurity Malaysia"
5. **Apply brand language** (§8) — use primary vocabulary consistently
6. **Align all materials** — one-pagers, demo materials, social media, press releases, stakeholder briefings, booth graphics

# Owner

DAF (strategic direction) → branding team (execution)

# Deadline

September 1, 2026 — materials must be ready for the Claims QC deadline (T-7 before CyberDSA, Sep 28 per DEC-20260818-009)

- Messaging-to-avoid checklist enforced across all drafts
