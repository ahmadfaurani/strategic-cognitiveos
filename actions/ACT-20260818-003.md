# Action

DAF should proactively reach out to Dr. Azree during the proposal drafting period to:

1. Share Aras infrastructure specifications (cluster nodes, GPU types, networking, storage capabilities)
2. Offer to review component 4.4 (Deployment Requirements) for alignment
3. Suggest infrastructure configurations that Aras can support
4. Position Aras as a collaborative partner in the proposal development, not just an external evaluator

This engagement should be informal and supportive — the goal is to ensure the proposal's infrastructure section naturally describes what Aras can provide, making the subsequent Aras evaluation a confirmation rather than a negotiation.

September 5, 2026 — engage early enough in the drafting period to influence the proposal content. Leaves 6 days for UPM to finalise.

- Confirmation that UPM is aware of Aras capabilities for proposal alignment
