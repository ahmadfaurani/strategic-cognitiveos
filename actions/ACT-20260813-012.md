# Action

Produce a single CSM–Aras Master Workstream Register, mapping every initiative to:

- CSM owner
- Aras owner
- Current status
- Next action
- Dependency
- Target outcome
- Commercial pathway
- Deadline

# Context

Athena identifies this as "the most valuable next artefact." The register consolidates all CSM–Aras activities under the 5-pillar Joint Operating Model structure (INIT-20260813-005), replacing the current scattered ACT items with a single coherent programme view.

**Formally commissioned by DAF (DEC-20260813-001, 13 Aug 2026)** as the first Joint Operating Model deliverable.

Master Workstream Register document, organised by the 5 pillars (P1 Cyber Intelligence, P2 Sovereign AI Platform, P3 AI Cyber R&D, P4 Product & GTM, P5 Capability Development), with cross-cutting governance layer.
