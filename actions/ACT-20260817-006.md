# Action: Institutionalize Weekly Review Cadence

**Assigned by:** DAF (self-assigned, per DEC-20260817-001)
**Owner:** DAF
**Due:** August 19, 2026 (before first meeting)

## Description

Implement the operational infrastructure for the Tuesday 2:30 PM Weekly Portfolio, GTM & Delivery Review meeting established in DEC-20260817-001.

## Required Deliverables

1. **Calendar Invite** — Recurring meeting, every Tuesday 2:30 PM MYT
   - Title: "Portfolio, GTM & Delivery Review"
   - Duration: 60 minutes
   - Location: Menara TM Level 30 (with virtual option)
   - Attendees: DAF, Hadri, Fuad (core); Shuhada, Azzatullina, Amelia (as needed)
   - Description: Include agenda template and purpose statement

2. **Agenda Template** — Standardized 60-minute structure
   - Portfolio Review (15 min)
   - GTM Alignment (20 min)
   - Delivery Accountability (15 min)
   - Risk & Escalation (10 min)

3. **Meeting Notes Template** — For capturing decisions, actions, risks
   - Attendance log
   - Action items (owner, due date, status)
   - Decisions made (with DEC record ID if significant)
   - Risks identified (with RSK record ID if significant)

4. **First Meeting Preparation** — Portfolio status snapshot
   - All P0/P1 initiatives with current status
   - Deadline tracking (what's due this week, what slipped)
   - Dependency map (cross-workstream blockers)

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Calendar invite sent and accepted by core participants
- [ ] Agenda template distributed before first meeting
- [ ] Meeting notes template ready for use
- [ ] First meeting (Aug 19) conducted with full agenda
- [ ] Action items from first meeting recorded in CognitiveOS

## Context

This action operationalizes DEC-20260817-001. Without proper infrastructure, the meeting risks becoming ad-hoc and losing its governance value. The templates and cadence must be established before the first session.
