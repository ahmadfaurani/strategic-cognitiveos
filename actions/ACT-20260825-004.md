# Action

DAF and Hadri will align the engagement approach for Zulfeka before pushing the CSM commercial workstream further.

## Required Outcome

Treat CSM commercial activation as two separate gates:

1. **Role definition** — define what the CSM-side Commercial Program Manager owns, what authority the role carries, its operating responsibilities and how it interfaces with Aras.
2. **Person activation** — once the role is clear, engage Zulfeka to activate or nominate the appropriate CSM-side owner.

## Engagement Principle

The objective is not merely to request a nominee. The engagement should make the desired operating model easy for Zulfeka to adopt by presenting a clear role, bounded responsibilities and a low-friction activation path.
