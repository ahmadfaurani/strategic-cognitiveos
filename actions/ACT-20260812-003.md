# Action Statement

DAF should assess the strategic implications of MyCERT's acceptance and their revealed GenAI research work for:

1. **Cross-workstream alignment** — MyCERT's threat response mission and GenAI work may connect to the TI integration workstream (INIT-20260804-003). If MyCERT is building AI-assisted threat detection/analysis, the Co-Design Lab compute could accelerate TI integration outcomes.

2. **CyberDSA positioning** — A confirmed CSM/MyCERT cohort with active GenAI prototyping on Aras infrastructure strengthens the demonstration potential for CyberDSA October 2026 (per DEC-20260804-001). Should the Co-Design Lab engagement be elevated from "strategic amplifier" to "demonstration asset" for CyberDSA?

3. **Use case guidance** — Should Aras guide MyCERT's Co-Design Lab use case toward existing workstreams (TI integration, CMERP, VoronCitadel) or let MyCERT define their own direction? What is the strategic preference?

4. **MyCERT intelligence** — "Several local research products integrating with Generative AI" is a significant intelligence point. Understanding what MyCERT is building will inform Aras's product strategy and competitive positioning.
