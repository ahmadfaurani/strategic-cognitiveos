# ACT-20260823-002 — Incorporate PM/TPM Role Boundary into TBH-001 JD

## Action

When drafting the TBH-001 job description, incorporate the 14-function PM/TPM responsibility boundary table from ASSESS-20260823-001 §6. The PM coordinates product-governance processes (facilitate, maintain cadence, track progress) but does NOT own product content (roadmap, backlog, release plans — those belong to the future TPM role).

## Required Output

- Job description with explicit responsibility boundary
- Boundary exclusions section: "PM does NOT own: product roadmap content, product backlog prioritisation, release plans, technical architecture, commercial decisions, executive relationship ownership"
- SPOF test: "If this person leaves, what breaks?" — answer should be "coordination cadence", not "product direction"
