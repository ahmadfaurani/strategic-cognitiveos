# Amelia: Assess Media Contact Readiness + Substantiation Brief

## Objective

Amelia to assess her media contacts for potential CyberDSA 2026 coverage — BUT must obtain a substantiation brief from DAF/Ember before using the "Malaysia's First National Grade, Sovereign Technology Company" framing externally.

## Context

DAF's soft activation email (CONV-20260816-002) asks Amelia to discuss media coverage based on the framing "Malaysia's First National Grade, Sovereign Technology Company."

ADEP-001 due diligence (D3) has flagged this claim as **[LOW] unsupported** — no comparative analysis exists against other Malaysian technology companies, and no external authority has validated the "first" designation.

## Specific Requirements

1. **Amelia to identify** which media contacts could provide CyberDSA coverage
2. **Before any external engagement:** Amelia must receive an approved external language brief that either:
   - Substantiates "first" with evidence (if available), OR
   - Reframes to "among Malaysia's first" or "a Malaysian-built sovereign technology capability"
3. **Approved external language** to be provided by DAF with Ember support

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Media contact list identified and shared with DAF
- [ ] Substantiation brief or approved alternative language received before any media engagement
- [ ] No external use of "Malaysia's First" without approved substantiation

## Evidence Required

- Media contact list (names, outlets, relationship)
- Approved external language brief (signed off by DAF)

## Escalation Condition

If a media contact requests engagement before the substantiation brief is ready, escalate to DAF before proceeding.
