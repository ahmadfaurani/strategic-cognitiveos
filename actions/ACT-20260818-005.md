# Action

The visual/booth design team must:

1. **Design the booth as one architecture** — not three product stations
2. **Implement the visual stack:**
   ```
   SOVEREIGN CYBERSECURITY
   Governance & Compliance (VoronCitadel)
       ↓
   Threat Intelligence & AI (GovSec TIP)
       ↓
   Operational Cybersecurity
       ↓
   Emerging Technology Security (chain:SENTRY)
       ↓
   National-Grade Deployment
       ↓
   Malaysian Infrastructure | Malaysian Data | Malaysian Engineering | Malaysian Institutions
   ```
3. **Position three products as components** within the unified architecture — not as separate displays
4. **Apply the campaign line** visually: "Built in Malaysia. Integrated for Malaysia. Engineered for Sovereignty."
5. **Use the brand equation** in supporting materials:
   Malaysian Engineering + Sovereign Infrastructure + Cybersecurity Technology + Institutional Expertise + Operational Integration = National Sovereign Cybersecurity Capability

# Owner

DAF (strategic direction) → branding/visual design team (execution)

September 15, 2026 — design concept ready for review before Claims QC deadline (Sep 28)

- CyberDSA 2026 booth design concept (layout + visual hierarchy)
- Digital/display materials reflecting one-architecture narrative
- Print collateral aligned to brand language
