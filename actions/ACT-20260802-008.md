Create a decision rights matrix clarifying: (1) decisions only DAF can make, (2) decisions that can be made by delivery owners with notification, (3) decisions that can be made independently by delivery owners, (4) decisions that require collective input.

# Required Output

Decision rights matrix covering:
- Strategic direction decisions (DAF only)
- Commercial commitments (DAF only above threshold)
- Technical architecture (delivery owner with DAF notification)
- Operational decisions (delivery owner independent)
- Stakeholder communication (defined by engagement record)
- Resource allocation (DAF for budget, delivery owner for time)
