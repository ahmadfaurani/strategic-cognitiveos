## Analytical Framework

### Marginal Seat Classification

Seats were classified as "marginal" based on:
1. **Majority < 1,000 votes** (core criterion)
2. **Margin < 5%** of total valid votes
3. **Multi-cornered contest dynamics** in 2022

### Turnout Impact Analysis

Turnout scenarios were modeled based on:
- Historical turnout patterns (2022 state vs. GE15)
- Analyst projections from multiple sources
- Youth voter mobilization potential
- Outstation voter return estimates

### Party Advantage Assessment

Party advantages at different turnout levels were assessed using:
- Organizational strength metrics
- Historical performance in high/low turnout elections
- Demographic composition of each party's base
- Expert analyst commentary

## Data Verification

### Cross-Referencing

All numerical data was cross-referenced across:
- Minimum 2 independent news sources
- Official election results where available
- Multiple analyst reports

### Discrepancy Resolution

Where sources disagreed:
- Priority given to official SPR data
- If unavailable, majority consensus among sources
- Analyst projections clearly labeled as estimates

## Limitations

### Known Limitations

1. **2026 Data Projections**: Youth voter percentages and turnout estimates are projections based on 2022 baselines and analyst forecasts
2. **Candidate Lineups**: Final candidate nominations not yet complete (Nomination Day: June 27, 2026)
3. **Coalition Dynamics**: PN coalition structure may change before election (PAS-Bersatu split ongoing)
4. **External Factors**: Economic conditions, scandals, or unexpected events could shift dynamics

### Assumptions Made

1. Weekend polling (Saturday) will increase outstation voter return
2. No major redelineation of constituency boundaries since 2022
3. Undi18 automatic registration continues to expand youth electorate
4. No significant changes to electoral roll integrity
