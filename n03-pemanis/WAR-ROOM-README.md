## 3. STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT

**Overall Seat Dynamics:**

N03 Pemanis is a **BN-leaning seat** with a 3-corner fight in 2026. In 2022, BN won with 50.33% against PH (26.05%) and PN (22.19%). The seat has a Malay majority (62.6%) with significant Chinese minority (33.3%).

**Path to Victory:**

Extremely difficult. PH needs:
1. Chinese turnout >85%
2. Significant Malay swing (15%+) from BN to PH
3. PN voters breaking toward PH (unlikely given PN-MIPP is PAS-aligned)

**Realistic Goal:** Improve vote share, build ground for future, minimize losses.

**Key Voter Segments to Target:**
- Chinese voters (33.3%): Base turnout maximization
- Indian voters (2.4%): Small but potential swing
- Youth (23.6% aged 18-29): Registration + first-time voters

**Opposition's Likely Strategy:**
- BN: Defensive, incumbency advantage, Malay consolidation
- PN-MIPP: Attack BN from Islamic angle, siphon Malay votes

**Critical Success Factors:**
1. Chinese turnout >85%
2. Hold PH vote share >30%
3. Prevent BN from exceeding 55%
