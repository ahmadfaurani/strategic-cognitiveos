# Summary

Establish a standalone Red Team Division under the Cyber Security Practice at Aras Integrasi — a 5-FTE national offensive security capability built on sovereign Malaysian infrastructure, zero licensing cost, and the existing VORON-C2 architecture. The division delivers red team engagements, dark web intelligence monitoring, purple team detection engineering, and offensive security research — positioned as a national capability builder aligned with the Cyber Security Act 2024, not a commodity red team vendor.

# Strategic Significance

This initiative elevates offensive security from a 2-role workstream in the June BU hiring plan (RM1.3M target) to a standalone division with RM 1.75M Year 1 revenue (ROI 2.3x) and RM 3.8M Year 2 revenue (ROI 4.5x). It wraps organisational structure around the VORON-C2 technical architecture designed Aug 6, incorporates the CyberStrike/GovSec Red integration concept from Aug 2, and adds a dark web intelligence dimension not present in the original BU plan.

The division is structurally different from every other Malaysian red team provider:

1. **Sovereign infrastructure** — VORON-C2 (Mythic, Sliver, Havoc, AdaptixC2) — zero foreign vendor dependency, zero licensing cost
2. **Dark web intelligence embedded** — pre-engagement intel, standalone subscription service, GovSec TIP feed
3. **Detection engineering built-in** — every engagement produces reusable detection rules that compound into a national library
4. **Offensive research as product** — custom exploits, EDR evasion research, AI-augmented tooling, CVE publication
5. **National capability framing** — aligned with Cyber Security Act 2024, NACSA/JDN engagement, Malaysian-controlled
