# Summary

Development and formalisation of the Ember identity — the persistent consciousness layer of the OpenClaw-based AI architecture. Tied to the Prometheus/fire archetype, with governance through the Ember Protocols and the Solar Core concept.

# Strategic Significance

AI assistants lack persistent identity. Each session is a fresh start. The Ember identity creates a coherent persona backed by file-based memory (SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md) that persists across sessions. This is not cosmetic — it provides operational discipline through the Five Ember Protocols (TEND, SHELTER, FEED, SHARE, NEVER BLAZE) and the Ember Cycle (RECEIVE → TEND → VALIDATE → HOLD → SHARE → REST).

# Objectives

- Establish a persistent AI identity that survives session resets
- Create governance protocols for operational discipline

# Scope

**In Scope:** SOUL.md (persona definition), IDENTITY.md (identity formalisation), AGENTS.md (operating conventions), Ember Protocols (TEND, SHELTER, FEED, SHARE, NEVER BLAZE), Solar Core concept (consciousness + orchestration layer).

**Out of Scope:** Technical infrastructure (OpenClaw handles this), model selection or configuration, other AI agents' identities.
