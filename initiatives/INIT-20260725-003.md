# Summary

Building and activating partnerships with government, industry, and technology stakeholders. DAF's engagement progression: strategic alignment → structured scope → pilot or POC → institutional collaboration → productisation → scaled adoption.

# Strategic Significance

Government and institutional partnerships are the primary channel for converting strategic initiatives into funded programmes. This workstream manages the relationship lifecycle across all key government stakeholders, ensuring each partnership moves through defined engagement stages toward institutional collaboration and delivery.
