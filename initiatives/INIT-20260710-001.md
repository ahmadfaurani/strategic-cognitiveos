# Summary

RMiT (Risk Management in Technology) compliance campaign targeting 250 BNM-regulated financial institutions. Stakeholder collection, battle cards, and engagement strategy for cybersecurity service offerings.

# Strategic Significance

Bank Negara Malaysia's RMiT framework requires regulated financial institutions to implement specific cybersecurity controls. This creates a market opportunity for cybersecurity services. The campaign maps institutions, stakeholders, and compliance gaps to drive targeted engagement.

# Mission Alignment

- Cybersecurity productisation
- Commercial market development
- Compliance-driven revenue generation

# Objectives

- Map 250 BNM-regulated FIs across tiers
- Collect stakeholder profiles (target: 143 FIs, 1,001 stakeholders)
- Build battle cards for engagement
- Estimate revenue potential (RM 28.5M–71.5M)
- Continue Tier 1 bank collection (16 remaining)
