# Summary

Transforming technical cybersecurity capabilities into market-ready products, packages, and service propositions. DAF's role: define the proposition, target stakeholder, strategic narrative, commercial pathway, and partnership model around technical assets.

# Strategic Significance

Aras Integrasi has significant cybersecurity technical capabilities but they exist as projects rather than products. This workstream drives the transformation from technical capability to market-ready product, with clear readiness gates, commercial pathways, and target stakeholders for each product.

# Mission Alignment

- Cybersecurity productisation
- Product management discipline
- Commercial market development

# Objectives

- Define product readiness gates and enforce them
- Advance GovSec TIP from pilot-ready to delivery-ready
- Advance VoronDRQ from proposition to pilot-ready
- Move concept-stage products (ChainSentry, VoronScout, LE-UIP, SEC-AF) toward proposition
- Establish commercial pathway for each product

# Scope

**In Scope:** Product definition and readiness gating for GovSec TIP, VoronDRQ, ChainSentry, VoronScout, LE-UIP, and SEC-AF. Commercial pathway definition, target stakeholder mapping, competitive positioning.

**Out of Scope:** Government partnership engagement (Workstream C), commercial pipeline management (Workstream E), organisational build-out (Workstream F), direct RMiT compliance campaign (INIT-20260710-001).

- VoronScout — active, target: CSM/enterprise, concept, Tier 2

# Commercial Model

Product readiness gates: concept → proposition → pilot-ready → delivery-ready → scaled. Each gate requires defined criteria before external positioning. Conversion targets: engagement-to-pilot, pilot-to-contract, partnership-to-delivery.

# Readiness Assessment
