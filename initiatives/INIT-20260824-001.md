# Summary

The Bursa Malaysia VoronCitadel Sectorial POC is the first named POC target originating from the CSM channel. Initiated by Azrul on Aug 20, the POC was discussed in detail at the Aug 24 meeting at Menara CyberAxis. DAF's post-meeting technical summary established a phased approach:

**Phase 0 (Build Phased):**
1. TPRM Deployment — vendor onboarding, tiering, assessment, remediation, enterprise-risk integration
2. Shared Vendor Validation — validate common-vendor model against actual participant vendor inventories
3. Federated Compliance Document Check — maintain participant environments while aggregating compliance evidence at Bursa level

**Validation Phase:**
1. URD/SRS
2. Architecture & Capacity Validation
3. Bursa Acceptance
4. Scale Decision

## Strategic Significance

- **First POC from CSM channel** — validates CSM × Aras commercial pipeline
- **Bursa Malaysia = national stock exchange** — Tier-1 financial sector CNII
- **Act 854 compliance angle** — sector-level oversight, federated GRC view
- **TPRM-first approach** — grounds POC in proven VoronCitadel capability
- **24-entity federation preserved as hypothesis** — not overcommitting to unvalidated architecture
- **4-month timeline** — aggressive but bounded by phased scope
