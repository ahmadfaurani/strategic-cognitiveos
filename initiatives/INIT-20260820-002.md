# Summary

RADAR (Data Research Acquisition & Development) is established as an agency within the CognitiveOS architecture. It serves as the intake and registry authority for OSINT/news coverage intelligence events.

## Mandate

- **Data Research:** Systematic acquisition of open-source intelligence from news, media, and public records
- **Acquisition:** Intake SOP execution for all incoming news/media intelligence
- **Development:** Development of intelligence products from acquired data (INT records, assessments, briefs)
- **Cronjob Governance (DEC-20260820-005):** Oversight of all cronjobs — collection, monitoring, maintenance. All 5 active cronjobs now operate under RADAR authority.
- **RMR Enforcement (DEC-20260820-005):** Mandatory RMR marking on all intakes. Standard taxonomy template compliance enforced.

## Radar Mark Registry (RMR)

The RMR is the registry namespace under RADAR. All intelligence intake events from news/media sources receive a Radar Mark — a permanent registry entry linking the source document, intelligence assessment, stakeholders, organizations, and risks produced from the intake.

### RMR Entry Format

Each RMR entry links:
- Source document (DOC-)
- Intelligence assessment(s) (INT-)
- Stakeholders identified (STK-)
- Organizations identified (ORG-)
- Risks identified (RSK-)
