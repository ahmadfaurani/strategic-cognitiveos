# Summary

Threat Intelligence integration between Aras Integrasi's GovSEC platform and CyberSecurity Malaysia's threat intelligence capabilities. Hadri (Aras Solutions Architect) invited Mohamad Hafiz Bin Rahman (CSM) to a TI integration planning session with three proposed dates (Aug 6, 7, 10, 2026 at 11AM). Six objectives cover TI data sources/feed integration, threat taxonomy/classification/sharing protocols, incident response workflows, automated threat correlation, security/compliance/governance, and integration timeline milestones.

# Strategic Significance

The TI integration is the intelligence layer of the GovSec platform — distinct from but complementary to the CMERP platform integration (INIT-20260804-002). While CMERP integration establishes platform-to-platform interoperability, the TI integration delivers the core value proposition of GovSec TIP: real-time threat detection, analysis, and response capabilities for government security initiatives.

This is the third GovSec-related CSM engagement initiated on August 4, 2026, alongside VoronCitadel GTM activation (INIT-20260804-001) and CMERP integration continuation (INIT-20260804-002). Together, these three initiatives represent a comprehensive CSM-Aras partnership across commercial (VoronCitadel), platform (CMERP), and intelligence (TI) dimensions — all feeding into the GovSec Sovereign Threat Intelligence product being co-developed by ARAS and CyberSecurity Malaysia.

The accelerated timeline (earliest proposed session Aug 6 — only 2 days from the email) suggests TI integration may be the most urgent track, possibly because threat intelligence feeds are foundational to GovSec TIP's core functionality.
