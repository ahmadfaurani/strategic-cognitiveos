# Summary

Aras Integrasi has invited CyberSecurity Malaysia to participate in the **AI Systems Co-Design Lab** (Cohort 01) — a governed environment providing selected partners with GPU compute access to experiment, prototype, and build applied AI solutions. The invitation, sent by Hadri to four CSM team members, offers GPU compute tokens, notebook environments, model endpoints, RAG pipelines, and API tooling. Intake is intentionally limited; CSM is asked to provide a brief description of their intended use case, problem, and expected output before selection.

# Strategic Significance

This is the **fourth CSM-Aras workstream** initiated on August 4, 2026, alongside VoronDRQ GTM (INIT-20260804-001), CMERP integration (INIT-20260804-002), and TI integration (INIT-20260804-003). However, it is structurally distinct from the other three:

1. **Aras as capability provider** — Unlike the integration workstreams where Aras and CSM jointly build, the Co-Design Lab positions Aras as an infrastructure/capability host. CSM is the consumer/partner accessing Aras-provided compute resources.

2. **Cross-workstream accelerator** — The Co-Design Lab could accelerate the existing three tracks. CSM could use the compute to prototype AI features for CMERP, build threat intelligence ML models for the TI integration, or develop AI-assisted components of the VoronDRQ platform.

3. **Commercial gateway** — Once CSM is on the platform, ongoing compute usage creates a recurring revenue/engagement model. The "governed environment" framing suggests metered or allocated access, not a one-time grant.

4. **CyberDSA positioning** — A live Co-Design Lab engagement with CSM could serve as a tangible demonstration of Aras's sovereign AI platform capability at the CyberDSA October 2026 joint product launch (see DEC-20260804-001).

The intentionally limited intake creates exclusivity. CSM is being positioned as a "selected partner" — this strengthens the institutional relationship and signals seriousness of the Aras-CSM partnership to external observers.
