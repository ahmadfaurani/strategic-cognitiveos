# Summary

Continuation of technical integration work between Aras Integrasi's GovSec platform and CyberSecurity Malaysia's CMERP system. Both CMERP and LebahNet (CSM sovereign national honeypots) are data integration sources for GovSec Sovereign Threat Intelligence — the product being co-developed by ARAS and CyberSecurity Malaysia. Hadri (Aras Solutions Architect) invited the CSM integration team — led by Fathi Kamil Bin Mohad Zainuddin — to a two-session integration continuation on Aug 12–13, 2026. Objectives: review prior milestones, resolve outstanding technical items, align testing protocols and validation requirements.

# Strategic Significance

GovSec × CMERP integration is the technical backbone that enables GovSec TIP to interoperate with CSM's operational environment. Without this integration, GovSec remains a standalone product rather than an embedded platform within the national cybersecurity authority's workflow. Successful integration is a prerequisite for advancing GovSec TIP from pilot-ready to delivery-ready (Workstream B gate, INIT-20260725-002).

This is distinct from the VoronCitadel GTM activation (INIT-20260804-001) — that initiative is commercial pipeline; this is technical platform integration.

# Products

- GovSec TIP — Sovereign Threat Intelligence Platform (Aras Integrasi, co-developed with CSM)
- CMERP — CyberSecurity Malaysia's full suite of advanced malware threat detection, containment & removal tool (https://www.cmerp.my/). Components: CMERP Incharge (malware info collection/detection/analysis), Insight (malware activity detection), Indefense (malicious traffic redirection), Isolate (quarantine), Infix (malware removal), ADF (recursive DNS based on threat intelligence)
- LebahNet — CSM sovereign national honeypot network (https://dashboard.honeynet.org.my/about). Initiated 2002, overhauled 2007. Provides network trends and malicious activity data for MyCERT incident handling. Also hosts Malware Research and Coordination Facility for OIC-CERT/APCERT members. Data source for GovSec Sovereign Threat Intelligence.
