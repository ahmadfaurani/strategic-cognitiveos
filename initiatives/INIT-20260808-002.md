# Summary

Build the internal team and communications framework required to sustain the VoronDRQ GTM outreach programme. This initiative covers: (1) formal resource mobilisation for COO approval, (2) job descriptions for 4 priority roles, (3) an internal communications plan to sequence and frame the ask to Kenny Kok (COO) and Azzatullina Pawanchik (CMO/Marketing), and (4) alignment with existing Workstream F (Organisational Capability Building).

This is the enabling initiative — INIT-20260804-001 (GTM Activation) cannot scale beyond the pilot without the people, tools, and governance defined here.

# Strategic Significance

The GTM programme has a clear strategy (two-track approach), a complete sales kit, and a 193-organisation database. What it doesn't have is the team to execute at sustained scale. DAF is currently carrying programme ownership, discovery, CSM liaison, and strategic direction simultaneously. Fuad is product owner, demo lead, POC delivery lead, and claims validator — a single point of failure.

This initiative converts the resource requirements document into an actionable COO approval package: concrete job descriptions, a sequenced communications plan, and a clear ask. It moves the conversation from "here's what we need" to "here's who we need to hire, here's what they'll do, and here's what it costs."

# Objectives

1. Secure COO approval for 4 priority hires and programme budget
2. Secure CMO commitment for Marketing Operations resource and content development ownership
3. Establish internal communications cadence (weekly Marketing-Sales review, fortnightly pipeline review, monthly executive review)
4. Assign a Delivery Owner to offload DAF's operational coordination load
5. Eliminate Fuad as single point of failure through second demo lead and POC engineer
6. Confirm CRM platform and tooling budget
7. Assign Legal/Governance reviewer for claims governance

# Scope

**In Scope:** Job descriptions for 4 priority roles, internal communications plan, COO/CMO approval package, CRM/tooling confirmation, governance cadence establishment.

**Out of Scope:** CSM-side resourcing (CSM's own responsibility), product development/engineering roadmap, pricing strategy.
