# Summary

Athena's review identifies that the CSM–Aras relationship has progressed through four stages (Partnership → Product Collaboration → Technical Integration → Commercial Ecosystem) and is now approaching Stage 5 — Joint Operating Model. This is the missing piece. The proposal is to bring all CSM–Aras initiatives under one umbrella: the **CSM–Aras Strategic Cyber & Sovereign AI Programme**.

# Strategic Significance

The biggest risk is no longer lack of opportunity — it is portfolio fragmentation. Multiple legitimate workstreams are moving through different conversations, meetings, and owners. This creates three risks:

1. **Technical duplication** — Teams solve integration issues independently
2. **Commercial leakage** — Good technical collaboration does not automatically become revenue
3. **Stakeholder fatigue** — CSM receives multiple Aras initiatives without a single visible programme structure

The Joint Operating Model addresses all three by providing a single umbrella structure with clear governance.
