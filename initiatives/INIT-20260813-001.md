# Summary

The **PERJASA × Aras Government Sector AI Systems Co-Design Lab** is a strategic collaboration between Persatuan Juruanalisa Sistem Sektor Awam (PERJASA) and Aras Integrasi, coordinated through Jabatan Digital Negara (JDN), to build AI capability within the public sector through a structured co-design and prototyping programme. Unlike conventional technical training, this is a build-oriented platform where real government-sector problems are translated into valuable, evaluable AI use cases with clear execution pathways.

The programme centres on a **2-day strategic workshop** (proposed Sept 2–3, 2026) with 20–24 participants in four multidisciplinary groups (AI Infrastructure, AI Development, AI Cybersecurity, AI Productivity), each producing at least one prototype, workflow, or technical architecture. Beyond the workshop, the initiative builds a **PERJASA Government AI Cohort Alumni Network** — a sustained practitioner network of subject matter experts supporting government AI planning and implementation.

# Strategic Significance

This initiative is a **flagship sovereign AI workstream** under Workstream A (Sovereign AI & Government AI Adoption):

1. **Government AI adoption engine** — Translates national AI adoption intent into practical, demonstrable outcomes through real use cases, prototypes, and pilot pathways
2. **Institutional capability building** — Creates a self-sustaining expert network within PERJASA that grows cohort-over-cohort, reducing dependency on external providers
3. **JDN strategic alignment** — Positions JDN as the coordinating catalyst for government-wide AI adoption, strengthening the Aras–JDN institutional relationship
4. **GPU compute enablement** — Provides governed access to GPU compute infrastructure, creating a recurring engagement/usage model
5. **Alumni network as institutional asset** — The cohort alumni model creates reusable technical assets (reference architectures, implementation playbooks, control frameworks, validation methodologies) and a mentor pipeline for future cohorts
6. **Distinct from CSM track** — While INIT-20260804-004 (CSM Co-Design Lab) targets cybersecurity-specific AI use cases with MyCERT, this PERJASA/JDN track targets broader government-sector AI adoption across multiple domains

# Mission Alignment

- **Workstream A — Sovereign AI & Government AI Adoption** (primary): Directly advances the sovereign AI mission by building government AI capability and demonstrating governed GPU compute access
- **Workstream C — Government & Institutional Partnerships**: Deepens JDN engagement through a new capability dimension; PERJASA becomes a formal strategic partner

# Workshop Structure

## Day 1 — Strategic Alignment, Discovery and Design
- Strategic alignment and direction-setting
- Problem validation and AI suitability assessment
- Use case selection and prioritisation
- Solution design and technical architecture
- Data requirements, security controls, and AI governance

## Day 2 — Prototyping, Validation and Pilot Selection
- Prototype development
- Technical review and security assessment
- Responsible AI evaluation
- Joint user validation
- Pilot charter preparation
- Initiative selection for follow-up action

## Participant Groups (4)
1. **AI Infrastructure** — GPU compute, model endpoints, platform architecture
2. **AI Development** — Use case building, model fine-tuning, RAG pipelines, workflow automation
3. **AI Cybersecurity** — Security controls, governance, assurance, responsible AI
4. **AI Productivity** — Applied AI for government operational efficiency

## Decision Gates (3)
1. Problem validation and AI suitability confirmation
2. Design approval before prototyping
3. Final evaluation: pilot / refine / maintain as research / defer

## Post-Workshop: 90-Day Action Plan
- Workshop report preparation
- Pilot charter finalisation within 30 days
- Controlled pilot execution within 60–90 days

# PERJASA Government AI Cohort Alumni Network

The long-term strategic value of the programme. Alumni are not just programme graduates but are developed as:
- Subject matter experts in AI infrastructure, development, cybersecurity, productivity, data governance, and responsible AI
- Reference panel for government AI strategic planning and initiative implementation

- Mentors and facilitators for subsequent cohorts
- Working groups organised by expertise or government AI development priorities
- Producers of reusable technical assets (reference architectures, implementation playbooks, control frameworks, validation methodologies)
- A continuity bridge between individual capacity building and government AI agenda planning/execution
