# Summary

GovSec TIP enters a development freeze and pivots to CyberDSA Product Launch Readiness. The platform (v3.0, August 2026) has reached feature maturity with Threat Visualization, Executive Dashboard, AI Analyst (RAG), Threat Hunting Workbench, Malaysian Compliance, and the Core Platform MVP. The initiative covers: stabilising and hardening the current build, closing critical technical and security gaps, validating end-to-end workflows and integrations, completing development handover to Hadri, and preparing the platform and demonstration flow for CyberDSA October 2026.

# Strategic Significance

This is the operationalisation of the CyberDSA October 2026 milestone (DEC-20260804-001). GovSec TIP is the flagship product for the joint CSM-Aras launch. The development freeze (DEC-20260810-002) marks the transition from feature development to launch preparation — a critical inflection point.

The initiative consolidates five parallel workstreams under a single launch readiness framework, ensuring coordinated execution across stabilisation, security hardening, workflow validation, handover, and demo preparation. It also constrains scope: only features critical for launch may be added during the freeze period.

The CSM collaboration tracks (Telemetry Integration, Score Card Framework, CBOM Agent — DEC-20260810-001) must integrate with the frozen v3.0 platform, not a moving target. This initiative ensures the platform is stable enough for those integrations to proceed.

# Readiness Assessment

**Current:** Prototype — v3.0 has comprehensive features but has not been through stabilisation, security hardening, or end-to-end validation. The platform has been in active feature development (v1.0 → v3.0 in ~5 months) without a dedicated hardening cycle.

**Target:** Demo-ready — stable, hardened, validated, with a defined and rehearsed demonstration flow suitable for CyberDSA October 2026.

**Advancement gate:** prototype → demo-ready requires:
- No critical bugs in demo flow paths
- Security gaps triaged and critical issues closed
- End-to-end workflows validated
- Hadri handover documentation complete
- Demo flow defined and rehearsed
