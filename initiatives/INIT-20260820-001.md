# Summary

Aras Integrasi's participation at the 6th International Cyber Resilience Conference (CRC 2026), 1-3 September 2026 at Bangi Resort Hotel, Selangor. RM5K sponsorship confirmed (reduced from RM10K by organiser) and sent to finance for processing. DAF has directed exploration of a customised participation package that goes beyond branding sponsorship to include exhibition/product demonstration, technical speaking/panel opportunity, pre/post-event promotional visibility, and targeted stakeholder engagement with government, industry, and academic participants.

- **Multi-sector access:** Royal Malaysia Police, Ministry of Home Affairs, Bank Rakyat, NTU Singapore, University of Plymouth, UniSZA, IEEE Malaysia
- **Product showcase:** Full sovereign tech portfolio (GovSec TIP, VoronCitadel, VoronScout, chain:SENTRY)
- **Academic alignment:** Supports ongoing UPM Purple Teaming proposal track and ISC2 chapter engagement
- **Positioning:** "Technology/cyber-resilience partner" framing — not just sponsor
- **Timing risk:** Conference is T-12 days; customised package negotiation window is narrow
- **Resource contention:** Concurrent with CyberDSA 2026 (T-49) preparation
