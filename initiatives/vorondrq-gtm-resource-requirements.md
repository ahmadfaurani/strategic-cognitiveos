**Prepared for:** Working Session — Marketing, Sales, Cyber Security Practice

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the people, technical, content, and financial resources required to sustain the VoronDRQ market outreach programme from initial campaign through targeted demonstrations and POC delivery. It is intended to inform resource allocation decisions by the COO and CMO before programme activation.

The programme is structured as a sustained cycle: **Outreach → Discovery → Demonstration → POC → Commercial Conversion → Next Wave.** Each phase has distinct resource demands. Under-resourcing any phase creates a bottleneck that compounds downstream — a gap in demo capability blocks POC pipeline; a gap in POC engineering blocks commercial conversion.

## 8. Critical Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Fuad as single point of failure** | Product owner, demo lead, POC delivery lead, claims validator — if 2–3 POCs run concurrently, no capacity | (1) Hadri trained on demo delivery for at least 2 demo paths, (2) Recruit or assign a POC engineer to handle provisioning and basic delivery, (3) Limit concurrent POCs to 2 until second technical resource confirmed |
| **CSM training unconfirmed** | No joint account owners assigned — programme cannot fully activate | (1) Confirm Aug 12–14 training dates immediately, (2) Prepare contingency for Aras-only activation on first wave |
| **CRM not configured** | Cannot track accounts, stakeholders, activities, or pipeline — campaign runs blind | (1) Confirm CRM platform within 1 week, (2) Allocate Marketing Operations resource for configuration sprint |
| **Content assets don't exist** | Week 1, 2, and 3 offers reference executive brief, diagnostic, and roundtable — none built | (1) Parallelise content development — brief + diagnostic + account thesis template are Week 1 critical path, (2) Fuad starts diagnostic framework while Marketing develops brief |
| **Contact data only 65.4% actionable** | Outreach to unverified contacts risks credibility and deliverability | (1) 1-week verification sprint before launch, (2) Prioritise Lighthouse accounts first (higher relationship confidence) |
| **DAF concentration risk** | DAF carrying programme ownership, discovery, CSM liaison, and strategic direction | (1) Assign Delivery Owner to offload operational coordination, (2) Second discovery lead to share engagement load |
| **Claims governance gap** | No Legal review assigned — risk of unverified claims in external materials | (1) Assign Legal/Governance reviewer before any external distribution, (2) Claims review built into campaign checklist |
