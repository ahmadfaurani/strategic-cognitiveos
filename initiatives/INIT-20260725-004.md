# Summary

Intelligence as a structured operational capability, not merely a reporting function. This workstream covers political and strategic intelligence collection, analysis, and operationalisation for Malaysian state and national elections.

# Strategic Significance

Political intelligence is a core differentiator for DAF's strategic positioning. The ability to collect, analyse, and operationalise political signals — from election monitoring to PIR frameworks — creates unique value at the intersection of cybersecurity, intelligence, and strategic advisory. This workstream builds that capability systematically.

# Mission Alignment

- Political intelligence collection and analysis
- Intelligence operations framework
- Election monitoring capability

# Objectives

- Maintain and expand Johor PRN 2026 monitoring (6 constituencies active)
- Develop Negeri Sembilan state election readiness
- Maintain 10 PIRs with daily collection pipeline
- Refine War Room Operating Model
- Develop R.I.S.I.K Cognitive Campaign Framework
- Build sentiment/narrative/influence analysis capability
- Establish journalist & stakeholder registries

# Scope

**In Scope:** Johor PRN 2026 monitoring, Negeri Sembilan state election readiness, PIR Framework (10 PIRs), War Room Operating Model, R.I.S.I.K Cognitive Campaign Framework, voter/household/geospatial data, sentiment/narrative/influence analysis, journalist & stakeholder registries.

**Out of Scope:** Cybersecurity productisation (Workstream B), government partnerships (Workstream C), commercial pipeline (Workstream E).
