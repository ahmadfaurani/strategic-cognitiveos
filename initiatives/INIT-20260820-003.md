Activation of VoronCitadel into active POC mode. Current state: 3 paying customers (RM 414K ARR), single-tenant Docker Compose, 3-person team with no Head of Engineering. Target: 6-7 POC customers + 3 paying in immediate horizon, scaling to 8 paying customers (RM 1.104M ARR) in 18 months.

Infrastructure layer is provided by Teras AI Platform (DEC-20260820-007), eliminating the need for multi-tenant RLS build as a POC prerequisite. Teras handles deployment isolation, GPU compute, governance, and monitoring — VoronCitadel deploys as an application on Teras.
