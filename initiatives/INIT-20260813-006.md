## Purpose

Replace the one-to-one relationship model with a multi-layer stakeholder coverage model for priority CSM stakeholders during the CyberDSA product-launch period. Each priority CSM stakeholder should have:
1. Primary relationship owner (accountable for continuity, relationship context, action closure)
2. Secondary/operational cover (ensures continuity when primary unavailable)
3. Technical/commercial specialist (joins for specialised conversations)
4. Executive escalation (organisational authority, resource commitment, issue resolution)

**Target state:** ~3 active Aras relationship paths per CSM stakeholder (relationship/programme, specialist, executive/commercial) rather than 1 dependency path.

## Objectives

1. Increase continuity and resilience of CSM relationship during CyberDSA product-launch period
2. Ensure CSM stakeholders can reach the correct Aras person quickly
3. **Prevent DAF and Hadri from becoming single points of failure** for every CSM interaction
4. Increase direct visibility of Fuad, Farul, Azza, Kenny, Shuhada and commercial/event stakeholders
5. Create clear ownership for pre-event preparation, launch-day engagement, post-event opportunity conversion
6. Demonstrate organisational depth and commitment to CSM through structured coverage
