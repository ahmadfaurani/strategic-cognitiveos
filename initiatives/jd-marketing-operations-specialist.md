# Job Description — Marketing Operations Specialist

**Role Title:** Marketing Operations Specialist — VoronDRQ GTM
**Department:** Marketing (WIG/MTAI group)
**Reports To:** Azzatullina Pawanchik (Marketing) with matrix reporting to Faurani Jaafar (Practice Director)
**Location:** Level 30, Menara TM, KL (hybrid)
**Employment Type:** Full-time
**Initiative:** INIT-20260808-002 / INIT-20260804-001

---

## Purpose

Build and operate the marketing infrastructure that powers the VoronDRQ GTM programme — CRM configuration, email automation, campaign tracking, attribution, and reporting. This role converts the campaign strategy designed by Azzatullina and the Practice into a measurable, instrumented machine. Without this role, the campaign runs blind: no tracking, no attribution, no pipeline visibility.

## Key Responsibilities

### CRM Platform Configuration & Management
- Configure the CRM platform for campaign tracking: account records, stakeholder contacts, activity logging, pipeline stages, custom fields for the 7-stage VoronDRQ pipeline
- Build the campaign dashboard — real-time view of accounts activated, stakeholders engaged, sessions booked, demos delivered, POCs in flight
- Maintain data hygiene — deduplication, parent-group mapping (subsidiaries under buying centres), contact verification status tracking
- Manage user access and permissions for Sales, Marketing, and Practice team members

### Email Automation & Sequence Management
- Build and manage email sequences in the automation platform — Week 1, Week 2, Week 3 cadence with personalisation tokens
- Configure stop-on-response rules: automated follow-ups stop immediately after a substantive reply, account transitions to human-led engagement
- Track email performance: open rates, reply rates, meeting bookings, objection themes
- A/B test subject lines, opening sentences, and calls to action — report results to Marketing and Practice

### Campaign Tracking & Attribution
- Track every account interaction against the parent buying centre — not individual subsidiaries
- Maintain the attribution model: which channel (email, LinkedIn, executive engagement, roundtable) generated each engagement
- Track stakeholder movement through the buying committee sequence: GRC/Compliance → CISO → CRO → CIO → Audit → CFO
- Flag accounts where stakeholders are not moving through the sequence — surface to Delivery Owner and Account Owners

### Reporting
- Weekly: campaign performance report for the Marketing-Sales review — accounts engaged, replies, objections, message themes requiring adjustment, meetings booked, meetings completed
- Fortnightly: pipeline attribution report — which activities generated which pipeline value
- Monthly: programme health dashboard for executive review — full funnel view from awareness to commercial conversion

### LinkedIn Sales Navigator Management
- Manage LinkedIn Sales Navigator account — maintain account lists, stakeholder identification, connection tracking
- Support Account Owners with LinkedIn search and targeting — identify the right stakeholders at each institution
- Track LinkedIn engagement metrics alongside email metrics

### Content Coordination
- Coordinate content development timelines between Marketing and Practice — executive brief, readiness diagnostic, roundtable deck, case studies
- Ensure all content passes claims review with Legal/Governance before external distribution
- Maintain the content calendar — what's needed, when it's needed, who's responsible, approval status

## Requirements

### Essential
- 2–4 years marketing operations experience in B2B technology, cybersecurity, or regulated industry
- Hands-on CRM experience — HubSpot, Salesforce, or equivalent (configuration, not just usage)
- Email automation platform experience — sequence building, personalisation, tracking, stop rules
- Data discipline — understands that campaign intelligence is only as good as data quality
- Reporting capability — can build dashboards and produce reports that executives can act on
- Understanding of B2B sales funnels and pipeline stages — knows the difference between a lead, a qualified opportunity, and a POC
- Comfortable working across Marketing, Sales, and Technical teams as the connective tissue

### Desirable
- Experience with LinkedIn Sales Navigator (admin level)
- Experience with account-based marketing (ABM) tools and methodologies
- Experience in financial services marketing
- Marketing automation certification (HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot, or equivalent)
- Basic understanding of Malaysian regulatory frameworks (enough to track stakeholder roles and content relevance)

## Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

- CRM platform configured and live before campaign launch (by Sep 1)
- Campaign dashboard operational — all 15 pilot accounts visible with real-time status
- Email sequences built and tested — Week 1, 2, 3 cadence with personalisation tokens and stop rules
- First weekly campaign performance report delivered by Week 1 of campaign

- Zero untracked accounts — every interaction logged in CRM within 24 hours
- Content calendar maintained — all 8 assets tracked with owner, deadline, and approval status
