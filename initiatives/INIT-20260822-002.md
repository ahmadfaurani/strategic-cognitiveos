# INIT-20260822-002 — VoronCitadel GTM Strategy Execution

## Summary

Formal GTM strategy document from WIG Marketing (Said Farid, Norshaza) for VoronCitadel category creation in Malaysian market. Three-layer approach: (1) Credibility & Outreach via CSM LOI + partner referrals, (2) Content/Media/PR via LinkedIn + video series + BFM Radio, (3) Events & Conversion via CYBERDSA + Executive Roundtable + workshops. Five-month execution window August-December 2026.

## Objectives

- Build recognition, credibility and curiosity for VoronCitadel as a GRC platform
- Category education: teach GRC as a concept before selling the product
- Convert interest into pipeline through structured events and briefings
- Target: 300 contacts → 40 meetings → 15 assessments → 3 pilots (deck targets)
- DAF's model: 193 orgs → 1,300 stakeholders → 260 engaged → 78 meetings → 23 POCs → 7 sales → RM 1.176M

## Scope

**In Scope:** GTM campaign design, budget allocation, channel selection, event activation, content production, partner programme, lead handoff process, conversion measurement.

**Out of Scope:** Product development (Hadri/Fuad track), CSM technical integration (separate), pricing strategy (retail RM 368k / early adopter RM 168k already defined).
