# Summary

A comprehensive programme to consolidate all three Cybersecurity Flagship Products (VoronCitadel, GovSec TIP, ChainSentry) into a centralised product repository and establish a proper product baseline across six documentation categories: MVP Specification, Product Roadmap, Product Backlog, Commercialisation Readiness, Sales & GTM Materials, and Product Governance. The programme operationalises DAF's directive to shift from continuous feature development to productisation, commercial readiness, and GTM execution. Development is frozen across all three products except for critical fixes, security remediation, demo/POC-affecting issues, and previously committed integration requirements.

# Strategic Significance

This initiative represents the most structured product management directive DAF has issued. It addresses the core gap in Workstream B: products exist technically but lack the documentation, governance, and commercial packaging needed for structured GTM execution. The initiative also introduces "VoronCitadel" as a product name — assessed as "Ready" for commercialisation and sales materials, suggesting it is the most commercially mature product in the portfolio.

The programme directly supports the transition from DAF's 6-stage decision-making pattern (recognise → expand → identify → document → align → mobilise) by providing the structured documentation that enables stakeholder alignment and commercial mobilisation. It also addresses the "Productisation 4/5" capability gap identified in DAF's self-assessment — strong proposition, dependent on delivery maturity.

# Mission Alignment

- Cybersecurity Productisation (Workstream B) — directly advances all products from prototype/concept to productised assets
- Commercial & Market Development (Workstream E) — commercial readiness and sales materials enable market engagement
- Organisational Capability Building (Workstream F) — product governance establishes ownership, accountability, and change management

# Objectives

1. **Establish centralised product repository** — single source of truth for all 3 flagship products
2. **Compile Product Roadmaps** — current baseline, near-term requirements, future capabilities, with Must/Should/Future prioritisation
3. **Compile Product Backlogs** — outstanding technical items, defects, integration requirements, enhancement requests
4. **Assess Commercialisation Readiness** — target customers, deployment model, licensing/packaging, POC requirements, production dependencies
5. **Compile Sales & GTM Materials** — one-pager, sales deck, demo flow, tech FAQ, sales kit aligned to actual MVP capabilities
6. **Establish Product Governance** — product owner, technical owner, version baseline, documentation owner, change/release management
7. **Separate current from future** — clear demarcation in all documentation between what exists today and what is planned
8. **Five first outputs (DAF directive):**
   - Central repository structure for all three products
   - Current documentation inventory
   - Missing-document / readiness gap list
   - Initial product roadmap for each product
   - Recommended priorities required before active GTM and customer POCs
9. **Review gate:** Once baseline established → DAF reviews all 3 products collectively → agree commercialisation priorities, GTM readiness, next development cycle
10. **Objective:** "stabilise what we have, document it properly, package it as a product, and take it to market"
