# Summary

Initiative to establish a common analytical framework for organisational cyber posture, threat exposure, intelligence confidence, operational readiness, and compliance/risk indicators. The Score Card can eventually connect GovSec + SiberSUITE + VoronCitadel, creating a unified cybersecurity posture view across the entire Aras–CSM product stack. Initially focused on CNII (Critical National Information Infrastructure) sector scoring, with expansion path to cover any organisation using the joint technology stack.

Joint Aras-CSM initiative to explore and develop an evidence-driven Cybersecurity Score Card Framework providing operational and management visibility into cybersecurity posture, risk, exposure, and remediation progress. Focus is on CNII (Critical National Information Infrastructure) sector scoring. The framework will consume telemetry and analytics from GovSec (which in turn consumes SiberSUITE telemetry) to produce actionable posture metrics for CNII sectors.

# Strategic Significance

CNII sector cybersecurity scoring addresses a documented national need — no comprehensive, evidence-driven scoring framework currently exists for Malaysian CNII sectors. This initiative creates a defensible product proposition that:

- Differentiates the joint CSM-Aras offering at CyberDSA October 2026
- Leverages GovSec's analytics layer to transform raw telemetry into decision-grade posture intelligence
- Positions Aras and CSM as joint providers of a national-grade CNII visibility tool
- Creates a recurring value proposition (score card requires continuous telemetry → ongoing engagement)

The Score Card sits at the end of the integration pipeline: SiberSUITE → Telemetry → GovSec Analytics → CBOM Agent → **Cybersecurity Score Card**. It is the user-facing deliverable that makes the entire pipeline valuable to CNII sector stakeholders and decision-makers.

# Mission Alignment

# Objectives

1. Jointly explore evidence-driven framework for CNII sector cybersecurity scoring
2. Define scoring dimensions (posture, risk, exposure, remediation progress)
3. Establish data sources and telemetry inputs required for scoring
4. Design operational and management reporting views
5. Align with existing GRC/governance frameworks (ISO 27001, NIST CSF, MyCyberSecurity)
6. Produce a demonstrable score card prototype for CyberDSA October 2026

# Scope

**In Scope:** CNII sector scoring framework design, scoring methodology, data input requirements, reporting/dashboard design, operational and management view definitions, alignment with GRC frameworks, prototype development for CyberDSA.

**Out of Scope:** Telemetry integration architecture (covered by INIT-20260804-003), CBOM Agent development (covered by INIT-20260810-002), commercial GTM for Score Card (deferred until framework is validated).
