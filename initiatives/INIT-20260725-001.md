# Summary

Government AI value cannot be created through infrastructure procurement alone. Compute, platforms, applications, people, security, and adoption must function as one coordinated system. This workstream covers all sovereign AI and government AI adoption initiatives.

# Strategic Significance

DAF's positioning: government AI requires a supply–platform–demand operating model. Infrastructure (GPU), platform (sovereign AI), applications (use cases), people (skills), security (AI assurance), and adoption (change management) are interdependent. Aras Integrasi serves as the integrator across these layers.

# Mission Alignment

- Sovereign AI infrastructure and platform
- Government AI adoption and change management
- Strategic AI ecosystem development

# Objectives

- Establish PERJASA AI Adoption Framework with JDN
- Advance JDN/JDM engagement toward pilot
- Launch Perdana Digital AI Cohort
- Define Sovereign AI Platform & GPU Enablement proposition
- Advance PMO Strategic Data Lake proposition
- Build AI Incubator & Co-design Programmes with universities

# Scope

**In Scope:** All sovereign AI and government AI adoption initiatives including PERJASA, JDN/JDM engagement, Perdana Digital, sovereign AI platform, PMO Data Lake, and AI incubator programmes.

**Out of Scope:** Cybersecurity productisation (Workstream B), commercial pipeline management (Workstream E), organisational build-out (Workstream F).
