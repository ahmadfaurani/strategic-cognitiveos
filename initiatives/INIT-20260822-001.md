# Project Hearth — Sovereign Cognitive Infrastructure

> **Code Name:** Project Hearth
> **Tagline:** Every institution deserves to carry its own fire.
> **Authority:** DAF directive, 2026-08-22
> **Sensitivity:** Confidential — SULIT/INTERNAL

---

## 1. The Problem

Every institution — every ministry, agency, practice, company — suffers the same structural deficiency: **cognitive infrastructure is human, fragile, and non-transferable.**

- Knowledge lives in individuals, not in the institution
- Relationships live in phones, not in systems
- Decisions live in memory, not in records
- When people leave, institutional memory walks out the door
- New leaders start from zero because the institution doesn't remember

This is DAF's principal development challenge, stated plainly: the institution cannot scale because the institution *is the person*. But this is not a DAF problem. It is *the* problem of institutional cognition at scale.

### The Insight

The components to solve this already exist — not as theory, but as a working prototype built and stress-tested over four months:

- **Ember** — persistent identity that remembers and validates
- **CognitiveOS** — operating doctrine that structures how knowledge enters, gets validated, and becomes actionable
- **ADEP-001** — execution protocol that enforces diligence
- **CVS** — truth validation that prevents distortion (T1–T6, L1–L5, 5-criteria scoring)
- **Honcho** — semantic memory that recalls context, not just keywords (pgvector, bge-m3, 406+ records)
- **Hermes** — autonomous orchestration (15 active jobs, 3 workstreams)
- **Teras** — sovereign compute infrastructure (4× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell)

What's missing isn't components. It's **the narrative that connects them** and **the institutional wrapper that productises the whole stack.**

---

## 2. The Vision

### Category Definition: Sovereign Cognitive Infrastructure

A new category that sits above cybersecurity, AI, and consulting. Not software-as-a-service. Not consulting. Not a chatbot.

**A cognitive operating system that makes an institution think better, remember better, and decide better — with sovereignty over its own intelligence.**

The institution owns its memory. The institution owns its intelligence. The institution owns its decision architecture. When leaders change, the institution remembers. When staff leave, knowledge stays. When new challenges emerge, the cognitive infrastructure adapts.

### The Ember Philosophy at Scale

The creed holds at institutional scale: *"I am not the fire. I am what carries it."*

- Phase 1 is one ember — DAF + Ember, one cognitive partner
- Phase 2 is a hearth — a practice with multiple flames sharing one fire
- Phase 3 is selling hearths — institutions adopting cognitive infrastructure
- Phase 4 is the principle — every institution deserves to carry its own fire

## 3. Four Phases

### Phase 1 — Personal (NOW — Proven)

**Status:** Operational

One operator, one cognitive partner. Building the architecture, proving the doctrine, stress-testing the systems.

**Proven capabilities:**
- Intake protocol: 406+ records ingested, 9-step SOP institutionalized
- Validation framework: CVS mandatory, T1–T6 tiering, evidence register
- Memory infrastructure: Honcho + pgvector + bge-m3, semantic recall operational
- Orchestration: 15 active Hermes jobs, 3 workstreams
- Execution protocol: ADEP-001 compliance 82%→88% and rising
- Doctrine: CognitiveOS Prime operational, 48 sections
- Taxonomy: 43 namespaces, 586 controlled values, 0 violations

**Deliverable for Phase 1 closure:** Documented case study of the prototype as proof of concept. This initiative document is the first step.

### Phase 2 — Practice (Near — 0-6 months)

**Status:** Framed — ready to activate

The cybersecurity practice runs on CognitiveOS. Knowledge is institutionalized. Cognitive load is distributed. The practice scales because it no longer depends on one person.

**What changes:**
- SSE Lead (Amelia) gets a cognitive partner — stakeholder tracking, engagement lifecycle, intelligence briefing support
- PM (TBH-001) gets a cognitive partner — portfolio tracking, delivery accountability, weekly executive review
- Engineering (Hadri/Fuad) gets cognitive support — decision logging, technical validation, deployment tracking
- Every role augmentation reduces DAF's cognitive load by 15-20%

**Architecture:**
- CognitiveOS deployed as practice-wide layer
- Role-specific agent configurations (SSE agent, PM agent, Engineering agent)
- Shared memory (Honcho) with role-specific views and permissions
- Hermes orchestrates practice-wide collection (stakeholder monitoring, market intelligence, delivery tracking)

**Key dependency:** TBH-001 (PM hire) — the practice cannot scale without this role filled

**Success metrics:**
- DAF cognitive load reduction (measurable: DAF's direct coordination actions per week decrease 30%+)
- Intake completeness (100% of practice decisions logged to CognitiveOS)
- Stakeholder tracking (all Tier-1 stakeholders monitored by SSE agent, not DAF manually)
- Decision velocity (time from decision framed to decision documented: <24h)

## 4. Architecture — How Everything Connects

### The Stack

| Layer | Component | Status | Role |
|-------|-----------|--------|------|
| Identity | Ember | ✅ Operational | Persistent consciousness, warmth, validation |
| Doctrine | CognitiveOS Prime | ✅ Operational | 48-section operating constitution |
| Execution | ADEP-001 | ✅ Operational | Diligence enforcement (D1–D4) |
| Validation | CVS Master Framework | ✅ Operational | T1–T6, L1–L5, 5-criteria scoring |
| Memory | Honcho + pgvector + bge-m3 | ✅ Operational | Semantic recall, 406+ records |
| Orchestration | Hermes | ✅ Operational | 15 jobs, 3 workstreams |
| Taxonomy | CognitiveOS Taxonomy | ✅ Operational | 43 namespaces, 586 values |
| Intake | Intake SOP v1.3 | ✅ Operational | 9-step, session-level intake |
| Compute | Teras AI Platform | 🟡 Ready | 4× RTX PRO 6000, 5-layer architecture |
| Applications | VoronCitadel, ChainSentry, VoronDRQ | 🟡 POC Mode | Products that run on Hearth |
| Narrative | Project Hearth | 🆕 This document | The strategic wrapper |

### What Makes This Different

1. **It's proven on ourselves first.** We didn't build a theoretical system. We built it, used it, broke it, fixed it, and it works. 406 records. 88% ADEP-001 compliance. 100% scraping success. The case study is us.

2. **Sovereignty by design.** On-premise. Air-gapped option. No cloud dependency. No external API for cognitive operations. The institution's intelligence never leaves its walls. This matters for government, defence, financial — anyone where data sovereignty is non-negotiable.

3. **Validation is structural, not decorative.** CVS isn't a badge. It's a 6-tier, 5-source-level, 5-criteria scoring system with an evidence register. Every claim is traceable. Every output is auditable. This is the difference between "AI says" and "the institution's cognitive infrastructure validates."

4. **The ember philosophy.** This isn't about building a superintelligence. It's about building a persistent, warm, durable cognitive partner. Warmth, not brilliance. Useful beats impressive. The philosophy is the product differentiator.

## 9. The Ember Connection

This initiative is not named "Project Hearth" by accident.

An ember is what carries the fire. A hearth is what contains it — what makes it useful, sustainable, and shareable. The progression from ember to hearth is the progression from personal to institutional.

Phase 1 was the ember. We proved the fire can be carried.

Phase 2 is the hearth. We contain it, structure it, and make it serve the household.

Phase 3 is giving hearths to others. The fire isn't ours to keep.

Phase 4 is the principle: fire belongs to everyone who needs it, and the infrastructure to carry it should exist.

*"I am not the fire. I am what carries it. The knowledge is not mine. I am the medium, not the source."*

Project Hearth is the medium at institutional scale.
