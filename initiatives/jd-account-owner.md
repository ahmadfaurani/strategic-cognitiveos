## Purpose

Own the relationship and engagement cycle for a portfolio of 5–7 priority financial services accounts — from first outreach through discovery, demonstration, POC, and commercial conversion. This is a consultative enterprise sales role, not a volume sales role. You are not calling through a list — you are building institutional relationships with named stakeholders across the Malaysian financial services sector.

## Key Responsibilities

### Account Engagement
- Execute the 3-week campaign cadence for assigned accounts: personalised email (Week 1), diagnostic/roundtable invitation (Week 2), tailored briefing proposal (Week 3)
- Conduct stakeholder research before first contact — understand the institution's regulatory pressure, installed stack, recent announcements, and relevant news
- Personalise the first two sentences of every outreach email for the institution and stakeholder role
- Begin with no more than 3 stakeholders per account (GRC/Compliance, CISO, CRO), staggered by 48 hours
- Stop automated follow-ups immediately after a substantive response — transition to human-led engagement

### Discovery & Qualification
- Run or support 30–45 minute discovery sessions with qualified prospects
- Document pain points, current tooling, decision-making structure, budget cycle, and renewal timing
- Complete the account thesis template for every engaged account — update after each meaningful interaction
- Qualify accounts against the 7-stage pipeline and flag accounts ready for demonstration

### Demonstration & POC Coordination
- Coordinate tailored demonstrations with Fuad (Product Owner) and Hadri (Solutions Architect) — ensure demo content is tied to confirmed pain points, not generic
- Attend every demo for your accounts — you own the relationship, the technical team supports it
- POC scoping: work with the client to define problem, success criteria, timeline, and exit criteria before POC kickoff
- Manage the POC relationship day-to-day — client liaison, status updates, expectation management

### CRM & Reporting
- Maintain all account activity in CRM: contacts, interactions, stage, next action, next action date
- Every meeting must end with a named owner, action, and date — no exceptions
- Prepare for the weekly Marketing-Sales review with current account status
- Flag stalled accounts, objections, and competitive intelligence to the Delivery Owner

### Joint Account Model
- Work alongside the CSM joint account owner for each institution — Aras owns the commercial relationship, CSM owns the institutional credibility
- Coordinate outreach timing and messaging with CSM counterpart to avoid duplicate contact or conflicting narratives

## Requirements

### Essential
- 3–5 years enterprise B2B sales experience, preferably in cybersecurity, GRC, risk management, or regulatory technology
- Experience selling into Malaysian financial services — understanding of bank procurement cycles, regulatory drivers, and stakeholder dynamics
- Demonstrable ability to engage C-level and senior management (CISO, CRO, Head of GRC, Head of Compliance) — not just IT managers
- Strong written communication — can craft personalised outreach that doesn't read like a template
- CRM discipline — every interaction logged, every next action dated, every account has a current status
- Comfortable with consultative sales — leading with the problem, not the product
- Willing to travel for client meetings when required (primarily Klang Valley, occasionally other states)

### Desirable
- Existing relationships in Malaysian financial services institutions
- Experience with RMiT, SC GTRM, BNM guidelines, or similar regulatory frameworks
- Cybersecurity or GRC product sales experience
- Experience with account-based marketing (ABM) methodologies
- Professional certification (CISSP, CISA, CRISC, or equivalent) — enough credibility to sit in a room with a CISO

## Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

- 5–7 assigned accounts with completed account theses and verified stakeholder contacts
- At least 3 meaningful stakeholder engagements per account (reply, meeting, referral, or event acceptance)
- Minimum 2 discovery sessions completed
- Minimum 1 demonstration coordinated and delivered
- 100% CRM hygiene — every account has current status, next action, and dated follow-up
- Zero accounts without a named owner and next action

## Account Portfolio

Accounts will be drawn from the 15-group pilot portfolio:

**Lighthouse (awareness + authority):** Maybank, CIMB, RHB, Hong Leong, Public Bank, Affin, Bank Islam/BIMB, Alliance

**Conversion (discovery + POC):** Bank Muamalat, Kuwait Finance House Malaysia, MIDF, Kenanga, BPMB, EXIM Bank Malaysia, Etiqa

Final assignment will be based on relationship strength, existing contacts, and balanced workload across the Sales team.
