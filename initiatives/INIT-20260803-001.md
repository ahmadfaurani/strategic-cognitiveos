# Summary

Joint ELSA–Aras Integrasi strategic collaboration to propose an AI-assisted revenue recovery and tax compliance platform to LHDN (Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri). ELSA leads stakeholder engagement, commercial negotiation, and programme leadership. Aras Integrasi serves as technology and implementation partner covering AI architecture, data integration, risk scoring, security, governance, and auditability. The initiative is currently at exploratory/proposal-development stage with a draft NDA under legal review by MTAI. The approach is pilot-first with five progressive milestones.

# Strategic Significance

This initiative represents a convergence of three mission alignments:

1. **Government AI Adoption** — AI-assisted tax compliance is a direct sovereign AI use case for a major federal institution
2. **Commercial Growth** — LHDN is a high-value government client; successful pilot creates a repeatable commercial model for AI-assisted government services
3. **Productisation** — The AI risk-scoring and compliance intelligence platform, if built and validated, becomes a reusable product for other regulatory bodies (BNM, SSCM, customs)

The collaboration model leverages ELSA's LHDN relationship and commercial muscle while Aras owns the technology stack — a clean division that plays to each organisation's strengths. The pilot-first approach de-risks adoption for LHDN and provides a proof point before scaling.

This initiative upgrades LHDN from a general GRC prospect (STK-20260725-012) to a specific, structured engagement with a defined proposition, partner, and pathway.

# Scope

**In Scope:**
- NDA execution and legal vetting
- Joint value proposition and proposition development
- AI platform architecture and risk-scoring capability design
- Data integration with LHDN tax filings, compliance records, and approved third-party data
- Controlled pilot with LHDN officer review and approval authority
- Audit trail, governance controls, and performance dashboards
- Secure deployment aligned with LHDN data sovereignty requirements

**Out of Scope:**
- Nationwide implementation (gated behind pilot success)
- Direct enforcement authority (remains with LHDN)
- ELSA internal commercial terms with LHDN (ELSA-led, Aras supports)
- LHDN policy or statutory framework changes
