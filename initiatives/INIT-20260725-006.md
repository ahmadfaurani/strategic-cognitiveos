# Summary

Building the organisational infrastructure to support DAF's strategic reach. The defining transformation: from DAF being the primary driver of every initiative to becoming the architect of an institution capable of driving initiatives at scale.

# Strategic Significance

DAF's principal development challenge is transitioning from being the primary driver of every strategic initiative to becoming the architect of an institution capable of driving those initiatives at scale. This workstream addresses the organisational infrastructure — roles, KPIs, governance, knowledge management — needed to make that transition.

# Mission Alignment

- Organisational capability building
- Knowledge management institutionalisation
- Strategic bandwidth protection

# Objectives

- Build cybersecurity business unit structure
- Recruit for technical, intelligence, product, and consulting roles
- Define KPI framework and job responsibilities
- Establish delivery accountability and governance
- Fill operational support roles
- Implement reusable knowledge management (PI-OS foundation)
- Transition from individual dependency to institutional capability

# Scope

**In Scope:** Cybersecurity BU build-out, recruitment and role definition, KPI framework, delivery governance, operational support roles, knowledge management institutionalisation, organisational design and transition planning.

**Out of Scope:** Commercial pipeline (Workstream E), product definition (Workstream B), government relationships (Workstream C), political intelligence (Workstream D).

# Key Deliverables

- Cybersecurity BU build-out — active, defining roles and recruiting
- KPI Framework — active, FY2026 KPIs defined
- Knowledge Management — active, PI-OS is the foundation
- Delivery Governance — proposition, needs dedicated programme lead
- Second-line Technical Ownership — concept, critical dependency risk

| Role | Purpose | Status |
