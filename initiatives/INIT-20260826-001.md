# MCMC Sovereign Social Media AI Driven Capability Development

## Summary

DAF has directed Hadri to arrange a working-level session with the MCMC team to explore AI-driven capability development for sovereign social media monitoring. The engagement is structured as a fact-finding and capability assessment session covering five areas:

1. Existing capabilities currently available within MCMC
2. Current capability and operational gaps
3. Potential integration opportunities with existing systems and initiatives
4. New AI capabilities that may need to be developed
5. Future technical, data and operational requirements

The discussion is to remain aligned toward enabling the broader **Sovereign Monitoring vision**. The immediate objective is to get the working session confirmed and activate a structured fact-finding process that can subsequently be translated into capability-development requirements and potential workstreams.

DAF has proposed a warm-up session on Friday at 11 AM to discuss the overall agenda before the workshop. An agenda has been attached to the email for MCMC's review.

## Strategic Significance

This initiative creates a **direct MCMC capability-development track** — distinct from but complementary to the R.I.S.I.K initiative:

- **R.I.S.I.K (INIT-20260803-002):** UiTM-Aras collaboration to AI-enable the R.I.S.I.K analytical framework, with MCMC identified as **target funder** (RM5M proposal)
- **This initiative (INIT-20260826-001):** Direct engagement with MCMC as **client** for sovereign social media AI capability development — fact-finding first, then capability requirements and workstreams

Both converge on MCMC as a strategic institution. If successful, this track could:
- Create a direct commercial relationship with MCMC (beyond funding R.I.S.I.K)
- Position Aras as MCMC's AI capability development partner
- Provide a potential deployment target for Project Hearth (INIT-20260822-001) — sovereign cognitive infrastructure for a regulatory body
- Unify the R.I.S.I.K analytical framework with MCMC's operational requirements
