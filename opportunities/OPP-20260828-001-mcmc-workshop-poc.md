# MCMC AI Capability Development Workshop + POC Opportunity

## Opportunity

The MCMC AI Capability Development Workshop (formally proposed in DAF's Aug 28 email) creates a structured pathway to a POC opportunity for AI-driven social media intelligence capability enhancement at MCMC.

## Workshop as Conversion Mechanism

The workshop's 5 objectives are designed as a funnel:
1. **Establish baseline** → understand what MCMC has
2. **Identify gaps** → where capability falls short
3. **Review telemetry/architecture** → technical integration assessment
4. **Explore AI enhancements** → what new capabilities can address gaps
5. **Prioritise roadmap + POC** → convert findings into actionable engagement

Objective 5 explicitly calls for a "potential POC opportunity" — the workshop is designed to surface and scope a POC.

## Strategic Value

### Commercial
- Direct commercial relationship with MCMC as capability development partner
- Distinct from R.I.S.I.K (INIT-20260803-002) where MCMC is target funder — here MCMC is the client
- POC creates proof point for broader government AI adoption

### Strategic
- Sovereign AI mission — government regulator adopting sovereign AI capabilities
- Potential Project Hearth (INIT-20260822-001) deployment target — sovereign cognitive infrastructure for a regulatory body
- Potential Teras AI Platform (Farul's 5-layer architecture) as infrastructure layer
- Convergence point for R.I.S.I.K analytical framework + MCMC operational requirements
