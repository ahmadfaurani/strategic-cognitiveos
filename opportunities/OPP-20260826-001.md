# MCMC Sovereign Social Media AI Capability Development — Opportunity

## Opportunity Description

Direct engagement with MCMC to develop AI-driven sovereign social media monitoring capabilities. This creates a **second engagement vector** with MCMC:

1. **Existing track (R.I.S.I.K):** MCMC as **target funder** for UiTM-Aras analytical platform (RM5M, INIT-20260803-002)
2. **New track (this opportunity):** MCMC as **client** for AI capability development — fact-finding first, then workstreams

If successful, this track could:
- Establish Aras as MCMC's AI capability development partner
- Create a direct commercial relationship (beyond R.I.S.I.K funding)
- Provide a deployment target for Project Hearth (sovereign cognitive infrastructure for a regulatory body)
- Converge with R.I.S.I.K to create a unified MCMC engagement (doctrine + capability)

## Source

DAF's email to Hadri (CONV-20260826-001), following a recent engagement with MCMC.

## Potential Value

**High** — direct commercial relationship with a regulatory body. Multi-workstream capability development programme potential. Strategic positioning as MCMC's AI partner.

## Timeline

- **Immediate:** Session coordination (Hadri, this week)
- **Short-term:** Fact-finding session conducted (Q3 2026)
- **Medium-term:** Capability requirements translated into workstreams (Q4 2026)
- **Long-term:** Workstream execution and commercial engagement (Q4 2026–2027)

## Probability

**Medium** — depends on:
- MCMC responsiveness to session coordination
- Alignment of Aras capabilities with MCMC's operational gaps
- MCMC budget availability and internal prioritization
- Conversion from fact-finding to contracted workstreams
