# Opportunity

RSWG compliance mandate creates a 30-broker commercial pipeline for VoronCitadel.

## Source

Bursa Malaysia RSWG Recommendation Paper (DOC-20260827-001), received Aug 27, 2026 from DAF.

## Market Segmentation

| Group | Brokers | Profile | VoronCitadel Fit | Estimated Value |
|-------|---------|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Group 1** | 11 | Bank-backed, top 10, online trading + retail | Platform integration / co-existence with internal teams | RM500k-1M each (enterprise) |
| **Group 2** | 13 | Retail, online trading — limited internal security | Full compliance platform (sweet spot) | RM168k-368k each |
| **Group 3** | 6 | Foreign brokers, global parent security | Local compliance adaptation | RM100k-200k each (niche) |

## Why This Is Different From Normal GTM

[T3 ASSESSMENT] This is not a push-sell. This is a regulatory pull:
- Brokers **must** comply — non-compliance has regulatory consequences
- Deadline creates urgency — Dec 31, 2026 is hard
- Bursa itself defined the requirements — VoronCitadel aligns with the standard-setter
- CISO mandate creates a named buyer in each organization
