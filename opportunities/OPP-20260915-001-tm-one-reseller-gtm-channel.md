# Opportunity

Telekom Malaysia / TM One Cybersecurity as a **primary reseller and go-to-market channel** for the sovereign cybersecurity product stack co-developed with CSM.

# Source

- DAF management follow-through proposal, 14 Sep 2026 (agenda item 5) — CONV-20260915-001.
- Mirrored verbatim in Amelia Nadia's 24 Sep invitation to CSM leadership, 15 Sep 2026.

# Strategic Rationale

- **National-telco reach:** TM One serves enterprise and public-sector segments — a reseller channel complements direct engagement and the CSM co-development credibility chain (4 Sep signed engineering paper).
- **Ecosystem adjacency:** Aras Integrasi operates from Menara TM (Level 30 North Wing); WIG from Level 31 South Wing — physical and institutional proximity to the TM ecosystem.
- **Sovereign narrative fit:** "Locally developed sovereign cybersecurity technologies, deployed through national infrastructure champions" is a coherent positioning for TM One Cybersecurity as channel.
- **CyberDSA synergy:** A TM-track announcement or anchored interest before/at CyberDSA 2026 (Oct 5–7) strengthens the joint market-activation story.
