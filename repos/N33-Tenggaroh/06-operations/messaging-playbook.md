## Executive Summary

**Core Narrative:** "Tenggaroh Untuk Semua" (Tenggaroh For All)

**Strategic Positioning:**
- **BN:** "Stability, but failed delivery" — incumbent fatigue, service gaps
- **PN:** "Religious exclusivism, economic populism" — appeals to rural Malay, scares moderates
- **PH:** "Unity, moderation, cost-of-living focus" — multi-ethnic alternative, pragmatic solutions

**Key Audiences:**
1. **Chinese Voters (Tier 1 PDs):** Education, economic fairness, representation
2. **Moderate Malays (Tier 2 PDs):** Cost of living, governance, religious harmony
3. **Indian Voters (PEKAN MERSING KECHIL, PENGKALAN BATU):** Temple land, economic programs, education
4. **FELDA Youth (Tier 3 PDs):** Land inheritance, employment, palm oil prices

---

## Core Messaging Framework

### Master Message (All Audiences)

**Headline:** "Tenggaroh Untuk Semua"

**Body:**
> "N33 Tenggaroh adalah rumah kita semua — Melayu, Cina, India, asli Johor. Kita hadapi cabaran yang sama: kos sara hidup meningkat, peluang pekerjaan untuk anak muda, perkhidmatan awam yang lambat. PH bawa pendekatan bersatu: bukan janji kosong, tapi penyelesaian praktikal. Md Yusof Dawam (PKR) adalah calon kita — Melayu-Muslim, sederhana, prihatin. Bersama kita bina Tenggaroh untuk semua."

**Translation (English):**
> "N33 Tenggaroh is our shared home — Malay, Chinese, Indian, native Johor. We face the same challenges: rising cost of living, youth employment opportunities, slow public services. PH brings a unity approach: not empty promises, but practical solutions. Md Yusof Dawam (PKR) is our candidate — Malay-Muslim, moderate, caring. Together we build Tenggaroh for All."

**Key Themes:**
1. **Unity:** Multi-ethnic coalition (Malay candidate + Chinese base + Indian support)
2. **Cost of Living:** Inflation, fuel, housing, employment — affects all communities
3. **Service Delivery:** Local council responsiveness, clinic hours, infrastructure
4. **Hope:** Rebuild from 2022 collapse — PH is back, relevant, fighting

## Ethnic-Specific Messaging

### Chinese Voters (Tier 1 PDs: 154/33/10, 12, 01, 02)

**Primary Concerns:**
- Education (DongJiaoZhong, vernacular school funding, UEC recognition)
- Economic fairness (government procurement, business licenses)
- Representation (local council appointments, service delivery)
- Security (crime rates, neighborhood safety)

**Message Frame:** "Pendidikan, Keadilan, Perwakilan"

**Talking Points:**

| Issue | PH Position | BN Record | PN Position |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Vernacular Schools** | Fund equally, no discrimination | Underfunded, approval delays | Oppose vernacular system |
| **UEC Recognition** | Recognize (with conditions) | Reject (national unity concerns) | Strongly oppose |
| **Government Procurement** | Open, merit-based | Bumiputera quotas (exclusionary) | Islamic procurement (religious bias) |
| **Business Licenses** | Streamline, no discrimination | Bureaucratic delays, rent-seeking | Islamic compliance requirements |
| **Local Council** | Appoint based on merit | BN-appointed, patronage-based | Shariah-compliant appointments only |
| **Crime/Safety** | Community policing, better lighting | Inadequate response | Moral policing (vice raids) |

**Sample Script (Door-to-Door, Chinese):**
> "Selamat pagi/tengahari/petang. Saya [nama], sukarelawan PH untuk N33 Tenggaroh. Cik/cikni ingat tak 2022, PH dapat hanya 7% di sini? Tapi kita tahu ramai penyokong PH tak keluar mengundi. Tahun ni, kita ada peluang balik — tapi perlu cik/cikni bantu. PH janji: (1) Sekolah vernakular dapat funding sama rata, (2) UEC diakui (dengan syarat), (3) Lesen perniagaan lebih mudah, tak ada diskriminasi. Calon kita, Md Yusof Dawam, bukan Melayu ekstremis — dia sederhana, faham keperluan semua kaum. Boleh saya kira cik/cikni sebagai penyokong PH?"

**Objection Handling:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "PH dah bagi harapan kosong, tak buat apa-apa." | "Betul, PH gagal 2018–2020. Tapi kita belajar dari kesilapan. Kali ni, kita fokus pada isu praktikal: kos sara hidup, pendidikan, perkhidmatan. Bukan janji besar, tapi penyelesaian kecil yang berkesan." |
| "Undi PH buang undi, PH takkan menang." | "Betul, PH takkan menang di Tenggaroh. Tapi 14–15% undi PH penting — ia tunjuk BN tak boleh menang mudah, ia paksa BN dengar suara kita. Undi PH bukan buang undi, ia undi protes yang strategik." |
| "Saya lebih suka DAP, kenapa PH/PKR?" | "DAP adalah sebahagian dari PH. Di peringkat negeri, kita guna logo PH untuk tunjuk kita gabungan semua kaum — PKR, DAP, AMANAH. Undi PH = undi DAP + PKR + AMANAH." |

### Moderate Malay Voters (Tier 2 PDs: 154/33/09, 08, 05)

**Primary Concerns:**
- Cost of living (inflation, fuel, food, utilities)
- Employment (youth jobs, wage growth, TVET access)
- Governance (corruption, accountability, service delivery)
- Religious harmony (PH's stance on Islam, Malay identity)

**Message Frame:** "Kos Sara Hidup, Tadbir Urus Baik, Harmoni Agama"

**Talking Points:**

| Issue | PH Position | BN Record | PN Position |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Cost of Living** | Targeted subsidies (fuel, food), wage reform | Blanket subsidies (leakage, inefficiency) | Islamic welfare (zakat-based, limited scale) |
| **Youth Employment** | TVET expansion, startup grants, internships | Limited programs, patronage-based | Islamic entrepreneurship (halal industry focus) |
| **Corruption** | Institutional reform (MACC independence), transparency | 1MDB legacy, state-level scandals | Clean (PAS Kelantan record mixed) |
| **Service Delivery** | Performance metrics, citizen feedback | Bureaucratic, slow | Shariah-compliant services (limited scope) |
| **Islam/Malay Identity** | PH respects Islam, not anti-Malay | UMNO claims Malay protector status | Islamic state agenda (hardline) |

**Sample Script (Door-to-Door, Malay):**
> "Assalamualaikum. Saya [nama], sukarelawan PH untuk N33 Tenggaroh. Abang/kakak tahu tak kos sara hidup naik setiap tahun? Minyak, beras, elektrik — semua makin mahal. PH bawa penyelesaian: (1) Subsidi bersasar — yang betul-betul perlu dapat bantuan, (2) TVET untuk anak muda — kemahiran dapat kerja bagus, (3) Tadbir urus baik — kurang rasuah, lebih perkhidmatan. Calon kita, Md Yusof Dawam, adalah Melayu-Muslim — dia faham keperluan kita, bukan anti-Islam seperti yang BN/PH kata. Boleh saya kira abang/kakak sebagai penyokong PH?"

**Objection Handling:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "PH anti-Melayu, anti-Islam." | "Itu propaganda BN/PN. PH ada pemimpin Melayu-Muslim ramai — Anwar Ibrahim, Saifuddin Nasution, Nurul Izzah. Calon kita di Tenggaroh, Md Yusof Dawam, adalah Melayu-Muslim. PH bukan anti-Islam — kita anti-rasuah, anti-tindasan." |
| "BN lebih baik jaga Melayu." | "BN dah 60+ tahun jaga Melayu — kenapa masih ada Melayu miskin? FELDA kedua generasi masih tunggu tanah, rumah. PH bawa pendekatan baru: bantu semua yang perlu, tak kira kaum — tapi pastikan Melayu dapat hak adil." |
| "PAS lebih Islamik, saya nak undi PN." | "PAS memang Islamik. Tapi tanya diri: Kelantan under PAS — rakyat lebih sejahtera ke? Kos sara hidup turun ke? PH fokus pada Islam yang praktikal: kurang rasuah, lebih kebajikan, bukan sekadar simbolik." |

### Indian Voters (PEKAN MERSING KECHIL, PENGKALAN BATU)

**Primary Concerns:**
- Temple land security (eviction threats, legalization)
- Economic programs (MICOR alternatives, startup grants)
- Education (scholarships, TVET access)
- Representation (local council, state assembly appointments)

**Message Frame:** "Kuil, Ekonomi, Pendidikan"

**Talking Points:**

| Issue | PH Position | BN Record | PN Position |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Temple Land** | Legalize existing temples, no eviction | Selective enforcement, eviction threats | No clear policy (Islamic land laws) |
| **Economic Programs** | Startup grants, microcredit (no ethnic quotas) | MICOR (limited reach, patronage-based) | Halal industry focus (exclusionary) |
| **Scholarships** | Merit + needs-based (no ethnic quotas) | Bumiputera quotas (limited Indian access) | Islamic scholarships only |
| **TVET Access** | Expand slots, industry partnerships | Limited slots, Bumiputera priority | Islamic TVET (halal industry) |
| **Representation** | Appoint based on merit, community input | MIC appointments (party loyalty) | No Indian representation (Islamic state) |

**Sample Script (Door-to-Door, Indian/Tamil):**
> "Vanakkam. Naan [peyar], PH-oda tholarzilaiyalar N33 Tenggaroh-ku. Ungalukku theriyuma 2022-la PH-ku 7% thaan kidaichu? Aana intha thadava, namakku nalla vaayppu irukku. PH vaathiyadhu: (1) Kovil nilam — evict panna koodadhu, legalize pannanum, (2) Poruladaram — startup grant, microcredit, (3) Kalvi — scholarship, TVET access. Namma candidate Md Yusof Dawam — avar ellaruk-kum uriyavar. Ennoda kelvi: neenga PH-ku support panneengala?"

**Objection Handling:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "MIC jootha better, BN-oda thaan Indian-ku nalla nadakkum." | "MIC 60+ years aala irukku — innum Indian problem thirandhadha? Kovil nilam problem, poruladaram problem, kalvi problem. MIC umma use pannitu irukku. PH thaan real change." |
| "PH-ku Indian support kudutha, enna kidaichu?" | "2018-la PH-ku Indian support kuduthinga — 2018–2020 la enna nadandhuchu nu theriyum. Sheraton Move-ku apram PH weak aayiduchu. Aana intha thadava, PH serious ah fight pannum. Ungaloda support avasiam." |
| "Undu waste aagadhu, PH yethavathu jeikuma?" | "PH Tenggaroh-la jeikkadhu — aana 14–15% undu mukkiyam. BN-ku easy win kidaikadhu, BN-ku namma voice kettuthaan aaganum. Undu waste illa — strategic protest vote." |

## Rapid Response Protocol

### Issue Escalation

| Issue Type | Severity | Response Time | Responsible |
|------------|----------|---------------|-------------|
| False rumor/misinformation | HIGH | <2 hours | Social Media Manager + Campaign Manager |
| Candidate scandal (PH) | CRITICAL | Immediate | Campaign Manager + Legal Team |
| Opponent attack (BN/PN) | MEDIUM | <4 hours | Communications Team |
| Volunteer misconduct | MEDIUM | <24 hours | Volunteer Coordinator |
| Policy gaffe (PH leader) | HIGH | <4 hours | Campaign Manager + State War Room |

### Response Templates

**False Rumor/Misinformation:**
> "Berita palsu sedang tular tentang [topik]. Fakta sebenar: [correct information]. Jangan kongsikan berita palsu. Sumber rasmi: [link to PH statement]."

**Opponent Attack:**
> "BN/PN buat tuduhan [tuduhan]. Ini tidak benar. Rekod PH menunjukkan [counter-evidence]. Rakyat Tenggaroh layak dapat pemimpin jujur, bukan pembohong."

**Candidate Scandal:**
> "Kami sedar tentang tuduhan terhadap [candidate]. Siasatan dalaman sedang dijalankan. Kami akan beri kenyataan lanjut dalam 24 jam. Terima kasih atas kesabaran."
