## Executive Summary

**Rewards:**

### Community Building

**Volunteer-Only Events:**
- Weekly team dinner (rotating PDs)
- Post-rally celebration (light refreshments)
- End-of-campaign party (all volunteers + families)

**Communication:**
- WhatsApp group (daily updates, memes, encouragement)
- Volunteer newsletter (weekly highlights, upcoming events)
- One-on-one check-ins (Volunteer Coordinator → volunteers)

---

### Attrition Mitigation

**Common Attrition Reasons:**
- Burnout (overwork, insufficient rest)
- Lack of clarity (unclear role expectations)
- Disengagement (feeling unappreciated)
- Personal conflicts (schedule clashes, family issues)

**Mitigation Strategies:**
- **Burnout:** Enforce rest days (max 5 days/week for D2D volunteers), rotate shifts
- **Clarity:** Provide written role descriptions, regular check-ins
- **Engagement:** Recognition program, community events, meaningful work assignments
- **Flexibility:** Accommodate schedule changes, offer remote roles (phone bank, data entry)

**Attrition Target:** <10% during campaign period (10–15 volunteers max)

**Contingency:** Maintain 20% volunteer buffer (recruit 120–130 for 106 needed)
