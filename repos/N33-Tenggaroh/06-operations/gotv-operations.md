## Executive Summary

**GOTV Objective:** Maximize PH supporter turnout on Election Day. Target: 75–80% turnout in Tier 1 PDs (Chinese-concentration), 60–65% in Tier 2 PDs (mixed).

**Critical Insight:** PH's ceiling is determined by **Chinese turnout**. 2022 Chinese turnout (~40%) produced 7.13% PH share. 2026 target Chinese turnout (75–80%) produces 14–17% PH share.

**GOTV Model:** Three-phase operation — Early Voting (Days -7 to -1), Election Day Mobilisation (6:00 AM – 5:00 PM), Final Surge (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM).

## Contingency Planning

### Scenario 1: Low Turnout (<50% by 2:00 PM)

**Triggers:**
- Hourly turnout <8% of registered voters
- Weather disruption (heavy rain, flooding)
- Transport shortage (>50 pending requests)

**Responses:**
- Emergency social media blitz ("Your Vote Matters!")
- Deploy all reserve vehicles (priority: elderly, families)
- Extend door-to-door sweep (target: undecided + low-propensity supporters)
- Community leader mobilisation (temple committees, mosque imams, community associations)

### Scenario 2: High Turnout (>70% by 2:00 PM)

**Triggers:**
- Hourly turnout >12% of registered voters
- Long queues (>30 min wait time)
- Transport fleet at 80%+ capacity

**Responses:**
- Accelerate transport dispatch (reduce wait time)
- Food/water distribution at polling stations (queue support)
- Volunteer rotation (prevent burnout)
- Focus on "low-propensity" supporters (those who haven't voted by 4:00 PM)

### Scenario 3: Critical Incident (Intimidation, Violence, Legal Challenge)

**Triggers:**
- Voter intimidation reported
- Physical altercation at polling station
- Legal challenge filed (e.g., voter eligibility dispute)

**Responses:**
- Immediate escalation to GOTV Commander + Legal Team
- Document incident (photos, videos, witness statements)
- File official complaint with SPR/Police (if warranted)
- Social media blackout (avoid escalation via public channels)
- Volunteer safety priority (withdraw if threatened)

## Post-Election Operations (6:00 PM – 10:00 PM)

### Vote Count Monitoring

**Staffing:**
- 2 election agents per polling station (48 agents total)
- 1 data coordinator (centralized tally tracking)
- 1 social media manager (result dissemination)

**Process:**
1. Polling station closes at 6:00 PM
2. Vote counting begins immediately (6:15 PM – 9:00 PM)
3. Election agents report results via WhatsApp (photo of official tally sheet)
4. Data coordinator aggregates results (Google Sheets)
5. GOTV Commander verifies + announces (10:00 PM target)

### Volunteer Debrief

**Timeline:** 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM (post-count)

**Agenda:**
- Thank-you remarks (GOTV Commander)
- Preliminary result announcement (if available)
- Lessons learned (volunteer feedback)
- Next steps (community programs, 2030 positioning)
