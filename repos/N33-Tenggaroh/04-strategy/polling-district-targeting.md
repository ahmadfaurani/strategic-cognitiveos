**Classification:** INTERNAL — WAR ROOM USE ONLY

**Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Confidence:** [MEDIUM] — Based on demographic analysis + campaign best practices

## Executive Summary

**Operational Reality:** PH cannot compete evenly across all 24 PDs. Resource constraints (RM70,000 budget, 106 volunteers) require **surgical targeting** — maximize ROI per voter contact.

**Targeting Philosophy:**
- **Tier 1 (Must-Win):** 4 Chinese-concentration PDs — 40% budget, 50% volunteer time
- **Tier 2 (Persuasion):** 3 mixed PDs — 35% budget, 30% volunteer time
- **Tier 3 (Symbolic):** 14 FELDA + Island PDs — 15% budget, 10% volunteer time
- **Tier 4 (Skip):** 3 Malay-concentration rural PDs — 5% budget, 5% volunteer time (symbolic only)

**Vote Target:** 5,500–6,000 votes (14–15% share)
