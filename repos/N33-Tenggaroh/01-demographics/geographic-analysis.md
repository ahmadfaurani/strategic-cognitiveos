### Characteristics

- **Ethnic Homogeneity:** 98.98% Malay — one of the most ethnically concentrated FELDA belts in Johor
- **Youth Bulge:** 69.5% under-40 — second-generation FELDA settlers dominate
- **Economic Base:** Palm oil smallholders, FELDA employment, plantation work
- **Infrastructure:** FELDA scheme housing, centralized facilities (clinics, schools, community halls)

### Political Significance

**BN's Firewall:**
- Traditional UMNO stronghold via FELDA office networks
- 2022: BN won ~95%+ of FELDA vote (estimate based on statewide patterns)
- 2026: UMNO candidate (vs 2022 MIC) should retain baseline, but Raven Kumar's personal vote may not fully transfer

**PN's Opportunity:**
- PAS brand resonates with rural Malay youth
- Cost-of-living messaging (palm oil prices, fertilizer costs, land rights)
- Target: 35–40% FELDA vote if Malay consolidation occurs

**PH's Challenge:**
- Near-zero presence (2022: ~5–7% in FELDA)
- Target: 10–15% via second-generation youth messaging
- Channel: Social media (bypass FELDA office gatekeepers), land rights advocacy

### Key FELDA PDs

**FELDA TENGGAROH 2 (3,944 voters)** — Largest FELDA PD, symbolic importance. High youth concentration (67.5% under-40).

**FELDA TENGGAROH 5 (3,383 voters)** — Second-largest FELDA PD. 99.5% Malay.

**FELDA NITAR 2 (2,989 voters)** — Third-largest. Slightly more diverse (98.2% Malay, 0.2% Chinese, 0.2% Indian).

**PN's Secondary Opportunity:**
- Malay-majority non-FELDA PDs (SRI PANTAI, JAMALUANG TIMOR) — 6,427 voters

- **Extreme Isolation:** 45 min to 3 hours by boat from Mersing jetty
- **Small Populations:** 32 to 211 voters per PD
- **Ethnic Homogeneity:** 98.6% Malay — fishing communities
- **Economic Base:** Fisheries, small-scale agriculture, tourism (Pulau Sibu resort)

### Political Significance

**Logistical Challenge:**
- Election day access requires boat coordination
- Early voting assistance critical (elderly, disabled, fishermen with irregular schedules)
- Weather-dependent — rough seas can suppress turnout

**BN's Traditional Advantage:**
- Long-term incumbent presence (Raven Kumar visited islands regularly)
- Service-delivery promises (water tanks, solar power, boat subsidies)
- 2022: BN likely won 70%+ island vote (estimate)

**PH's Opportunity:**
- Neglect narrative — infrastructure promises (desalination, satellite internet, medical evacuation)
- If PH achieves 30% island vote → 207 votes (potentially decisive in tight race)

**Operational Priority:**
- Pre-arrange boat transport for election day
- Identify community leaders on each island (village heads, religious teachers, resort operators)
- Target: 60% turnout → 414 votes

## Turnout Logistics by Geography

### FELDA Belt

- **Access:** Paved roads, centralized locations — easy access
- **Turnout Propensity:** High (65–75% expected)
- **Challenge:** FELDA office gatekeepers control access — PH/PN need alternative channels

### Non-FELDA Urban/Semi-Urban

- **Access:** Mixed — urban PDs easy, semi-rural requires village-level navigation
- **Turnout Propensity:** Variable (40–70%) — Chinese PDs historically low turnout
- **Challenge:** Chinese apathy — requires intensive door-to-door mobilisation

### Island PDs

- **Access:** Boat-only — weather-dependent, logistically complex
- **Turnout Propensity:** Low to moderate (50–60%) — access barriers
- **Challenge:** Pre-arrange transport, identify community leaders, early voting assistance

## Strategic Implications

### For PH/PKR

**Priority Geography:**
1. **Chinese-Concentration PDs** (4 PDs, 3,605 voters) — Must-win, target 80% support
2. **SRI PANTAI** (5,869 voters) — Largest PD, moderate Malay persuasion
3. **PEKAN MERSING KECHIL** (4,419 voters) — Indian vote + mixed Malay

**Minimal Viable Strategy:**
- Contest Chinese PDs at full intensity (80% turnout target)
- SRI PANTAI + PEKAN MERSING KECHIL at moderate intensity (20–25% support target)
- FELDA + Islands at symbolic level (10% support target, limit losses)

**Expected Yield:** ~5,500–6,000 votes (14–15% share)

### For BN

**Priority Geography:**
1. **FELDA Belt** (19,305 voters) — Firewall, target 60%+ support
2. **SRI PANTAI** (5,869 voters) — Moderate Malay, target 50%+ support
3. **Island PDs** (690 voters) — Traditional advantage, target 70%+ support

**Defensive Strategy:**
- Hold FELDA baseline (UMNO candidate helps vs 2022 MIC)
- Defend SRI PANTAI from PN inroads
- Maintain island presence (service-delivery promises)

**Expected Yield:** ~11,500–12,500 votes (42–50% share, depending on turnout)

### For PN

**Priority Geography:**
1. **FELDA Belt** (19,305 voters) — Primary opportunity, target 35–40% support
2. **Malay-Concentration Non-FELDA** (SRI PANTAI, JAMALUANG TIMOR — 6,427 voters) — Secondary target

**Disruptor Strategy:**
- PAS ceramah in FELDA settlements (cost-of-living, religious messaging)
- Viral WhatsApp content targeting second-generation FELDA youth
- If PN exceeds 2022 base (9,172 → 11,000+), BN margin collapses

**Expected Yield:** ~10,000–11,500 votes (35–40% share, depending on Malay consolidation)
