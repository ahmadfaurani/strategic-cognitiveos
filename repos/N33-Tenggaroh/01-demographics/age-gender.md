## Youth Bulge Analysis

**Key Finding:** 67.04% of electorate is under 40. This is the single most important structural feature of N33 Tenggaroh.

### Youth Distribution by PD Type

| PD Type | Total Voters | Youth (18-29) | Youth % | Under 40 | Under 40 % |
|---------|--------------|---------------|---------|----------|------------|
| FELDA/RISDA | 19,305 | 4,209 | 21.8% | 13,423 | 69.5% |
| Non-FELDA | 19,696 | 5,989 | 30.4% | 12,725 | 64.6% |
| **Total** | **39,001** | **10,198** | **26.15%** | **26,148** | **67.04%** |

**Assessment:**
- FELDA has slightly lower youth percentage (21.8% vs 30.4%) but higher under-40 percentage (69.5% vs 64.6%)
- Non-FELDA has higher youth concentration — likely Chinese-concentration PDs skew younger
- **Strategic Implication:** Cost-of-living, employment, housing messaging resonates across both FELDA and non-FELDA youth

## Young Voters (18–20 years)

**Total:** 1,853 voters (4.75%)

**Characteristics:**
- Registered between 2022–2026 (automatic registration at 18)
- No voting history — high mobilisation uncertainty
- Digital-native — social media primary channel
- Issues: Education costs, entry-level employment, housing affordability

**Operational Priority:** Early voter education, polling station awareness, transport assistance.

---

## Youth Core (21–30 years)

**Total:** 7,710 voters (19.77%)

**Largest single age cohort** — larger than any other 10-year band.

**Characteristics:**
- Includes 2022 first-time voters (now experienced)
- Economic pressure: entry-level jobs, wage stagnation, cost of living
- FELDA second-generation: land inheritance, housing, palm oil sector volatility
- Non-FELDA: urban employment, education debt, career mobility

**Strategic Messaging:**
- **FELDA Youth:** Land rights, housing assistance, employment diversification
- **Non-FELDA Youth:** Job creation, wage growth, skills training, broadband access

---

## Working Adults (31–45 years)

**Total:** 15,950 voters (40.90%)

**Largest age band** — combined with youth (18–30), under-45 voters are 87.8% of electorate.

**Characteristics:**
- Family formation stage — education, healthcare, housing priorities
- Economic stability concerns — inflation, subsidies, job security
- FELDA: Smallholder economics, palm oil prices, fertilizer costs
- Non-FELDA: Business licenses, procurement fairness, service delivery

**Strategic Messaging:**
- Cost of living (inflation, fuel, essentials)
- Children's education (school quality, scholarships)
- Healthcare access (clinic hours, hospital wait times)

---

## Senior Voters (60+ years)

**Total:** 7,682 voters (19.70%)

**Characteristics:**
- Higher turnout propensity — reliable voters
- Issues: Healthcare, pension, elderly care, infrastructure accessibility
- FELDA: Retirement benefits, healthcare access, village facilities
- Non-FELDA: Urban services, public transport, medical facilities

**Strategic Messaging:**
- Healthcare improvements (clinic upgrades, specialist access)
- Pension/assistance programs (BR1M, state elderly aid)
- Infrastructure (road repairs, street lighting, wheelchair access)

## Strategic Implications

### For PH/PKR

**Youth Mobilisation (18–29):**
- Target: 65% turnout → 6,630 votes from youth bloc
- If PH achieves 40% youth support → 2,650 votes
- Channel: Social media (TikTok, Instagram), campus outreach, young candidate visibility

**Working Adults (31–45):**
- Target: 70% turnout → 11,165 votes
- If PH achieves 25% support → 2,790 votes
- Channel: WhatsApp, workplace outreach, economic messaging

**Combined Youth + Working Adult Target:** ~5,400 votes (14% of total electorate) — aligns with PH's 14–15% ceiling target.

### For BN

**Senior Voters (60+):**
- Traditional BN base — higher turnout propensity
- Target: 80% turnout → 6,145 votes
- If BN achieves 60% senior support → 3,690 votes
- Channel: Rumah terbuka, elderly visits, healthcare promises

**FELDA Youth (31–45):**
- Second-generation FELDA — cost-of-living messaging
- Target: Limit PN inroads, maintain 50%+ support
- Channel: FELDA office networks, land rights announcements

### For PN

**FELDA Youth (18–29):**
- PAS brand resonates with rural Malay youth
- Target: 35% support in FELDA youth → 1,470 votes
- Channel: Mosque networks, religious youth groups, social media (Arabic-English bilingual content)

**Malay-Concentration PDs (All Ages):**
- Target: 40%+ support in 15 Malay-majority PDs (20,553 voters)
- If achieved → 8,220 votes
- Channel: PAS ceramah, religious scholars, viral WhatsApp content
