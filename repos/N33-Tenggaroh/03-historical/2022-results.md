## Key Takeaways from 2022

### 1. Turnout Was Decisive

**Low Turnout (57.2%) Benefited BN:**
- Chinese turnout collapsed (~40% vs ~75–80% in 2018)
- PH's Chinese base didn't show up → PH collapsed from ~28% to 7.13%
- BN's machinery (FELDA offices, village heads) delivered reliable Malay turnout
- **Lesson:** 2026 turnout recovery (>70%) structurally favors PH/PN

### 2. Raven Kumar's Personal Vote Mattered

**MIC Candidate in 83% Malay Seat:**
- Structural mismatch should have killed BN's chances
- Raven Kumar's personal vote (long-time ADUN, EXCO) overcame ethnic deficit
- Estimated: 75–80% Malay support despite MIC label
- **Lesson:** 2026 UMNO candidate corrects mismatch, but Raven Kumar's personal vote may not transfer

### 3. PN's Malay Consolidation Was Real

**42.78% PN Vote Share:**
- Best PN/PAS performance in Tenggaroh history
- Driven by FELDA/rural Malay support (cost-of-living, religious messaging)
- **Lesson:** 2026 PN inherits this base — can grow to 50%+ if Malay consolidation occurs

### 4. PH's Collapse Was Catastrophic

**7.13% PH Vote Share:**
- Worst PH performance in Tenggaroh history
- Chinese apathy + Malay erosion = perfect storm
- **Lesson:** 2026 PH must rebuild from ashes — requires Chinese mobilisation + moderate Malay persuasion

### 5. Pejuang Was Irrelevant

**1.00% Pejuang Vote Share:**
- Lost deposit (<5% threshold)
- Mahathir's personal vote minimal in Johor
- **Lesson:** 2026 third parties (if any) will be similarly irrelevant
