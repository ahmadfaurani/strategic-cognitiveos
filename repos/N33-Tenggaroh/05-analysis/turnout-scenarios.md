# N33 Tenggaroh — Turnout Sensitivity Analysis

**Analysis Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Data Source:** SPR Electoral Roll (as of 19 June 2026), MEMORY.md#L280-L310  
**Confidence:** [MEDIUM] — Based on historical patterns + demographic modelling

## Executive Summary

**Key Finding:** N33 Tenggaroh is **highly turnout-sensitive**. 2018 high turnout (~84%) produced PH win by 98 votes. 2022 low turnout (57.2%) produced BN win by 1,356 votes. **Turnout differential flipped the seat.**

**2026 Scenarios:**
- **Low Turnout (55–60%):** BN wins comfortably (50% vote share, +1,600 margin)
- **Moderate Turnout (65–70%):** BN wins narrowly (46% vote share, +2,000 margin)
- **High Turnout (75–80%):** BN wins very narrowly (38–42% vote share, +300–2,000 margin)

**Decisive Variable:** Chinese turnout in 4 Chinese-concentration PDs (3,605 voters). If Chinese turnout recovers to 75–80% (from 2022's ~40%), PH gains 1,500–2,000 votes — enough to compress BN margin to <500 votes in three-way contest.

| **PH Vote Share** | ~47% (winner) | 7.13% | -40 pp |
| **PH Votes** | ~15,200 | 1,529 | -13,700 |
| **PN/PAS Vote Share** | ~5% | 42.78% | +38 pp |
| **PN/PAS Votes** | ~1,600 | 9,172 | +7,600 |

**Key Insight:** Absolute vote counts matter less than **relative turnout by ethnic bloc**:
- 2018: Chinese turnout ~75–80% → PH competitive
- 2022: Chinese turnout ~35–40% → PH collapsed
- **Lesson:** Chinese turnout is the swing variable

## Turnout Sensitivity Modelling

### Chinese Turnout Impact

**4 Chinese-Concentration PDs (3,605 voters):**

| Chinese Turnout | PH Votes from Chinese PDs | Total PH Votes (Est.) | PH Vote Share |
|-----------------|---------------------------|----------------------|---------------|
| 40% (2022 level) | 1,150 | 1,500 | 6% |
| 50% | 1,440 | 2,200 | 9% |
| 60% | 1,730 | 3,000 | 12% |
| 70% | 2,020 | 4,000 | 15% |
| 75% | 2,160 | 4,800 | 17% |
| 80% | 2,300 | 5,500 | 20% |

**Assumption:** PH achieves 80% support in Chinese-concentration PDs. Remainder of PH vote comes from mixed PDs (SRI PANTAI, PEKAN MERSING KECHIL, etc.).

**Key Insight:** Chinese turnout delta (40% → 80%) = +4,000 PH votes = **14 percentage point swing**. This is the single most important operational variable for PH.

### Malay Turnout Impact

**15 Malay-Concentration PDs (20,553 voters):**

| Malay Turnout | BN Votes (40% support) | PN Votes (37% support) | PH Votes (23% support) |
|---------------|------------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| 60% | 4,930 | 4,560 | 2,840 |
| 65% | 5,340 | 4,940 | 3,070 |
| 70% | 5,750 | 5,320 | 3,300 |
| 75% | 6,160 | 5,700 | 3,530 |
| 80% | 6,570 | 6,080 | 3,760 |

**Assumption:** Malay vote splits 40% BN, 37% PN, 23% PH (optimistic for PH, realistic for PN).

**Key Insight:** Malay turnout delta (60% → 80%) = +1,640 BN votes, +1,520 PN votes, +920 PH votes. **BN and PN benefit more from high Malay turnout than PH** (because BN/PN have higher Malay support bases).

### Combined Impact

**Net Effect of Turnout Changes:**

| Scenario | Chinese Turnout | Malay Turnout | BN Margin | Winner |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|-----------|--------|
| 2022 Replay | 40% | 60% | +1,600 | BN |
| Moderate Recovery | 60% | 65% | +1,200 | BN |
| High Turnout (Balanced) | 75% | 75% | +300 | BN (narrow) |
| High Turnout (Chinese Surge) | 80% | 70% | -500 | **PH/PN** (depends on split) |

**Critical Finding:** If Chinese turnout reaches 80% AND Malay turnout is 70% (not 75%+), BN margin turns **negative** (BN loses). This is PH's winning path — but requires PN cooperation (Malay vote split).

### Early Voters (TENTERA, POLIS, PGA)

- TENTERA & PASANGAN: 0 in Tenggaroh
- POLIS & PGA: 290 voters (154/33/07 PEJABAT KERAJAAN)
- PTH LUAR NEGARA: 0 in Tenggaroh

**2022 Early Vote (Estimated):**
- BN: ~60–65% (police/PGA lean BN)
- PN: ~25–30% (Malay police/PGA)
- PH: ~10–15% (Chinese/Indian police, minimal)

**2026 Projection:**
- BN: ~175 votes (60%)
- PN: ~87 votes (30%)
- PH: ~29 votes (10%)

**Impact:** Early voters are ~1% of total electorate — marginal but potentially decisive in tight race (<500-vote margin).

### Election Day Turnout Drivers

**Positive Drivers (Increase Turnout):**
- Competitive race (voters feel their vote matters)
- Good weather (no monsoon rains)
- Transport availability (free rides to polling stations)
- Community mobilisation (mosque announcements, temple committees, FELDA office drives)

**Negative Drivers (Suppress Turnout):**
- Uncompetitive race (voters feel outcome is predetermined)
- Bad weather (heavy rains, flooding)
- Transport barriers (no cars, remote PDs, island access issues)
- Apathy/demobilisation (especially Chinese voters)

## Operational Recommendations

### For PH/PKR

**Priority:** Maximize Chinese turnout in 4 Chinese-concentration PDs.

**Actions:**
- Early voter registration drive (18–20 age group)
- Postal voter assistance (overseas, absentees)
- Election day transport coordination (free rides)
- Social media countdown (daily reminders, polling station info)
- **Target:** 75–80% Chinese turnout → 2,300 votes from Chinese PDs

**Secondary:** Persuade moderate Malays in mixed PDs.

**Actions:**
- Townhalls in SRI PANTAI, PEKAN MERSING KECHIL
- Economic messaging (cost of living, not ideology)
- Candidate visibility (Malay-Muslim, moderate image)
- **Target:** 20–25% support in mixed PDs → 2,000–2,500 votes

**Realistic Ceiling:** 5,500–6,000 votes (14–15% share) at 70–75% overall turnout.

### For BN

**Priority:** Maintain FELDA turnout, defend mixed PDs.

**Actions:**
- FELDA office networks (get-out-the-vote drives)
- Village head mobilisation (rural PDs)
- Service-delivery promises (infrastructure, assistance)
- **Target:** 60–65% FELDA turnout, 50%+ support

**Secondary:** Limit PN inroads in Malay-concentration PDs.

**Actions:**
- UMNO candidate religious credibility (mosque visits, Haji title)
- Cost-of-living messaging (federal aid packages)
- **Target:** Hold PN to 35–40% Malay support

**Realistic Floor:** 11,500–12,500 votes (42–50% share) at 60–75% overall turnout.

### For PN

**Priority:** Maximize Malay turnout in FELDA/rural PDs.

**Actions:**
- PAS ceramah circuit (nightly in FELDA PDs)
- Mosque network mobilisation (imam announcements)
- Cost-of-living messaging (palm oil prices, fertilizer costs)
- **Target:** 65–70% Malay turnout in FELDA, 35–40% support

**Secondary:** Symbolic presence in mixed PDs.

**Actions:**
- Acknowledge Chinese/Indian voters (minimal resources)
- Focus on Malay voters in mixed PDs (SRI PANTAI, JAMALUANG TIMOR)
- **Target:** 20–25% support in mixed PDs

**Realistic Ceiling:** 10,000–11,500 votes (26–35% share) at 65–75% overall turnout.
