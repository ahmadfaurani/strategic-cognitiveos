## Product Positioning

### GovSec TIP — Sovereign Threat Intelligence Platform

**Positioning Statement:**
> *"Sovereign capability for national digital resilience — built in Malaysia, for Malaysia"*

**Core Value Proposition:**
GovSec TIP is a production-grade, nationally deployable cyber intelligence and cyber fusion platform that enables Malaysian government agencies to achieve operational visibility, threat intelligence, and AI-enabled decision support with full data sovereignty.

**Target Customer Profile:**
- **Primary:** Public-facing government agencies (JPN, IMD, LHDN, MAMPU)
- **Secondary:** Enforcement & intelligence agencies (PDRM, NACSA, MINDEF, MACC)
- **Tertiary:** Regulatory bodies & critical infrastructure (BNM, MCMC, TNB, MOH)

**Key Capabilities:**
1. **Threat Intelligence Fusion** — Aggregation of threat feeds, OSINT, SIEM, SOAR integration
2. **Population Intelligence** — Demographic analytics, citizen sentiment monitoring
3. **AI-Enabled Decision Support** — Automated summarization, entity extraction, narrative generation
4. **Sovereign Deployment** — On-prem, air-gapped capable, Malaysia-only data residency
5. **Compliance & Audit** — Full query logging, RBAC, audit trails, security accreditation

**Use Cases by Segment:**

| Segment | Primary Use Case | Business Value |
|---------|------------------|----------------|
| **Public-Facing Agencies** | Citizen sentiment monitoring, service quality analytics | Improved citizen trust, service efficiency |
| **Revenue-Generating Agencies** | Tax fraud detection, compliance monitoring, risk scoring | Revenue protection, audit automation |
| **Enforcement Agencies** | Threat actor profiling, crime pattern analysis, intelligence fusion | Case clearance rate, threat visibility |
| **Intelligence & Security** | Population intelligence, cross-agency data fusion, AI correlation | National security, early warning systems |
| **Regulatory Bodies** | Sector-wide threat monitoring, compliance dashboards, regulatory reporting | Systemic risk visibility, regulatory oversight |

**Competitive Differentiation:**
- ✅ **Sovereign capability** — Built and operated in Malaysia
- ✅ **CSM endorsement** — National cyber defence authority partnership
- ✅ **AI-native architecture** — LLM-enabled intelligence, not just dashboards
- ✅ **Data residency** — Malaysia-only, no foreign cloud dependency
- ✅ **Co-branding** — "CSM-powered by Aras" positioning

**Pricing Model:**
- **POC Phase:** RM 100K-200K (4-6 weeks, fixed-fee)
- **Deployment Phase:** RM 500K-800K (one-time implementation)
- **Subscription:** RM 250K-400K/year (annual, tiered by users/features)
- **Support:** RM 50K-150K/year (Bronze/Silver/Gold SLA)

### VoronDRQ GRC — Digital Risk Quantification Platform

**Positioning Statement:**
> *"Quantify cyber risk in financial terms — automate compliance, satisfy regulators, protect shareholder value"*

**Core Value Proposition:**
VoronDRQ GRC is a compliance-driven digital risk quantification platform that enables BNM-regulated financial institutions to automate RMiT/GTRM compliance, quantify cyber risk using FAIR-based methodology, and deliver board-level risk dashboards with regulatory audit readiness.

**Target Customer Profile:**
- **Primary:** Tier 2 banks (Alliance, AmBank, HLB, RHB, Islamic banks)
- **Secondary:** E-wallet providers & digital banks (TnG, GrabPay, Boost, AEON Digital)
- **Tertiary:** Fintech companies & DFIs (Funding Societies, SME Bank, TEKUN, insurance/takaful)

**Key Capabilities:**
1. **BNM RMiT Automation** — Automated control mapping, compliance dashboards, real-time monitoring
2. **GTRM Reporting** — Governance of Technology Risk Management, board-ready reports
3. **Digital Risk Quantification** — FAIR-based risk modeling, financial impact scoring
4. **Regulatory Audit Readiness** — Automated evidence collection, audit trails, control testing
5. **Third-Party Risk Management** — Vendor risk scoring, supply chain visibility

**Use Cases by Segment:**

| Segment | Primary Use Case | Business Value |
|---------|------------------|----------------|
| **Tier 2 Banks** | RMiT compliance automation, GTRM board reporting | 70% reduction in compliance reporting time |
| **E-Wallet Providers** | BNM licensing compliance, cybersecurity risk monitoring | Faster licensing approval, regulatory confidence |
| **Digital Banks** | Cloud-native compliance, automated control monitoring | Scalable compliance without proportional headcount |
| **Fintech Companies** | Investor-ready risk metrics, partnership compliance | Fundraising enablement, bank partnership qualification |
| **DFIs** | Government compliance mandates, risk oversight | Public accountability, ministry reporting |

**Competitive Differentiation:**
- ✅ **BNM-specific** — Purpose-built for RMiT/GTRM, not generic GRC
- ✅ **FAIR-based quantification** — Financial risk language for board discussions
- ✅ **CSM partnership** — Regulatory credibility through CSM endorsement
- ✅ **Rapid deployment** — 4-6 week POC, 8-12 week production (vs. 6-12 months for legacy GRC)
- ✅ **Compliance-first** — Pre-mapped controls, automated evidence, audit-ready

**Pricing Model:**
- **POC Phase:** RM 100K-150K (4-6 weeks, fixed-fee)
- **Deployment Phase:** RM 250K-400K (one-time implementation)
- **Subscription:** RM 150K-250K/year (annual, tiered by users/entities)
- **Support:** RM 40K-100K/year (Bronze/Silver/Gold SLA)

## Strategic Rationale: Two-Product Focus

**Why Only GovSec and VoronDRQ?**

1. **Market Clarity:** Two clear value propositions (sovereign intelligence vs. compliance automation) are easier to communicate than four fragmented offerings.

2. **Resource Concentration:** Engineering, sales, and delivery resources are focused on winning in two markets rather than spreading thin across four.

3. **Faster Revenue:** GovSec and Voron have the clearest procurement pathways (government direct award, BNM regulatory pressure) and shortest sales cycles (60-90 days).

4. **CSM Partnership Alignment:** CSM's mandate (national cyber defence, regulatory oversight) aligns perfectly with GovSec (government threat intel) and Voron (financial sector compliance).

5. **Scalability:** Once GovSec and Voron achieve market penetration, additional modules can be introduced as future product extensions.

6. **Delivery Excellence:** Two products enable standardized deployment playbooks, predictable delivery timelines, and higher customer satisfaction.

**Decision:** Focus exclusively on GovSec TIP + VoronDRQ GRC for Phase 1 (Months 1-12). Additional product extensions will be considered in Phase 2 (Month 13+) pending GovSec and Voron market validation and revenue targets achievement.
