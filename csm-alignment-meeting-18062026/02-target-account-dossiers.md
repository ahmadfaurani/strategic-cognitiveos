## 📊 Account Prioritization Framework

Accounts are tiered based on:
1. **Strategic fit** with 2 solution domains (GovSec TIP + VoronDRQ GRC)
2. **CSM relationship strength** and influence
3. **Budget availability** and procurement timeline
4. **Sales cycle length** (government vs. commercial)

### Tier Definitions

| Tier | Definition | Sales Cycle | POC Budget | Priority |
|------|------------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **P0 (Critical)** | Direct CSM relationship, ICP perfect fit, budget confirmed | 30-60 days | RM 100K-200K | Immediate |
| **P1 (High)** | Strong fit, CSM engagement exists, budget likely | 60-90 days | RM 100K-150K | Week 2-3 |
| **P2 (Medium)** | Good fit, longer cycle, develop relationship | 90-120 days | RM 50K-100K | Week 4-6 |
