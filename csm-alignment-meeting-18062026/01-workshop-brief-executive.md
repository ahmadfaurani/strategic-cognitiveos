## 💼 Revenue Sharing Model

| Scenario | CSM Share | Platform Share | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Government Accounts** (MINDEF, NACSA, agencies) | 60% | 40% | CSM branding critical for government trust |
| **GLC/Regulated Sector** (BNM, MCMC, TNB, Petronas) | 50% | 50% | Equal contribution (CSM network + Platform delivery) |
| **Private Sector** (Banks, telcos, enterprises) | 40% | 60% | Platform delivery heavier, CSM network less critical |
| **Training/Educational** (UPNM, universities) | 50% | 50% | Joint IP, co-delivery model |

**Revenue Recognition:**
- POC revenue: Upon POC completion + acceptance sign-off
- Deployment revenue: 50% upfront, 50% upon go-live
- Subscription revenue: Annually (upfront payment)
