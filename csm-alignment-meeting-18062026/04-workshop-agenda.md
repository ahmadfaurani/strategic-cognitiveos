## 🎤 Key Talking Points

### Opening (JSC Chair)
> "This partnership represents a strategic opportunity to accelerate Malaysia's sovereign cyber defence capabilities while creating sustainable commercial outcomes. Our 90-day execution plan is designed for speed and measurable results."

### Strategic Context (JSC Co-Chair)
> "We're not just deploying technology—we're building a joint capability. The 3-phase model ensures we move from POC to production within 60 days, with clear revenue targets at each stage."

### Commercial Model (Commercial Lead)
> "Our revenue sharing model is designed for fairness: 60/40 for government accounts where CSM branding is critical, 50/50 for GLCs where we contribute equally, and 40/60 for private sector where Platform delivery dominates."
