## 💵 Revenue Sharing Model

### Revenue Share by Sector

| Scenario | CSM Share | Platform Share | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Government Accounts** (MINDEF, NACSA, agencies, PDRM) | 60% | 40% | CSM branding critical for government trust |
| **GLC/Regulated Sector** (BNM, MCMC, TNB, Petronas, SC) | 50% | 50% | Equal contribution (CSM network + Platform delivery) |
| **Private Sector** (Banks, telcos, enterprises, Maybank) | 40% | 60% | Platform delivery heavier, CSM network less critical |
| **Training/Educational** (UPNM, universities, BruCERT) | 50% | 50% | Joint IP, co-delivery model |
| **Regional Partnerships** (BruCERT, ASEAN CERTs) | 50% | 50% | Strategic partnership, co-branding |

---

### Revenue Recognition

| Revenue Type | Recognition Point | Payment Terms |
|--------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **POC Revenue** | Upon POC completion + acceptance sign-off | 100% upfront (before POC kickoff) |
| **Deployment Revenue** | 50% upfront, 50% upon go-live | 50% on contract signing, 50% on production live |
| **Subscription Revenue** | Annually (upfront payment) | 100% upfront (annual) |
| **Implementation Revenue** | 50% upfront, 50% upon completion | 50% on kickoff, 50% on handover |
| **Support Revenue** | Annually (upfront payment) | 100% upfront (annual) |

## 📜 MoA Framework

### MoA Structure (Non-Blocking, Execution-First)

| Section | Content | Status |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **1. Purpose** | Strategic partnership for GovSec TIP + VoronDRQ GRC solutions | 🔲 Draft |
| **2. Scope** | Joint development, co-branding, revenue sharing, IP co-ownership | 🔲 Draft |
| **3. Roles** | CSM: Branding, network access, validation; Platform: Infrastructure, delivery, engineering | 🔲 Draft |
| **4. Revenue Sharing** | 60/40 (govt), 50/50 (GLC), 40/60 (private) — see Section 2 | 🔲 Draft |
| **5. IP Ownership** | 50/50 co-ownership, joint registration, CSM branding | 🔲 Draft |
| **6. Term** | 3 years (renewable), termination clause (90-day notice) | 🔲 Draft |
| **7. Confidentiality** | TLP:AMBER for internal docs, mutual NDA | 🔲 Draft |
| **8. Dispute Resolution** | Escalation to JSC Chair → Mediation → Arbitration (KL) | 🔲 Draft |
| **9. Signatories** | CSM Chairman + Platform Director | 🔲 Draft |

**MoA Principle:** Execution-first, formalize in parallel. POC deployment can begin before MoA finalization (with WG approval).
