## 🏗️ Governance Structure Overview

This document outlines the **two-tier governance structure** for CSM-Platform partnership: Joint Steering Committee (JSC) for strategic decisions and Working Group (WG) for operational execution.

---

## 🚨 Escalation Matrix

This matrix defines the escalation path for issues based on severity.

| Issue Type | Severity | First Escalation | Second Escalation | Final Escalation | Response Time |
|------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|------------------|---------------|
| **POC deployment blocked** | P0 | WG Lead (Hadri) | JSC Co-Chair (DAF) | JSC Chair (Al-Ishsal) | 24 hours |
| **Contract negotiation stalled** | P0 | WG Co-Lead (Zaharudin) | JSC Member (Zulfeka) | JSC Chair (Al-Ishsal) | 24 hours |
| **Technical integration delay** | P1 | Technical Lead (Fuad) | WG Lead (Hadri) | JSC Co-Chair (DAF) | 48 hours |
| **Budget/resource constraint** | P1 | WG Lead (Hadri) | JSC Co-Chair (DAF) | JSC Chair (Al-Ishsal) | 48 hours |
| **Stakeholder availability** | P2 | Program Manager (Normalinda) | WG Co-Lead (Zaharudin) | JSC Member (Zulfeka) | 72 hours |
| **Communication/timeline** | P2 | Program Manager (Normalinda) | WG Lead (Hadri) | WG Co-Lead (Zaharudin) | 72 hours |

### Severity Definitions

| Severity | Definition | Examples |
|----------|------------|----------|
| **P0 (Critical)** | Blocks revenue-generating activity, requires immediate escalation | POC deployment blocked, contract negotiation stalled |
| **P1 (High)** | Delays timeline, requires WG or JSC intervention | Technical integration delay, budget constraint |
| **P2 (Medium)** | Inconvenience, can be resolved within WG | Stakeholder availability, communication issues |
