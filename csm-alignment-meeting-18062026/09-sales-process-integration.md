## 📊 Overview

This document maps the **6-stage sales cycle** to the CSM-Platform 90-day execution plan, ensuring alignment with the official sales kit methodology.

### Two Sales Processes

| Product | Sales Process | Duration | Stages |
|---------|---------------|----------|--------|
| **VoronDRQ** (Commercial/GRC) | 6-stage commercial sales | 10-16 weeks | Prospecting → Qualification → Discovery → Solution Design → Negotiation → Close |
| **GovSec TIP** (Government) | 6-stage government sales | 90-150 days | Introduction → Technical Eval → Pilot (30d) → Procurement → Contract → Implementation |

## 🎯 VoronDRQ Sales Process (Commercial)

### Stage 1: Prospecting (Weeks 1-2)

**Objective:** Identify and qualify potential accounts

| Activity | Owner | Output | 90-Day Plan Mapping |
|----------|-------|--------|---------------------|
| Account research | Partnership Managers | Account list (P0/P1/P2) | Day 1-7 (02-target-account-dossiers.md) |
| Initial outreach | Partnership Managers | Intro meetings scheduled | Day 8-14 (P0 intro emails) |
| CSM endorsement | CSM Sponsor | Intro email/endorsement | Day 8-14 |
| Demo environment prep | Technical Lead | VoronDRQ demo ready | Day 6-7 |

**Exit Criteria:**
- ✅ 3-5 P0/P1 accounts identified
- ✅ Intro meetings scheduled
- ✅ CSM endorsement secured

---

### Stage 2: Qualification (Week 3)

**Objective:** Validate fit, budget, authority, timeline (BANT)

| Activity | Owner | Output | 90-Day Plan Mapping |
|----------|-------|--------|---------------------|
| Discovery call | Commercial Lead + CSM | BANT qualification | Day 15-21 (P0 intro meetings) |
| Pain point validation | Commercial Lead | Pain point document | Day 15-21 |
| Budget confirmation | Commercial Lead | Budget range confirmed | Day 15-21 |
| Decision maker identified | Commercial Lead | Stakeholder map | Day 15-21 |
| Timeline agreed | Commercial Lead | Sales timeline | Day 15-21 |

**Exit Criteria:**
- ✅ Pain points validated (RMiT compliance, TCO reduction)
- ✅ Budget confirmed (RM 450K-800K Year 1)
- ✅ Decision maker identified
- ✅ Timeline agreed (10-16 weeks)

**Qualification Scorecard:**

| Criteria | Score (1-5) | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Pain (clear need) | | RMiT compliance, vendor consolidation |
| Budget (confirmed) | | RM 450K-800K range |
| Authority (decision maker) | | CISO, Head of Tech Risk |
| Timeline (urgent) | | Q3 2026 deployment target |
| **Total** | **/20** | **Qualify if ≥15** |

---

### Stage 3: Discovery (Weeks 4-6)

**Objective:** Deep-dive into requirements, current state, success criteria

| Activity | Owner | Output | 90-Day Plan Mapping |
|----------|-------|--------|---------------------|
| Requirements workshop | Technical Lead + CSM | Requirements document | Day 17-23 (technical discovery) |
| Current state assessment | Technical Lead | Architecture diagram | Day 17-23 |
| Success criteria defined | Commercial Lead | KPI document | Day 17-23 |
| Stakeholder interviews | Commercial Lead | Stakeholder feedback | Day 17-23 |
| Demo (90-minute flow) | CSM Presenter + Platform | Demo feedback | Day 22-28 |

**Exit Criteria:**
- ✅ Requirements documented
- ✅ Current state assessed
- ✅ Success criteria defined (KPIs)
- ✅ Demo completed with positive feedback

**Discovery Questionnaire (Key Questions):**
1. What GRC tools are you currently using?
2. What are the biggest pain points with your current setup?
3. How do you currently measure and report technology risk?
4. What RMiT compliance challenges are you facing?
5. How many vendors are in your current GRC stack?
6. What would success look like in 90 days?

---

### Stage 4: Solution Design (Weeks 7-10)

**Objective:** Design tailored solution, POC scope, success metrics

| Activity | Owner | Output | 90-Day Plan Mapping |
|----------|-------|--------|---------------------|
| POC scope definition | Technical Lead + CSM | POC SOW | Day 24-30 (POC proposal) |
| Success metrics defined | Commercial Lead | KPI baseline | Day 24-30 |

| POC proposal | Commercial Lead | POC proposal (RM 200K) | Day 24-30 |
| POC agreement | Commercial Lead | Signed POC contract | Day 31-42 |

**Exit Criteria:**
- ✅ POC scope agreed (30 days, RM 200K)
- ✅ Success metrics defined (KPIs)
- ✅ Technical architecture reviewed
- ✅ POC proposal submitted
- ✅ POC contract signed

**POC Scope Template:**

| Component | Description | Success Metric |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| GRC Module | Risk register, controls, compliance | 100% RMiT coverage |
| ASM | External asset discovery | 500+ assets monitored |
| TPRM | Third-party risk assessments | 20+ vendors assessed |
| Copilot AI | Natural language queries | 50% report time reduction |

### Stage 5: Negotiation (Weeks 11-14)

**Objective:** Negotiate deployment terms, pricing, contract

| Activity | Owner | Output | 90-Day Plan Mapping |
|----------|-------|--------|---------------------|
| POC execution | Delivery Lead | Weekly status reports | Day 31-60 (POC Weeks 1-4) |
| POC mid-point review | Delivery Lead | Mid-point report | Day 45-50 |
| POC final report | Delivery Lead | Acceptance sign-off | Day 57-60 |
| Conversion proposal | Commercial Lead | Deployment proposal | Day 59-60 |
| Contract negotiation | Commercial Lead | Terms agreed | Day 61-70 |
| Contract signing | Commercial Lead | Signed deployment contract | Day 71-80 |

**Exit Criteria:**
- ✅ POC completed successfully
- ✅ POC acceptance sign-off obtained
- ✅ Conversion proposal submitted
- ✅ Contract terms negotiated
- ✅ Deployment contract signed

**Negotiation Levers:**
- **Pricing:** RM 500K-800K license + RM 200K implementation
- **Revenue Share:** 50/50 (GLC/regulated sector)
- **Payment Terms:** 50% upfront, 50% on go-live
- **Support:** RM 300K-500K/year (annual subscription)

## 🎯 Stage Gate Criteria

### VoronDRQ Stage Gates

| Stage Gate | Criteria | Go/No-Go Decision |
|------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Gate 1 (End of Prospecting)** | ≥3 P0/P1 accounts qualified | Go to Qualification |
| **Gate 2 (End of Qualification)** | BANT confirmed, score ≥15/20 | Go to Discovery |
| **Gate 3 (End of Discovery)** | Requirements doc, demo positive | Go to Solution Design |
| **Gate 4 (End of Solution Design)** | POC SOW signed | Go to POC Execution |
| **Gate 5 (End of POC)** | POC acceptance, KPIs met | Go to Negotiation |
| **Gate 6 (End of Negotiation)** | Contract terms agreed | Go to Close |

---
