# Summary

Three-part email thread covering the full lifecycle of Aras Integrasi's participation in CRC 2026:

1. **Invitation (Jul 13, Dr. Ji-Jian Chin → Fuad, DAF):** Aras Integrasi invited to sponsor the 6th International Cyber Resilience Conference (CRC 2026), 1-3 September 2026 at Bangi Resort Hotel, Selangor. Initially approached Fuad in his capacity as ISC2 Malaysia Chapter Academic Director. Sponsorship packages range from supporting-level to Platinum (RM10K+). Four Platinum slots available. Customised arrangements (workshops, technical sessions) welcomed.

2. **DAF Strategic Framing (Jul 15, DAF → Farul, Azza):** DAF recommended exploring a customised participation package rather than pure branding sponsorship. Proposed inclusion of: exhibition/product demo presence, technical speaking/panel opportunity, pre/post-event promotional visibility, targeted government/industry/academic stakeholder engagement, positioning Aras as technology/cyber-resilience partner. Highlighted product showcase opportunity (GovSec TIP, VoronCitadel, VoronScout, chain:SENTRY). Requested views from technology and marketing perspectives.

3. **Hadri Finance Confirmation (Aug 19, Hadri → Orange Ng):** Confirmed sponsorship request and management approval letter sent to finance department. Amount reduced from RM10K to RM5K (organiser lowered sponsorship tiers). No further invoices — full amount stated on original invoice. Conference is 1-3 September 2026.
