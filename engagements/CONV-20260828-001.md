# Summary

DAF sends a formal email to Azrul Nazim (CSM) with a working NDA Framework document attached. The email outlines 11 key principles of the framework and requests CSM alignment before routing to legal teams.

## Strategic Significance

This email marks the transition from technical/commercial alignment to legal framework establishment in the Bursa POC engagement. The NDA Framework:

1. **Protects Aras Background IP** — VoronCitadel platform, tools, software remain Aras property
2. **Accommodates Bursa Foreground IP** — Bursa-specific outputs (PO documents, OMS schemas, architecture) remain Bursa property
3. **Creates sublicensing mechanism** — CSM receives perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence with right to sublicense to Bursa
4. **Blocks derivative commercialisation** — Aras cannot reuse Bursa/CSM-derived information for unrelated engagements
5. **Mandates IP disclosure** — Aras must disclose dependencies, third-party components before incorporating into POC
6. **Establishes PDPA compliance** — Malaysian PDPA requirements for personal data handling
7. **Prevents unauthorised marketing** — No use of Bursa name, POC results, or branding without written approval
8. **Sets up Service Agreement pathway** — NDA is initial framework; Service Agreement follows for implementation/production

DAF specifically flags 4 provisions for CSM review: Foreground IP ownership, Background IP licensing, Bursa sublicensing rights, and non-reuse provisions — these are the commercially sensitive terms most likely to require negotiation.
