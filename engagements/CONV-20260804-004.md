# Summary

DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice) sent a strategic mobilisation directive to Hadri synthesising the three CSM workstream emails received on Aug 4 and establishing a firm delivery milestone: **joint product launch with CSM at CyberDSA in early October 2026**. The email explicitly flags Workstream 3 (TI integration) as separate from Workstream 2 (CMERP integration), confirms that Workstreams 2 and 3 are essential for GovSec product-market readiness, and proposes internal mobilisation including newly hired interns as interim support. # Strategic Significance

This is the first communication that establishes the **CyberDSA early October milestone** as the firm delivery target for the GovSec × CSM joint product launch. It transforms the three individual CSM integration tracks from standalone technical exercises into components of a unified commercial delivery programme with a hard deadline.

The email also introduces two new strategic dimensions not previously captured:

1. **Resource capacity escalation** — Three parallel CSM tracks create visible pressure on delivery capacity, calendar, and technical resources. The Senior GovSec resource hiring is recategorised as a delivery-readiness requirement, not just a staffing matter.

2. **Interim mobilisation plan** — DAF proposes deploying newly hired interns for research, documentation, meeting preparation, and action tracking as a stopgap, while explicitly acknowledging this is strictly interim and insufficient for the scope/stakeholder intensity/technical complexity of the engagements.

This conversation bridges the three outbound emails (CONV-20260804-001, -002, -003) into a consolidated strategic picture and establishes the governance framework for managing them as a unified delivery programme.
