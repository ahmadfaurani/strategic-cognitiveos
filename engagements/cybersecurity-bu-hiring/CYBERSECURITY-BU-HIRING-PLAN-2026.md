## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document presents the hiring plan for the Cybersecurity Business Unit (BU), escalated as a **MUST-HIRE requirement** to support a **targeted annual revenue of RM12 million**. The plan requests approval to open **12 critical roles** across leadership, lead, and individual contributor levels, with staged onboarding tied to revenue milestones.

**Core Investment Thesis:**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Revenue Target** | RM12 million (annual, steady state) |
| **Time Horizon** | 18 months (full ramp) |
| **Fully Loaded Hiring Cost** | RM2.4M–RM3.2M (annual) |
| **Cost-to-Revenue Ratio** | 20%–27% |
| **Target Gross Margin** | 40%–50% |
| **ROI Multiple** | 2.5x–3.0x revenue-to-cost |

**Recommendation:** Approve full org structure immediately, execute onboarding in 3 revenue-linked waves.
