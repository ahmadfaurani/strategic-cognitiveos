# Alignment Meeting Outcome (Aug 11)

## Agreed Two-Workstream Structure

| Workstream | Primary Collaboration | Agreed Focus | Initial Output |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------|----------------|
| Targeted Lighthouse Accounts | Sales + Cybersecurity Practice/Product Team | Prioritise 10–15 high-potential accounts with existing relationships, identifiable requirements and stronger entry pathways | Validated account shortlist, assigned account owners, structured engagement plans |
| Broader GTM Outreach | Marketing, supported by Sales and Cybersecurity Practice | Develop broader market outreach based on proposed prospect and stakeholder database | Campaign messaging, content formats, outreach channels, marketing-qualified leads |

## Agreed Operating Model

| Team | Primary Responsibility |
|------|----------------------|
| Sales | Validate account relationships, nominate priority accounts, assign account ownership, lead commercial follow-up |
| Marketing | Develop campaign messaging, content and outreach channels for the broader prospect universe |
| Cybersecurity Practice/Product Team | Provide prospect intelligence, stakeholder mapping, sales enablement materials, product positioning, discovery support, demonstrations |
| Joint Team | Establish lead-handoff arrangements, campaign timelines, success measures, pipeline-review cadence |

## 6 Immediate Next Steps

| # | Action | Proposed Owner |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | Validate and shortlist initial 10–15 lighthouse accounts | Sales + Cybersecurity Practice |
| 2 | Confirm existing relationships and assign account owners | Sales |

| 5 | Confirm lead-handoff process, timeline and success measures | Sales + Marketing + Cybersecurity Practice |
| 6 | Establish regular GTM and pipeline-review cadence | Joint Team |
