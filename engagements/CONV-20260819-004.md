**Key content:**

### Approved Headline
**National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment**

### Core Narrative
At CyberDSA 2026, Aras Integrasi is demonstrating more than individual cybersecurity products. The central story is the emergence of a Malaysian capability to design, integrate and operationalise sovereign cybersecurity technology across the full security lifecycle.

Together with strategic ecosystem partners (CyberSecurity Malaysia), we are bringing together governance and compliance, threat intelligence, AI-enabled security operations and emerging-technology security into a cohesive national-grade capability.

**Key market message:** Malaysia can build, integrate and operationalise its own critical cybersecurity capabilities — designed around Malaysian requirements, deployed within Malaysian infrastructure and strengthened through Malaysian institutional collaboration. Aras Integrasi's role is to help engineer that capability into operational reality.

### 6-Layer Narrative Framework

| Layer | Core Message |
|-------|-------------|
| National Story | Malaysia is developing the capability to build and operationalise its own cybersecurity technology ecosystem |
| Aras Story | Aras Integrasi acts as a Malaysian sovereign technology integrator, bringing infrastructure, platforms, cybersecurity technology and institutional partners together |
| Technology Story | Sovereign cybersecurity requires integrated capability across governance, intelligence, operations and emerging technology protection |
| Partnership Story | National capability is strengthened through collaboration between industry engineering capabilities and institutional expertise, including CyberSecurity Malaysia |
| CyberDSA Story | CyberDSA provides the platform to demonstrate these capabilities operating together as a cohesive cybersecurity stack |
| Market Story | Government, CNII, GLC and regulated organisations can engage Aras around capability deployment, rather than simply procuring individual products |

### Action Items
- DAF asks if the above can serve as anchor for booth, product messaging, social media, media engagement and Flash Talk — while allowing branding team flexibility in creative translation
- DAF requests Azzatullina Pawanchik synch up tomorrow or Friday (Aug 20-21)

### Key Reframe from Rashid's Proposal
- "National Grid of Sovereign AI Full Stack for Malaysia" → "National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment"
- "Grid" → "Grade" (capability quality, not infrastructure topology)
- "AI Full Stack" → "Full-Stack Sovereign Technology" (broader than AI-only; encompasses full cybersecurity lifecycle)
- "for Malaysia" → "Deployment" (operational action, not aspirational statement)
