Salam Everyone!

I wanted to frame our CyberDSA participation slightly differently before we move further into the sponsorship, booth and event mechanics.

Over the past several months, we have progressively assembled capabilities across sovereign AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, intelligence, platforms and applications.

CyberDSA could be the point where we bring these together under a single market proposition:

If we execute this properly, CyberDSA becomes less about exhibiting individual technologies and more about demonstrating what Aras is increasingly capable of delivering as an integrated sovereign technology organisation.
