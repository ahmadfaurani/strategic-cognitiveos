## Summary

DAF shared a comprehensive stakeholder coverage plan that replaces the one-to-one CSM relationship model with a four-layer multi-contact architecture (Primary → Secondary → Specialist → Executive Escalation). The plan defines:

1. **10 priority CSM stakeholders** with named Aras primary/secondary/specialist coverage
2. **9 Aras stakeholder roles** with defined CSM coverage scopes
3. **Four-layer coverage architecture** (relationship, operational, specialist, executive)
4. **Communication ownership model** mapping 7 topic areas to owners/audiences
5. **Strategic engagement flow** (before/during/after) for priority customer meetings
6. **CyberDSA responsibilities** for each Aras stakeholder
7. **Coverage governance** — 6 rules including "no single points of failure"
8. **8 readiness metrics** — all targeting 100% coverage or 0 SPOF
9. **8 immediate actions** with owners and required outputs
10. **Operating position** — institutional coverage, not more people cc'd

## Key Strategic Shifts

- **From one-to-one to multi-layer**: Each priority CSM stakeholder should have ~3 active Aras relationship paths
- **DAF load reduction**: Plan explicitly aims to prevent DAF and Hadri from being single points of failure
- **Direct technical relationships**: Fuad to build direct relationships with CSM technical stakeholders (Hafiz, Zaharudin, Iqbal, Amirul, Nazri) before CyberDSA
- **Commercial activation**: Zulfelka brought into commercial progression path alongside DAF
- **Account operations formalised**: Shuhada owns account verification, CRM, meeting coordination
- **Event activation team named**: Amelia Nadia, Rashid Bin Ramli, Said Farid Zainudin
