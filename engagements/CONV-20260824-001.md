On Monday August 24, 2026, 10:00–11:00 AM MYT, DAF met with Azrul at Menara CyberAxis for the Bursa Malaysia VoronCitadel POC deep dive (originally proposed by Azrul on Aug 20 in DEC-20260820-003). ## Meeting Context

- Hadri's calendar invite (Aug 21, originally for CyberDSA GTM coordination)

- Azrul's Aug 20 email kicking off VoronCitadel POC for Bursa (DEC-20260820-003)

## Key Technical Direction (from DAF's post-meeting summary)

### Phase 0 Structure
- **Phase 0 anchored in Third Party Risk Management (TPRM)** using current VoronCitadel capability as primary validation anchor
- **Scope: 3–4 participating organisations** (NOT the wider ~24-entity model immediately)
- TPRM workflow: vendor onboarding → tiering → assessment → remediation → integration of third-party risk into each organisation's enterprise-risk view

### Shared Vendor Validation
- Validate the proposed common-vendor model against actual participant vendor inventories
- NOT to be treated as an architectural or commercial assumption without validation

### Federated Compliance Document Check
- Participating organisations maintain their respective environments
- Approved compliance evidence / document-status information can be evaluated or aggregated at Bursa level

### Federated Management View
- Bursa's required aggregate visibility to be defined: data access, document access, tenant isolation, reporting hierarchy

### Critical Distinction
The broader ~24-entity federation is a **target operating-model hypothesis** — NOT a committed scope for Phase 0.

### Two-Phase Progression

**Build Phased:**
1. TPRM Deployment
2. Shared Vendor Validation
3. Federated Compliance Document Check

**Validation Phase:**
1. URD/SRS
2. Architecture & Capacity Validation
3. Bursa Acceptance
4. Scale Decision
