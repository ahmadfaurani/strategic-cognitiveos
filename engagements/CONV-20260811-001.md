# Summary

DAF issued a formal directive to pause all further feature development across the three Cybersecurity Flagship Products (VoronCitadel, GovSec TIP, ChainSentry) and shift focus to productisation, commercial readiness, and GTM execution. The directive establishes a comprehensive 6-category product documentation framework to be compiled into a centralised product repository, with clear separation between current capabilities and future plans. Development activities are restricted to: critical bug fixes, security remediation, demo/POC-affecting issues, and previously committed integration requirements for commercialisation. DAF directs Fuad to pass the freeze to the devsecops intern with Fuad and Hadri as overseers.

# Strategic Significance

This is a major escalation of the Aug 10 development freeze (DEC-20260810-002), which applied only to GovSec TIP. This directive extends the freeze to all three flagship products and reframes the strategic posture: from building features to building product assets. The productisation framework (MVP Spec, Roadmap, Backlog, Commercialisation Readiness, Sales & GTM Materials, Product Governance) represents the most structured product management directive DAF has issued to date. It directly addresses Workstream B's core challenge: transitioning from continuous development to commercial readiness.

The email also introduces "VoronCitadel" as a product name not previously recorded in CognitiveOS — it is assessed as "Ready" for commercialisation and sales materials, while GovSec and ChainSentry are "Partial." This suggests VoronCitadel is the most commercially mature product and may be the productised name for what was previously tracked as VoronDRQ.

# Key Points

1. **Formal pause** on all further feature development across all three Cybersecurity Flagship Products
2. **Permitted development** limited to: critical bug fixes, security remediation, demo/POC/customer-engagement-affecting issues, previously committed integration requirements for commercialisation
3. **DevSecOps intern** to be informed of the freeze by Fuad, with Fuad and Hadri as overseers
4. **Immediate priority:** consolidate everything into a centralised product repository and establish a proper product baseline
5. **Six-category documentation framework** for each product:
   - Current MVP Specification (already exists for all 3)
   - Product Roadmap (pending) — baseline, near-term requirements, future capabilities, Must/Should/Future prioritisation
   - Product Backlog (pending) — outstanding tech items, defects, integration requirements, enhancement requests
   - Commercialisation Readiness (varies: VoronCitadel=Ready, GovSec=Partial, ChainSentry=Partial) — target customers, deployment model, licensing/packaging, POC requirements, production dependencies
   - Sales & GTM Materials (varies: VoronCitadel=Ready, GovSec=Partial, ChainSentry=Partial) — one-pager, sales deck, demo flow, tech FAQ, sales kit
   - Product Governance (all pending) — product owner, technical owner, version baseline, documentation owner, change/release management
6. **Clear separation** required between what exists today and what is planned for the future — ensures Sales/Marketing work from accurate product baseline and do not unintentionally position roadmap capabilities as current functionality
7. **Five specific first outputs requested from Fuad** (with Hadri support on solution/deployment/customer-facing requirements):
   - Central repository structure for all three products
   - Current documentation inventory
   - Missing-document / readiness gap list
   - Initial product roadmap for each product
   - Recommended priorities required before active GTM and customer POCs
8. **Review gate:** Once baseline is established, DAF will review the three products collectively and agree on commercialisation priorities, GTM readiness, and next development cycle
9. **Team meeting directive:** Email trail to be used as primary discussion direction for team meeting; execution bottlenecks to be flagged
10. **Objective statement:** "stabilise what we have, document it properly, package it as a product, and take it to market"

**Root email (Fuad, Apr 20):** Fuad submitted the VoronDRQ MVP Specification document for review. The document covers the full platform blueprint including a Phase 2 roadmap. It reflects alignment with 5 regulatory frameworks (ISO 27001:2022, SC GTRM, BNM RMiT, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST RMF), positioning VoronDRQ as a compliance-first platform for Malaysian and APAC markets. Fuad noted the specification was purposefully aligned with the upcoming CSM capital market engagement in May 2026, and coordinated a platform demo for April 27, 2026. He flagged 5 operational dependencies for production rollout (see below).

**Follow-up (Fuad, Aug 10):** Fuad sent the latest VoronCitadel MVP document (based on the latest MVP they already ran, including high-level architecture). He noted the sales kit may need updating to reflect the latest features. This email triggered DAF's expanded directive.

**VoronDRQ / VoronCitadel Operational Dependencies (flagged by Fuad, Apr 20):**
1. **UI/UX Refinement** — including white-labelling support for the CSM engagement
2. **AI Copilot RAG Pipeline & LLM Integration** — core intelligence layer underpinning the Analyst Workbench
3. **Compliance Framework Data Layer Population** — structured seeding of all 5 supported regulatory frameworks (ISO 27001:2022, SC GTRM, BNM RMiT, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST RMF)
4. **Integration Connector Build-Out & Testing** — SIEM, ticketing, CMDB, IdP, and threat intelligence connectors
5. **Multi-Tenant Data Isolation & Security Hardening** — RBAC, RLS enforcement, audit logging, and pre-launch penetration testing

**Note:** These 5 dependencies were identified 4 months before the development freeze. They represent the production rollout requirements that should now feed into the VoronCitadel Product Backlog (ACT-20260811-003). Under the freeze terms, these qualify as "previously committed integration requirements for commercialisation" — permitted development.
