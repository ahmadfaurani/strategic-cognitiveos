# Summary

Fathi Kamil bin Mohad Zainuddin, Head of MyCERT Department at CyberSecurity Malaysia, has formally accepted the AI Systems Co-Design Lab (Cohort 01) invitation on behalf of the MyCERT department. The response confirms that MyCERT is currently building several local research products integrating with Generative AI and finds Aras Integrasi's offering suitable for their requirements. Fathi Kamil has submitted a list of personnel from the MyCERT department to explore the models provided by Aras Integrasi as part of the pilot phase, referencing an attached document with the personnel list.

# Strategic Significance

This is a **milestone event** for INIT-20260804-004 — the AI Systems Co-Design Lab initiative advances from "framed" (invitation sent, response pending) to "prototype" (partner accepted, personnel submitted). This is the first formal acceptance of a Cohort 01 partnership.

Key strategic dimensions:

1. **MyCERT as department-level engagement** — The response comes from the Head of MyCERT Department, not just individual technologists. This signals institutional-level buy-in from a operational CSM unit, not just interest from individual researchers.

2. **Existing GenAI work revealed** — MyCERT is "currently building up several local research products integrating with Generative AI." This is significant intelligence: CSM/MyCERT already has active GenAI development programmes. The Co-Design Lab accelerates existing work rather than starting from scratch.

3. **Pilot phase framing** — Fathi Kamil explicitly frames the engagement as a "pilot phase," aligning with Aras's cohort model. The personnel list is being submitted for exploration of Aras-provided models.

4. **Cross-workstream implications** — MyCERT's existing GenAI product development could benefit from or connect to the three existing CSM-Aras workstreams (VoronDRQ GTM, CMERP integration, TI integration). MyCERT operates CSM's Cyber Threat Response and Resolution Centre, which is directly relevant to the TI integration track.

5. **CyberDSA positioning** — A confirmed CSM/MyCERT cohort with active GenAI prototyping strengthens the CyberDSA October 2026 demonstration potential for Aras's sovereign AI platform.

6. **Personnel list attachment** — The email references an attached document containing the list of MyCERT personnel for the pilot. This attachment was not included in the forwarded email and needs to be obtained.
