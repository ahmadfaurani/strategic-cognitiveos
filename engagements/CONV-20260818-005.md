# Summary

DAF provided the complete CyberDSA 2026 Key Media & Brand Narrative via Telegram direct message. The document directs the branding team to frame the CyberDSA story around Malaysian sovereign technology capability rather than three individual products. The narrative framework includes:

## Key Directives from DAF

1. **Broaden the narrative** — "I would make the media narrative broader than the three products. The story should be about Malaysian sovereign technology capability, with CyberDSA acting as the proof point and the products as evidence."

2. **Campaign line** — "Built in Malaysia. Integrated for Malaysia. Engineered for Sovereignty."

3. **Corporate positioning** — "Aras Integrasi — Malaysian Sovereign Technology Integrator"

4. **Branding hierarchy** — Sovereign Capability → Integrated Stack → Technology Pillars → Individual Features (never reversed)

5. **Messaging guardrails** — Avoid: "three new products," "another cybersecurity vendor," "AI-powered cybersecurity solutions," "locally hosted foreign technology," "Made in Malaysia" without capability explanation, unsubstantiated deployment claims, implied government endorsement beyond agreed collaboration

6. **Visual unity** — Three products as components of one architecture, not three unrelated booths
