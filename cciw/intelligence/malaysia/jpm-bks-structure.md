## Executive Summary

Bahagian Keselamatan Strategik (BKS) operates as the primary analytical and coordination hub for contemporary and multi-domain security threats under Jabatan Perdana Menteri (JPM). Functions are split across two core entities:

1. **Unit within Pejabat Perdana Menteri (PMO)** — Executive risk assessment, cognitive security, policy integrity
2. **Cluster under Majlis Keselamatan Negara (MKN)** — Tactical inter-agency coordination, operational simulations, traditional/non-traditional security domains

## 2. Core Functions & Operational Portfolios

### A. Kluster Keselamatan Strategik (MKN)

MKN divides Strategic Security roles into critical perimeters supporting Dasar Keselamatan Negara (DKN):

#### 2.1 Cyber & Technology Domain Coordination
- **Partner:** Agensi Keselamatan Siber Negara (NACSA)
- **Mandate:** Monitor protection of Critical National Information Infrastructure (CNII)
- **Operations:** Coordinate large-scale cybercrime response strategies

#### 2.2 Territorial Security (Land, Maritime, Air)
- **Border Enforcement Coordination:** Counter incursions and cross-border smuggling
- **Maritime Monitoring:** Operations in Malaysian Maritime Zone (ZMM)
- **Special Zones:** Strengthen East Coast Sabah Special Security Area (ESSCOM)

#### 2.3 Crisis Management & High Readiness
- **Leadership:** Mega-scale security simulations for international events
- **Example:** Inter-agency security coordination for ASEAN Summit 2026

#### 2.4 Security & Geostrategy Management
- **Analysis:** Regional geopolitical shifts (e.g., South China Sea tensions)
- **Assessment:** Impact on national sovereignty

---

### B. Bahagian Keselamatan Strategik (PMO)

Functions as advisory and dynamic monitoring unit at Prime Minister's Office level:

#### 2.5 Cognitive Domain Defence (Counter-Disinformation)
- **Monitoring:** Social media narratives
- **Counter-Measures:** Counter information manipulation campaigns (perception management) driven by foreign or domestic agendas threatening public harmony

#### 2.6 Supply Resilience & Systemic Risk
- **Assessment:** Non-traditional threats (food supply chain risks, economic leakage)
- **Policy:** Ensure national protection policies are threat-resistant

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives & Operational Direction

### 3.1 Policy-Operations Integration
Per DKN core principles, BKS acts as nexus translating Prime Minister's security directives into pragmatic actions by enforcement agencies, reducing bureaucracy during national crises.

### 3.2 High-Impact Leakage Reduction
Coordinate transparency-based security doctrine to prevent national policies from being exploited by subversive elements or organized crime.

### 3.3 Absolute Digital Domain Sovereignty
Given threat landscape shift toward hybrid warfare, priority focus on building strategic forensic capabilities and national cyber resilience.
