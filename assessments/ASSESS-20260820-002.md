# Assessment Target

ChainSentry (chain:SENTRY) MVP — blockchain forensics & investigative intelligence workbench.

## Assessment Type

Readiness assessment — scored across 8 dimensions, weighted by how much each determines safe pilot exposure.

## Methodology

Two scores reported:
- **Implementation readiness** — what the codebase does today, on the review branch
- **Deployed readiness** — what the running instance actually serves

A single blended figure was deliberately NOT produced — it would average away the fact that most remediation is complete but none of it is serving.

## Readiness Scores (8 Dimensions)

| Dimension | Weight | Implementation | Deployed | Principal Constraint |
|-----------|--------|----------------|----------|----------------------|
| Core investigative capability | 20 | 95 | 95 | EVM-only tracing (stated boundary, not defect) |
| Intelligence holdings & coverage | 10 | 70 | 80 | 6 of 18 source roles degraded |
| Security & authorisation | 20 | 80 | 30 | Unauthenticated routes, ungated fixtures, public docs, unenforced audit |
| Evidence integrity & defensibility | 12 | 75 | 55 | No cryptographic signing; deployed audit is append-only by comment only |
| Access & identity | 10 | 40 | 20 | No TLS, no named accounts, only 2 of 5 roles |
| Deployment & release integrity | 10 | 60 | 15 | 43 uncommitted mods, no migration ledger |
| Reliability & recoverability | 10 | 30 | 30 | Backup never restored; no recovery objectives |
| Quality gates & regression safety | 8 | 65 | 10 | Gates repaired on branch; 62 type errors, 11 stale tests remain |

## Readiness Trajectory

| Milestone | Deployed Readiness | Movement | Cause |
|-----------|-------------------|----------|-------|
| Today | 47% | — | 12 gaps closed but undeployed; credentials unrotated |
| After Phase 0 | 77% | +30 | Rotation closes exposure; release lands 12 gaps; migrations restore schema control |
| After Phase 1 | 90% | +13 | TLS, named accounts, enforcement, isolation proven, sources dispositioned |
| MVP threshold | 85% | — | Level at which external casework is defensible |

## Key Findings

1. **22-point gap is the central finding** — 12 closed gaps sitting in a branch that has never been deployed
2. **Security dimension shows the largest impl/deploy split** — 80 vs 30 — because the unauthenticated diagnostic route, ungated fixtures, public documentation, and unenforced audit are all live on the deployment
3. **Single highest-leverage act is Phase 0** — writes almost no new code, moves deployed readiness +30 points
4. **Capability does not compensate for exposure** — an open credential exposure blocks any pilot regardless of feature completeness
