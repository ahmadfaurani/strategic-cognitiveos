# Assessment Target

Commercial readiness of the three Cyber Security Practice flagship products: **GovSec TIP, VoronCitadel and chain:SENTRY**.

## Assessment Type

Readiness assessment based on DAF product-owner review and the current canonical product sales-kit repositories.

## Findings

| Product | Commercial Status | Current State | Next Validation Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| GovSec TIP | **Commercially Ready** | Sales kit treated as accurate and complete baseline | Sales/end-user engagement to expose real gaps |
| VoronCitadel | **Commercially Ready** | Sales kit ready; Bursa-specific engineering remains active | TPRM + federated compliance document-check execution and Bursa/CSM validation |
| chain:SENTRY | **Commercially Ready** | Sales kit treated as current baseline | Sales/end-user engagement to expose real gaps |

### Portfolio Interpretation

The portfolio should no longer default to internal product/collateral refinement as the primary activity. For GovSec TIP and chain:SENTRY, the next meaningful information comes from the market. For VoronCitadel, market readiness is established but targeted Bursa engineering remains on the critical path.

## Recommendations

1. Use real customer engagement as the gap-validation mechanism.
2. Feed validated objections, missing proof points and feature requirements back into the sales kits and product backlog.
3. Keep Fuad focused on Bursa TPRM and federated document-check engineering.
4. Shift the next portfolio review to **delivery readiness** — implementation capacity, environments, ownership, support model and repeatability.
