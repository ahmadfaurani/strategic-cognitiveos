# Assessment Target

DAFClawbot TBH-001 PM hiring justification — the analytical document presenting 69 active actions, 37 delegatable, 54% reduction, 74 hours/week recovered, and one-sale-payback argument.

## Assessment Type

Fact-check + readiness assessment + role-boundary review

## Methodology

Section-by-section verification of quantitative claims against source data (action registry, TBH-REGISTRY.md, operating model document). Arithmetic reconciliation of workstream row counts. Logical assessment of time-allocation assumptions. Role-scope comparison against approved operating model.

## Findings Summary

### Quantitative Corrections

| Claim | Assessment | Status |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 69 total active actions | 71 workstream rows ≠ 69 unique actions; ACT-20260817-006 appears in 2 workstreams | ⚠️ Deduplicate by Action ID |
| 74 hours/week recovered | Assumes uniform 2hrs/week per action; not operationally credible | ❌ Remove |
| 54% reduction | Valid as action-ownership, not as workload | ⚠️ Reframe |
| One sale pays for PM | Revenue ≠ contribution margin | ⚠️ Reframe |

### Role-Boundary Correction

PM ≠ TPM. The approved operating model identifies Technical Product Manager as a separate P1 role. TBH-001 should coordinate product-governance processes but should NOT own product roadmap, backlog, or commercialisation documentation.

## Recommended Position

**Is there a real PM capacity gap?** Yes — strongly substantiated.

**Is TBH-001 justified?** Yes.

**Is it strategically important?** Yes — P1 operational capability.

**Should it precede engineering hires?** Reasonable, provided engineering recruitment continues in parallel.

**Overall TBH-001 case:** Strong — approve subject to corrected baseline and role boundaries.

## Final Assessment

TBH-001 should proceed. The strongest justification is not "69 actions are too many." It is that the Cyber Security Practice has reached a scale where programme coordination, execution governance and follow-through are permanent operating functions, while those functions remain concentrated on the Director.

The evidence already identifies that structural vacancy in the approved operating model. The quantitative analysis strengthens the argument — but only after three corrections: deduplicate the 69-action baseline, describe 54% as action-ownership reduction, and remove the 74-hour/week and one-sale-payback claims unless supported by actual time and margin data.

With those corrections, the justification moves from a persuasive argument to a management-defensible staffing case.
