## 1. Situation

- The kertas cadangan on MCMC's table is built on **RM5.0M** (DEC-20260815-001, formalised 15 Aug).
- The operative programme figure is now **RM3.8M** — Detailed Analytical Cost Structure V2 (DOC-20260908-005, dated 23 Aug, adopted by DAF directive 8 Sep).
- MCMC's session agenda cites the cost structure pillar; the printed agenda may reference the RM5.0M submission.
- Delta: **−RM1,200,000 (−24%)**, same scope architecture.

## 2. Talking Points (recommended sequence)

1. **Lead with discipline, not price.** "We have refined the costing since submission. The programme is the same — the ask is sharper."
2. **Same programme, same guarantees:** 9-component architecture, 12 months, 7-person dedicated team, full lifecycle to operational acceptance, stage-gate acceptance (G1 end-M2 low-risk entry), capability-investment framing.
3. **Where the RM1.2M came out** (value engineering, not scope cut):
   - Disciplined reuse of already-built assets (R.I.S.I.K doctrine complete — 23 sections; PRISM 1.0 live platform base; static KKOM prototype delivered)
   - Controlled AI consumption (quota + consumption governance on metered inference)
   - Leaner team loading against a real delivery baseline (consistent with our adopted 7-FTE operating model)
   - Tightly governed external spend
4. **Present V2 quarterly phasing** as governance credibility: Q1 RM888.75K → Q2 RM993.75K → Q3 RM988.75K → Q4 RM928.75K — front-loaded architecture, back-loaded deployment/assurance/training.
5. **Close on acceptance:** cost structure remains an analytical planning model with stage-gate acceptance checkpoints — MCMC controls commitment pacing via G1/G2.
