## Draft Notes (for DAF review — not part of email)

### Strategy Behind This Email

1. **Reframing:** Shifts the conversation from "approve RM50K for a booth" to "are we aligned on what CyberDSA represents strategically?" The RM50K becomes a consequence of alignment, not the headline.

2. **Farul's role:** Positioned as technical validator. The "national-grade" claim needs CTO credibility. This gives Farul a meaningful stake beyond just approving a budget — he's being asked to validate the technology narrative.

3. **Kenny's role:** Positioned as operational authority. His COO direction creates organisational alignment. Not asking him to decide — asking him to signal.

4. **Soft ask:** "Not a decision today. A conversation." This removes pressure while creating a 30-min window where DAF can present the positioning document live and handle objections in real-time.

5. **Tone:** Professional, warm, respectful of both their authorities. Doesn't presume alignment — invites it. Doesn't command — requests.

6. **CC list:** Keeps the operational team (Hadri, Amelia, Fuad, Azza) informed without making them the audience. They see that leadership alignment is being sought.

### Why This Works

- The existing war-room email (CONV-20260815-004) was the hard activation — directives, deadlines, criteria. This is the soft counterpart — meaning, alignment, shared stake.
- Farul has been CC'd on multiple threads but hasn't been directly engaged as a decision-maker. This changes that.
- Kenny has been engaged on GTM/sales matters but not yet on CyberDSA specifically. This brings him in at the right altitude.
- The "national-grade, full-stack sovereign technology deployment" framing connects CyberDSA to the broader MTAI/Aras strategic narrative — it's bigger than one event.
