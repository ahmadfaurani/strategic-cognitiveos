# RSK-20260815-003

**Risk:** Development freeze may delay product improvements needed for CyberDSA demo readiness.

**Context:** DAF called a development freeze (CONV-20260811-001, CONV-20260815-003). All product feedback and improvement suggestions to be rolled into Product Roadmap Document for structured devops team. However, some CSM feedback may contain launch-critical items needed before CyberDSA.

**Mitigation:** DAF's feedback classification framework (ACT-20260815-008) distinguishes:
1. Launch-critical requirements — must be addressed before CyberDSA
2. GTM enablement requirements — needed for sales effectiveness
3. Post-launch roadmap items — future enhancements

Only Category 1 items break the freeze. Classification must be completed by CP2 (Sep 5).
