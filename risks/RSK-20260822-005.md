# RSK-20260822-005 — MQL Channel Attribution Gap

## Risk

The MQL framework (DEC-20260822-004) defines marketing as the owner of MQL generation. However, the co-branding decision (DEC-20260821-006) means CSM × Aras joint activities may generate meeting requests through CSM channels. The MQL framework does not yet have an attribution rule for CSM-sourced MQLs.

## Impact

If channel attribution is undefined:
- Marketing may dispute ownership of CSM-sourced MQLs ("those aren't ours")
- CSM-sourced MQLs may not be counted in marketing's KPI
- Double-counting or under-counting of total MQLs
- Finger-pointing if MQL targets are missed

## Probability

Medium — CSM co-branding is active (DEC-20260821-006), CSM events are planned. The question will arise as soon as a CSM channel generates a meeting request.

## Mitigation

**Suggested attribution rule (to confirm before Tuesday):**
- Marketing owns marketing-channel MQLs
- Partnership/BD owns CSM-channel MQLs
- Both roll up to total MQL count
- Each channel has its own cost-per-MQL sub-metric

This must be resolved before Tuesday's GTM alignment meeting.
