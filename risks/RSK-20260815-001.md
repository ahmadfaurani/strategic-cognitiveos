# Risk Description

There is currently no single designated point of contact for conveying information, updates, and follow-up matters between CSM and Aras Integrasi. This creates coordination friction, slower response times, and risk of information falling through cracks. Communication currently flows through multiple ad-hoc channels without a centralised coordinator.

# Risk Category

Stakeholder alignment — communication and coordination gap between partner organisations.

# Related Initiative

INIT-20260804-001: CSM × Aras VoronDRQ Joint GTM Activation
INIT-20260813-005: CSM–Aras Joint Operating Model

# Probability

**Medium** — The gap exists now and has been acknowledged by both parties. DAF previously requested CSM to appoint a PIC via email. The risk is partially mitigated by the proposal of Aisha as PIC.

# Impact

**Medium** — Without a PIC: communication delays, missed follow-ups, duplicated effort, and potential for misalignment between the two organisations. Not critical to GTM execution but creates operational drag.

# Risk Assessment

**Medium × Medium = Medium** — Operational friction risk. Not strategically threatening but degrades execution efficiency over time.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Aisha has been proposed as the dedicated communication PIC (STK-20260815-001)
2. CSM higher management meeting expected next week to formally confirm (ACT-20260815-004)
3. Once confirmed, Aisha becomes the centralised channel for all CSM-Aras communication
4. CyberSec team endorsed the proposal based on Aisha's existing working relationship and internal coordination ability

# Mitigation Owner

faurani-jaafar

# Status

**Mitigating** — Proposal made, formal confirmation pending. Risk will be reduced to Low once Aisha is formally appointed.

# Trigger Conditions

- CSM higher management does not confirm Aisha's appointment
- No alternative PIC proposed by CSM
- Communication delays exceeding 72 hours between CSM and Aras on time-sensitive matters
- Multiple parallel communication threads creating confusion about authoritative positions
