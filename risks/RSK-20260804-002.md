# Risk Description

The Senior GovSec resource hiring remains pending. DAF has escalated this from a manpower consideration to a **delivery-readiness requirement**. The scope, stakeholder intensity, and technical complexity of the three CSM engagements exceed what an interim intern can support. Early closure of the pending hiring is necessary to protect execution quality, maintain momentum with CSM, and meet the CyberDSA launch target.

**Update (10 Aug):** This risk has been partially mitigated by Hadri (Lead Architect, STK-20260803-007) absorbing the technical architecture dimension. However, the **product management dimension** remains unstaffed — Hadi (STK-20260810-003, GovSec Product Manager) has not yet been onboarded. RSK-20260810-001 is the specific instantiation of the product management gap.

**Link:** RSK-20260810-001 (Hadi onboarding) is the downstream, specific form of this broader risk. The senior GovSec resource gap is now split: technical = mitigated (Hadri), product management = active (Hadi pending).

# Risk Factors

- **Hiring status unknown:** Current pipeline stage (candidates identified, interviews scheduled, expected start date) is not documented in CognitiveOS
- **Intern is stopgap only:** DAF explicitly states intern deployment is "strictly an interim measure" — insufficient for scope/stakeholder intensity/technical complexity
- **No fallback:** If Senior GovSec resource is not onboarded before CyberDSA preparation intensifies in September, delivery quality is at risk
- **Three parallel tracks:** All three CSM workstreams require senior-level technical engagement, not just documentation support

# Impact

- **If realised:** Execution quality degrades, CSM engagement momentum stalls, CyberDSA launch readiness compromised
- **Stakeholder impact:** CSM perceives Aras as under-resourced — credibility risk in joint partnership

# Mitigation

- Escalate Senior GovSec resource hiring as delivery-readiness requirement — ACT-20260804-015
- Interim intern deployment for non-technical tasks (research, documentation, meeting prep, action tracking) — ACT-20260804-014
- Clearly scope intern tasks to avoid over-reliance on interim measure
- Define target onboarding date aligned to CyberDSA preparation timeline
