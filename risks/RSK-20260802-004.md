# Risk Description

Multiple government stakeholders (CSM, NACSA, JDN, PMO, LHDN) are engaged at different stages. Each expects deliverables, briefings, and follow-through. Without delivery capacity matched to commitment level, relationships degrade when promises aren't kept.

# Risk Category

stakeholder-alignment

# Related Initiative

PRJ-20260725-C001 — Workstream C: Government & Institutional Partnerships

# Probability

Medium — some commitments are still in early stage

# Impact

High — damaged institutional relationships are hard to repair

# Risk Assessment

**High (Probability: Medium × Impact: High)**

Government relationships are multi-year investments. A single missed commitment can close doors permanently.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Track all commitments in CognitiveOS commitment records (COM-)
2. Map commitment level to delivery capacity before making new promises
3. Create engagement records (ENG-) for each stakeholder with current stage
4. Quarterly review of commitment-to-capacity ratio

# Mitigation Owner

faurani-jaafar

# Status

**Active** — CSM collaboration has intensified (3 parallel tracks, DEC-20260810-001) with specific integration sessions scheduled. Stakeholder commitments now have concrete delivery dates and owners. However, the 4 parallel CSM tracks + CyberDSA + ELSA-LHDN + UiTM R.I.S.I.K still exceed available delivery capacity without full delegation.

- Stakeholder follows up on a commitment that has no owner
- Delivery team reports capacity overload
