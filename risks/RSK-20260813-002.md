# Risk

There is currently no dedicated CSM–Aras programme management mechanism for the GTM pipeline leads. The 193-organisation target universe is established and the operating model is proposed (Aras Marketing → MQL → Aras+CSM qualification → discovery → POC → commercial conversion), but without a programme management mechanism, leads risk being generated faster than the joint team can qualify and progress them.

# Risk Description

**Three consequences of this gap:**

1. **Technical duplication** — Teams solve integration issues independently
2. **Commercial leakage** — Good technical collaboration does not automatically become revenue
3. **Stakeholder fatigue** — CSM receives multiple Aras initiatives without a single visible programme structure

# Related Initiative

INIT-20260804-001 — CSM × Aras VoronCitadel Joint GTM Activation

# Probability

**High** — The gap is confirmed; MQL generation is already scoped (193-org database, campaign templates). Without a mechanism, leads will be generated with no joint process to handle them.

# Impact

**High** — Commercial leakage and stakeholder fatigue directly undermine the GTM motion. CSM engagement could stall at the qualification stage.

# Mitigation

ACT-20260813-010 — Establish GTM programme management mechanism (P0, deadline Aug 21).
