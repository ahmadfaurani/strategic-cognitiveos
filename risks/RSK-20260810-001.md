# Risk Statement

Hadi has not been officially onboarded yet as GovSec Product Manager. He is needed to own the product management dimension of CyberDSA launch readiness (INIT-20260810-003), complementing Hadri's technical architecture ownership. If Hadi's onboarding is delayed beyond ~Aug 24, the CyberDSA launch readiness window compresses to ~5 weeks, which may be insufficient for product management onboarding + launch preparation.

Note: Hadri (Lead Architect, STK-20260803-007) is already established at Aras and is NOT the onboarding risk. The handover documentation (ACT-20260810-005) is delivered to Hadri as Lead Architect. Hadi (STK-20260810-003) is the incoming Product Manager whose onboarding is the risk.

# Risk Owner

DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice) — owns the hiring and onboarding decision.

# Affected Initiatives

- INIT-20260810-003 — GovSec CyberDSA Product Launch Readiness (product management dimension)
- INIT-20260804-003 — GovSec × TI Integration (product ownership)
- INIT-20260810-001 — Cybersecurity Score Card Framework (product ownership)
- INIT-20260810-002 — CBOM Agent (product ownership)

# Likelihood

Medium — Hadi has not yet been officially onboarded. No confirmed start date.

# Impact

Critical — if Hadi is not onboarded by late August:
- Product management dimension of CyberDSA launch lacks dedicated owner
- Hadri (Lead Architect) must absorb product management tasks alongside architecture duties
- Commercial positioning, demo narrative, and launch logistics may be underprepared
- Product management ownership gap across all four CSM collaboration tracks

# Mitigation

1. **Prioritise Hadi's onboarding** as a critical path item — escalate to HR/management if needed
2. **Begin handover documentation immediately** (ACT-20260810-005) so it's ready when Hadi arrives
3. **Interim ownership:** Hadri (Lead Architect) covers technical dimension; DAF covers product management dimension until Hadi is onboarded
4. **Remote engagement:** Hadi can begin familiarising with GovSec product context before official onboarding if materials can be shared
5. **Knowledge transfer sessions:** schedule 2-3 sessions in the first week of Hadi's onboarding for product context, launch requirements, and CyberDSA demo scope

# Monitoring

- Weekly check on Hadi's onboarding status during August
- If not onboarded by 2026-08-24, escalate to DAF for direct intervention
- If not onboarded by 2026-08-31, reassess CyberDSA launch feasibility and consider contingency (DAF as acting Product Manager, Hadi as post-launch PM)

# Status
