# Risk Description

The expanded productisation directive (DEC-20260811-001) requires compiling 6 documentation categories across 3 products (18 deliverables total) while the team is concurrently executing GovSec CyberDSA launch readiness (INIT-20260810-003). Ahmad Fuad is the primary delivery owner for both the productisation documentation AND the GovSec handover documentation (ACT-20260810-005, due Aug 24). Hadri is concurrently responsible for the CyberDSA Product Launch Checklist (ACT-20260810-006, due Aug 31) and CSM integration follow-ups (ACT-20260810-001, due Aug 17). The documentation effort may compete with or delay CyberDSA readiness work.

# Risk Category

Resource constraint — delivery capacity contention between parallel high-priority workstreams.

# Related Initiative

INIT-20260811-001 — Cybersecurity Flagship Product Consolidation & Productisation Programme
Also affects: INIT-20260810-003 — GovSec CyberDSA Product Launch Readiness

# Probability

Medium — Fuad has demonstrated capacity for documentation work, but the scope (18 deliverables) is unprecedented. The devsecops intern may provide support but their capability and availability are unconfirmed.

# Impact

High — if CyberDSA readiness is delayed by productisation documentation effort, the October launch milestone (DEC-20260804-001) is at risk. Conversely, if CyberDSA work takes priority, productisation documentation may stall, leaving the other two products (VoronCitadel, ChainSentry) without proper baselines for commercial conversations.

# Risk Assessment

Medium probability × High impact = High priority. This risk compounds existing capacity risks (RSK-20260802-001, RSK-20260802-006) and the Hadi onboarding dependency (RSK-20260810-001).

# Mitigation Strategy

1. **Prioritise GovSec TIP documentation** — overlap with CyberDSA readiness means GovSec documentation serves both initiatives
2. **Leverage devsecops intern** — delegate documentation compilation tasks (backlog, architecture diagrams, deployment requirements) to intern with Fuad/Hadri review
3. **Phase the documentation** — VoronCitadel first (assessed "Ready", may need least work), then GovSec (parallel with CyberDSA), then ChainSentry (least commercially urgent)
4. **Weekly progress tracking** — monitor documentation progress vs CyberDSA readiness during weekly reviews
5. **Scope management** — if capacity is constrained, prioritise Product Governance and Commercialisation Readiness over Sales & GTM Materials (which already partially exist for VoronCitadel)
