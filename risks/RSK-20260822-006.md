# RSK-20260822-006 — MQL Volume Shortfall Risk

## Risk

The conversion model assumes 78 MQLs (meeting requests) from 260 engaged stakeholders. If marketing cannot commit to 78 MQLs — either due to capacity constraints (RSK-20260822-004) or unrealistic conversion assumptions — the entire funnel math breaks.

## Impact

| MQL Commitment | Meetings (30%) | POCs (30%) | Sales (30%) | Revenue |
|---------------|----------------|------------|-------------|---------|
| 78 (target) | 23 | 7 | 7 | RM 1,176,000 |
| 50 | 15 | 4.5 | ~4 | ~RM 672,000 |
| 40 (low) | 12 | 3.5 | ~3 | ~RM 504,000 |
| 25 (worst) | 7.5 | 2.25 | ~2 | ~RM 336,000 |

Below 50 MQLs, the revenue projection no longer justifies the RM 175-295k marketing budget. At 25 MQLs, cost-per-MQL rises to RM 7,000-11,800 — well above the defensible range.

## Probability

Medium — marketing capacity gap (RSK-20260822-004) is already identified. 1,300-contact outreach exceeds current bandwidth (Norshaza flagged Aug 11). If marketing can't scale outreach, MQL volume will fall short.

## Mitigation

1. Have fallback math ready for Tuesday (ACT-20260822-007)
2. If marketing commits below 50 MQLs, recalculate ROI before approving budget
3. Consider splitting MQL generation across channels (marketing + partnership/CSM) to distribute the load
4. If capacity is the constraint, address RSK-20260822-004 first (dedicated FTE for ecosystem build — DAF already flagged this in Layer 1 feedback)
