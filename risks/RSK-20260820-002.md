# Risk Assessment

## Risk Description

The Sayed Amir Muzzakkir corruption trial exposes a pattern of political intermediation in PDRM technology procurement:

1. Political aide (Sayed) brokered access to Home Minister for contractor (Nexuscorpgroup)
2. Minister annotated supporting letter ("Datuk KSU for consideration")
3. Aide demanded ~RM9M cut of RM19M consultancy fee
4. RM350,000 bribe paid via dummy invoice mechanism
5. Contractor won RM381M PDRM RMPNet tender

This pattern poses reputational and operational risk to any Aras/CSM engagement with PDRM or law-enforcement technology procurement:

- **Reputational risk:** Association with PDRM procurement environment where political intermediation occurred
- **Operational risk:** Encountering similar intermediation expectations in Aras/CSM product positioning
- **Strategic risk:** Law-enforcement-enablement workstream may need integrity due diligence framework

## Risk Assessment

- **Probability:** Medium — pattern exposed but not necessarily systemic
- **Impact:** Medium — reputational and operational, not existential
- **Trend:** Unknown — depends on trial revelations and MACC enforcement trajectory
