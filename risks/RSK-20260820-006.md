# Risk Description

The address-security integration is a typed stub on the development trunk: both of its entry points return no result unconditionally. The deployed instance still carries the working implementation.

This means:
- Deploying the trunk unchanged would introduce a regression in the address-security screening layer
- Combined with the already-degraded live screening layer (rejected credential), deploying the trunk unchanged would leave 2 of 4 screening layers contributing nothing
- The release manifest must record this explicitly and either restore the integration or restate the screening claim

## Risk Category

Technical debt + regression risk — the trunk has diverged from the deployment on a critical screening function.

## Probability & Impact

- **Probability:** High — if the trunk is deployed without addressing the stub, the regression is automatic
- **Impact:** High — 2 of 4 screening layers would be non-functional, materially reducing sanctions/risk screening depth

## Mitigation Strategy

**Phase 0, Deliverable 0.2 (M2 on critical path):**
1. Restore the address-security integration on the trunk, OR
2. Record the reduced screening in the manifest and restate the screening claim in the specification

Either path is acceptable. What is NOT acceptable is deploying without the decision.

**Sequencing:** M2 must precede M3 (manifest) — the manifest cannot describe the release honestly until the screening question is answered.

## Trigger Conditions

- Trunk deployed without addressing the address-security stub
- Screening depth reduced without being recorded in manifest
- Claims about 4-layer screening depth made without 2 layers actually functioning
