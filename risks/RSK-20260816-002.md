# Risk: "Commercially Viable Product" Claim Unsupported

## Risk Description

DAF's soft activation email (CONV-20260816-002) includes the parenthetical claim: "A proper tech company capable of operationalizing a commercially viable product."

Current product readiness state (per product-readiness-index.md):
- VoronCitadel: Production-deployed, moving toward commercial-ready
- VoronCitadel: Pilot stage
- GovSec TIP: Prototype (v3.0 spec)
- chain:SENTRY: Prototype with 6 critical gaps

RSK-20260810-003 (likelihood: High, impact: High) explicitly identifies: "No pricing, packaging, or post-demo conversion path."

No product currently has a proven commercial model.

## Impact

If Farul, Kenny, or any recipient questions "which product is commercially viable?", the honest answer contradicts the email's claim. This creates credibility risk for the positioning narrative.

## Mitigation Strategy

1. **Prepare talking points** for if/when questioned:
   - VoronCitadel is production-deployed and in active GTM with CSM (strongest proof point)
   - Other products on trajectory toward CyberDSA demo readiness
   - "Commercially viable" is the destination, not the current state — the email's "increasingly capable of delivering" framing supports this

2. **Future communications:** Consider softening to "capable of operationalizing commercially viable products" (trajectory) rather than "a commercially viable product" (current state)

3. **Connect to RSK-20260810-003:** The commercial readiness gap risk already tracks this. Ensure the mitigation plan for RSK-20260810-003 is progressing.

## Probability × Impact

- **Likelihood: High** — Farul in particular may probe this claim given his technical authority role
- **Impact: Medium** — credibility damage is recoverable with honest framing, but weakens the positioning
