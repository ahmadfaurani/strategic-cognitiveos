# RSK-20260822-004 — Marketing Capacity Gap

## Risk

Norshaza's Aug 11 email explicitly raised capacity concerns: "Our main consideration at this stage is manpower and capacity to manage the outreach and subsequent follow-ups across such a large target audience" (1,300 contacts across 193 organisations).

## Impact

If Marketing cannot execute at 1,300-contact scale, the entire GTM outreach model degrades. DAF's conversion model (260 engaged → 78 meetings → 23 POCs) requires the full stakeholder database as input. Reduced outreach = reduced pipeline.

## Probability

High — Norshaza flagged this concern unprompted. Marketing has not confirmed they can resource it. No resourcing plan has been agreed.

## Mitigation

- Phase the outreach: start with 52 Tier 2 orgs (DAF's original proposal), not all 193
- Assign a dedicated FTE for outreach coordination (connects to ACT-20260822-006)
- Use HubSpot or CRM automation for contact management and tracking
- Weekly MQL review cadence (DAF proposed) to catch throughput issues early
- Consider outsourcing email outreach to a specialist firm if internal capacity insufficient
