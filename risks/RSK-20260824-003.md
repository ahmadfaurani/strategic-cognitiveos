## Risk Category

**Operational** — Single-point-of-failure on DAF for POC execution.

- **Probability: High** — DAF is already carrying all 4 roles; the pattern is active, not potential

1. TBH-001 (PM) hiring in progress — will absorb ACT-004 (project coordination)
2. ACT-20260824-006: HoE to be escalated to TBH Registry by Aug 28 — will absorb engineering leadership
3. Weekly Cognitive Loop Review monitors DAF capacity concentration
4. This risk record provides audit trail for the pattern
