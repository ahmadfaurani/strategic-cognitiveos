# Risk Description

The GovSec × CMERP engineering document closure requires a sequential gate chain:

1. **Comments closed** (ACT-20260826-004, Fuad) — technical foundation
2. **Internal sign-off** (ACT-20260826-006, Tuan Fatah) — internal validation
3. **CSM sign-off** (ACT-20260826-007, Zaharudin) — external validation
4. **Baseline confirmation** (ACT-20260826-008, Hadri) — exit gate

Each gate depends on the prior. Hadri's Aug 13 email targeted Aug 28 completion — that date has arrived without confirmed closure. DAF's Aug 26 T-40 directive introduces T-minus accountability but the actual ETA has not yet been committed by Hadri + Fuad.

Risk factors:
- Outstanding engineering comments may be more complex than anticipated
- Tuan Fatah has not yet been engaged (newly identified stakeholder)
- Zaharudin sign-off depends on CSM responsiveness (external dependency)
- Single coordination owner (Hadri) — continuity risk
- Hadri's Aug 28 target was self-imposed before T-minus anchoring — may slip further

## Risk Category

timing — sequential gate chain with external dependency and no committed T-minus ETA yet

## Probability & Impact

Probability: medium | Impact: high

## Mitigation Strategy

1. T-minus anchoring (DEC-20260826-002) — creates timeline discipline
2. Hadri owns coordination with explicit escalation mandate — blockers flagged immediately
3. DAF requesting consolidated ETA from Hadri + Fuad — shared accountability
4. Sequential gate visibility — makes blockers visible early
5. Escalation date Aug 28 (T-38) — if no commitment received, DAF escalates
