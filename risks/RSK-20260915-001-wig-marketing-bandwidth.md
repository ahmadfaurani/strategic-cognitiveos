# RSK-20260915-001 — WIG Marketing Bandwidth Constraint

## Risk Description

WIG marketing/comms (Said, Norshaza, Fitrie, Azza's office) carries the VoronCitadel GTM execution load while other Wong International subsidiaries ramp their own GTM/comms programmes in the same window. CMO flags manpower shortfall; interns proposed as the relief mechanism.

## Impact

- **Medium** — execution dilution risk: late/thin Wave 1 assets, slipping MQL handover cadence, degraded CyberDSA event readiness; no single point of failure (plan phases and functional owners provide redundancy), but cumulative slippage compounds against a fixed Oct 5–7 event date.

## Probability

- **Medium** — CMO herself raised it on approval day; other-subsidiary demand is confirmed real, not hypothetical; intern supply/supervision not yet arranged.

## Mitigation Strategy

Quantify and resource the intern request; bind coordination load to the focal-point role; sequence Wave 1 starts against real bandwidth; route intern onboarding through Fitrie's comms plan where supervision is native.

## Trigger Conditions

Wave 1 asset slippage; MQL handover misses; comms-plan deliverables late; Azza re-raises with specifics; CyberDSA items competing for the same people.
