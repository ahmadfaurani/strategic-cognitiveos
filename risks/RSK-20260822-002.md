# RSK-20260822-002 — Vendor-Detection Risk

## Risk Statement

Wan Roshaimi's career background (BAE Detica, PwC) means he will apply vendor-detection instinct to the CSM × Aras co-branding proposition. He will test whether 'sovereign' means sovereign or means 'dependent on Aras instead of foreign vendor.' If the evidence package overclaims or conflates future-state with present-state, credibility collapses.

## Mitigation

1. Disciplined technical transparency — not stronger branding
2. Evidence package must demonstrate: local engineering capability, architectural ownership, data/interface clarity, transparent dependencies, implementation state, technical control, honest roadmap boundaries
3. Deliberate exclusion of SiberSUITE, CBOM Agent, Cybersecurity Score Card from present-state claims (per DEC-20260822-001)
4. Integration State Matrix as central control mechanism — allows Wan Roshaimi to immediately determine what exists, what is proven, what is unresolved, what is future-only

## Risk Rating

- **Probability:** High — Wan Roshaimi's background makes this near-certain
- **Impact:** Critical — credibility collapse would terminate co-branding opportunity
- **Mitigation status:** Active — DEC-20260822-001 governing principle + 8-artifact evidence package + 9-state classification system

## Relationship to RSK-20260822-001

RSK-20260822-001 covers the operational follow-through gap (ACT-20260810-001 overdue). RSK-20260822-002 covers the stakeholder perception risk. Both must be mitigated for Gate 4 survival.
