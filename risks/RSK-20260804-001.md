# Risk Description

Three parallel CSM workstreams (VoronDRQ GTM, CMERP Integration, TI Integration) are progressing simultaneously with compressed and overlapping timelines. Delivery ownership is concentrated on a single Solutions Architect (Hadri) for WS2 and WS3, and a single strategic owner (DAF) across all three. The pressure on delivery capacity, calendar, and technical resources is becoming increasingly visible.

# Risk Factors

- **Single delivery owner:** Hadri is the delivery owner for both WS2 (CMERP, Aug 12/13) and WS3 (TI, Aug 6/7/10) — any unavailability creates immediate cascading delays
- **Single strategic owner:** DAF carries strategic load across all three workstreams plus Director-level responsibilities
- **Overlapping timelines:** WS3 sessions (Aug 6/7/10) may overlap with preparation for WS2 sessions (Aug 12/13)
- **Senior GovSec resource pending:** No senior technical resource to share delivery load
- **Intern is interim only:** Scope, stakeholder intensity, and technical complexity exceed intern-level capacity

# Impact

- **If realised:** Delayed technical scoping, reduced preparation quality, stakeholder engagement gaps, missed milestones
- **Downstream effect:** CyberDSA October launch readiness compromised — product readiness, demonstration quality, launch credibility

# Mitigation

- Deploy intern for interim support (research, documentation, meeting prep, action tracking) — ACT-20260804-014
- Create consolidated engagement calendar to identify and resolve scheduling conflicts — ACT-20260804-012
- Create integrated technical delivery plan for WS2 + WS3 to sequence dependencies — ACT-20260804-013
- Onboard Senior GovSec resource as soon as possible — ACT-20260804-015
- Early escalation of technical dependencies and delivery risks
