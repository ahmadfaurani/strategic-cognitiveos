# Risk Description

Strategic positioning and stakeholder narratives describe capabilities at a maturity level that exceeds actual product state. GovSec TIP is pilot-ready but most other products (ChainSentry, VoronScout, LE-UIP, SEC-AF) remain at concept stage. External commitments made against immature products risk credibility damage.

# Risk Category

product-maturity

# Related Initiative

PRJ-20260725-B001 — Workstream B: Cybersecurity Productisation

# Probability

High — gap between narrative and reality currently exists

# Impact

High — stakeholder credibility erosion if products fail to deliver on promised timelines

# Risk Assessment

**High (Probability: High × Impact: High)**

The readiness gate framework exists in CognitiveOS but is not yet enforced. Products are being positioned before passing their readiness gates.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Enforce readiness gate criteria — no external commitment beyond current gate level
2. Define explicit criteria for each gate: concept → proposition → pilot-ready → delivery-ready → scaled
3. Audit current stakeholder commitments against product readiness
4. Align narrative with actual state — "concept" products described as exploratory, not deliverable

# Mitigation Owner

faurani-jaafar

# Status

**Mitigating** — Development freeze (DEC-20260810-002) directly addresses this: GovSec TIP is now being hardened from prototype toward demo-ready rather than accumulating new features. Product readiness index enforces gating (prototype = no external commitments beyond demonstration). GovSec TIP v3.0 has comprehensive features but needs hardening cycle.

However, the narrative-vs-reality gap remains for other products (ChainSentry, VoronScout, LE-UIP, SEC-AF still at concept/pending). Risk is mitigated for GovSec specifically but unaddressed across the broader portfolio.

**Related mitigations:**
- DEC-20260810-002 — Development freeze forces hardening over features
- INIT-20260810-003 — CyberDSA Product Launch Readiness (prototype → demo-ready)
- Product readiness index now maintained with gate enforcement

# Trigger Conditions

- Stakeholder requests demo or pilot of a concept-stage product
- External timeline commitments made without delivery owner confirmation
- Product fails during stakeholder evaluation
