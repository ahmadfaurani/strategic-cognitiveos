# Risk Statement

CyberDSA October 2026 is the joint product launch event with CSM. GovSec TIP will be demonstrated to government, CNII sector, and cybersecurity stakeholders. However, there is no defined:
- Pricing model (licensing, subscription, per-seat, enterprise)
- Product packaging (what's included in the GovSec offering)
- Post-demo conversion pathway (how demo interest converts to paid pilots or contracts)
- Commercial proposition for the CSM-Aras joint offering (revenue sharing, white-label, integration fee)

Without these, CyberDSA generates visibility but not pipeline. The launch becomes a technical demo without commercial follow-through, repeating the pattern identified in RSK-20260802-007 (strategic documents not converted to funded execution).

# Risk Owner

DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice) — owns commercial strategy for GovSec TIP.

# Affected Initiatives

- INIT-20260810-003 — GovSec CyberDSA Product Launch Readiness (commercial dimension unaddressed)
- INIT-20260725-006 — Workstream E: Commercial & Market Development (parent)

# Likelihood

High — no commercial workstream is active for GovSec TIP specifically. The 5-priority readiness framework (DEC-20260810-002) covers technical readiness but not commercial readiness.

# Impact

High — CyberDSA generates marketing value but no qualified pipeline. Stakeholders who see the demo have no path to engage commercially. CSM joint offering lacks a commercial framework. Post-event momentum is lost.

# Mitigation

1. **Define GovSec TIP commercial model** — pricing, packaging, and licensing structure before CyberDSA
2. **Create post-demo conversion pathway** — demo → EOI → pilot scope → commercial pilot → contract
3. **Develop CSM joint commercial proposition** — revenue sharing model, integration pricing, white-label options
4. **Prepare commercial collateral** — one-pager, pricing guide, pilot proposal template
5. **Assign commercial owner** — DAF or Hadi (upon onboarding) should own the commercial dimension

# Monitoring

- Commercial model definition progress (weekly check during Sept)
- CSM joint proposition discussions
- Post-CyberDSA pipeline conversion tracking

# Status

Active — risk identified during risk register review. The 5-priority readiness framework (DEC-20260810-002) covers technical readiness but not commercial. This is a gap in the launch preparation scope.
