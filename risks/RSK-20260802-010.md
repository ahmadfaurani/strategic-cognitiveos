# Risk Description

Multi-institutional collaborations (CSM, NACSA, JDN, PMO, universities) often have ambiguous decision rights. Who can commit what? Who owns which deliverable? Without explicit authority matrices, partnerships drift into deadlock or uncoordinated action.

# Risk Category

stakeholder-alignment

# Related Initiative

PRJ-20260725-C001 — Workstream C: Government & Institutional Partnerships

# Probability

Medium — some partnerships have clear authority, others don't

# Impact

Medium — delays and friction, not total failure

# Risk Assessment

**Medium (Probability: Medium × Impact: Medium)**

Not catastrophic but creates friction and slow execution in partnerships.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Create engagement records (ENG-) for each partnership with explicit authority matrix
2. Define decision rights at partnership formation, not during crisis
3. Use CognitiveOS commitment records to track who committed to what
4. Regular partnership review — confirm authority is still clear
