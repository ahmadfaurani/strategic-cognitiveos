ELSA's draft NDA was prepared from their perspective. Hafeez specifically asked DAF to review if there is "any missing or misleading" language in Aras's scope. If the NDA does not fully capture the breadth of Aras's intended technology scope — AI platform, data integration, risk-scoring engine, security controls, governance framework, auditability — it could constrain Aras's role or create ambiguity in the collaboration.

**High priority** — Medium probability × High impact. Must be resolved during MTAI's legal review before NDA execution.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. MTAI (Samantha Lai) to specifically review NDA scope language for completeness of Aras technology stack
2. Ensure NDA explicitly lists: AI platform, data integration, risk-scoring capabilities, security controls, governance framework, auditability, and technical delivery
3. Flag any language that could be interpreted as limiting Aras to a subcomponent rather than the full technology stack
4. Compare NDA scope against the role definitions in DAF's 23 July email and Hafeez's 24 July confirmation
