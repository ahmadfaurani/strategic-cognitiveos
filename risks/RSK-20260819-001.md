# RSK-20260819-001 — Personality-Dependent CSM Relationship Risk

## Risk Description

The CSM × Aras relationship risks becoming **personality-dependent** if established CSM contacts (specifically Wan Roshaimi — STK-20260812-001, and Zaharudin — STK-20260804-011) are automatically aligned as POC-support resources merely because they are known contacts, rather than because their roles match defined requirements.

## Impact

- **High** — If the relationship becomes personality-dependent:
  - Personnel changes or role reassignments at CSM would jeopardise engagement continuity
  - Relationship strains with specific individuals would cascade into workstream disruption
  - The partnership loses institutional resilience and becomes fragile
  - Misallocation of individuals to roles that don't match their actual function

## Probability

- **Medium** — The tendency to default to known contacts is natural, especially under time pressure (CyberDSA October deadline). Without an explicit guardrail, the path of least resistance is to assign known people to tasks.

## Mitigation

Per DEC-20260819-004:

1. **Technical participation activated only after requirement definition** — define what is needed first, then identify who at CSM should own it
2. **Appropriate CSM owner identification** — let Azrul (Partnership & Alliance Anchor) help identify the correct CSM stakeholder through institutional navigation, rather than defaulting to known contacts
3. **No auto-alignment** — Wan Roshaimi and Zaharudin are established contacts but should not be automatically assigned POC-support roles
4. **Institutional pathway over personality** — the relationship should flow through institutional channels, not individual relationships
