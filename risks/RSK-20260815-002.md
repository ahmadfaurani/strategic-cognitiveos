# Risk Record

## Risk Description

The RM5.0M R.I.S.I.K AI Platform programme cost structure is prepared but MCMC has not been engaged. Multiple risk dimensions:

### 1. Pricing Sensitivity
- RM5.0M may exceed MCMC's expected budget range for a single AI platform initiative
- MCMC may not have experience evaluating AI platform investments of this scale
- The "investment in capability, not software development fee" framing requires careful positioning

### 2. Budget Cycle Alignment
- MCMC's fiscal year budget planning cycle is unknown for this programme
- Government procurement cycles may require 3-6 months for approval
- If MCMC 2027 budget is already locked, the programme may face delays until 2028 cycle

### 3. MCMC Appetite Uncertainty
- MCMC has not been engaged on R.I.S.I.K specifically
- MCMC's existing digital content / misinformation programmes may already have vendors
- MCMC may prefer a different approach (in-house, different partner, smaller scope)

### 4. Referral Pathway Dependency
- No direct MCMC relationship established
- Engagement likely requires PMO referral (Khairil Hilmi, Dr. Noor Dasrafeezal) or UiTM introduction
- Cold approach to MCMC chairman may not be effective

## Impact Assessment

**If MCMC does not approve funding:**
- R.I.S.I.K programme remains at doctrine/plan stage without execution pathway
- UiTM collaboration (DEC-20260807-001) loses its operational vehicle
- 12-month timeline slips
- Alternative funder must be identified (PMO direct? Different ministry?)

**If MCMC approves at reduced scope:**
- Programme may need to be phased (e.g., RM2.5M for Phase 1: Framework + IP + core AI engineering)
- Team size may need reduction
- Timeline may extend beyond 12 months

## Probability Assessment

**MEDIUM** — MCMC's regulatory mandate aligns well with R.I.S.I.K's capabilities, but:
- No prior engagement on this specific programme
- RM5M is a significant commitment for a regulatory body
- Government budget cycles are unpredictable

## Mitigation Strategies

1. **Stage-gate approach:** Offer MCMC the G1 (End Month 2) checkpoint as a low-risk entry point — fund mobilisation first, evaluate before committing full RM5M
2. **PMO referral:** Leverage PMO officials CC'd on UiTM collaboration for warm introduction to MCMC
3. **UiTM co-signature:** Academic partnership validation reduces perceived risk
4. **Phased proposal option:** Prepare a RM2.5M Phase 1 variant (Framework + IP + core AI engineering) as fallback
5. **Value benchmarking:** Prepare comparison against comparable government AI platform investments

## Monitoring Triggers

- MCMC proposal delivered → track response timeline
- MCMC requests scope reduction → activate phased proposal option
- MCMC budget cycle constraint identified → adjust timeline
- PMO referral pathway blocked → identify alternative engagement route
