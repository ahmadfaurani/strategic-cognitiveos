# RSK-20260822-003 — GTM Conversion Timeline Compression

## Risk

The GTM deck's own sequencing acknowledges that Layers 1 (Credibility) and 2 (Content) "will look quiet until October and that is expected." This leaves only November-December (2 months) for Layer 3 (Events & Conversion) to produce 3 pilot conversations.

## Impact

If Layers 1-2 don't build sufficient awareness by October, Layer 3 events (Executive Roundtable, workshops) won't attract qualified decision-makers. The conversion window compresses to <2 months.

## Probability

Medium — the timeline is realistic IF all dependencies (CSM LOI, content production, PR) start on time in August. Any slip in August foundation activities pushes the quiet period into October, compressing conversion to 1 month.

## Mitigation

- Start Layer 1 and Layer 2 activities in parallel (not sequential) from August
- Use DAF's existing 11k+ LinkedIn reach to jump-start thought leadership without waiting for paid campaign
- Pre-book Executive Roundtable invitations in September (before October awareness threshold)
- Consider moving Executive Roundtable to late October instead of November
