Three strategic tracks converge on the week of August 24–25, 2026:

1. **VoronCitadel POC document** — ACT-20260820-004, due Aug 24 10am MYT. 8 sections. DAF owns 5 (1, 4, 6, 7, 8), Hadri owns 3 (2, 3, 5). Externally committed — Azrul/CSM expecting Monday deep dive. CRITICAL.

2. **chain:SENTRY Phase 0** — ACT-20260820-010/011/012. 5-day sequential window. Credential rotation (M1) is the first step. Security owner not yet assigned. Critical path M1→M7, each step blocks the next.

3. **CSM working group sync-up** — ACT-20260820-003. Week of Aug 25. Amelia coordinating. First focused operational engagement post-MOU.

All three require DAF's direct involvement. HoE not yet hired (RSK-20260820-003) — no engineering delegation available. Hadri is chain:SENTRY product owner but also needed for VoronCitadel POC sections (2, 3, 5).

## Risk Category

Timing + resource contention — three tracks, one DAF, one Hadri, no HoE.

## Probability & Impact

- **Probability:** High — the convergence is already scheduled, not hypothetical
- **Impact:** Medium — no individual track fails, but quality may degrade under simultaneous pressure. Worst case: POC document rushed, Phase 0 delayed, CSM sync-up postponed.

## Mitigation Strategy

**Sequencing the week:**
1. Aug 21–24: VoronCitadel POC document takes priority. DAF focuses on 5 sections. Hadri delivers 3 sections by Aug 23.
2. Aug 25: chain:SENTRY Phase 0 Day 1 starts (credential rotation — M1). This is operational, not analytical — can be delegated to Farul as security owner.
3. Aug 26–27: Phase 0 continues (M2 regression decision, M3 manifest). Hadri owns M2.
4. Aug 27–29: CSM sync-up. Late in the week, after Phase 0 M1–M3 are progressing.

**Alternative:** Delegate Phase 0 Day 1 (credential rotation) to Farul as security owner if DAF capacity is constrained by POC document prep.
