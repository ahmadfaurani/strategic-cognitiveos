# No Head of Engineering — Blocks VoronCitadel POC Scaling

## Risk Description

No Head of Engineering in place as of 2026-08-20. Current team:
- **DAF** — Director, strategic owner, not hands-on engineering
- **Hadri** — Solutions Architect, fully consumed by 4 CSM tracks, ~0 FTE for VoronCitadel
- **Fuad** — Practice CTO, ~0.3 FTE allocable to VoronCitadel (architecture guidance, code review)

Actual hands-on engineering capacity: ~0.3 FTE.

## Impact

- Cannot onboard POC customers — no one to script Teras deployment, configure VoronCitadel instances, or handle technical onboarding
- Cannot build remaining application-layer dependencies (UI/UX, connectors, RAG config)
- Cannot provide technical face for CSM technical sessions or Wan Roshaimi's Gate 4 review (Aug 31)
- CyberDSA presentation (6 weeks) has no technical authority to validate architecture

## Probability

**High** — Without HoE, POC Mode cannot activate. This is not a risk that might happen — it is a current blocker.

## Mitigation

Hire Head of Engineering immediately (ACT-20260820-007, RM 18,888/mo).

Alternative: Evaluate Hadri for HoE role (RSK-20260820-004 for dual-role risk).
