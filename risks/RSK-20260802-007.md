# Risk Description

DAF produces high-quality strategic documents, frameworks, and propositions. These are effective for alignment and stakeholder engagement. However, the conversion from strategic document to funded pilot to contract is not systematic. Documents without conversion pathways are intellectual exercise, not commercial execution.

# Risk Category

commercial-viability

# Related Initiative

PRJ-20260725-E001 — Workstream E: Commercial & Market Development

# Probability

Medium — some conversions happening but not systematic

# Impact

High — strategic value not realised commercially

# Risk Assessment

**High (Probability: Medium × Impact: High)**

The gap between strategy and revenue is the commercial viability risk. Strong propositions must have defined conversion targets.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Define conversion targets for every strategic proposition: engagement → pilot | pilot → contract
2. Track conversion metrics in CognitiveOS
3. Create commercial pipeline discipline — monthly review
4. Attach budget/revenue estimates to each flagship initiative
5. Assign commercial ownership — who owns the "ask"

# Mitigation Owner

faurani-jaafar

# Status

**Active** — CyberDSA October 2026 is the first concrete commercial milestone for GovSec TIP. The joint CSM-Aras launch creates a forcing function for commercial conversion. However, no pricing model, packaging, or post-demo commercial pathway has been defined yet. Strategic documents remain unconverted to funded execution.

- Pilot completed without contract proposal
- Stakeholder engagement has no next commercial step
