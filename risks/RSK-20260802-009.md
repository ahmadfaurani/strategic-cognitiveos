# Risk Description

Switching between 6 workstream clusters (sovereign AI, cybersecurity, government partnerships, political intelligence, commercial, organisational capability) within a single day or week imposes cognitive cost. Decision quality degrades with context switching. Strategic thinking requires sustained attention on one problem space.

# Risk Category

resource-constraint

# Related Initiative

PRJ-20260725-F001 — Workstream F: Organisational Capability Building

# Probability

High — inherent in current working pattern

# Impact

Medium — quality degradation, not total failure

# Risk Assessment

**Medium (Probability: High × Impact: Medium)**

Chronic, not acute. Degrades quality of strategic thinking over time.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Block focus days — one workstream cluster per day where possible
2. Protect 2 hours/day for strategic thinking on single topic
3. Batch stakeholder meetings by workstream cluster
4. Use CognitiveOS to offload context — don't hold it all in head
5. Weekly review to plan the week's focus areas, not daily reactive switching

# Mitigation Owner

faurani-jaafar

# Status

**Mitigating** — Development freeze (DEC-20260810-002) reduces active cognitive switching by narrowing focus to 5 priorities. Hadri absorbing technical architecture reduces DAF's technical context-switching. CognitiveOS governance reduces administrative overhead. However, DAF still spans 6 workstream clusters and multiple stakeholder ecosystems.

**Related mitigations:**

- Strategic documents show signs of rushed thinking
