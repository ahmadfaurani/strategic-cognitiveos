# Risk Description

DAF is simultaneously driving 6 workstream clusters (A–F) comprising 20+ individual initiatives. No single individual can maintain strategic quality across all of these concurrently. The principal development challenge identified in his professional assessment is the transition from primary driver to architect of institution. Until delegation matures, execution capacity is structurally overloaded.

# Risk Category

delivery-capacity

# Related Initiative

# Probability

High — current state, already occurring

# Impact

High — quality degradation, missed deadlines, stakeholder fatigue, burnout

# Risk Assessment

**Critical (Probability: High × Impact: High)**

This is the #1 strategic risk. Every other risk on this register is compounded by this one. If DAF's execution bandwidth breaks, all workstreams suffer simultaneously.

# Mitigation Strategy

1. Apply portfolio tier framework strictly — only Tier 1 flagship initiatives get DAF's direct attention
2. Assign delivery owners for each workstream cluster (currently all `unassigned`)
3. Implement weekly executive review to surface capacity constraints early
4. Defer or watch-list Tier 3 initiatives — no active effort
5. Protect focused strategy time — block 2 hours/day for strategic thinking, not operational response
