# Hadri HoE Dual-Role Risk

## Risk Description

Hadri is under evaluation for Head of Engineering role. If promoted:
- **Salary saving:** RM 5,000/mo (RM 60K/year) — Hadri costs RM 13,888 vs external HoE at RM 18,888
- **CSM track loss:** Hadri's 4 CSM tracks lose their delivery owner
- **Dual-role risk:** HoE + CSM delivery = two full-time jobs
- **Sustainability:** Not sustainable past Month 3-4 without Senior GovSec hire

## Impact

- **If Senior GovSec hired within 90 days:** Smooth transition, CSM tracks maintained, RM 60K saving realized
- **If Senior GovSec not hired by Day 90:** Both roles suffer — HoE work degrades from split attention, CSM tracks degrade from neglect
- **Worst case:** CSM partnership relationship damaged, which is the commercial foundation for VoronCitadel

## Probability

**Medium** — Depends on (1) DAF's decision to promote Hadri, (2) ability to hire Senior GovSec within 90 days

## Mitigation

1. If Hadri promoted: **hard 90-day deadline** to hire Senior GovSec for CSM track handoff
2. If Senior GovSec not onboarded by Day 90: escalate to DAF for interim coverage
3. Monitor Hadri's dual-role capacity monthly

## Assessment [T3-ASSESSMENT]

Hadri as HoE with a hard 90-day deadline to hire Senior GovSec. The RM 60K saving is real but the execution risk is high. Decision authority: DAF — pending Hadri cognitive profile suitability review.
