# Risk Description

The joint product launch with CSM at CyberDSA in early October 2026 is a firm delivery milestone. Workstreams 2 (CMERP integration) and 3 (TI integration) are essential to achieving GovSec product-market readiness. Any delay in technical scoping, validation, or dependency resolution during August could directly affect product readiness, demonstration quality, and launch credibility at CyberDSA.

# Risk Factors

- **Compressed August window:** WS3 sessions as early as Aug 6 (2 days from mobilisation email), WS2 sessions Aug 12/13 — August is the critical scoping month
- **Dependency chain:** Technical scoping (August) → Integration validation (September) → Demo preparation (late September) → CyberDSA launch (early October)
- **CSM response latency:** Historical pattern of CSM response delays (training confirmation delays, event calendar unanswered since Jun 24)
- **Resource gaps:** Senior GovSec resource pending, intern is interim only — insufficient technical depth for accelerated delivery
- **Dual integration complexity:** WS2 (platform) and WS3 (intelligence) may have interdependencies that compound delay risk

# Impact

- **If realised:** Joint product launch at CyberDSA is delayed, downsized, or presented at insufficient quality
- **Strategic impact:** Loss of launch credibility with CSM, reduced market visibility for GovSec TIP, weakened Aras-CSM partnership positioning
- **Commercial impact:** Delayed pipeline conversion, missed Q4 commercial window
