## Account Selection Framework

### Scoring Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Strategic Value** | 30% | Alignment with CSM mission, national security impact |
| **Readiness** | 25% | Existing CSM relationship, expressed interest, budget availability |
| **POC Feasibility** | 20% | Technical fit, access requirements, timeline |
| **Revenue Potential** | 15% | Deal size, expansion potential, recurring revenue |
| **Reference Value** | 10% | Case study potential, industry influence |

**Scoring:** 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent)  
**Weighted Score:** Sum of (Score × Weight) for all criteria

### Organizational Profile

**Mission:**
> "To coordinate and strengthen Malaysia's national cyber defence capabilities, protect critical national information infrastructure, and ensure cyber sovereignty."

**Structure:**
```
National Cyber Agency
├── National Cyber Defence Centre (NCDC)
├── Critical National Information Infrastructure (NCII) Division
├── Cyber Threat Intelligence Division
├── Incident Response Division
├── Policy & Compliance Division
└── Public-Private Partnership Division
```

**Key Stakeholders:**
| Role | Title | Influence | Interest | Engagement History |

| Head, IR | Head | Medium | High | Incident collaboration |

### Pain Points & Needs

| Priority | Pain Point | Impact | Current Solution | Gap |
|----------|------------|--------|------------------|-----|
| **P1** | Limited threat intelligence visibility | Delayed detection, response | Manual feeds, open source | No sovereign, classified intel |
| **P2** | NCII operator coordination challenges | Inconsistent protection | Email, phone calls | No secure federation platform |
| **P3** | Incident response coordination | Slow containment | Ad-hoc processes | No unified command platform |
| **P4** | Compliance monitoring | Manual, slow | Spreadsheets, audits | No real-time visibility |
| **P5** | Skills gap | Limited capacity | Training programs | Need force multiplier |

**Opportunity Alignment:**
- GovSec addresses P1 (threat intel), P2 (federation), P3 (IR coordination)
- VoronDRQ addresses P4 (compliance), P5 (automation)
- Partnership model enables sovereign control + local support

### POC Opportunity

**Proposed POC Scope:**
| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Objective** | Deploy GovSec for threat intelligence federation with 3-5 NCII operators |
| **Duration** | 8-10 weeks |
| **Success Criteria** | - Intelligence shared within 15 min of detection<br>- 100% participant satisfaction<br>- Zero security incidents<br>- Documented playbook |
| **Participants** | NCA (lead) + 3-5 NCII operators (TBD) |
| **Platform** | GovSec (sovereign intelligence federation) |
| **Deployment Model** | On-premises at NCA + cloud instances for participants |
| **Investment Required** | RM 150-250K (Platform + CSM contribution) |
| **CSM Endorsement** | Required (Tier 1 approval) |

**POC Timeline:**
```
Week 1-2:   POC scoping + success criteria definition
Week 3-4:   Technical architecture + security clearance
Week 5-6:   Platform deployment + configuration
Week 7-8:   Live intelligence federation exercises
Week 9-10:  Evaluation + conversion planning
```

### Engagement Strategy

**Approach:**
1. **CSM Sponsor** schedules strategic briefing with DG/Director level
2. **Partnership Manager** coordinates working session with technical team
3. **Technical Lead** presents GovSec capabilities + POC proposal
4. **Joint team** agrees on POC scope + success criteria
5. **CSM endorsement** obtained (if required)
6. **POC kickoff** scheduled

**Messaging:**
> "CSM and [Platform] propose a sovereign intelligence federation platform that enables real-time threat intelligence sharing with NCII operators while maintaining full data sovereignty and compliance with national security requirements."

**Key Benefits:**
- ✅ Sovereign control — data stays in Malaysia
- ✅ Real-time federation — 15-minute intelligence sharing
- ✅ Secure — classified handling, audit trails
- ✅ Compliant — meets national security standards
- ✅ Scalable — from 3 to 300 NCII operators

**Objection Handling:**
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have intelligence sharing" | "Current methods are manual and slow. GovSec automates and accelerates to 15 minutes." |
| "Security clearance concerns" | "Platform is designed for classified environments. We'll complete all required clearances." |
| "Budget constraints" | "POC is jointly funded. Deployment cost is modest compared to breach impact." |
| "Integration complexity" | "GovSec integrates with existing systems. We'll handle all technical work." |
| "Change management" | "We provide training and support. CSM endorsement ensures adoption." |

- Only sovereign-grade platform with CSM endorsement
