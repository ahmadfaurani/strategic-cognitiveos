## Appendix: Discussion Guides

### For Segment 1 (Strategic Alignment)

**CSM Priorities Probe:**
- "What are the top cyber threats CSM is focused on right now?"
- "How does intelligence federation fit into CSM's 2026 roadmap?"
- "What would make this partnership visibly successful by December 2026?"

**Platform Value Probe:**
- "Where do you see gaps in current threat intelligence capabilities?"
- "What would 'sovereign control' mean for your operations?"
- "How do you currently share intelligence with NCII operators?"

---

### For Segment 2 (Target Accounts)

**Account Selection Probe:**
- "Which NCII operators are most engaged with CSM right now?"
- "Where do you see the strongest appetite for sovereign intelligence?"
- "Which account would make the best reference case study?"

**POC Feasibility Probe:**
- "What would CSM need to see to endorse a POC?"
- "What are typical POC timelines for government agencies?"
- "What are common blockers in government POCs?"

---

### For Segment 3 (Mobilization Structure)

**Role Clarity Probe:**
- "Who on your team will own day-to-day coordination?"
- "Who has authority to approve co-branded materials?"
- "Who should we escalate to if decisions are delayed?"

**Decision Rights Probe:**
- "What decisions require CSM Director-level approval?"
- "What can the Partnership Manager approve independently?"
- "How do you handle public communications approvals?"
