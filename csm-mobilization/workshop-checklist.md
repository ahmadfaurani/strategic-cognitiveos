## 🎤 Facilitator Script (Key Moments)

### Opening (2:30 PM)
> "Good afternoon, everyone. Thank you for making time for this workshop. Over the next 90 minutes, we're going to make concrete decisions about how CSM and [Platform] will work together. This is a working session — we're here to decide, not just discuss. By 4:00 PM, we will have confirmed roles, target accounts, and a 90-day plan with owners. Let's get started."

### After Each Decision
> "Let me confirm what we've just decided: [repeat decision]. Are we all agreed on this? [Wait for confirmation]. Great, I'm capturing this in the decision log."

### If Discussion Goes Off-Track
> "This is an important point, but it's outside our scope for today. Let me add it to the 'Open Issues' list and we can schedule a follow-up session."

### If Running Behind
> "We're running a bit behind. Let's park this discussion and schedule a follow-up. We still need to cover [remaining agenda items]."

### Closing (3:55 PM)
> "Before we close, let me review the action items we've agreed: [read through action log with owners and deadlines]. The decision log will be sent out within 24 hours. Thank you all for a productive session!"

## 🚨 Contingency Plans

### If Key Attendee Can't Make It
- **CSM Sponsor:** Reschedule workshop (decision authority required)
- **CSM Manager:** Proceed if delegate with decision authority attends
- **Technical Lead:** Can send delegate; confirm roles later
- **Platform Team:** Ensure all roles covered

### If Tech Fails (Virtual)
- **Backup:** Phone conference bridge ready
- **Documents:** Email copies in advance so attendees can follow along
- **Decision Log:** Capture manually, type up after

### If Discussion Stalls
- **Timebox:** "Let's spend 5 more minutes on this, then decide or escalate"
- **Parking Lot:** Add to open issues, schedule follow-up
- **Escalate:** "Let's take this offline with [relevant stakeholders]"

### If Running Over Time
- **Priority Check:** "We have 10 minutes left. Should we finish the 90-day plan or discuss [remaining item]?"
- **Schedule Follow-Up:** Book 30-min session within 48 hours
- **End on Time:** Respect everyone's calendar
