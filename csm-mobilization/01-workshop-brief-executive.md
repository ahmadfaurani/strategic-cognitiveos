This workshop establishes the **operational foundation** for the CSM–Platform partnership. Without clear role definitions, decision rights, and committed timelines, partnership momentum will stall.

| **NCII Protection** | Strengthen critical infrastructure security | Deploy GovSec to priority NCII operators |
| **Capability Building** | Build public-private partnership model | Establish reference customers in government |
| **Market Activation** | Demonstrate partnership value to stakeholders | Achieve 1-2 deployments by Q4 2026 |

### 1.2 What Success Looks Like

- ✅ All role owners confirmed (CSM + Platform)

## 2. Workshop Design Rationale

### 2.1 Why 90 Minutes?

| Segment | Duration | Rationale |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Opening | 10 min | Relationship building, set expectations |
| Strategic Alignment | 20 min | Ensure shared understanding of "why" |
| Target Accounts | 20 min | Concrete decisions require focused discussion |
| Mobilization Structure | 25 min | Most complex — roles, decision rights, escalation |
| 90-Day Plan | 10 min | Commitment — owners and dates |
| Closing | 5 min | Action review, next steps |
| **Total** | **90 min** | **Optimal for decision-making without fatigue** |

### 2.2 Why This Agenda Order?

1. **Strategic Alignment First** — Ensures everyone shares the same "why" before deciding "what" and "who"
2. **Target Accounts Before Structure** — Knowing the target informs role assignments
3. **Structure Before Plan** — Can't assign owners until owners are confirmed
4. **Plan Last** — Commitment comes after clarity on scope, targets, and roles

### 2.3 Decision-Making Approach

**Consensus-Seeking, Time-Boxed:**
- Each decision gets dedicated time
- Facilitator confirms consensus verbally
- If no consensus in timebox → escalate or park
- All decisions captured live in decision log

**Decision Thresholds:**
| Decision Type | Approval Required |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Partnership scope | CSM Sponsor + Platform Sponsor |
| Target accounts | CSM Sponsor + Platform Sponsor |
| Role assignments | Self-nomination + Sponsor approval |
| Decision rights | CSM Sponsor + Platform Sponsor |
| 90-day plan | Partnership Managers (both) |
