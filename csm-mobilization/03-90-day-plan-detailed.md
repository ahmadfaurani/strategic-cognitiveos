# 90-Day Mobilization Plan — Detailed Execution Guide

**Classification:** TLP:AMBER  
**Date:** 16 June 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Partnership Managers (CSM + Platform)

---

## Executive Summary

This 90-day plan provides **day-by-day operational guidance** for mobilizing the CSM–Platform partnership from workshop (18 June) through first deployment conversion (18 September 2026).

**4 Phases:**
1. **Mobilisation (Days 1-21)** — Foundation, team, materials
2. **POC Readiness (Days 22-49)** — Enablement, briefings, POC kickoff
3. **Market Activation (Days 50-70)** — Events, content, pipeline
4. **Scale & Convert (Days 71-90)** — POC conversion, expansion planning

**Key Milestones:**
- Day 1 (18 Jun): Alignment Workshop ✅
- Day 7 (25 Jun): Co-branded materials complete
- Day 14 (2 Jul): POC framework finalized
- Day 21 (9 Jul): CSM enablement complete
- Day 30 (18 Jul): First account briefings complete
- Day 40 (28 Jul): First POC kickoff
- Day 45 (2 Aug): NCSS 2026 execution
- Day 50 (7 Aug): MyAISI execution (if participating)
- Day 70 (27 Aug): First POC mid-point review
- Day 80 (6 Sep): POC conversion proposal
- Day 90 (16 Sep): 90-day review + Q4 planning
