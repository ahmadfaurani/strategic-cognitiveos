## Executive Summary

This proposal defines the **mobilization ownership structure** for the CSM-GovSec-VoronDRQ partnership. It clarifies who owns what, who decides what, and how decisions get made across **5 key roles** spanning both organizations.

**Recommendation:** Adopt a **lightweight, dual-track structure** with clear escalation paths. CSM owns strategic endorsement + stakeholder introductions. Platform owns commercial execution + technical delivery. Joint ownership for partnership-facing activities.

## Decision Rights Framework

### **Decision Categories**

| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Strategic** | Partnership direction, scope, high-level commitments | Target accounts, partnership model, public announcements |
| **Operational** | Day-to-day execution, scheduling, coordination | Working sessions, content production, POC tracking |
| **Technical** | Platform capabilities, integration, POC criteria | Architecture, success criteria, demo environment |
| **Commercial** | Pricing, contracts, revenue | Proposals, terms, revenue sharing |

---

### **Decision Matrix**

| Decision Type | CSM Sponsor | CSM Manager | Platform Sponsor | Platform Manager | Joint Approval |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|------------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Target account selection** | ✅ Approve | ℹ️ Input | ✅ Approve | ✅ Propose | Yes |
| **Partnership scope changes** | ✅ Approve | ℹ️ Input | ✅ Approve | ℹ️ Input | Yes |
| **Public communications** | ✅ Approve | ✅ Review | ✅ Approve | ✅ Draft | Yes |
| **POC endorsement** | ✅ Approve | ℹ️ Input | ✅ Approve | ℹ️ Input | Yes |
| **POC success criteria** | ℹ️ For Info | ✅ Review | ℹ️ For Info | ✅ Draft | Yes (Tech Leads) |
| **Co-branded content** | ✅ Approve | ✅ Review | ✅ Approve | ✅ Draft | Yes |
| **Event participation** | ✅ Approve | ✅ Propose | ✅ Approve | ✅ Propose | Yes |
| **Commercial terms** | ℹ️ For Info | ℹ️ For Info | ✅ Approve | ✅ Draft | No |
| **Working session scheduling** | ℹ️ For Info | ✅ Autonomous | ℹ️ For Info | ✅ Autonomous | No |
| **POC technical implementation** | ℹ️ For Info | ℹ️ For Info | ℹ️ For Info | ✅ Autonomous | No (Tech Leads) |
| **Resource allocation** | ✅ Approve | ℹ️ Input | ✅ Approve | ✅ Propose | Yes |

**Legend:**
- ✅ Approve = Final decision authority
- ✅ Draft/Propose = Creates proposal for approval
- ✅ Review = Reviews before escalation
- ℹ️ Input/For Info = Consulted or informed
- ✅ Autonomous = Can decide independently

## Escalation Path

### **Level 1: Operational Issues**

| Issue Type | Owner | Resolution Time |
|------------|-------|-----------------|
| Scheduling conflicts | Partnership Managers | 2 business days |
| Content review delays | Marketing Leads | 3 business days |
| Technical questions | Technical Leads | 2 business days |
| Action item follow-up | Partnership Managers | Weekly |

**Process:** Partnership Managers coordinate directly. If unresolved in 3 days, escalate to Level 2.

---

### **Level 2: Tactical Blockers**

| Issue Type | Owner | Resolution Time |
|------------|-------|-----------------|
| POC scope disagreements | Technical Leads + Partnership Managers | 5 business days |
| Resource constraints | Partnership Sponsors | 5 business days |
| Timeline slippage | Partnership Managers + Sponsors | 5 business days |
| Stakeholder availability | CSM Partnership Manager | 5 business days |

**Process:** Partnership Sponsors engage directly. If unresolved in 7 days, escalate to Level 3.

---

### **Level 3: Strategic Issues**

| Issue Type | Owner | Resolution Time |
|------------|-------|-----------------|
| Partnership scope changes | CSM CEO + Platform CEO | 10 business days |
| Major commercial terms | Platform CEO (CSM informed) | 10 business days |
| Public announcement disputes | CSM CEO + Platform CEO | 10 business days |
| Partnership termination | CSM CEO + Platform CEO | 30 business days |

**Process:** CEO-level discussion. Documented decision required.
