# N18 Sri Medan — War Room Brief (PRN Johor 2026)

**Signal Date:** 2026-06-27  
**Escalation:** MEDIUM  
**Contest:** Three-cornered fight (BN, PH, PN)  
**Tier:** Tier-2 BN-Safe / Contestable

---

## Executive Summary

N18 Sri Medan is a Malay-majority UMNO fortress (89.8% Malay, 33,875 voters) under P147 Parit Sulong, Batu Pahat. BN incumbent Datuk Zulkurnain Kamisan defends against two challengers: Cikgu Hishamuddin (PH-PKR) and Ahmad Rosdi Bahari (PN-PAS).

**Bottom Line:** BN enters as strong favourite (70–75% win probability) with 60.5% 2022 vote share and incumbency advantage. PN-PAS is the main threat (20–25% upset probability) with 31.7% 2022 base and rural Malay religious networks. PH-PKR is structurally disadvantaged (<10% upset probability) after winning only 7.1% in 2022.

**Decisive Battle:** Not statewide branding — it is kampung-level turnout, Malay trust retention, and whether PAS can convert dissatisfaction into a disciplined rural swing beyond its 30–32% historical ceiling.

**Key Risk:** Malay vote leakage to PAS in rural PDs + depressed BN turnout. If PN exceeds 35% in focus PDs and BN turnout drops below 65%, margin compresses dangerously.
