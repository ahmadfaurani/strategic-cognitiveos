# N33 Tenggaroh (P158 Mersing) — Tier-2 BN-Leaning / PN Threat

**Signal Date:** 2026-06-27  
**Escalation:** MEDIUM  
**Contest:** Three-cornered fight (BN, PH, PN)

**Seat Snapshot:**
- Registered Voters (2026): 39,001
- Polling Districts: 24
- 2022 Turnout: 57.2%
- 2022 Majority: 1,356 votes (6.3%) — BN retained but margin collapsed
- Incumbent Coalition: BN (switching from MIC to UMNO candidate in 2026)

**2026 Candidates:**
- BN: Mohd Youzaimi Yusof (UMNO)
- PH: Md Yusof Dawam (PKR)
- PN: Muhamad Amerul Muhamad (Bersatu)

**Historical Trend — Margin Collapse:**
- 2013: BN-MIC 61.7% (safe, 85.5% turnout)
- 2018: BN-MIC 25.7% (compressed, 80.4% turnout)
- 2022: BN-MIC 6.3% / 1,356 votes (57.2% turnout)

**2022 Vote Breakdown:**

- PN: 9,172 votes (42.78% of valid vote)
- PKR: 1,529 votes (7.13%)

**Demographics:**
- Malay: 82.7% (decisive majority)
- Chinese: 13.5%
- Indian: 1.5%
- Bumi Sabah/Sarawak: ~1.6%
- Youth 18–29: 23.7%
- Under 40: 54.4% (structurally decisive)

**Constituency Character:**
- Geography: 1,745 km² (large, dispersed)
- FELDA settlement belt (Tenggaroh 01–07, Tenggaroh Timur 01)
- Jemaluang/Mersing semi-rural communities
- Island polling districts (Pulau Sibu, Pulau Tinggi, Pulau Besar, Pulau Aur, Pulau Pemanggil)
- Economic base: Palm oil/FELDA settler economy, plantation work, smallholder agriculture

**Strategic Assessment:**
- **BN**: Incumbent but vulnerable. UMNO candidate corrects MIC legacy mismatch (82.7% Malay majority). Risks: MIC network demobilisation, Indian/Chinese trust erosion, PN Malay consolidation. Path: hold 2022 base + recover MIC networks + prevent Malay leakage.
- **PN**: Primary challenger with credible path. Already achieved 42.78% in 2022. Simple math: small net swing from BN wins. Path: maintain 2022 base + convert 5–7% BN defection.
- **PH**: Underdog (2022 base only 7.13%). Risk of spoiler effect. Path requires 3x–4x growth + Chinese turnout surge + FELDA/Malay penetration.

**Critical Issues:**
- FELDA second-generation agenda (housing, land, inheritance, youth employment)
- Palm oil income sensitivity (commodity prices → smallholder economics)
- Rural road/service delivery (1,745 km² dispersed constituency)
- Island/coastal logistics (5 island PDs — small numbers matter in tight race)
- Youth retention (54.4% under 40)
- Malay representation sentiment (BN's UMNO switch confirms centrality)

**War Room Assessment:**
- BN Retention Probability: Moderate (55–60%)
- PN Upset Probability: Moderate to High (30–35%)
- PH Breakthrough Probability: Low (10–15%)
- PH Spoiler Effect: High — PH vote share could determine BN vs PN winner

**Key Watchpoints:**
- Malay vote split (BN vs PN) — decisive factor
- FELDA turnout differential (low turnout created 2022 volatility)
- MIC legacy network retention post-candidate switch
- Island PD logistics (Pulau Sibu, Tinggi, Besar, Aur, Pemanggil)
- Youth mobilisation (18–29 = 23.7%)

**Bottom Line:** N33 Tenggaroh is no longer safe BN/MIC fortress. It is a Malay-majority, FELDA-heavy, three-way battleground. BN's UMNO candidate is defensive correction to 2022 near-miss. PN is primary challenger. PH must expand beyond 2022 base or risk being vote-splitter.
