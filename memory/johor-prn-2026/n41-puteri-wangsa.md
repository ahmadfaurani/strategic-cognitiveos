# N41 Puteri Wangsa (P158 Tebrau) — Tier-1 Urban Battleground

**Signal Date:** 2026-06-27  
**Escalation:** HIGH  
**Contest:** Five-cornered fight (PH, BN, MUDA, Bersama, Independent)

**Key Dynamics:**
- MUDA won in 2022 (Amira Aisya, 7,114 majority) but incumbent NOT defending
- PH deploying Dr Maszlee Malik (former Education Minister) — signals serious intent to reclaim
- MUDA fielding Rashifa Aljunied (26, Chief of Staff to MUDA President) — youth appeal play
- BN/MCA candidate Teow Chia Ling focusing on local-service credibility (congestion, public facilities)
- Bersama entering progressive southern Johor seat via Nicholas Paul Vincent

**Demographics:**
- 128,723 voters (2nd highest in Johor)
- Chinese 51.4%, Malay 35.7%, Indian 10.4%
- Youth 18–29: 35.5% (44,349 voters) — structurally decisive
- 2022 turnout only 47.9% vs 86.9% in 2018 — mobilisation is the battleground

**Critical Issues:** Flooding/drainage, traffic congestion, youth representation, urban services

**Assessment:** Not a straightforward PH–MUDA contest. Fragmented urban-progressive seat where turnout, youth mobilisation, Chinese voter consolidation, and service-delivery credibility will determine margin. MUDA faces retention challenge without incumbent's personal mandate.
