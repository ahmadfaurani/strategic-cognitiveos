## 5. Chain of Command (Directives)

| Level | Authority | Directive Type | Enforcement |
|-------|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| **1** | Perdana Menteri | National Security Policy (DKN) | Cabinet approval |
| **2** | Ketua Pengarah MKN | Arahan MKN | Mandatory across all ministries |
| **3** | Timbalan KP (Strategik) | Operational Orders | Inter-agency coordination |
| **4** | Bahagian Chiefs | Tasking Orders | Agency-specific implementation |

**Legal Basis:**
- **Arahan MKN No. 1** - Establishment & responsibilities (Emergency Powers Act 1979)
- **Akta MKN 2016 (Act 776)** - Statutory basis (commenced 1 Aug 2016)
- **Section 37** - All MKN matters are TOP SECRET
- **Section 38** - No legal action against MKN for function execution
