## Overview

**Arahan MKN** (MKN Directives) are mandatory orders issued by the National Security Council under the authority of:
- **Arahan MKN No. 1** - Foundational directive (Emergency Powers Act 1979)
- **Akta Majlis Keselamatan Negara 2016** (Act 776) - Statutory basis

**Enforcement:** All Arahan MKN are **MANDATORY** across all ministries and agencies.

**Legal Protection:** Section 38, Act 776 - No legal action can be brought against MKN for function execution.

## Statutory Framework

### UNDANG-001: Akta Majlis Keselamatan Negara 2016 (Act 776)
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Long Title** | An Act to establish the National Security Council and to provide for matters connected therewith |
| **Act Number** | Act 776 |
| **Passed Dewan Rakyat** | 3 December 2015 |
| **Passed Dewan Negara** | 22 December 2015 |
| **Royal Assent (deemed)** | 18 February 2016 |
| **Commencement** | 1 August 2016 [P.U. (B) 310/2016] |
| **Key Parts** | - Part II: Establishment of National Security Council<br>- Part III: Duties of Director General of National Security<br>- Part IV: Declaration of Security Areas<br>- Part V: Special powers in security areas<br>- Part VI: General provisions |
| **Critical Sections** | - **Section 37**: All NSC matters are TOP SECRET<br>- **Section 38**: No legal action against NSC for function execution |
| **Status** | ✅ Active (law) |
| **Sources** | en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_Act_2016, official gazette |
| **Full Text** | ✅ Available - Federal Gazette, Parliament website |

- Criticized by opposition parties and civil society for broad powers

## Directive Lifecycle

```
1. DRAFTING
   (BKS-PMO + SKS-MKN, with inter-agency consultation)
   │
   ▼
2. APPROVAL
   (Majlis Keselamatan Negara meeting, chaired by PM)
   │
   ▼
3. ISSUANCE
   (Ketua Pengarah MKN signs directive)
   │
   ▼
4. IMPLEMENTATION
   (All ministries/agencies must comply)
   │
   ▼
5. MONITORING
   (SKS-MKN tracks compliance, conducts audits)
   │
   ▼
6. REVISION (as needed)
   (e.g., Arahan No. 20 revised 2013)
```
