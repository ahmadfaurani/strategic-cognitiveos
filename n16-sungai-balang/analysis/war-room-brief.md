## 🎯 CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### 1. Chinese Voter Turnout (Non-Negotiable)
- **Target:** 85%+ in Tier 1 PDs (6,600+ votes)
- **Risk:** 2022 saw Chinese abstention; must reverse
- **Tactics:** Early voting logistics, transport, family mobilization

### 2. Malay Vote Fragmentation
- **Target:** Keep BN below 55%, PN below 25%
- **Opportunity:** PN entry splits anti-PH vote
- **Tactics:** Quiet persuasion, community leader endorsements, welfare contrast

### 3. Youth Mobilization (18-30 cohort)
- **Target:** 65% turnout (vs 2022's ~45%)
- **Potential:** +4,000-5,000 votes vs 2022
- **Tactics:** Digital campaign, candidate social media, issue-based messaging (jobs, housing)

### 4. Female Voter Outreach
- **Target:** 55%+ female support (cost-of-living messaging)
- **Demographic:** 50.6% of electorate; often decisive
- **Tactics:** Women's wing mobilization, kitchen meetings, candidate accessibility
