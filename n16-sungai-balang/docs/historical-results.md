## 🔑 Lessons for 2026

### For PH
1. **Turnout is everything** — 2018's 84.9% got PH within 174 votes. 2022's 61.8% collapsed PH to 18.5%.
2. **Chinese consolidation non-negotiable** — Must get 85%+ turnout in 4 Chinese-majority PDs.
3. **Malay persuasion possible** — 2018 proved 37% Malay support achievable with right messaging.
4. **Three-cornered risk** — Must prevent race from becoming BN-vs-PN binary (PH irrelevant).

### For BN
1. **Low turnout = safety** — 61.8% turnout in 2022 gave 12.6% margin with only 42.8% vote share.
2. **Split opposition = victory** — PH-PN competition divides anti-BN vote.
3. **Incumbency machinery matters** — JPKK network delivers older, stable voters.
4. **Malay consolidation critical** — If PN gets >30% Malay vote, BN margin collapses.

### For PN
1. **Malay vote fragmentation = opportunity** — 2018 PAS got 23.9% with 3-cornered fight.
2. **Youth mobilization key** — 18-30 cohort (28.1%) is PN's growth area.
3. **Ceiling problem** — Cannot win without >40% Malay vote (requires BN collapse).
4. **Economic messaging > religious** — Cost-of-living resonates more than Islamist platform.
