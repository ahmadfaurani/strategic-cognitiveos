## 🏛️ 2026 Candidate Field

### BN-UMNO: Selamat Takim (Incumbent)
- **Profile:** Seasoned local grassroots figure
- **Strength:** JPKK network, village-level familiarity, incumbency machinery
- **Base:** Rural traditionalist, older voters, established welfare recipients
- **Vulnerability:** Anti-establishment fatigue, BN-PH federal coalition friction

### PN-PAS: Ustaz Muhammad Amin Sailan
- **Profile:** PAS traditional core, religious network leader
- **Strength:** Surauf/PASTI network, organic mobilization, youth religious appeal
- **Base:** Islamist rural grassroots, conservative Malay voters
- **Vulnerability:** Zero Chinese appeal, ceiling ~73% of electorate

### PH-PKR: Ayna Soraya Badaruddin
- **Profile:** Fresh face, progressive reformist
- **Strength:** Consolidated Chinese base, youth appeal, pluralist messaging
- **Base:** Chinese voters (25%), urban progressives, first-time voters
- **Vulnerability:** "Squeeze" risk in rural Malay heartlands, turnout dependency
