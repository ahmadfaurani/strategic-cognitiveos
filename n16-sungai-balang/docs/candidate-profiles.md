## 🟠 SELAMAT TAKIM (BN-UMNO) — The Incumbent

### Profile Summary
- **Role:** Incumbent ADUN (since 2022)
- **Party:** UMNO (Barisan Nasional)
- **Candidate Type:** Seasoned local grassroots figure
- **Campaign Theme:** "Reassurance of Stability"

### Operational Footprint
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Machinery** | Institutionalized JPKK (Jawatankuasa Pembangunan dan Keselamatan Kampung) network |
| **Mobilization** | Village heads, welfare delivery channels, state-backed infrastructure continuity |
| **Voter Contact** | Direct localized service, house-to-house visits, kampung town halls |
| **Core Message** | Stability, experience, federal-state coalition benefits |

### Strategic Strengths
1. **Incumbency Advantage** — Established service delivery record, name recognition
2. **Village Network Depth** — JPKK machinery penetrates all 19 PDs
3. **Older Voter Loyalty** — Strong 60+ demographic support (21.9% of electorate)
4. **Machine Discipline** — UMNO's traditional ground game remains intact
5. **Federal Coalition Cover** — Can claim credit for both state + federal delivery

### Core Vulnerabilities
1. **Anti-Establishment Fatigue** — Voters seeking change after long UMNO dominance
2. **BN-PH Federal Friction** — Traditional UMNO voters confused by coalition with DAP/PKR
3. **Malay Vote Erosion** — PN/PAS inroads among conservative rural voters
4. **Complacency Risk** — Incumbents often under-campaign in "safe" seats
5. **Youth Disconnect** — Limited appeal to 18-30 cohort (28.1% of electorate)

## 🟢 USTAZ MUHAMMAD AMIN SAILAN (PN-PAS) — The Challenger

### Profile Summary
- **Role:** PAS candidate, religious community leader
- **Party:** PAS (Perikatan Nasional)
- **Candidate Type:** Islamist grassroots organizer
- **Campaign Theme:** "Islamic Governance, Economic Justice"

### Operational Footprint
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Machinery** | Surauf network, PASTI schools, religious study circles |
| **Mobilization** | Organic, low-cost, persistent community presence |
| **Voter Contact** | Religious gatherings, youth Islamic groups, social media |
| **Core Message** | Malay-Muslim representation, anti-corruption, economic justice |

### Strategic Strengths
1. **Religious Network Depth** — PAS commands everyday community infrastructure
2. **Youth Appeal** — Islamist messaging resonates with 18-30 cohort
3. **Organic Mobilization** — Lower cost-per-vote than BN/PH machinery
4. **Protest Vote Magnet** — Attracts anti-BN, anti-establishment sentiment
5. **PN Heavyweight Support** — Sanusi Md Nor, Idris Ahmad flank campaign

### Core Vulnerabilities
1. **Non-Malay Ceiling** — Zero Chinese appeal (25.1% of electorate)
2. **Ideological Hard Filter** — Moderate Malays may reject PAS brand
3. **Turnout Dependency** — Needs >70% Malay turnout to maximize
4. **Three-Cornered Math** — Risk of splitting anti-BN vote with PH
5. **Economic Messaging Gap** — Religious appeal may not address cost-of-living

- **Party:** PKR (Pakatan Harapan)
- **Candidate Type:** Progressive reformist, youth appeal
- **Campaign Theme:** "Change, Opportunity, Inclusive Growth"

### Operational Footprint
| Attribute | Details |

| **Mobilization** | Digital-first, youth volunteers, community events |
| **Voter Contact** | Town halls, social media, door-knocking (Chinese PDs) |
| **Core Message** | Reform, cost-of-living relief, youth employment, pluralism |

### Strategic Strengths
1. **Consolidated Chinese Base** — 25.1% of electorate, high PH loyalty
2. **Youth Appeal** — Progressive messaging resonates with 18-30 cohort
3. **Pluralist Platform** — Attracts moderate Malays, Indian voters
