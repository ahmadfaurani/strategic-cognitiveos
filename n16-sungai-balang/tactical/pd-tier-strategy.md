## 🎯 Tier Framework Overview

The 19 polling districts (PDs) of N16 Sungai Balang are categorized into **5 tiers** based on:
- Ethnic composition (Malay/Chinese ratio)
- Youth concentration (18-30 age group)
- Historical voting patterns (2018, 2022)
- Strategic importance to PH campaign

| Tier | Category | PDs | Voters | PH Strategy | Resource Priority |
|------|----------|-----|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1** | Must-Win Chinese-Majority | 4 | 8,887 | 90%+ PH support, 85%+ turnout | 45% |
| **Tier 2** | Contestable Mixed | 3 | 5,028 | 45-50% PH support, deny BN supermajority | 35% |
| **Tier 3** | Youth Hubs | 6 | 7,808 | 65%+ youth turnout | 15% |
| **Tier 4** | BN Stronghold | 2 | 3,972 | Damage limitation (25-30%) | 3% |
| **Tier 5** | Malay-Rural | 5 | 8,144 | Defensive (30-35%, prevent PN inroads) | 2% |

**Total:** 19 PDs, 31,039 voters

## 🟡 TIER 2: CONTESTABLE MIXED PDs

### Strategic Imperative
These 3 PDs are the **battleground** that determines the winner. PH doesn't need to win all of them, but must prevent BN/PN supermajorities.

### PD Breakdown

#### 146/16/07 — Parit Jawa (1,533 voters) — **KEY BATTLEGROUND**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Malay %** | 64.4% (987 voters) |
| **Chinese %** | 33.7% (517 voters) |
| **Youth %** | 24.0% (368 voters) |
| **Female %** | 51.7% |
| **2022 Lean** | BN +12% |
| **2026 Target** | PH 45%+, deny BN >50% |

**Why Decisive:**
- Mixed economic profile: Fishing community (Malay) + Chinese business
- Early sentiment indicator: If PH underperforms here, campaign pivots to damage control
- 2018: PH came within single digits; 2022: BN won comfortably (low Chinese turnout)

**Priority Actions:**
- Week 1 flagship: Candidate holds first major rally here
- Fishing community outreach: Economic pain messaging (fuel costs, catch decline)
- Chinese business mobilization: SME support, regulatory relief messaging
- Watch metric: Rally attendance, door-knock response rate

**Vote Target:** 650-700 votes (42-45% of 1,533)

1. **Digital Campaign**
   - TikTok challenges, Instagram reels, WhatsApp broadcast lists

3. **GOTV Logistics**

### Malay Persuasion Tactics
1. **Quiet Outreach**
   - Community leader endorsements (village heads, religious figures)
   - Welfare delivery contrast (federal programs vs state promises)
   - Avoid direct confrontation with BN/PN machinery

2. **Issue Messaging**
   - Cost-of-living: Fuel, food, utility costs
   - Agriculture/fisheries: Subsidies, market access, infrastructure
   - Rural development: Roads, drainage, flood mitigation, village facilities

3. **PN Containment**
   - Highlight PN's lack of delivery record
   - Contrast PAS state governance (if applicable) with federal PH programs
   - Moderate Malay voters: Emphasize PH's inclusivity vs PAS exclusivity
