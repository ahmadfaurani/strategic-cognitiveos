## 🎯 Overview: The 3 Decisive PDs

Out of 19 polling districts in N16 Sungai Balang, **3 PDs will determine the winner**:

| PD Code | District | Voters | Why Decisive |

---

### Why Parit Jawa Is Decisive

**1. Mixed Economic Profile**
- **Malay voters:** Predominantly fishing community (coastal location)
- **Chinese voters:** Small business owners, shopkeepers, service sector
- **Shared pain point:** Cost-of-living, fuel prices, economic squeeze

**2. Early Sentiment Indicator**
- 2018: PH won this PD (anti-BN wave, high turnout)
- 2022: BN won comfortably (Chinese abstention, low turnout)
- 2026: First PD to reveal whether economic pain translates to anti-incumbent vote

**3. Canary in the Coal Mine**
- If PH underperforms here in Week 1 → Campaign pivots to damage control
- If BN underperforms → Fishing community economic pain is real
- If PN surges → Malay protest vote is consolidating Islamist

---

### Candidate Strengths in Parit Jawa

| Candidate | Strength | Weakness |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Selamat Takim (BN)** | Incumbent service delivery, village machinery | Economic pain attribution (voters blame incumbent) |
| **Ustaz Amin (PN)** | Malay religious network, cost-of-living messaging | Chinese voter ceiling (max 5-10% Chinese support) |
| **Ayna Soraya (PH)** | Chinese business base, pluralist messaging | Malay fishing community penetration |

### PH Campaign Tactics for Parit Jawa

**Week 1: Flagship Rally**
- Hold first major campaign rally here (symbolic importance)
- Messaging: "Economic justice for fishing families + small businesses"
- Candidate immersion: Visit fish auction, Chinese shop houses, meet both communities

**Week 2-3: Targeted Outreach**
- **Malay fishing community:** Fuel subsidy messaging, catch decline solutions, coastal infrastructure
- **Chinese business:** SME support, regulatory relief, cost-of-living relief
- **Youth (24%):** Jobs messaging, gig economy support, digital campaign

**Final Week: GOTV**
- Chinese turnout: 85%+ target (440+ votes)
- Malay persuasion: 35%+ target (345+ votes)
- Youth mobilization: 60%+ turnout (220+ votes)

**Vote Target:** 650-700 votes (42-45% of 1,533)

---

### Why Sarang Buaya Laut Is Decisive

**1. Highest Youth Concentration in Seat**
- 35.3% youth (18-30) vs 28.1% seat average
- Youth are swing voters: Less party loyalty, more issue-based
- PN/PAS has strong Islamist youth appeal; PH has progressive reform appeal

**2. PN's Flagship Test**
- If PN wins here → Signal of Malay youth Islamist surge
- If PN underperforms → Ceiling problem (can't convert religious network to votes)
- BN fears: If PN takes this PD, Malay vote fragmentation is real

**3. Chinese Minority as Tiebreaker**
- 27.3% Chinese (464 voters) — large enough to tip balance
- If Chinese turnout 85%+ → PH competitive
- If Chinese apathy → PN/BN binary fight

---

### Candidate Strengths in Sarang Buaya Laut

| **Ayna Soraya (PH)** | Chinese base, progressive youth appeal, digital campaign | Malay religious network gap, rural youth penetration |

### Why Parit Kassim Is Decisive

**1. Largest Mixed PD in Seat**
- 2,541 voters — small swing = big vote impact
- 10% swing = 254 votes (larger than 2018's 174-vote margin)
- Microcosm of overall seat dynamics (71.7% Malay, 27.6% Chinese)

**2. Incumbent Machinery Test**
- Selamat Takim must prove BN ground game dominance here
- If BN underperforms → Incumbent vulnerability signal
- If BN wins comfortably → Machinery intact, narrative control

**3. Moderate Malay Persuasion Battleground**
- 71.7% Malay — not monolithic; significant moderate segment
- Cost-of-living, service delivery, welfare contrast can move 10-15%
- PN competes for conservative Malay; PH competes for moderate Malay
