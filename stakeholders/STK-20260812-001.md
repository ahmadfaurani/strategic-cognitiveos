# High-Value Conversation Territories

1. **Sovereign cybersecurity capability** — Strong fit when tied to measurable Malaysian operational capacity, data sovereignty and institutional collaboration
2. **AI-enabled cyber defence** — Lead with assurance, explainability, operator control and operational utility (not AI novelty)
3. **Cyber resilience** — Governance, business continuity, recovery and institutional readiness
4. **Emerging threat readiness** — Post-quantum migration, new threat classes, anticipatory defence (fits his public thought-leadership themes)
5. **Technical validation & POCs** — Clear success criteria, evidence collection, integration requirements, post-POC deployment pathway

# Engagement Guardrails

**DO:**
- Anchor on mission, resilience and measurable outcomes
- Bring technical evidence and a realistic operating model
- Show how the capability integrates with existing CSM ecosystem assets
- Define governance, ownership and post-POC path early

**AVOID:**
- Leading with product features or generic AI claims
- Assuming technical endorsement equals procurement authority
- Presenting a POC without success criteria or operationalisation plan
- Overstating institutional alignment before formal validation
