# Name

UiTM Centre for Media and Information Warfare Studies

# Role

Academic and research partner. The Centre provides academic leadership, research expertise, and understanding of Malaysia's media, behavioural, and socio-cultural environment for the AI-enabled R.I.S.I.K Framework collaboration.

# Organisation

Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) — Centre for Media and Information Warfare Studies

# Strategic Relevance

The Centre is the academic anchor for the R.I.S.I.K AI enablement initiative. Their domain expertise in media and information warfare analysis, combined with their understanding of the Malaysian socio-cultural context, provides the research foundation that makes the framework credible and operationally relevant. This partnership elevates R.I.S.I.K from a concept (in Workstream D) to an academically-grounded, AI-enabled national capability.
