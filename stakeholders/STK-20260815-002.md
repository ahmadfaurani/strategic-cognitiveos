# Stakeholder Record

## Organisation Profile

**Name:** Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC)
**Type:** Government regulatory commission
**Sector:** Communications, multimedia, digital content
**Jurisdiction:** Malaysia
**Relevance to R.I.S.I.K:** MCMC has direct regulatory interest in:
- Social media platform regulation
- Digital literacy and misinformation counter-strategies
- Content rules and election communication compliance
- Digital content governance

## Role in R.I.S.I.K Programme

**Capacity:** Target funder / proposing partner
**Engagement Level:** Identified — not yet engaged
**Relationship:** Prospective client (RM5.0M programme)

MCMC is positioned as the primary target for the R.I.S.I.K AI Platform proposed selling price. The platform's focus on Reality, Issue, Sentiment, Influence, and Kontra Narrative directly aligns with MCMC's regulatory mandate around digital content governance, misinformation counter-strategies, and strategic communications.
