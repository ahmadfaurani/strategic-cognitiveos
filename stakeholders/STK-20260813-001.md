# Name

PERJASA — Persatuan Juruanalisa Sistem Sektor Awam (Public Sector System Analysts Association)

# Role

Coordinating partner and primary catalyst for government AI adoption. PERJASA is positioned as the institutional partner that coordinates and drives the AI Co-Design Lab programme, with Aras Integrasi providing technical design, GPU compute, prototyping, and security/governance support.

# Organisation

PERJASA — professional association for public sector system analysts. Works closely with Jabatan Digital Negara (JDN) and Kementerian Digital.

# Strategic Relevance

PERJASA is the institutional vehicle through which the Government Sector AI Co-Design Lab is coordinated. The alumni network model creates a self-sustaining expert pool within PERJASA that can support government AI planning and implementation cohort-over-cohort. This is a foundational partnership for Workstream A (Sovereign AI & Government AI Adoption).

# Influence Level

**High** — PERJASA's role as coordinating partner gives it institutional ownership of the cohort programme, alumni network, and the direction of AI capacity building within the public sector analyst community.

# Interest Level

**High** — The Co-Design Lab directly serves PERJASA's mission of building public sector system analyst capability. The alumni network model provides PERJASA members with professional development pathways and positioning as subject matter experts.
