# Name

Pusat Pembangunan Keselamatan Siber dan Kriptologi (CSCDC)

# Role

National cyber defence and cryptology authority — consolidated entity formed from PTPKM (Pusat Pengurusan dan Teknologi Kriptologi Malaysia) and CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM) operational expertise. Operates under JPM/MKN/NACSA governance.

# Organisation

- **Parent:** Jabatan Perdana Menteri (JPM)
- **Governance:** Majlis Keselamatan Negara (MKN) / Agensi Keselamatan Siber Negara (NACSA)
- **Predecessors:** PTPKM + CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM)
- **Classification:** SULIT-level operations

# Strategic Relevance

CSCDC is a newly consolidated national cyber authority with a RM 4M communication division budget, 18 positions to fill, and a 90-day mobilisation window. This represents:

1. **Greenfield engagement opportunity** — new entity, new leadership, new budget, no incumbent vendor relationships
2. **Cybersecurity productisation channel** — CSCDC's infrastructure gaps (social listening, content studio, encrypted portal) map to Aras Integrasi's product portfolio
3. **Sovereign AI intersection** — PQC Sandbox is Malaysia's first post-quantum cryptography initiative, sitting at the nexus of national cybersecurity and sovereign AI infrastructure
4. **War room methodology transfer** — CSCDC is building cyber crisis communication war rooms; DAF has operated political war rooms with transferable methodology
5. **Stakeholder ecosystem access** — CSCDC's governance chain (KSN, MKN, NACSA, CEO CSCDC) opens doors to JPM-level engagement

# Influence Level

**High** — CSCDC is the consolidated national authority for cyber defence and cryptology under JPM. Its communication framework touches every CNII operator, government agency, and the public. The four-signature approval chain (CCO → CEO → KSN/MKN → DG NACSA) indicates senior government-level decision-making.

# Interest Level

**High** — CSCDC has an active RM 4M budget, a final-draft framework ready for implementation approval, and a 90-day mobilisation mandate. They need partners, infrastructure, and methodology during mobilisation — not after.

# Engagement Objective

Position Aras Integrasi as a **capability builder and strategic partner** (not a vendor) during CSCDC's 90-day mobilisation window. Specific objectives:

1. Map CSCDC leadership and acting CCO
2. Establish relationship with CEO CSCDC and/or CCO
3. Identify infrastructure and capability gaps that Aras Integrasi can fill
4. Offer war room methodology and Community Champions programme design support
5. Explore PQC Sandbox as Sovereign AI intersection
