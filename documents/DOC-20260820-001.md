## Key Facts

- **Accused:** Sayed Amir Muzzakkir Al Sayed Mohamad, 43, political secretary to former Home Minister Datuk Seri Hamzah Zainudin
- **Charges:** 3 counts — 2× accepting RM350,000 bribe (Dec 4 & 16, 2020, Yayasan Tanah Aku office, Solaris Dutamas), 1× soliciting RM350,000 (early Dec 2020, Nexuscorpgroup office, Shah Alam)
- **Statute:** Section 16(a)(A) MACC Act 2009 — max 20 years prison + fine ≥5× bribe or RM10,000
- **Bribe source:** Wan Azhar Mohamed Yusof, MD of Nexuscorpgroup Sdn Bhd
- **Project:** PDRM RMPNet tender — Maintenance and Supply of Spare Parts for Infrastructure Equipment, Servers, Software and RMPNet System Radio Users
- **Contract value:** RM381,112,042.44 (incl SST) / RM359,539,662.50 (excl SST)
- **Consultancy fee:** RM19 million (5% of contract value) — paid to Al Khairi Group Berhad (MD: Khairi Mat Jahya)
- **Amount demanded by Sayed:** ~RM9 million (portion of RM19M fee)
- **Amount paid:** RM350,000 (via dummy invoice from Khairi's company, paid by Wan Azhar)
- **Witness:** Datuk Seri Mohd Khairi Mat Jahya, 43, MD of Al Khairi Group Berhad — 15th prosecution witness
- **Minister annotation:** Hamzah wrote "Datuk KSU for consideration" on Nexuscorpgroup supporting letter (April 28, 2020 meeting)
- **Presiding judge:** Judge Rosli Ahmad
- **DPP:** Atirah Mohd Azuarizanuddin
- **Defence counsel:** Datuk Yaacob Md Sam
- **Trial status:** Continues Aug 21, 2026
