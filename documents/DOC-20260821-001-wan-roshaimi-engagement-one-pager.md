## Engagement Strategy

| Stage | Focus | Execution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Lead With** | National / institutional outcome | Frame the problem, affected stakeholders, desired end-state |
| **Prove** | Technical and operational credibility | Architecture, deployment evidence, POC criteria, measurable outcomes |
| **De-Risk** | Governance and integration | Controls, data handling, assurance model, interoperability, ownership |
| **Operationalise** | Sustainable Malaysian capability | Local support, skills transfer, sovereign infrastructure, scale pathway |
| **Close** | Specific next decision | Technical validation session, POC gate, architecture review, named next step |

## Engagement Sequencing

1. **First meeting (Gate 4):** Technical narrative + co-branding validation. Lead with national outcome, prove with architecture, de-risk with governance, close with defined next step.
2. **Second meeting (post-CyberDSA):** Integration architecture — GovSec × SiberSUITE, Score Card, CBOM. How capabilities fit CSM's technology roadmap and CSCDC transition.
3. **Ongoing:** Technical partnership cadence — quarterly architecture review, emerging technology evaluation, joint PQC / emerging threat work.

---

## Guardrails

**DO:**
- Anchor on mission, resilience and measurable outcomes
- Bring technical evidence and a realistic operating model
- Show how the capability integrates with existing CSM ecosystem assets
- Define governance, ownership and post-POC path early

**AVOID:**
- Leading with product features or generic AI claims
- Assuming technical endorsement equals procurement authority
- Presenting a POC without success criteria or operationalisation plan
- Overstating institutional alignment before formal validation
- Treating him as a delivery resource — he validates, he doesn't execute
