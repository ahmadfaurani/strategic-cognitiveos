# Summary

Comprehensive 8-page workshop agenda for the PERJASA Government AI Systems Co-Design Workshop, confirmed for September 2–3, 2026.

## Structure

1. **Workshop mandate** — implementation-oriented mechanism, not conventional training. Primary outcome: prioritised portfolio of government AI initiatives with validated prototypes and 90-day continuation plan.
2. **Delivery model** — Discover → Design → Build → Validate across 2 days, with decision gates at each stage.
3. **Objectives** — 6 success conditions covering shared direction, use-case identification, implementation capability, governed GPU access, embedded cybersecurity, and pilot selection.
4. **Participation** — 24–32 participants in 4 multidisciplinary teams across 4 focus areas: AI Infrastructure, AI Development, AI Cybersecurity, AI Productivity.
5. **Core nucleus** — 5 named participants: Razale Ibrahim (JDN), Azwan Azmi (JDN), Meor Mohd Shahrulnizam (JDN), Raja Mohammad Hafiz (MOH), Hussein Mohamed (SUK Negeri Sembilan).
6. **Pre-workshop preparation** — 6 readiness activities with accountable owners + 5 participant pre-work requirements.
7. **Day 1** — Strategic Alignment, Discovery and Design (08:30–17:30). 12 sessions including problem discovery, use-case assessment, solution co-design, architecture clinics, and Gate 2 design review.
8. **Day 2** — Prototyping, Validation and Pilot Selection (08:30–18:00). 11 sessions including prototype sprints, mentor clinics, security review, validation, pilot design, Gate 3 final demonstrations, and portfolio decision.
9. **Evaluation** — 8-dimension, 100-point scoring framework. Progression threshold: 70/100. Four decision categories: Proceed to Pilot, Refine Before Pilot, Continue as Research, Hold.
10. **Submission package** — 9 required deliverables per team.
11. **Governance roles** — 12 defined roles from PERJASA Leadership to Evaluation Panel.
12. **Operating controls** — 6 controls covering data, access, security, human authority, documentation, IP.
13. **Post-workshop** — 90-day continuation timeline with 6 milestones from report issuance to portfolio review.
14. **Completion criteria** — 6 criteria including validated outputs, scored decisions, named pilot owners, documented security findings, confirmed practitioner community.
