# CyberDSA 2026 Market Positioning Narrative

## **National-Grade, Full-Stack Sovereign Technology Deployment**

*Authored by DAF — 19 August 2026*
*T-49 days to CyberDSA 2026 (5–7 October 2026, MITEC Kuala Lumpur)*

---

## Core Narrative

At CyberDSA 2026, Aras Integrasi is demonstrating more than individual cybersecurity products.

The central story is the emergence of a Malaysian capability to design, integrate and operationalise sovereign cybersecurity technology across the full security lifecycle.

Together with strategic ecosystem partners, (CyberSecurity Malaysia), we are bringing together governance and compliance, threat intelligence, AI-enabled security operations and emerging-technology security into a cohesive national-grade capability.

**The key market message is:**

> Malaysia can build, integrate and operationalise its own critical cybersecurity capabilities — designed around Malaysian requirements, deployed within Malaysian infrastructure and strengthened through Malaysian institutional collaboration.

> Aras Integrasi's role is to help engineer that capability into operational reality.

---

## 6-Layer Narrative Framework

### Layer 1 — National Story
Malaysia is developing the capability to build and operationalise its own cybersecurity technology ecosystem.

### Layer 2 — Aras Story
Aras Integrasi acts as a Malaysian sovereign technology integrator, bringing infrastructure, platforms, cybersecurity technology and institutional partners together.

### Layer 3 — Technology Story
Sovereign cybersecurity requires integrated capability across governance, intelligence, operations and emerging technology protection.

### Layer 4 — Partnership Story
National capability is strengthened through collaboration between industry engineering capabilities and institutional expertise, including CyberSecurity Malaysia.

### Layer 5 — CyberDSA Story
CyberDSA provides the platform to demonstrate these capabilities operating together as a cohesive cybersecurity stack.

### Layer 6 — Market Story
Government, CNII, GLC and regulated organisations can engage Aras around capability deployment, rather than simply procuring individual products.

## Reframe from Rashid's Proposal

| Element | Rashid (Aug 18) | DAF (Aug 19) | Rationale |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|-----------|
| Core concept | "National Grid" | "National-Grade" | Capability quality, not infrastructure topology |
| Scope | "Sovereign AI Full Stack" | "Full-Stack Sovereign Technology" | Broader than AI-only; encompasses full cybersecurity lifecycle |
| Orientation | "for Malaysia" | "Deployment" | Operational action, not aspirational statement |
| Overall | Product/solution framing | National capability framing | Shifts from "what we sell" to "what Malaysia can do" |

## Application Areas

This narrative is designed as the anchor for:
- **Booth** — visual design, messaging, demonstrations
- **Product messaging** — how individual products are positioned within the national capability story
- **Social media** — pre-event, during-event, post-event content
- **Media engagement** — press releases, journalist interactions
- **Flash Talk** (15–20 min Exhibition Stage slot) — narrative structure for speaking opportunity

DAF explicitly allows branding team flexibility in how the narrative is translated creatively.

## Strategic Alignment

This market positioning narrative aligns directly with:

1. **INT-20260819-001 (Dr. Megat analytical report)** — Recommended narrative: "Malaysia is reaching the point where sovereign cybersecurity capability can move from policy ambition into operational deployment." The market narrative is the external-facing version of the same proposition.

2. **DOC-20260819-001 (6-Stakeholder Activation Framework)** — The narrative provides the market-facing message that the 6-gate activation framework produces internally. Gates 1-5 build CSM alignment; the narrative is what that alignment produces for external consumption.

3. **MCSS 2025–2030** — DAF's narrative mirrors NACSA's own "sovereign cyber security resilience" language. The market narrative positions Aras as implementing existing national direction, not inventing a new concept.

4. **CSM Partnership** — CSM is explicitly named as strategic ecosystem partner in the narrative. This is the public articulation of the Gate 1-5 internal alignment.
