# CyberDSA 2026 — Executive Stakeholder Engagement & Activation Framework

**Document 1 — Operational Playbook for CyberSecurity Malaysia and NACSA Stakeholder Activation**
**Version 1.0 | 19 August 2026 | Internal Activation Framework**

## Strategic Intent

Secure the stakeholder alignment required to position the Aras Integrasi × CyberSecurity Malaysia collaboration at CyberDSA 2026 as a credible, commercially actionable and nationally aligned sovereign cybersecurity capability.

## 6-Stakeholder Dependency Chain

| 1 | Azrul (STK-20260813-008) | Partnership & Alliance | Anchor CSM–Aras partnership, secure internal sponsorship | Partnership buy-in and sponsorship |

**Dependency chain:** Azrul → Zulfeka → Bala → Wan Roshaimi → Zaharudin → Dr. Megat

This sequence progressively builds the proposition from internal partnership legitimacy → commercial relevance → market readiness → technical confidence → operational feasibility → national strategic alignment.

## 6 Decision Gates

| Gate | Lead Stakeholder | Close Required | Control Point |
|------|-----------------|---------------|---------------|
| Gate 1 — Partnership Alignment | Azrul | Confirm partnership intent, joint CyberDSA narrative, CSM sponsorship pathway | Do not activate broader CSM asks until partnership narrative is stable |
| Gate 2 — Commercial Ownership | Zulfeka | Agree commercial success definition, target priorities, post-event follow-through | No event activation without conversion path |
| Gate 3 — Market & Media Activation | Bala | Agree external messaging, spokesperson coordination, amplification | No fragmented product-by-product messaging |
| Gate 4 — Technical Confidence | Wan Roshaimi | Validate technical posture, architecture fit, integration boundaries | Technical leadership validates; Aras remains responsible for POC execution |
| Gate 5 — Operational Coordination | Zaharudin | Agree implementation touchpoints, access, coordination, issue-resolution | Keep coordination light, practical, owner-based |
| Gate 6 — National Strategic Alignment | Dr. Megat | Present mature proposition, explore NACSA alignment | Approach with evidence of prior CSM alignment, not unformed endorsement request |

## Activation Doctrine

1. Align before asking — stakeholders understand outcome and why their role matters before receiving a task
2. One stakeholder, one primary ask — each engagement closes on one principal commitment
3. Build internal coherence before external validation — CSM alignment precedes NACSA strategic alignment
4. Make participation valuable — each stakeholder sees direct institutional benefit
5. Convert verbal support into operating behaviour — every positive discussion produces a named next action, owner and follow-up date
6. Keep the narrative integrated — three product pillars are proof of an integrated sovereign capability, not disconnected product launches

## Core Narrative Spine

**Aras Integrasi and its ecosystem partners are demonstrating the capability to design, integrate and operationalise sovereign cybersecurity technology end-to-end — delivering national-grade, full-stack protection for Malaysia's critical digital infrastructure.**

- National Story: Malaysia building capability to develop and operationalise critical cybersecurity technology
- Partnership Story: CSM × Aras demonstrates how institutional partnership accelerates sovereign technology development
- Capability Story: Differentiator is integration across governance, intelligence, AI-enabled security operations and emerging-technology security
- Commercial Story: Partnership should create deployable solutions, qualified opportunities and repeatable route to market
- Proof Story: Individual products are evidence of the capability stack, not the entire story

## Key Risks (from Document §11)

1. Fragmented CSM buy-in → use Azrul's partnership alignment as umbrella; repeat one narrative spine
2. Commercial activity starts too late → secure Zulfeka's commercial model before final event preparations
3. Media overclaims partnership or endorsement → Bala-led approval path; distinguish partnership, alignment, participation and endorsement precisely
4. Technical team treated as delivery resource → state explicitly Aras owns POC delivery; Wan Roshaimi validates strategy/governance, Zaharudin enables coordination
5. Premature NACSA approach → engage Dr. Megat only after CSM alignment gates substantially complete
6. Stakeholder support remains verbal → every meeting closes with owner, due date and written recap
7. Too many parallel conversations → single owner and master tracker; sequence engagements and record decisions centrally

## Final Operating Principle

Every stakeholder engagement should create reusable institutional alignment that remains valuable after CyberDSA 2026.
