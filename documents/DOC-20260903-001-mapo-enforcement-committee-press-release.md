# Summary

**Meeting:** Mesyuarat Jawatankuasa Penguatkuasaan Antipemerdagangan Orang dan Antipenyeludupan Migran Bil. 1/2026

**Chair:** CP Datuk M. Kumar A/L S. Muthuvelu, Pengarah Jabatan Siasatan Jenayah (PJSJ)

**Venue:** Dewan Gemilang, Setia Perkasa 1, Kompleks Setia Perkasa, Pusat Pentadbiran Kerajaan Persekutuan, Putrajaya

**Platform:** MAPO (Majlis Antipemerdagangan Orang dan Antipenyeludupan Migran) — under KDN

**Attendees (13 agencies/departments):**
1. Jabatan Peguam Negara — Datuk Ahmad Sazali Omar (Ketua Unit Jenayah Am dan Ketenteraman Awam)
2. Jabatan Tenaga Kerja Sarawak — Puan Jamillah Putit (Pengarah)
3. MAPO Secretariat/KDN — Puan Masnizah Ramli Abdul Manan (Timbalan Setiausaha Bahagian)
4. APMM — Laksamana Pertama (M) Shahref Steve Mac Queen Abdullah (Pengarah Bahagian Siasatan Jenayah Maritim)
5. Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia — Tuan Asani Ladjani (Pengarah Bahagian Pencegahan ATIPSOM & AMLA)
6. Jabatan Tenaga Kerja Semenanjung — Encik Zairuladira Ab. Ghani (Timbalan Pengarah Bahagian Penguatkuasaan)
7. AKPS — SAC Lee King Chuan (Pengarah Bahagian Siasatan Pendakwaan)
8. PDRM D3/ATIPSOM Bukit Aman — SAC Soffian Santong (Ketua Penolong Pengarah Bahagian D3)
9. Jabatan Tenaga Kerja Sabah — Dr. Augustine Wong Ming Kong (Ketua Penolong Pengarah, Bahagian Standard Perburuhan)
10. Jabatan Kastam Diraja Malaysia — Puan Rabitah Nor Othman (Penolong Kanan Pengarah Kastam II, Bahagian Penguatkuasaan)
11. Cawangan Khas Bukit Aman — ACP Muhd Azlan Yusoff Mohd (Wakil Ketua Penolong Pengarah E6)

**Key Directives from Chair:**
1. Enforcement must shift from output metrics (numbers) to result-based outcomes (victim rescue, prosecution, syndicate disruption)
2. Intelligence-driven, proactive, high-impact enforcement
3. Strengthened inter-agency information sharing and operational coordination
4. Move as one team with shared objectives and direction

# Strategic Significance

This is a public capability demand signal from senior PDRM enforcement leadership. The framing — "intelligence-driven," "result-based," "information sharing," "strategic cooperation" — creates demand space for:
- Analytical/intelligence products (VoronDRQ, ChainSentry)
- Inter-agency coordination and information-sharing platforms
- Intelligence-led enforcement methodology support

MAPO operates under KDN — same institutional ecosystem as NACSA, JDN, and existing Workstream C partnerships. Hubungan institusi yang sudah wujud dalam portfolio DAF.
