## Registry Rules

1. **Sequential numbering** — RMR-001, RMR-002, etc.
2. **One mark per intake event** — each news article/media source gets one RMR entry
3. **Permanent** — marks are never reused, even if records are superseded
4. **Links all records** — each mark links DOC, INT, STK, ORG, RSK produced from the intake
5. **Under RADAR authority** — all marks registered under Data Research Acquisition & Development
