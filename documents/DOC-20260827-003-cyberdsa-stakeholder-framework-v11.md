# CyberDSA 2026 — Executive Stakeholder Engagement & Activation Framework V1.1

**Document 1 — Operational Playbook for CyberSecurity Malaysia and NACSA Stakeholder Activation**
**Version 1.1 | 23 August 2026 | Internal Activation Framework**

## What Changed From V1.0 (19 August 2026)

### Dependency Chain Reordered

| Position | V1.0 Sequence | V1.1 Sequence | Change |
|----------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Gate 3 | Bala (Marketing & Media) | Zaharudin (Operational Coordination) | **MOVED TO Gate 3** |
| Gate 4 | Wan Roshaimi (Technical) | Wan Roshaimi (Technical) | Same position, now follows Zaharudin |
| Gate 5 | Zaharudin (Operational) | Bala (Marketing & Media) | **MOVED TO Gate 5** |

**V1.0:** Azrul → Zulfeka → Bala → Wan Roshaimi → Zaharudin → Dr. Megat
**V1.1:** Azrul → Zulfeka → Zaharudin → Wan Roshaimi → Bala → Dr. Megat

### Rationale

1. **Operational before technical:** Operating conditions (access, coordination, implementation touchpoints) must be defined before technical validation — validate against real context, not in a vacuum.
2. **Marketing after technical:** Public claims must trace back to a validated technical position. Marketing activation follows technical clearance to prevent premature or undefended claims.
3. **Aligns with Wan Roshaimi protocol v1.2:** "Integration-backed candidate" not "jointly built." Aras owns POC delivery; CSM technical leadership validates strategy/governance against agreed operating conditions.

### New Sections Added

- **§7 Engagement Meeting Design** — Pre-read standard (2-page max), 45-min meeting structure, close discipline
- **§13 Executive Engagement Brief Template** — 10-field structured template for every stakeholder engagement
- **§14 Final Operating Principle** — Explicit immediate next-move language with full formal names

### Structural Improvements

- Full formal names used throughout (Azrul Nazim, Zulfeka bin Zainal Abidin, Ts. Mohammad Zaharudin Bin Ahmad Darus, Wan Roshaimi Wan Abdullah, Balamurugan Nallapan, Dr. Megat)
- Activation doctrine point 3 expanded to include explicit chain order
- Risk 7 ("Activation sequence is bypassed") now explicitly lists the governing chain
- Wave plan restructured to match new gate order

Secure the stakeholder alignment required to position the Aras Integrasi × CyberSecurity Malaysia collaboration at CyberDSA 2026 as a credible, commercially actionable and nationally aligned sovereign cybersecurity capability.

## Key Risks (V1.1 §11)

2. Commercial activity starts too late → secure Zulfeka's commercial model before final event preparations
3. Media overclaims partnership or endorsement → Bala activates after Wan Roshaimi's technical close; controlled approval path
4. Technical team treated as delivery resource → Zaharudin defines operating boundaries first; Wan Roshaimi validates strategy/governance; Aras remains POC delivery owner
5. Premature NACSA approach → engage Dr. Megat only after CSM gates 1–5 materially complete
6. Stakeholder support remains verbal → every meeting closes with owner, due date and written recap
7. Activation sequence is bypassed → one master tracker; enforce governing chain: Azrul → Zulfeka → Zaharudin → Wan Roshaimi → Bala → Dr. Megat

## Final Operating Principle

**CyberDSA Is the Catalyst — Not the End State.** The desired outcome is a durable CSM–Aras operating relationship that can take sovereign cybersecurity capabilities from partnership sponsorship, through commercial definition, operational enablement and technical validation, into controlled market activation and national strategic alignment. Every stakeholder engagement should create reusable institutional alignment that remains valuable after CyberDSA 2026.

**Immediate next move:** Activate Azrul Nazim first. Secure partnership and alliance sponsorship for the narrative and governing sequence, then progress deliberately through Zulfeka → Zaharudin → Wan Roshaimi → Bala. Approach Dr. Megat only after those CSM gates are materially aligned and the proposition is ready for national strategic discussion.
