## Slide Summary (12 slides)

### Slide 1: Title
- Aras Integrasi Teras AI Platform [INTERNAL ONLY]

### Slide 2: Vision Requirements
- Sovereign AI capability must deliver value, trust, and impact at scale
- 5 pillars: Reliable AI Services, Strong Governance, Data Sovereignty, Enterprise Adoption, Long-term Sustainability
- "These requirements cannot be achieved by GPU infrastructure alone"

### Slide 3: Integrated AI Ecosystem (Architecture)
- **5-layer architecture:**
  1. Users (Departmental Staff, Developers, Management, Platform Operators)
  2. Applications (Akal Suite, Akal Coder, Integrations, Vector Store)
  3. Governed AI Platform (Identity & Access, API Gateway, AI Guardrails, Observability & Monitoring, Audit & Compliance)
  4. AI Services & Foundation Models (LLM, RAG, Vision, Speech, OCR, Translation, Sentiment, Model Registry, Fine-tuning)
  5. Sovereign AI Infrastructure (GPU Compute, Storage, Network, Backup & DR, Physical Security, Security & Audit)

### Slide 4: AI Inferencing Service
- Teras transforms raw infrastructure into governed, enterprise-ready AI services through a single interface
- Intelligent model selection, routing, load balancing
- API Gateway: single governed entry point
- Service Catalogue: LLM, RAG, Vision, Speech, OCR, Translation, Sentiment
- Benefits: Simpler integration, optimised performance, stronger security, faster adoption, greater efficiency

### Slide 5: Performance Benchmarks (NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell)
- **Qwen3.5-397B (4 GPUs, Tensor-Parallel, NVFP4, vLLM):**
  - 1 user: 136 tok/s | 4 users: 342 tok/s | 8 users: 472 tok/s | 16 users: 605 tok/s
- **Qwen3.6-27B (2 GPUs, Tensor-Parallel, NVFP4, vLLM):**
  - 1 user: 169 tok/s | 4 users: 640 tok/s | 8 users: 1,400 tok/s | 16 users: 2,200 tok/s | 32 users: 2,800 tok/s
- Measured at BF16 without quantization on Teras AI Platform deployed on-premise

### Slide 6: Performance Beyond Hardware
- Optimised serving kernel: 20-25% higher throughput on identical GPUs
- Tiered model serving: routes each request to the right model
- Token usage attribution: by user, by application, by division
- Sovereign deployment: all inference, storage and logging remain within environment

### Slide 8: AI Where Employees Work
- Akal Assistant: natural language enterprise assistant
- Mobile: secure AI experience on the go
- Work: draft and analyse documents
- Developer APIs: embed AI capabilities in applications
- Browser: seamless web search and page assistance
- Integrations: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, MCMC Systems

### Slide 9: Governance at Enterprise Scale
- Identity & Access: RBAC, IdP Integration
- Security: SIEM Integration, Air-Gapped Deployment
- Compliance: Policy Enforcement, Immutable Audit Logs, Activity Monitoring, Regulatory Alignment
- Governance builds trust, enables adoption, reduces risk, drives value

### Slide 10: Long-Term Operations
- Managed Service: 24/7 operations, SLA-driven
- Capacity Planning: demand forecasting, right-sizing
- Monitoring: real-time health, usage/drift monitoring
- Model Refresh: performance evaluation, retraining, lifecycle management
- Training: role-based enablement, workshops, playbooks
- Technology Transfer: structured knowledge transfer, documentation, runbooks

### Slide 11: NSRD Solution
- Delivers unified infrastructure, AI services, governance, and enterprise applications
- Objectives → Outcomes: High Performance, Secure & Governed, Enterprise Adoption, Sustainable Growth
- Enterprise AI inferencing from day one, optimised serving, scalable compute
- Identity/access control, policy enforcement, end-to-end audit
- Ready-to-use AI applications, unified user experience, API-enabled development
- Managed operations, model evolution, training and knowledge transfer

## Strategic Implications

1. **Teras IS the infrastructure layer for VoronCitadel POC** — eliminates need for multi-tenant RLS build
2. **Governance boundary:** Teras governs AI usage (guardrails, identity, audit). VoronCitadel governs organizational risk (GRC, compliance frameworks, risk quantification)
3. **Deployment model:** VoronCitadel deploys as an application ON Teras (like Akal Suite)
4. **GPU hardware:** NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell on-premise
5. **Target customers:** MCMC (Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission), NSRD
