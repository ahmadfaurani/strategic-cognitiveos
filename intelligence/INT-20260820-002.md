# Intelligence Assessment

## Case Summary

Sayed Amir Muzzakkir Al Sayed Mohamad (43), political secretary to former Home Minister Datuk Seri Hamzah Zainudin, faces 3 corruption charges under MACC Act 2009 Section 16(a)(A):

1. **Accepting RM350,000** — Dec 4, 2020, Yayasan Tanah Aku office, Solaris Dutamas
2. **Accepting RM350,000** — Dec 16, 2020, same location
3. **Soliciting RM350,000** — Early Dec 2020, Nexuscorpgroup office, Shah Alam

Bribe source: Wan Azhar Mohamed Yusof (MD, Nexuscorpgroup Sdn Bhd) — paid to induce assistance for Nexuscorpgroup securing PDRM RMPNet tender (RM381.1M incl SST).

## Key Testimony (Witness 15 — Khairi Mat Jahya)

- Khairi (MD, Al Khairi Group Berhad) appointed consultant to Nexuscorpgroup for 5% fee → RM19M on RM359.5M contract
- Sayed introduced Khairi to Wan Azhar and arranged April 28, 2020 meeting with Hamzah
- Hamzah annotated supporting letter: "Datuk KSU for consideration"
- After contract awarded, Sayed demanded ~RM9M portion of RM19M consultancy fee
- Khairi refused verbal demands — no project details, documents, profile at time
- Wan Azhar requested dummy invoice for RM350,000 — Khairi prepared it to avoid balance payment withholding
- Wan Azhar paid RM350,000 to Sayed — Khairi shocked, never promised Sayed a cut
- Hamzah personally called Khairi to flag Sayed's misconduct before removing him

## Political Intelligence Significance

- **Hamzah Zainudin network:** Patronage structure exposed — political secretary leveraged ministerial access for rent extraction from police technology procurement
- **PDRM procurement:** RM381M RMPNet tender — political intermediation at ministerial aide level
- **Internal controls:** Hamzah's personal call suggests internal awareness of aide behaviour before formal action — pattern of informal management rather than institutional reporting
- **MACC enforcement:** Charges filed May 2024, trial Aug 2026 — 2-year gap, indicates deliberate case building
