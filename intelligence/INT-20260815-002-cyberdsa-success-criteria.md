# CyberDSA 2026 — Success Criteria Definition

**Mission:** CyberDSA 2026 Product Launch & Market Activation
**Event:** 5–7 October 2026, MITEC Kuala Lumpur
**Reference:** DEC-20260815-004

---

## Framework

Success criteria are organised across **6 dimensions**, each with **3 tiers**:

- **Minimum** — must be achieved or the mission fails
- **Target** — the expected successful outcome
- **Stretch** — exceeds expectation, creates strategic advantage

Each criterion has a defined owner, checkpoint alignment, and evidence standard.

## Evidence Standards

All criteria require **objective evidence** — not self-reported status. Acceptable evidence types:

- **Documents:** Signed-off scope docs, one-pagers, narratives, repository links
- **Logs:** Meeting calendars, outreach logs, follow-up logs, CRM exports
- **Demonstrations:** Bug-free walkthroughs, rehearsal completion, environment tests
- **Confirmations:** Written confirmation from external parties (CSM, CyberDSA organisers)
- **Sign-offs:** Named individual confirms acceptance (e.g., Hadri confirms handover)

"Ongoing" is not acceptable evidence for any criterion.
