## Executive Summary

This registry consolidates Malaysia's media landscape into three core deliverables:
1. **Expanded Source List** — All tiers, languages, and regions (formal + expanded universe)
2. **Contact Directory** — Available newsroom, editorial, and corporate contacts
3. **Ownership Mapping** — Corporate structures of major media conglomerates

**Baseline Statistics:**
- **Formal outlets:** ~100–150
- **Expanded universe:** 150–250+ (including regional, community, digital-native)
- **Languages:** Malay, English, Chinese, Tamil, Indigenous (Iban, Kadazan, etc.)
- **Major ownership clusters:** RTM (government), Media Prima, Astro, Media Chinese International, State governments

```
Government of Malaysia
  └── Ministry of Communications Malaysia
      └── Radio Televisyen Malaysia (RTM)
```

## 4. Regulatory Framework

### 4.1 Primary Regulator
- **MCMC (Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission)**
- **Governing Law:** Communications and Multimedia Act 1998
- **Licensing:** CASP (Content Applications Service Provider) for digital media

### 4.2 Print Media
- **Governing Law:** Printing Presses and Publications Act 1984
- **Requirement:** Annual publishing permits (renewable)

### 4.3 Broadcasting
- **RTM:** Government department (no separate licence)
- **Commercial TV/Radio:** Broadcasting licences from MCMC
