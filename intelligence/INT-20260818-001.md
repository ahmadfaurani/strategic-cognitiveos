# Intelligence Record

## Doctrine Overview

The R.I.S.I.K framework is a five-layer closed-cycle methodology for building resilience against information threats and narrative pressure. Authored by Prof. Madya Dr. Suhaimee Saabar (UiTM CMIWS), it is designed for practitioners of communication and open-source intelligence in the Malaysian context.

## Five-Layer Methodology

| Layer | Name | Purpose | Output Tool |
|-------|------|---------|-------------|
| R | Risikan | Intelligence gathering (OSINT/SOCMINT/HUMINT) | Borang Isyarat (Signal Form) |
| I | Isu | Issue identification and decomposition | Kad Isu (Issue Card) |
| S | Sentimen | Sentiment landscape assessment | Profil Sentimen |
| I | Influens | Actor/influence network mapping | Peta Aktor |
| K | Kontra-naratif | Counter-narrative response | Pelan Respons |

The cycle is closed — K feeds back into R. This enables effectiveness measurement: methods that cycle can show whether action was effective, not just whether action was taken.

## Four Gates [TAMBAHAN — proposed]

1. **Kelayakan (Qualification):** ≥2 independent signals or 1 grade A/B signal; working links; touches entity directly
2. **Keutamaan (Priority):** Triage scoring (sensitivity × reach = 1-5)
3. **Kewajaran (Appropriateness):** Is communication the right response, or is the real problem operational?
4. **Keberkesanan (Effectiveness):** Did the response work? (Cycle closure)

## Narrative Escalation Ladder

| Rung | Narrative | Facts work? |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Keluhan (Grievance) | Partly |
| 2 | Ketidakcekapan (Incompetence) | Yes |
| 3 | Keistimewaan (Favoritism) | Yes |
| 4 | Pengkhianatan (Betrayal) | Difficult — facts seen as evidence of cover-up |
| 5 | Konspirasi (Conspiracy) | No — denial strengthens narrative |

Window to act is before rung 4. After that, work shifts from correction to long-term trust-building.

## §2.5 AI in the Information Environment [TAMBAHAN]

### Core Principle: AI is NOT a Fourth Discipline
Disciplines are defined by source, not tool. AI is how information is processed, not where it comes from. "AI-INT" doesn't exist in any recognized intelligence doctrine.

### Four Ways AI Changes the Landscape
1. **AI as Tool** — translation, transcription, clustering, summarization. Accelerates Layers R and I(Influens). Does NOT replace judgment at any gate.
2. **AI as Threat Surface** — production cost collapsed, distribution cost did NOT. Don't elevate severity just because content is AI-generated; elevate for distribution.
3. **AI as New Audience** — 10% use chatbots for news (17% among 18-24). Click-through dropped 15%→8% with AI summaries. New question: "What does the AI system say about this entity?"
4. **Analyst as Attack Surface** — prompt injection (unmitigable per UK authority), hallucination (1,800+ fabricated citations), automation bias (mid-level users most at risk).

### Reference Poisoning (§2.5.4 — genuinely new threat)
LLM grooming: flooding information space so AI models absorb and repeat narratives. One network: 3.6M articles across 150 domains. 1/3 of chatbot responses repeated seeded narrative. Variable success rate — not a guaranteed weapon.

### AI Usage Rules (adapted from US intelligence community)
1. Accountability stays with analyst. AI output is not a source.
2. Every AI-originated claim traced to original source and verified.
3. Analyst must be able to review, question, and reject AI suggestions.
4. Declare to recipient when AI materially influences a product.
5. Treat monitored materials as untrusted when fed into AI tools.

## Source Grading

| Grade | Type | Treatment |
|-------|------|-----------|
| A | Official documents, statistical data | Basis of claims |
| B | Reputable mainstream media | Basis of claims |
| C | Partisan portals, identified influencers | Cross-verification needed |
| D | Secondary media, aggregators | Cross-verification needed |
| E | Anonymous with reviewable history | Phenomenon only |
| F | Screenshots without origin | Phenomenon only |

"E/F is evidence of what people believe, not evidence of what's true. Confusing these is the most embarrassing error."

## Three Governing Rules
1. No building before writing — every stage starts with written spec and acceptance criteria
2. No approval by demo alone — reviewer tests personally
3. Decisions must be written with reasons

## Asymmetric Error Principle
- Gate 1: False elevation worse than false negative (wasted analysis + response triggered)
- Layer I: Wrong issue definition makes all subsequent layers perfect but useless
- AI implication: AI should be biased toward precision over recall
