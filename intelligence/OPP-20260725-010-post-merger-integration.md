# Summary

CSCDC is a post-merger entity (PTPKM + CSM). Unit 2's mandate is cultural integration — ensuring unified messaging, shared culture, and internal cohesion across the merged workforce. DAF's USER.md profile identifies "Organisational Capability Building" as Workstream F, and his capability matrix rates organisational design at 4/5. Post-merger integration methodology is a strategic advisory opportunity.

# Strategic Significance

Post-merger integration is where most consolidations fail. The framework correctly identifies this risk by dedicating a full unit (Unit 2) to cultural integration. However, the functions listed (intranet, bulletin, townhalls, infosec awareness) are tactical, not strategic. The deeper integration challenge — unified workflows, shared identity, merged reporting lines, reconciled processes — requires organisational design expertise that goes beyond internal communication tools.

# Intelligence Type

Market — organisational design and change management opportunity

# Evidence

- Unit 2 explicitly mandates "penyatuan budaya kerja warga CSCDC pasca-penggabungan PTPKM dan CSM" (Page 8)
- Four functions: intranet, bulletin, townhalls, internal InfoSec awareness (Page 8)
- Framework acknowledges the merger as a key challenge by including it in the executive summary (Page 4)
- DAF's portfolio includes organisational capability building (Workstream F, PRJ-F001)

# Assessment

**[MEDIUM]** The framework treats cultural integration primarily as a communication problem (Unit 2 under Corporate Operations). The deeper organisational design challenge — process harmonisation, role clarification, team integration — may require external advisory. Aras Integrasi can position at the strategic level (integration methodology) rather than the tactical level (bulletin, townhalls).

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — The need is real and acknowledged. Whether CSCDC leadership sees it as an external advisory opportunity or handles it internally through HR/communication is uncertain.

# Implications

- Post-merger integration success determines the effectiveness of all 7 units
- If PTPKM and CSM cultures don't integrate, the communication division's outputs will reflect internal fragmentation
- DAF's organisational design experience (BU build-out, KPIs, delivery accountability) applies directly
- This is a strategic advisory opportunity, not a product or vendor opportunity

# Recommended Actions

- Prepare a post-merger integration methodology framework (beyond communication)
- Include assessment tools (cultural audit, workflow mapping, role clarification)
- Position as organisational design advisory, complementing Unit 2's communication function
