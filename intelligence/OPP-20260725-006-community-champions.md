# Summary

CSCDC must train 1,000 Cyber Security Community Champions across all Malaysian states in Year 1, with RM 200K allocated for training and management. DAF's political war room experience includes grassroots mobilisation, Duta Siber concepts, and community network activation. The methodology for recruiting, training, deploying, and managing distributed community networks transfers from political campaigning to cyber literacy.

# Strategic Significance

Community Champions is Pillar 4 (Community Resilience) in action — the framework's co-equal pillar for human-layer defence. The programme requires curriculum design, training infrastructure, geographic deployment, and ongoing management. DAF's experience building distributed community networks (political Duta Siber, RISIK models) provides a methodology template that few cybersecurity vendors can offer.

# Intelligence Type

Market — programme design and methodology transfer opportunity

# Evidence

- RM 200K for training and management (Page 17, Sector 4)
- RM 250K for literacy tour across 50 locations (Page 17, Sector 4)
- KPI: 1,000 Community Champions in Year 1 (Page 16)
- Unit 7 function 5: demographic literacy surveys (Page 10)
- Collaboration with KPM (Ministry of Education) and KPT (Ministry of Higher Education) required

# Assessment

**[MEDIUM]** RM 400K total (training + tour) for 1,000 champions across 14 states and 50 locations is lean. The programme design is likely undeveloped. Aras Integrasi can offer a methodology framework (recruitment → training → deployment → management → measurement) adapted from political mobilisation.

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — The budget and target are clear. Whether CSCDC will accept external programme design support vs developing internally is uncertain.

# Implications

- 1,000 champions across 14 states = ~70 per state — requiring state-level coordination
- Curriculum must cover deepfakes, scams, social engineering, identity theft (Page 10)
- KPM/KPT collaboration means curriculum must align with education standards
- Ongoing management (not just training) implies a sustained engagement model
- Demographic literacy surveys (function 5) require measurement infrastructure

# Recommended Actions

- Prepare Community Champions programme design framework (political → cyber adaptation)
- Include curriculum outline, training cascade model, and state-level deployment plan
- Position as methodology architect, not training vendor
