# Summary

CSCDC has allocated RM 120,000 for enterprise social listening and media monitoring (Meltwater/Brand24/CARMA). This budget is likely insufficient for a national government agency requiring 24/7 monitoring across multiple languages and platforms. The gap creates an opportunity for Aras Integrasi to provide managed social listening services or supplement the in-house capability.

# Strategic Significance

Social listening is Unit 3's core capability for brand management and Unit 1's media monitoring function. If underfunded, the entire Corporate Operations division's KPI (85% positive-to-neutral coverage, 25% quarterly engagement growth) is at risk. A managed service or shared infrastructure model could fill the gap at lower cost than CSCDC procuring enterprise licenses independently.

# Intelligence Type

Market — infrastructure provisioning opportunity

# Evidence

- RM 120K allocated (Page 17, Sector 2) — Meltwater enterprise licenses for government typically cost RM 200K–400K/year
- 24/7 monitoring requirement (Page 8, Unit 1 function 4)
- Sentiment analysis across BM/English/Chinese (multilingual requirement increases cost)
- KPI: >85% positive-to-neutral coverage measurement requires robust monitoring (Page 16)

# Assessment

**[MEDIUM]** The budget gap is clear and the capability need is documented. However, CSCDC may choose to share infrastructure with MCMC or JAPEN (inter-agency sharing is mentioned as a cost efficiency strategy). The opportunity depends on whether CSCDC procures independently or shares.

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — Budget gap is verifiable from the document. Whether CSCDC will accept external managed services vs building in-house is uncertain.

# Implications

- If CSCDC underfunds social listening, its KPIs become unmeasurable
- Managed service model could provide better value than CSCDC's own enterprise license
- Multilingual monitoring (BM/EN/CN) is a specialised requirement few vendors can meet
- Real-time crisis monitoring (Unit 4) depends on the same infrastructure

# Recommended Actions

- Include social listening capability in Aras Integrasi positioning brief
- Prepare cost comparison: managed service vs CSCDC direct procurement
- Explore inter-agency sharing models (MCMC/JAPEN) as alternative positioning
