# Summary

Unit 5 (Government & International Relations) is responsible for drafting speeches for the Prime Minister, Cabinet briefing papers, talking points for JPM Ministers and KSN, international diplomacy reports for ASEAN/UN/ITU, and special briefings for Parliament members. This is a high-trust, high-sensitivity function that requires deep understanding of both cybersecurity and government communication protocols.

# Strategic Significance

G2G briefing support is a relationship-building entry point. If Aras Integrasi can support Unit 5 with strategic content development (cyber policy papers, international positioning documents, parliamentary briefing materials), this creates direct engagement with JPM-level stakeholders — the most senior tier of DAF's target ecosystem.

# Intelligence Type

Market — strategic advisory and content development opportunity

# Evidence

- Unit 5 function 1: PM speeches, Cabinet briefing papers, KSN talking points (Page 9)
- Unit 5 function 2: MoU/MoA publicity for international cyber agreements (Page 9)
- Unit 5 function 3: International diplomacy reports for ASEAN, UN, ITU (Page 9)
- Unit 5 function 4: Parliamentary briefings on digital security (Page 9)
- No specific budget line for Unit 5 operations beyond staffing

# Assessment

**[LOW]** This opportunity is lower confidence because G2G speechwriting and Cabinet briefing are typically handled internally by trusted civil servants, not external consultants. However, specialist content support (cyber policy analysis, international positioning strategy, ASEAN cyber diplomacy frameworks) could be positioned as advisory rather than drafting.

# Confidence Level

**LOW** — Government speechwriting and Cabinet papers are sensitive functions unlikely to be outsourced. However, specialist cyber policy advisory is a narrower entry point.

# Implications

- Direct speechwriting is unlikely — but strategic content advisory is possible
- International diplomacy reports (ASEAN, ITU) require cyber policy expertise that may be scarce in civil service
- Parliamentary briefings need clear, non-technical explanations — DAF's translation skill applies
- This is a relationship-deepening opportunity, not a primary revenue opportunity

# Recommended Actions

- Position as cyber policy advisory (not speechwriting service)
- Offer to support international positioning documents (ASEAN cyber diplomacy)
- Prepare sample parliamentary briefing format on a current cyber topic
