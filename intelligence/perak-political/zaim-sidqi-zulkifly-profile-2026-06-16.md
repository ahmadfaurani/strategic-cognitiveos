## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

In the 2025 PKR party elections, he received backing from **22 AMK branches across Perak** to contest for Ahli Majlis Pimpinan Pusat AMK (Central Council Member) for the 2025-2028 term, indicating strong state-level support.
