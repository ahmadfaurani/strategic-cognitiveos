# Summary

The MAPO Enforcement Committee Meeting Bil. 1/2026 (3 September 2026), chaired by PDRM CID Director CP Datuk M. Kumar, publicly articulated three strategic enforcement directives that constitute a capability demand signal:

1. **Result-based enforcement** — shift from output metrics (case counts) to impact metrics (victim rescue, prosecution, syndicate disruption)
2. **Intelligence-driven enforcement** — proactive, risikan-based, high-impact operations
3. **Inter-agency information sharing** — strengthened cooperation, operational coordination, strategic partnerships

## Strategic Significance

This is not bureaucratic routine. A senior PDRM enforcement leader publicly articulating methodological gaps and capability needs creates:

- **Demand space** for analytical/intelligence products and inter-agency coordination platforms
- **Institutional pathway** via KDN adjacency — same ecosystem as NACSA (STK-20260819-001) and JDN (ORG-20260815-005)
- **Product positioning** alignment — VoronDRQ (analytical platform) and ChainSentry capabilities map to "intelligence-driven enforcement" demand
- **Governance alignment** — "result-based" metrics framing aligns with DAF's Workstream C governance trend advocacy
