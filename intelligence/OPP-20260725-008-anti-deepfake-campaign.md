# Summary

CSCDC has allocated RM 500,000 — the single largest campaign budget line item — for a National Anti-Deepfake & AI Scams Campaign spanning TV prime-time, digital billboards, and micro-targeted social media. This is the largest single-campaign opportunity in the framework and sits at the intersection of cybersecurity, AI awareness, and strategic communications.

# Strategic Significance

At RM 500K, this is the most funded campaign in the framework — signalling that CSCDC leadership sees AI-enabled threats (deepfakes, AI scams) as the highest-priority public awareness issue. This aligns directly with DAF's Sovereign AI workstream: public understanding of AI risks is a precondition for sovereign AI adoption.

The campaign requires:
- Creative strategy (message development for multiple audiences)
- Multi-channel production (TV, billboard, social, micro-targeted)
- Audience segmentation (youth, elderly, B40, urban, rural)
- Effectiveness measurement (awareness lift, behaviour change)

# Intelligence Type

Market — campaign strategy and production opportunity

# Evidence

- RM 500K allocation (Page 17, Sector 4) — largest single campaign line
- Multi-channel scope: TV prime-time, digital billboards, micro-targeted social (Page 17)
- Unit 7 function 2: national awareness on AI deepfake scams, digital identity theft, social engineering (Page 10)
- KPI: 30% improvement in public cyber literacy scores (Page 16)

# Assessment

**[HIGH]** This is the largest campaign budget and the most strategically aligned opportunity. DAF's combined cybersecurity + AI + strategic communications expertise is rare. The campaign also creates public-facing visibility — success here builds Aras Integrasi's brand as a cyber-AI communication partner.

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — The budget is clear, but whether CSCDC will run this campaign in-house (Unit 7 + Unit 3) or seek external campaign strategy support is unknown.

# Implications

- RM 500K for a national multi-channel campaign is workable but not lavish — efficiency matters
- Micro-targeted social media requires sophisticated audience segmentation and platform expertise
- TV prime-time creative must be high quality — deepfake demonstrations, scam reconstructions
- Campaign effectiveness measurement ties directly to the 30% literacy KPI
- This campaign could become a recurring annual programme (not one-time)

# Recommended Actions

- Prepare a campaign strategy framework (audience segmentation, message architecture, channel mix)
- Include creative concepts for deepfake awareness (demonstration content)
- Position as campaign strategy partner, with Unit 7 executing in-house
- Connect to Sovereign AI narrative: public AI risk awareness enables sovereign AI adoption
