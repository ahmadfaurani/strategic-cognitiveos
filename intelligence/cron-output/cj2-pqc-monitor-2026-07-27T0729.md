### Finding 11: NIST PQC Standards (FIPS 203/204/205) + IR 8547 — Deprecation by 2035
- **Source:** https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography (also https://csrc.nist.gov/News/2024/postquantum-cryptography-fips-approved)
- **Date:** 2024-08-13 (standards); IR 8547 transition guidance ongoing
- **Summary:** NIST released the three finalized PQC standards in August 2024: **FIPS 203 (ML-KEM — Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA — Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA — Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature).** NIST IR 8547 sets the transition: deprecate quantum-vulnerable algorithms and **remove them from standards by 2035**; high-risk systems must transition much earlier. Additional algorithms in progress: Falcon (digital signature) and HQC (4th-round KEM selection, 2025). NIST explicitly: "ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, and ML-KEM... can and should be put into use now." NCCoE Migration to PQC project provides vendor interoperability guidance. These are the algorithm families the Malaysia PQC Sandbox is testing (CyberSecurity Malaysia's 4 PQC families: lattice-based, code-based, hash-based, multivariate).
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-001 (CRITICAL — algorithms being tested), PIR-OPP005-006 (international — NIST baseline), PIR-OPP005-008 (timeline — 2035 global deprecation)
