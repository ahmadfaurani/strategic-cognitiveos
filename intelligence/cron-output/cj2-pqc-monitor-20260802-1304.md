## Executive Summary

This cycle produced **high-value Malaysian national intelligence** that materially advances resolution of the PIR set. NACSA's directly-extracted pages confirm that Malaysia has, since late 2025, established the formal policy and implementation architecture for PQC migration:

- **MyKriptografi** — National Cryptography Policy, Cabinet-approved **28 November 2025**, explicitly framed as Malaysia's "Digital Shield" against the **quantum computing era**, succeeding DKN 2013.
- **MyKriptografi Action Plan 2026–2030** — operational roadmap of **4 core pillars, 12 strategies, 32 programmes, 80 activities**, explicitly targeting "the quantum computing era" and NCII + industry + academia.
- **MCSS 2025–2030** — theme *"Sovereign Cyber Security Resilience"* — names **Quantum Computing Threats** (RSA/ECC vulnerable, "harvest now, decrypt later," PQC migration needed) as a top-tier strategic challenge.
- **Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854)** gazetted 26 June 2024 provides the legislative backbone; **9 directives** issued.

These documents constitute the public-policy envelope in which the **PQC Sandbox** (per the brief, RM 300K launch conference, CSCDC under JPM/MKN/NACSA) almost certainly nests — most plausibly under MyKriptografi Action Plan **Pillar 3** (PKTN — National Trusted Cryptographic Products adoption among NCII + industry) and/or **Pillar 4** (RDCI). The term "PQC Sandbox" and "CSCDC" did **not** surface in any public source this cycle — consistent with a pre-launch/internal designation or an as-yet-unindexed initiative.

Internationally, the cycle captured decisive 2026 developments: **US EO 14412 + OMB M-26-15** (federal PQC migration deadlines: key establishment **2030**, signatures **2031**, full **2035**; contractor FIPS-PQC mandate by 2030); **NIST** HAWK algorithm withdrawal (28 July 2026, AI-discovered vulnerability); **ANSSI/France** (2027 PQC qualification gate, first Thales/Samsung lattice-based certifications, G7 PQC declarations June 2026); **EU Coordinated PQC Roadmap** (23 June 2025); and the **PQCC** (2026 PQC Forum, 14 Sept 2026, MITRE McLean).

**Bottom line:** Malaysia's PQC effort is real, Cabinet-mandated, and time-aligned (2026–2030) with the global migration window — but the specific "PQC Sandbox" programme (scope, budget beyond RM 300K, industry engagement model, conference details, ASEAN positioning) remains **not publicly disclosed**.

### Finding 6: NIST PQC Standards Finalised — FIPS 203/204/205 + HQC + HAWK Withdrawal
- **Source:** https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography ; https://www.nist.gov/pqc
- **Date:** Standards released August 2024; HQC selected March 2025; HAWK withdrawn 28 July 2026
- **Summary:** NIST finalised three PQC FIPS in August 2024: **FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, key encapsulation)**, **FIPS 204 (ML-DSA, digital signatures)**, **FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA, stateless hash-based signatures)**. **HQC** selected March 2025 as a fifth (backup) algorithm for general encryption. Nine candidates advanced to the third round for additional digital signatures (May 2026). Notably, **HAWK** (a lattice-based signature candidate) was **withdrawn on 28 July 2026** after an AI-discovered vulnerability (Anthropic) — no impact on finalised ML-KEM/ML-DSA (different math). NIST IR 8547 sets the transition timeline: quantum-vulnerable algorithms deprecated and removed from standards by **2035**. NCCoE running a Migration to PQC project with industry/academia. IETF is incorporating PQC into TLS.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-001, PIR-OPP005-006, PIR-OPP003-005
- **Confidence:** HIGH
