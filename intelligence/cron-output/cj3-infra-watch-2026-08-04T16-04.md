- **Source:** https://captaincompliance.com/education/malaysia-data-localization/ ; https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-malaysia (29 Oct 2024)
- **Date:** PDPA amended June 2024 (DPO appointment, data portability, breach notification, biometric data as sensitive); Cyber Security Act August 2024
- **Summary:** Malaysia has **no strict data localisation requirement** — most personal data can be transferred abroad. Sector-specific rules apply (BNM for finance). For a government portal handling classified government data, the OSA/Rahsia classification regime governs, not PDPA.

## Strategic Implications for Aras Integrasi / CSCDC

1. **Portal is an UPGRADE, not a new build** — leverage existing PTPKM crypto + CSM systems. Position Aras Integrasi as the capability-builder integrating inherited assets, not displacing them. This strengthens the "not a vendor" framing.

2. **MyGPKI is non-negotiable for authentication** — design portal auth around MyGPKI integration from day one. FIDO2 may be a future enhancement layer but is not in current government standards.

3. **PQC-readiness is now a differentiator** — building crypto-agility + hybrid cryptography into the portal aligns directly with the 29 Oct 2025 national roadmap and positions CSCDC as a PQC reference implementation. This is a credible "capability-builder" value proposition.

4. **Cloud choice is classification-driven** — MyGovCloud@PDSA (sovereign) for Rahsia/classified tiers; MyGovCloud@CFA (AWS/Google/MS/TM) for lower tiers. A hybrid deployment is the likely optimum.

5. **RM 180K is not a hard cap** — Tier 3 procurement threshold allows supplementation via Controlling Officer committee; CSCDC's JPM institutional placement supports operating-budget supplementation. The real envelope is the RM 4,005,000 Phase 1 ceiling.

6. **Social listening budget (RM 120K)** — Brand24 Enterprise (~RM 80K) fits with margin; Meltwater Pro + public-sector discount is viable; CARMA mid-tier. **Capability gap exists** — neither MCMC nor JAPEN has a dedicated social listening/sentiment platform, so CSCDC's RM 120K fills a genuine national capability gap, not a duplicate.

7. **11 CNII sectors = portal audience scope** — portal must serve all 11 sector leads + their entities, with widely varying technical maturity (Tier 1–5). Design for tier-aware onboarding.

8. **Certification pathway = MyCC/Common Criteria** for ICT product security assurance; CCRA-recognised internationally. NACSA-licensed cybersecurity service providers mandatory for assessments.
