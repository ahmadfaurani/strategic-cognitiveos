### Finding 1: NACSA Chief Executive Order No. 9 — Legally Mandated PQC Migration Data Preparation
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/doc/Arahan%20KE%20NACSA%20No.%209.pdf
- **Date:** Effective 22 September 2025 (signed by Ir. Dr. Megat Zuhairy Megat Tajuddin, CEO NACSA)
- **Summary:** CEO No. 9, issued under Section 13 of the Cyber Security Act 2024 [Act 854], legally mandates all NCII entities to prepare cryptographic inventory data for PQC migration. NCII entities must submit a "PQC Migration Implementation Form" (Appendix A) covering all cryptographic assets (hardware, firmware, OS, applications, services) **within 3 months of written notice** from CEO NACSA. PTPKM is designated as coordinator under NACSA supervision to collect and analyse migration data. The directive requires NCII sector heads to coordinate within their sectors. This is the first hard regulatory instrument driving PQC adoption in Malaysia, though it focuses on data preparation rather than algorithm deployment deadlines.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-008 (mandated timeline for CNII/NCII operators)
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED] — primary source document from NACSA

- **Source:** https://acaderesearch.com/france-anssi-post-quantum-cryptography-mandate-2027/
- **Date:** 16 June 2026
- **Summary:** France's ANSSI announced the world's first hard PQC procurement timetable on 16 June 2026 at France Quantum: from 2027, ANSSI stops certifying security products lacking quantum-resistant encryption; from 2030, businesses expected to procure only quantum-safe products. ### Finding 16: ASEAN PQC Landscape — Assessment-Action Gap, Regulatory Pressure as Driver
- **Source:** https://www.aibp.sg/articles/post-quantum-crypto-asean
- **Date:** 29 June 2026
- **Summary:** AIBP analysis (drawing from a Bangkok roundtable with IBM, 9 June 2026) reports ~40% of Asia-Pacific organisations have conducted quantum risk analysis but fewer than 1 in 10 have a formal programme. Regional status (mid-2026): Japan and Singapore are frontrunners (assessment → active migration); Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, South Korea have awareness but organised action not begun. The key driver of the gap is regulatory pressure, not capability — Thailand required a central bank governor directive to convert awareness to action. Quantum threat timeline has shrunk dramatically (2019: ~20M qubits to break RSA-2048; March 2026: ~10,000 qubits for certain ECC attacks). IBM's Umut Cikla: "2026 is the year" to reach quantum-safe state by 2030. KBTG's Yarnvith Raksri: the goal should be "crypto agility," not one-time PQC migration.
