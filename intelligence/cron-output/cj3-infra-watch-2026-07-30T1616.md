- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/

### Finding 8: Digital Signature Act 1997 + 5 Licensed Certification Authorities (MyGPKI Ecosystem)
- **Source:** https://www.mcmc.gov.my/en/sectors/digital-signature ; https://www.mcmc.gov.my/en/sectors/digital-signature/list-of-licensees
- **Date:** DSA 1997 in force since 1 Oct 1998; CA licences current (2024–2030)
- **Summary:** MCMC administers the Digital Signature Act 1997 [Act 562] and oversees licensed Certification Authorities (CAs). Digital signatures provide Confidentiality, Identity Authentication, Non-repudiation, and Integrity. **Five CAs are currently active** (all also hold Repository + Date/Time Stamp recognition): (1) **Pos Digicert Sdn Bhd** (licence to 24 Dec 2030), (2) **MSC Trustgate.Com Sdn Bhd** (to 24 Jul 2028), (3) **Raffcomm Technologies Sdn Bhd** (to 30 Apr 2027), (4) **TM Technology Services Sdn Bhd** (to 31 Jul 2027), (5) **Vista Kencana Sdn Bhd** (to 27 Oct 2028). These CAs constitute the MyGPKI PKI ecosystem — the established authentication model for government portals. A custom or FIDO2-only model would be non-standard; PKI/MyGPKI via these licensed CAs is the compliant path.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-009 (authentication model — PKI/MyGPKI)

## Actionable Implications for CSCDC / Aras Integrasi

1. **Encryption & classification are now statutorily framed.** Any encrypted alert portal must use PKTN-evaluated cryptographic products (classified Public→Top Secret by SPPPKTN) per MyKriptografi (Nov 2025) and the Cyber Security Act 2024. Build this compliance into the portal spec from day one.
2. **Authentication = MyGPKI PKI via one of 5 licensed CAs** (Pos Digicert, MSC Trustgate, Raffcomm, TM Technology Services, Vista Kencana). FIDO2/custom would be non-standard; integrate MyGPKI, optionally layer FIDO2 as second factor.
3. **Portal should integrate with NC4/NCCMP** (NACSA's command/crisis structures) rather than duplicate them — alignment, not parallel build.
4. **Cloud placement governed by National Cloud Computing Policy (Jun 2025)** — retrieve full policy text to determine sovereign-vs-certified-third-party carve-out before architectural commitment.
5. **Social listening budget feasibility:** RM 120K comfortably covers Brand24 Enterprise; treat Meltwater as needing a direct quote (contact-sales). Flag Brand24's weaker non-English (Bahasa Malaysia) sentiment as a capability gap.
6. **Procurement route:** ePerolehan (eP) for tender/quotation, or eGPA if MOF approval needed. Investigate whether any social-listening vendor holds an existing MOF panel certificate to shorten procurement.
