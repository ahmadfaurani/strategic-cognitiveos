### 1. CSCDC Identity & Mandate (PIR-CSCDC-006)

### 2. Sovereign AI + PQC Convergence (PIR-OPP005-005)
The convergence is no longer aspirational — it is institutionalised. Three data points in July 2026 alone: (1) NQAIC MoU (govt), (2) CYDES sandbox re-launch with PM emphasis on mastering AI + cryptography, (3) VGAIN Cloud (private, post-quantum-ready sovereign AI). ### 3. Industry Engagement Window (PIR-INIT-CSCDC-007, PIR-OPP005-003)
The pilot cohort model is established and the first cohort (30+ companies) is already presenting POCs. New entrants would join as a subsequent cohort. The RM 300K Strategic Conference allocation suggests CSCDC is planning a conference-format engagement vehicle — likely an expansion/visibility event building on ICE-PQC 2025 and CYDES 2026.

### 4. Regulatory Risk Asymmetry (PIR-OPP005-008)
Malaysia's framework is non-binding (assess/report only via EO No. 9), contrasting sharply with binding US/EU/UK timelines. This creates a window for voluntary early movers but signals that hard mandates will eventually follow international precedent (likely 2030 horizon, per roadmap). KAZ-SIGN sovereignty play faces financial-sector resistance in favour of NIST standards.

### 5. PQC-Ready Portal Recommendation (PIR-OPP003-005)
Given the HNDL threat explicitly cited by Malaysia's own leadership and the sandbox's focus on PQC certificates and digital signatures, any encrypted alert portal handling long-confidentiality data should be PQC-ready (or crypto-agile) from launch. The sandbox's PKI/certificate focus means PQC-ready tooling and vendor support will mature within the programme window.
