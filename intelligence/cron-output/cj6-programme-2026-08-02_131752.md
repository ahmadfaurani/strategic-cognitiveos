## Strategic Implications for Aras Integrasi

1. **Curriculum does not need to start from zero.** CyberSAFE, My Cyber Hero, and the APAC Cybersecurity Fund provide modular, partly-accredited content (PAJSK, Global ACE). An Aras Integrasi "Community Champions" curriculum could integrate these with its Academy platform and AISLE-style sovereign-governed delivery.
2. **KPM collaboration is already live** (My Cyber Hero supporting partner). KPT linkage is available via existing university MoUs (UKM, UiTM, UTeM, UTHM, MSU, TATI UC) and MSCR. Aras Integrasi's YDN/SBP pilot establishes an education-channel precedent.
3. **The "champion" concept has a Malaysian precedent** (CyberSAFE Ambassadors, 3-4 per school). Scaling to 1,000 champions is a credible, fundable proposition — especially using a train-the-trainer multiplier model (cf. ACF's 12 trainers → 600,000 trainees goal).
4. **Certification scaffolding exists** (PAJSK marks, Global ACE, CyberGuru). A Community Champions certification could stack onto these.
5. **CSCDC's G2G/ASEAN posture is strong and active**, giving Aras Integrasi a credible institutional anchor (NACSA/CSCDC) for any programme pitching sovereign, locally-governed curriculum delivery — aligning with Aras Integrasi's "Sovereign AI" brand.
6. **RM 100K is modest** relative to international comparators (UK £187M, Australia ~A$7M, ACF 12-country fund). Industry co-funding (BlackBerry, Huawei, IBM model) and ASEAN/regional capacity-building grants are the realistic scaling path.
