## Executive Summary

This cycle produced substantial, high-confidence intelligence across all 13 PIRs. Malaysia's PQC Sandbox Proof-of-Concept Programme has been operational since November 2025, with a **grant-based selected cohort model** (not an open call or invited consortium). The National PQC Migration Plan 2025-2030 was officially launched November 12, 2025, at ICE-PQC 2025 at UPM Serdang. The Cyber Security Cryptology Development Centre (CSCDC) was subsequently established June 4, 2026, under PM Anwar Ibrahim's witness. Malaysia has explicitly positioned the initiative as an **ASEAN regional leadership** play — it is the first Southeast Asian country to publish a structured national PQC framework. The roadmap is **non-binding** with no mandatory deadlines for CNII operators. Sovereign AI infrastructure was launched May 2025 (MCI Group), creating a direct intersection with PQC for digital sovereignty. Internationally, NIST standards (FIPS 203/204/205) form the algorithmic backbone; the US issued binding federal mandates (EO 14412, OMB M-26-15) in June 2026; Singapore, EU, UK, Australia, and Germany have parallel but divergent approaches.

### Finding 7: NIST PQC Standards Finalised — FIPS 203/204/205 (August 2024); HQC Selected (March 2025)
- **Source:** https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography/post-quantum-cryptography-standardization; https://www.nist.gov/pqc
- **Date:** August 2024 (standards); March 2025 (HQC)
- **Summary:** NIST finalised three PQC standards on August 13, 2024: FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, lattice-based key establishment), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA, lattice-based digital signatures), and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA, hash-based signatures). A fourth algorithm, HQC (code-based), was selected for standardisation on March 11, 2025. NIST's PQC project leads the global effort to secure electronic information against the quantum threat. NIST IR 8547 (Initial Public Draft) proposes deprecating quantum-vulnerable public-key crypto after 2030 and disallowing after 2035. These NIST standards form the algorithmic backbone of Malaysia's PQC Sandbox and Migration Plan.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-001, PIR-OPP005-006
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED]

### Finding 8: US Federal PQC Mandate — EO 14412 + OMB M-26-15 (June 2026)
- **Source:** https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/securing-the-nation-against-advanced-cryptographic-attacks/; https://postquantum.com/post-quantum/us-federal-pqc-mandate-2026/
- **Date:** 22-24 June 2026
- **Summary:** The US transformed its PQC landscape from planning to enforceable mandate in June 2026. Executive Order 14412 (June 22) sets hard deadlines: key establishment (ML-KEM) migration by December 31, 2030, and digital signatures (ML-DSA/SLH-DSA) by December 31, 2031, for all federal high-value assets and high-impact systems. OMB M-26-15 (June 24) provides a five-phase migration timeline (2026-2035) and requires agency PQC Migration Plans due ~October 22, 2026. NSPM-12 (June 12) re-established the Committee on National Security Systems with binding directive authority, making CNSA 2.0 enforceable. The NSA's CNSA 2.0 mandates all new NSS acquisitions be quantum-safe by 2027. This US mandate creates international pressure for Malaysia and ASEAN to align.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-006, PIR-OPP005-008
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED]

### Finding 9: Global PQC Divergence — NSA, BSI, ANSSI Have Conflicting Requirements
- **Source:** https://postquantum.com/cnsa-2-0/global-pqc-requirements/; https://navigator.quantumgateway.foundation/pqc-efforts.html
- **Date:** May 2026 / March 2026 (last updated)
- **Summary:** Despite NIST finalising standards, no single global PQC standard exists. NSA's CNSA 2.0 mandates pure PQC (no hybrid as endpoint), excludes SLH-DSA and FN-DSA, and requires Level 5 parameters. Germany's BSI mandates hybrid mode (PQC + classical) permanently, recommends FrodoKEM and Classic McEliece alongside ML-KEM, and includes multi-tree SLH-DSA variants. France's ANSSI endorses FN-DSA (which NSA excludes). The EU Coordinated Implementation Roadmap (June 2025) sets milestones: national PQC plans by end 2026, high-risk systems secured by end 2030, full transition by 2035. Australia's ASD targets classical crypto elimination by end 2030. UK phases: planning by 2028, critical systems by 2031, full migration by 2035. China is developing entirely independent PQC standards. Malaysia aligns with NIST but has no specific algorithm mandates.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-006, PIR-OPP005-008
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED]
