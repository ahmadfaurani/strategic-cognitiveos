## Key Analytical Assessments

### 1. Malaysia-Singapore ASEAN PQC Convergence and Divergence (PIR-OPP005-010, PIR-OPP005-006)
Both Malaysia and Singapore finalised major quantum-safe instruments within a 23-day window in July 2026: Singapore's CSA published the Quantum-Safe Handbook V1 on 16 July; Malaysia re-launched the PQC Sandbox at CYDES on 9 July. However, the approaches diverge significantly:
- **Malaysia**: NACSA-led, invited consortium model, PQC Sandbox testing infrastructure, sovereign algorithm (KAZ-SIGN), non-binding strategic framework, Cabinet-approved MyKriptografi policy, no public-facing readiness tool.
- **Singapore**: Tri-agency (CSA+GovTech+IMDA), public consultation process, CII owner requirements (regulatory), Quantum Readiness Index self-assessment tool, commercial deployment (Singtel QSN), no sovereign algorithm.

Malaysia leads on sandbox testing infrastructure and sovereign algorithm development; Singapore leads on regulatory maturity, public engagement, and commercial deployment. For Malaysia's ASEAN convenor positioning, this creates a competitive dynamic — Malaysia is first with a sandbox and roadmap, but Singapore has the more internationally-recognised governance model. The CSCDC Strategic Conference could serve as the venue to differentiate Malaysia's value proposition (sovereign algorithm, testing infrastructure, 11 NCII sector coverage).
