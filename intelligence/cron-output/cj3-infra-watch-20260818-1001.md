### PIR-OPP003-005: PQC Readiness
- **Priority:** High
- **Previous Status:** Resolved
- **Current Status:** RESOLVED (Confirmed and Deepened)
- **Finding:** Malaysia is the **first Southeast Asian country** to publish a structured national PQC transition framework. The National PQC Readiness Roadmap was announced 29 October 2025 by the Ministry of Digital and NACSA during the PKI Consortium's Post-Quantum Cryptography Conference in Kuala Lumpur (600+ participants from 30+ countries). The roadmap has three pillars: (1) Technical Readiness — 2025-2030 migration plan, proof-of-concept PQC deployments, **sandbox initiative for testing quantum-safe implementations in government systems**; (2) Strategic Alignment — alignment with NIST and EU standards, hybrid cryptography adoption, cryptographic agility frameworks; (3) Human Readiness — workforce development with global PQC expertise. NACSA Chief Executive Ir. Dr. Megat Zuhairy emphasized it as a strategic framework, not a regulatory mandate (non-binding, no deadlines). CyberSecurity Malaysia has developed a PQC Migration Framework. The PQC Sandbox is directly referenced in Pillar 1 of the national roadmap — confirming the connection between the encrypted alert portal and the PQC Sandbox initiative.
- **Source:** https://postquantum.com/security-pqc/malaysia-nacsa-pqc-readiness-roadmap/ (PostQuantum.com, 31 Oct 2025); https://www.nacsa.gov.my/kriptografi-view.php (NACSA, last updated 3 Apr 2026); https://www.cybersecurity.my/portal-main/services/post-quantum-overview (CyberSecurity Malaysia, 2026)
- **Confidence:** High (multiple independent sources, including NACSA official + industry analysis + CyberSecurity Malaysia)
- **Analysis:** PQC-readiness from launch is confirmed as the national strategic direction, not a regulatory mandate. The portal should be PQC-aware (hybrid cryptography) from launch to align with the 2025-2030 roadmap. The PQC Sandbox (Pillar 1) is the testing ground — the portal can be a pilot deployment within the Sandbox framework.

## Cross-PIR Synthesis

**Theme 1: PQC-Readiness is the Strategic Spine**
Three PIRs (003-003, 003-005, 003-009) converge on a single conclusion: the national cryptography infrastructure is being modernized for the quantum era. MyKriptografi (Cabinet-approved Nov 2025), the National PQC Readiness Roadmap (Oct 2025), and PKTN evaluation all point to PQC-aware design as the baseline expectation. Any portal built in 2026 without PQC-hybrid architecture will be non-compliant with the national direction by 2028-2030.

**Theme 2: Government Cloud is the Only Hosting Path**
PIR-003-007 confirms a dual-tier model: MyGovCloud@PDSA for classified content, 4 Panel CSPs for less-sensitive content. The CFA provides bulk discounts and value-add. There is no alternative hosting path for a government security portal.

**Theme 3: Budget Insufficiency is Structural**
PIR-003-006 reveals that RM 180K is 15-35% of the required investment. This is not a negotiation gap — it's a structural misalignment between budget allocation and infrastructure requirements. The phase-gated procurement model is the only viable path.

**Theme 4: Social Listening Capability Gap is Confirmed**
PIR-OPP001-002 confirms that no Malaysian government agency (MCMC, JAPEN, or others) has enterprise social listening/sentiment analysis infrastructure. MCMC's ONSA is regulatory enforcement. The RM 120K budget for CSCDC is a greenfield procurement, not an augmentation.

**Theme 5: NC4 is the Existing Alert Infrastructure**
PIR-003-004 confirms NC4 as the active alert distribution portal. The CSCDC encrypted alert portal is a complementary new build, layering encryption-hardened distribution on top of NC4's coordination model. This means the portal has an established user base (NCII ICTSOs) but needs a new encrypted channel.
