### Finding 2: [NEW/DEPTH] NACSA EO9 Implementation-Friction Analysis — Ecosystem Not Ready
- **Source:** https://astralcrest.substack.com/p/nacsa-chief-executive-order-no-9
- **Date:** 8 March 2026 (analysis published)
- **Summary:** A practitioner analysis of **NACSA Chief Executive Order No. 9 (2025)** — the directive under the Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854) mandating National Critical Information Infrastructure (NCII) entities to assess and report cryptographic readiness — surfaces five implementation-friction observations directly material to migration realism:
  1. **HNDL threat known but not felt** — organisations are reactive; "harvest now, decrypt later" is understood but not yet driving urgency.
  2. **Heavy uncontrollable dependencies** — PQC migration success depends on hardware vendors (databases, HSMs, networking), adjacent connected parties (e.g. payment systems), and commercial software vendor timelines the entity cannot control.
  3. **Sovereign crypto adoption friction** — **KAZ-SIGN** (UPM-developed PQC digital signature) is being pushed by NACSA via the PQC Sandbox, but as of writing remains **not on the MyKripto AKSA (approved) list**, still under AKBA evaluation; **financial sector prefers tried-and-tested NIST standards**; future government tenders must specify KAZ-SIGN support (not necessarily enforced/used).
  4. **Partial cryptographic inventories** — organisations inventory only readily-available assets (key management, certificates, secrets, CLM/HSM/cloud/CI-CD); cryptographic assets exist across all OSI layers (physical root-of-trust → application) and are being missed.
  5. **Ecosystem not ready** — financial sector needs collective vendor/bank/acquirer upgrade; **no Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) mandate** imposes PQC-facilitating standards; e.g. **TLS 1.3 not enforced** for critical payment transport security.
  Net assessment: EO9 risks being treated as a compliance tick-box rather than a strategic move unless regulatory push (especially from BNM) and ecosystem-wide coordination follow.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-008 (CNII mandate — confirms EO9 is the mandate vehicle but ecosystem/BNM push absent), PIR-OPP005-001 (sovereign-vs-NIST algorithm tension concretised), PIR-OPP005-009 (talent/eco readiness gap — partial inventories, uncontrollable vendor dependencies), PIR-OPP005-003 (participation model — confirms Sandbox is the KAZ-SIGN test/embed vehicle)
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM (single analyst source, AI-grammar-checked; corroborates the KAZ-SIGN status already captured)
- **Tag:** [UNVERIFIED] (analyst opinion; official EO9 text not re-extracted this cycle)
