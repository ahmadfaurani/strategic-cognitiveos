### Finding 2: ONSA Online Safety (Period) Regulations 2025 — 24-Hour Takedown Mandate + AIFA Chatbot Stats + Safe Internet Campaign Reach
- **Source:** https://mcmc.bernama.com/news.php?id=2581673 (Bernama, via MCMC)
- **Date:** 16 July 2026 (written Dewan Rakyat reply)
- **Summary:** Under the Online Safety (Period) Regulations 2025 (subsidiary to the Online Safety Act 2025 / Act 866), licensed service providers must make **high-priority content (e.g. financial fraud) inaccessible within 24 hours** for assessment/verification. From 1 Jan 2022 to 31 May 2026, MCMC submitted **63,215 requests** to remove false content; **54,626 (86%) successfully taken down**. MCMC's **AIFA (Artificial Intelligence Fact-check Assistant) Chatbot** (launched 28 Jan 2025, supporting Sebenarnya.my) processed **197,403 messages at ~404/day**, with **1,016 fact-check articles** published (Jan 2022–May 2026). The **Safe Internet Campaign** ran across schools, higher-learning institutions, and **1,938 public programmes reaching 2,136,941 participants** nationwide (as of 15 Jun 2026). The Ministry frames its model as: law enforcement + platform collaboration + technology (AIFA) + digital literacy. **Quantifies MCMC's AI-assisted counter-misinformation infrastructure and reach — a citizen-verification habit and distribution network CSCDC anti-deepfake content could leverage.**

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS FOR CSCDC (Unit 3 / Anti-Deepfake Campaign) — Incremental Update

1. **#ThinkTwice is the template to beat — and the partner to court.** The 26.2M-view, four-language, government-agency-integrated #ThinkTwice campaign is the most directly transferable model for CSCDC's multilingual (BM/EN/CN) anti-deepfake content. CSCDC should (a) study the "Pause, Prevent, Protect" creative framework, (b) seek a formal partnership/inclusion in the #ThinkTwice hub or a parallel anti-deepfake vertical, and (c) note that AI voice-cloning/impersonation is already inside the scope — CSCDC can deepen the deepfake-specific layer the campaign currently touches lightly.
2. **AIFA = the citizen-verification habit to amplify.** MCMC's AIFA chatbot (197k messages, 404/day, 1,016 fact-checks) and Sebenarnya.my have built a daily verification habit. CSCDC anti-deepfake content should drive users to AIFA/Sebenarnya.my as the verification call-to-action rather than building a competing destination — leverage, don't duplicate.
3. **"Verify before sharing / tabayyun" is the government's chosen cultural frame** (Fahmi, F3). CSCDC creative should adopt and localise this frame (BM/EN/CN equivalents) for cultural resonance and inter-agency message alignment.
4. **TikTok/YouTube whitespace confirmed.** Even with the CSM follower jump, government organic short-form video is dwarfed by platform-partnered campaigns. CSCDC's RM 100K Content Studio has a clear runway on TikTok/YouTube — the multilingual (BM/EN/CN) short-form anti-deepfake niche is open.
5. **No change to in-house vs outsource or creative-agency posture** — those PIRs remain signal-based (in-house lean) and evidence-open (agency panel) respectively.
