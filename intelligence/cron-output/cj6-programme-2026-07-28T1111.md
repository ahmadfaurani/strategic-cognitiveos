## Executive Summary

This cycle produced high-value intelligence on the **structural and institutional architecture** surrounding CSCDC and Malaysia's national cyber security awareness ecosystem, but found **NO public evidence of a dedicated "1,000 Community Champions" programme** or its RM 100K curriculum budget. The CSCDC itself was only formally established on **4 June 2026** (≈8 weeks before this cycle), and the surrounding national strategy (MCSS 2025–2030) is fresh. This strongly suggests the Community Champions programme is either (a) still in internal conception/design, or (b) a private initiative (e.g., an Aras Integrasi capability-partner proposal) with no public footprint yet.

Key corroboration: NACSA's portal confirms an existing **My Cyber Hero (MYCH) 2026** national education initiative targeting **primary and secondary school students** — the closest existing "community awareness" delivery vehicle and a likely template/foundation for any champions programme. CyberSecurity Malaysia's **CyberSAFE®** programme and the NGO **MCCO** (registered April 2025, 500+ members) represent the established awareness/community infrastructure a champions programme would build on.

### Finding 9: Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854) — Legislative Framework Now in Force
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/
- **Date:** Gazetted 2024-06-26
- **Summary:** The **Cyber Security Act 2024 [Act 854]** was officially gazetted on 26 June 2024, superseding NSC Directive No. 26. The NST article separately reports a **Cybercrimes Bill** (finalising in 2026, replacing the Computer Crime Act 1997) and notes the **Dewan Negara passed a Cyber Security Bill 2026 on 20 July 2026** — likely amendments to the 2024 Act or complementary legislation.
