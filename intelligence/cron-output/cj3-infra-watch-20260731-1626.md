### Finding: Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854) — 11 NCII Sectors, Mandatory Reporting & Service-Provider Licensing
- **Source:** https://www.simplydata.com.my/understanding-the-nacsa-cybersecurity-act-2024/ (cross-referenced to https://www.nacsa.gov.my/act854.php)
- **Date:** Article published 24 March 2025; Act gazetted 26 June 2024
- **Summary:** Act 854 applies to organisations managing critical digital infrastructure across **11 designated sectors**: (1) Government, (2) Banking & finance, (3) Transportation, (4) Defence & national security, (5) Information/communication/digital, (6) Healthcare, (7) Water/sewerage/waste, (8) Energy, (9) Agriculture & plantation, (10) Trade/industry/economy, (11) Science/technology/innovation. NCII entities must: conduct annual risk assessments; biennial audits; follow a NACSA-CEO-approved Code of Practice; report incidents immediately to the NACSA Chief Executive; and engage **NACSA-licensed providers** for Penetration Testing and Managed SOC monitoring. Non-compliance carries fines up to RM500,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years. This defines the regulatory envelope for the CSCDC encrypted alert portal: it must serve designated NCII entities and any SOC/pen-test work must use licensed providers.
