**Competitive landscape:** The strongest publicly-visible incumbent vendor is **Alpine Integrated Solution Sdn Bhd (AIS)**, NACSA's NCSS co-organiser — a natural benchmark/competitor for any CSCDC communications or event engagement. Note the possible naming confusion between "Aras Integrasi" (tasking context) and "Alpine Integrated Solution" (OSINT).
