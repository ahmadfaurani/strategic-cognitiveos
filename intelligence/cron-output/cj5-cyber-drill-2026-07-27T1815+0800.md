### Finding 6: ONSA 2025 (Online Safety Act) — new legislative layer for online-harm response with provider duties and child-safety mandates
- **Source:** https://www.mcmc.gov.my/onsa
- **Date:** 2025 (Act); ONSA microsite current July 2026
- **Summary:** The **Online Safety Act 2025 (ONSA)** is Malaysia's primary legislative framework for online safety, administered by MCMC. It imposes four duties on licensed service providers: (1) mitigate exposure to harmful content; (2) provide accessible reporting mechanisms; (3) easier reporting and access to support; (4) safer online experience for children. Priority harmful-content categories: **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)** and **Financial Fraud & Scams**. Other reportable categories: obscene/lewd content, harassment, violent/terrorist content, self-harm inducement, hate/hostility incitement, illegal-substance promotion. Under-16 protections are a dedicated focus area. This is a **new statute (2025)** not captured in the prior cycle and represents the regulatory backbone for content-side crisis communication — relevant to how CSCDC's War Room would coordinate platform takedowns and public alerts during a cyber crisis involving misinformation or harmful content.

## ANALYSIS — KEY IMPLICATIONS FOR CSCDC (delta from 26 July cycle)

1. **National cyber crisis framework now confirmed as standing, not ad hoc.** Last cycle, NC4 was known only as a 2022 exercise and NCCMP was not surfaced. This cycle confirms **NCCMP is a formal standing plan** ("steps/tasks at national level during cyber crises") and **NC4 is a standing National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre** issuing alerts/advisories. This significantly upgrades PIR-OPP002-002 and PIR-OPP007-002 toward RESOLVED. CSCDC's RM 150K Playbook should **align with / plug into the existing NCCMP**, not duplicate it.

2. **Inter-agency coordination (PIR-OPP002-007, PIR-INIT-CSCDC-006) now RESOLVED across all four primary agencies.** This cycle mapped MCMC and PDRM roles and identified JPDP, completing the picture:
   - **NACSA** — national lead, policy/coordination, NC4 command centre, NCCMP, under MKN/PM's Dept
   - **CyberSecurity Malaysia / MyCERT** — technical incident response, EXCON capability, advisories, APCERT seat
   - **MCMC** — communications infrastructure regulation, ONSA 2025, Sebenarnya.my anti-misinformation, NADI centres, PRIME radio
   - **PDRM** — law enforcement / criminal investigation (RM2.7B scam losses, cyber crime), under Ministry of Home Affairs
   - **JPDP** — data-breach notification & enforcement (Akta 709), Data Breach Notification system
   - **MKN** — overarching crisis coordination, Situation Room, Arahan MKN No.1, Komunikasi Strategik
   The War Room coordination model is: **MKN (strategic command) → NACSA (cyber lead/NC4) → CSM/MyCERT (technical) + MCMC (comms/misinfo) + PDRM (enforcement) + JPDP (data protection)**.

3. **Live crisis-communication pathway demonstrated by the Maxis breach (22 July 2026).** The real-time sequence — *telco data breach → JPDP investigation (Akta 709) → MCMC Sebenarnya.my public clarification* — is a working example of the inter-agency holding-statement / public-communication mechanism CSCDC is asked to formalise. CSCDC's 30-minute holding-statement KPI should be benchmarked against the observed JPDP→Sebenarnya.my turnaround. This is the first concrete Malaysia-specific crisis-comms case study collected (advances PIR-OPP002-005 and PIR-OPP002-006 from PARTIAL).

4. **Sebenarnya.my is the operational misinformation-counter platform CSCDC's War Room should integrate.** MCMC's Sebenarnya.my is already performing the crisis-communication function (verifying viral claims, citing source ministries, publishing official clarifications) on an active cadence (July 2026 articles span data breaches, scams, GDP, fuel prices, dengue, national security). The holding-statement bank (PIR-OPP002-006) can be built as a structured extension of this existing platform rather than from scratch.

5. **SULIT clearance tier referenced via PKTN.** NACSA's Produk Kriptografi Terpercaya Negara (PKTN) classifies trusted cryptographic products from **"Public to Top Secret"** by SPPPKTN — the first open-source confirmation of a Top Secret classification tier in the national cyber architecture. This is relevant context for PIR-OPP002-004 (SULIT-cleared briefers): the classification infrastructure exists; designated War Room briefers would operate within this established tier system. The specific personnel and clearance status remain internal/HUMINT (OPEN).

6. **No new APCERT/CCDCOE exercise announcements since last cycle.** The international exercise calendar is stable: APCERT AGM 2026 (Busan, 3–6 Nov) and the annual APCERT drill cycle remain the forward-looking engagement points. Locked Shields/Crossed Swords remain the integrated-scope benchmarks. No new ASEAN-CERT exercise surfaced.

7. **Budget reality (RM 200K) — reaffirmed.** The Maxis breach investigation (JPDP + Sebenarnya.my + MCMC) illustrates a real national incident response at relatively low marginal cost (leveraging existing platforms). This supports the prior-cycle conclusion that RM 200K is realistic for a **tabletop/virtual integrated exercise** leveraging existing NCCMP/NC4/Sebenarnya.my infrastructure, not a greenfield live-fire exercise.
