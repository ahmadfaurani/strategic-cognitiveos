### Finding 1: NIST Selects HQC as 4th PQC Standard Algorithm (2025) — Expands Standards Landscape
- **Source:** https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography
- **Date:** HQC selected 2025; page accessed 2026-08-01
- **Summary:** NIST's PQC project page now confirms HQC (a code-based key encapsulation mechanism) was selected in 2025 as a 4th-round addition to the PQC standardisation portfolio, complementing the three finalised FIPS standards: ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204), and SLH-DSA (FIPS 205). The NCCoE Migration to PQC project continues demonstrating tools for finding and prioritising vulnerable systems. This expands the algorithm landscape Malaysia's PQC Sandbox evaluates beyond the original three FIPS standards.

### Finding 6: Crypto Agility Over One-Time Migration — Industry Consensus Emerging
- **Source:** https://www.aibp.sg/articles/post-quantum-crypto-asean
- **Date:** June 2026
- **Summary:** The AIBP-IBM roundtable established industry consensus that PQC readiness should be framed as "crypto agility" rather than a one-time migration.
