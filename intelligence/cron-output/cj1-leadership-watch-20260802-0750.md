## Executive Summary

CSCDC (Cyber Security and Cryptology Development Centre) was formally launched on **4 June 2026** by PM Anwar Ibrahim at PTPKM, with the **Chief Secretary to the Government (KSN), Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar, appointed as Chairman of the CSCDC Board of Directors**. CSCDC consolidates PTPKM + CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM) under the Prime Minister's Department (JPM), regulated by NACSA with MKN as the technical/operational arm — Cabinet-approved. NACSA CEO **Ir. Dr. Megat Zuhairy bin Megat Tajuddin** is the senior regulator/shepherd. CSM's acting CEO is **Roshdi bin Haji Ahmad** (since 14 Jan 2026). The Cyber Security Bill 2026 cleared both houses of Parliament (Dewan Rakyat 1 Jul, Dewan Negara 20 Jul 2026). The domain cscdc.my remains a **parked domain (not yet active)**, indicating pre-operational digital presence. Vendor **Aras Integrasi Sdn Bhd** has emerged as a deeply embedded AI/cyber partner of both CSM and NACSA, showcasing its GovSec platform at CSM-hosted events and briefing the PM — a critical competitive-landscape signal.
