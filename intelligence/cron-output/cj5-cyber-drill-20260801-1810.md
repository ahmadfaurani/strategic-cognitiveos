### Finding 6: NC4 — National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre (War Room Function)
- **Source:** https://www.nc4.gov.my/about-us
- **Date:** Accessed 2026-08-01 (Cyber Security Act 2024 in force since August 26, 2024)
- **Summary:** Established under the Cyber Security Act 2024 [Act 854], NC4 is Malaysia's centre for cyber crisis management, including monitoring cyber threats to critical systems. During peacetime, NC4 monitors the cyber security situation, measures readiness, issues warnings/advisories, informs the National Cyber Threat Level, and plans/improves the NCCMP and Response/Communication/Coordination Procedures. During crisis, NC4 coordinates and manages cyber crisis at the national level, reports to stakeholders, declares Medium Threat Level, provides expert services, and acts as technical advisor to the National Cyber Crisis Management Committee and Working Group. The National Cyber Threat Level operates on a 5-tier scale: LOW → MODERATE → CAUTION → HIGH → CRITICAL. NC4 is also designated as Malaysia's national CERT.

### Finding 9: Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854) — Incident Notification Framework
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/act854.php; https://hhq.com.my/posts/responding-to-cyber-security-incidents-the-strategic-guide-for-in-house-counsels-under-malaysias-cyber-security-act-2024/
- **Date:** Gazetted June 26, 2024; in force August 26, 2024
- **Summary:** The Cyber Security Act 2024 establishes the National Cyber Security Committee and outlines duties of NACSA's Chief Executive, NCII sector leads, and NCII entities. Key crisis communication provisions: NCII entities must appoint 3 authorised persons (1 management-level, 2 operational-level) within 21 days of designation. Upon discovering a cyber incident, immediate notification to cert@nc4.gov.my is required, with initial information submission within **6 hours** (particulars, incident type, severity, discovery method) and supplementary information within **14 days** (affected systems, TTPs, impact, actions taken). Penalties: fines up to RM500,000 and/or imprisonment up to 10 years. This 6-hour regulatory notification benchmark contextualizes CSCDC's 30-minute holding statement KPI — the holding statement would need to be issued well within the 6-hour regulatory window.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP002-002 (MKN crisis protocols), PIR-OPP002-006 (Holding statement — 6-hour regulatory notification KPI), PIR-OPP002-008 (Activation threshold — incident notification triggers), PIR-OPP002-007 (Inter-agency coordination)

### Finding 19: NSC Directive No. 26 — Superseded by Cyber Security Act 2024
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/directive26.php
- **Date:** Original directive pre-2024; superseded by Act 854 (August 26, 2024)
- **Summary:** NSC Directive No. 26 (National Cyber Security Management) was the executive directive that defined the overall governance of Malaysia's cyber security ecosystem.
