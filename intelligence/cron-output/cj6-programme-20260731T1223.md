## Executive Summary

This cycle collected intelligence on Malaysia's cybersecurity community awareness and capacity-building ecosystem, focusing on the NACSA / CyberSecurity Malaysia programme landscape, the national My Cyber Hero (MYCH) schools initiative, the CyberSAFE awareness platform (including its Ambassador and Speaker programmes), and the Malaysia Cyber Security Strategy (MCSS) 2025-2030. ASEAN cyber diplomacy positioning was addressed via the NCSS 2026 summit's ASEAN Norms Implementation Workshop and Malaysia-Singapore bilateral roundtable. International community-champion benchmark models (UK CyberFirst/TechFirst, Singapore CSA community workshops) were extracted for comparison. No public reference to a specific "CSCDC" branded "1,000 Community Champions" programme was found; the closest analogues are NACSA's My Cyber Hero schools programme and CyberSecurity Malaysia's CyberSAFE Ambassador initiative. This suggests the 1,000 Community Champions programme is either pre-launch, internal/unpublished, or a proposed design concept — a critical gap flagged for PIR-OPP006-001.
