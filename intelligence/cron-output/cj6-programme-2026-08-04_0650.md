## Executive Summary

This cycle's collection surfaced significant new intelligence on the **Cyber Security Cryptology Development Centre (CSCDC)** institutional architecture and Malaysia's broader cyber-capacity ecosystem. The CSCDC was formally established on **4 June 2026** under the Prime Minister's Department, consolidating the functions of PTPKM and CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM) into a single centralised entity regulated by NACSA and the National Security Council. However, **no public source confirms a dedicated "1,000 Community Champions" programme or an RM 100K curriculum allocation** attributable to CSCDC. The CSCDC's public mandate is heavily oriented toward **cryptology, post-quantum cryptography (PQC), R&D, and capacity building** — not grassroots community awareness. Community cybersecurity awareness appears to remain the domain of **CyberSecurity Malaysia's CyberSAFE® programme** (now being absorbed into CSCDC) and parallel NGO-led efforts, most notably **The Asia Foundation's APAC Cybersecurity Fund (ACF)** train-the-trainer model. Several reusable curriculum assets, certification frameworks, and international "champion" models were identified that could inform a CSCDC Community Champions build-out. Malaysia also continues to lead ASEAN cyber diplomacy (ASEAN Cybersecurity Cooperation Strategy 2026–2030) and passed the Cyber Security Bill 2026 (Senate, 20 July 2026).

### Finding 6: Global ACE Certification — Existing Accreditation Framework with Awareness-Educator Tracks
- **Source:** https://globalace.org/certification ; https://www.cybersecurity.my/portal-main/services/global-ace-certification-overview
- **Date:** Current (accessed 4 August 2026)
- **Summary:** CyberSecurity Malaysia's **Global ACE Certification** is a holistic professional certification scheme aligned to **ISO/IEC 17024** (people certifications), ISO/IEC 9000, and ISO/IEC 27001. It is **locally approved** by the Board of Technologists Malaysia (MBOT, Act 768) and JPK (Ministry of Human Resources), and **globally approved** by the OIC and OIC-CERT. Relevant certification tracks for a champions programme: **"Certified Cyber Security Awareness Educator"** (open to teachers in public/private primary, secondary, or higher-ed institutions) and **"Certified Information Security Awareness Manager"** (2+ years managerial experience). Other tracks include penetration testing, digital forensics first responder, SOC, data security. **This provides an off-the-shelf accreditation scaffold** for champion certification/accreditation (PIR-OPP006-007) without building a new framework.

## Analytical Assessment & Implications for Aras Integrasi

1. **Curriculum "from scratch" risk is overstated.** The single most actionable finding for PIR-OPP006-001/PIR-INIT-CSCDC-008: a Malaysian Community Champions curriculum need not be authored from zero. CyberSAFE® content, the ACF localised cyber-hygiene curriculum, and Global ACE's awareness-educator certification are existing, credentialed building blocks. Aras Integrasi's value proposition as a capability partner is strongest framed as **curriculum assembly/localisation + programme design layer** on top of these assets, plus the train-the-trainer multiplier model proven by ACF.

2. **CSCDC's public identity is cryptology-centric.** The "Community Champions" remit is not yet publicly attributed to CSCDC. This means either (a) it is an internal/planned function not yet announced, or (b) it sits with the CSM-legacy CyberSAFE team now consolidating into CSCDC. Engagement should account for the possibility that the champions programme is **pre-public-announcement** — a positioning window for Aras Integrasi.

3. **KPT collaboration is already happening** (BlackBerry-UKM-NACSA Trustworthy Professionals, witnessed by the Higher Education Minister). KPM (school-level) engagement is the gap — CyberSAFE talks and ISC2/UK-style school programmes show the model, but no KPM MOU for a champions curriculum was found. This is a concrete partnership-development lead.

4. **Accreditation is a solved problem.** Global ACE's "Certified Cyber Security Awareness Educator" track (MBOT + JPK + OIC-CERT approved) means a champion certification framework does not need to be invented — it can be adopted/adapted. This de-risks PIR-OPP006-007.
