## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Malaysia's PQC programme has crystallised into a multi-instrument national architecture since late 2025. The **National PQC Migration Plan 2025–2030** and **PQC Sandbox Proof-of-Concept (POC) Programme** were launched on 12 November 2025 at ICE-PQC 2025 (UPM Serdang), preceded by the **PQC Readiness Roadmap** unveiled 29 October 2025 at the PKI Consortium PQC Conference in Kuala Lumpur (600+ delegates, 30+ countries — one of the largest dedicated PQC events globally). On **4 June 2026**, PM Anwar Ibrahim inaugurated the **Cyber Security Cryptology Development Centre (CSCDC)** — the consolidated national cryptology entity under JPM/MKN/NACSA, formed from PTPKM + CyberSecurity Malaysia — with Chief Secretary Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar appointed CSCDC Board Chairman. The PQC Sandbox is explicitly the Sovereign AI intersection point: Malaysia's sovereign full-stack AI infrastructure (DeepSeek on AlterMatic DT250, PMO as early adopter) launched 19 May 2025.

Internationally, the **US White House Executive Order of 22 June 2026** (EO 14412 + OMB M-26-15) imposed the first hard federal PQC deadlines (HVA key-establishment by 31 Dec 2030, signatures by 31 Dec 2031, pilot by 31 Dec 2027), compressing the global timeline. NIST FIPS 203/204/205 (August 2024) anchor Malaysia's algorithm choices. Singapore CSA released a Quantum-Safe Handbook and Quantum Readiness Index. **NACSA Arahan No. 9** (under Cyber Security Act 2024 / Akta 854, §13) now mandates NCII entities to prepare PQC migration data — Malaysia's first regulatory hook on PQC, though full migration deadlines remain strategic (not binding). Malaysia explicitly positions the PQC Sandbox as a **regional ASEAN initiative** ("building a quantum-secure ASEAN").

## ANALYTIC ASSESSMENT

### Industry Engagement Model (PIR-OPP005-003 / PIR-INIT-CSCDC-007)
The PQC Sandbox is a **pilot cohort / invited consortium model**, NOT an open public call. Evidence: ICE-PQC 2025 featured an exchange of MoCs/MoUs between NACSA and strategic industry partners; the Day 2 programme was dedicated to presentations from a named cohort of ~30 POC Sandbox companies (Awantec, TM, BlackBerry, Securemetric, Trustgate, POS Digicert, SIRIM, etc.). Technology supporters (BlackBerry, Entrust, Google, Kaspersky, NXP, Phison, Ravel, Securosys, Thales) supply the agile PQC algorithm implementations. This is a curated, MoU-gated consortium — consistent with a sovereign-secure programme where vendor vetting is intrinsic. For Aras Integrasi's engagement strategy, the realistic entry path is via the technology-supporter/integrator layer or as an additional POC cohort member in a future intake, not an open competitive bid.

### Technical Scope (PIR-OPP005-001)
The Sandbox aligns with **NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA)** as the algorithm baseline. CyberSecurity Malaysia references four algorithm families (lattice-based, code-based, hash-based, multivariate). The POC programme tests quantum-safe implementations **in government systems** and includes hybrid cryptography and cryptographic-agility architectures. Use cases span the cohort: PKI/certification (Trustgate, POS Digicert, TM One), HSM/security hardware (Securemetric, OFO Tech), enterprise systems (BlackBerry, Kaspersky/Aswant), and national trust infrastructure (MySEAL). Specific per-company use-case results were not enumerated in open sources.

### Sovereign AI ↔ PQC Intersection (PIR-OPP005-005)
The PQC Sandbox is the cryptology/sovereign-AI intersection point. Both programmes sit under the JPM/MKN/NACSA sovereign digital infrastructure umbrella: CSCDC (PQC/cryptology) and the sovereign full-stack AI infrastructure (DeepSeek on AlterMatic DT250, with PMO as an early adopter) are parallel national-sovereignty instruments. NAIO's AI Technology Action Plan 2026–2030 is the AI-side counterpart to the PQC Migration Plan 2025–2030. The PMO being an early adopter of the sovereign AI platform strengthens the strategic logic for PQC-ready encrypted channels (incl. alert/reporting portals) feeding the sovereign data layer. "PERJASA" / "PMO Data Lake" as specific named constructs were not corroborated in open sources this cycle.

### PQC-Ready Encrypted Alert Portal (PIR-OPP003-005)
**Assessment: YES — PQC-readiness from launch is advisable.** Rationale: (1) NACSA Arahan No. 9 already mandates NCII PQC migration data preparation under the Cyber Security Act 2024 — any new encrypted system touching NCII data falls within the regulatory perimeter; (2) the HNDL threat means encrypted alert traffic is already being harvested — a classical-only portal launched today is exposed for its entire confidentiality lifetime; (3) the sovereign AI infrastructure's PMO integration places alert portals in the high-value-asset tier by association; (4) hybrid classical+PQC (e.g., ML-KEM + ECDH) is the recommended transitional architecture, consistent with NIST and the Malaysian roadmap's "early adoption of hybrid cryptography" guidance. Confidence: MEDIUM (analytic inference from regulatory + threat landscape).
