For the CSCDC encrypted alert portal, Pillar 1 (data protection) and Pillar 3 (PKTN utilisation among NCII entities) are the directly relevant implementation tracks. ## Cross-PIR Synthesis

**Theme 1: MyKriptografi Action Plan 2026-2030 is the New Operational Spine**
The discovery of the MyKriptografi Action Plan 2026-2030 (4 pillars, 12 strategies, 32 programmes, 80 activities) is the most significant new finding this cycle. It transforms the encrypted alert portal from a compliance project (aligned with a policy) to an operational project (aligned with a concrete implementation roadmap). Pillar 1 (data protection for Government/NCII) and Pillar 3 (PKTN utilisation among NCII entities) are the directly relevant tracks. Any portal proposal that references this Action Plan demonstrates deeper alignment than competitors who reference only the Cabinet-approved policy.

**Theme 2: Search Backend Blackout is an Operational Resilience Risk**
This is the 9th consecutive cycle with search backend failures. web_search, Firecrawl search, SearXNG, and DeerFlow's search API all returned zero results. Only direct URL extraction (web_extract) functioned. This means: (1) OPEN PIRs that require discovery-type searches cannot be advanced, (2) vendor landscape mapping is impossible, (3) news monitoring is blind. The intelligence collection infrastructure has degraded to known-URL-only extraction. This is an infrastructure issue requiring escalation.

**Theme 3: ePerolehan GPKI Integration Confirms Authentication Pipeline**
The ePerolehan portal's GPKI/OTP integration confirms that the government's PKI infrastructure extends to the procurement gateway level. This means the same MyGPKI authentication (PIR-OPP003-009) governs both the procurement process and the portal's user authentication — a unified identity infrastructure. For Aras Integrasi, this means a single GPKI integration could serve both procurement participation and portal user authentication.

**Theme 4: Social Listening Capability Gap is Confirmed but Vendor Landscape is Unmappable**
MCMC ONSA is definitively regulatory enforcement (not social listening). JAPEN's portal is down (500 error). No Malaysian managed social listening vendor was identifiable. The combination of confirmed capability gap + unmappable vendor landscape = strongest possible business case for Aras Integrasi to position as the managed service layer. But the vendor landscape assessment is constrained by the search blackout.

**Theme 5: NC4 Advisory Quiescence**
NC4 has not published a new advisory since 24 July 2026 (31 days). The last 4 advisories (NC4-ALR-2026-000001 through 000006) span March-July 2026. This quiescence may indicate: (1) operational stability, (2) transition period (post-Act 854 implementation), or (3) reporting lag. The National Cyber Threat Level remains "LOW" (last updated 5 April 2024). This is noteworthy but not actionable without internal NC4 context.
