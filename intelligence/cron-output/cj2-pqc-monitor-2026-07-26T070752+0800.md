## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Malaysia has materially advanced its PQC posture over the past nine months. The **National PQC Readiness Roadmap** was unveiled on **29 Oct 2025** at the PKI Consortium PQC Conference in Kuala Lumpur (2,500+ delegates, "world's largest dedicated PQC conference ever held"). The **National PQC Migration Plan 2025–2030** and the **PQC Sandbox Proof-of-Concept Programme** were officially launched on **12 Nov 2025** in Serdang by Chief Secretary Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar. On **4 Jun 2026**, PM Anwar Ibrahim visited PTPKM, witnessed the establishment of the **Cyber Security Cryptology Development Centre (CSCDC)** — the consolidated entity merging PTPKM + CyberSecurity Malaysia under the PM's Department, regulated by NACSA + the National Security Council — and personally spotlighted the **PQC Sandbox** as a flagship "digital sovereignty" initiative. Malaysia is positioning PQC Sandbox as the **cryptologic security layer of its sovereign AI agenda**, with explicit "quantum-secure ASEAN" regional framing. Algorithm scope aligns to **NIST FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA)** via hybrid classical-PQC deployments. The programme is currently a **non-binding strategic framework** (no mandated CNII compliance dates). Public-sector investment is anchored by a **RM 25 million (Budget 2025) allocation to UPM for PTPKM**, the home of the Sandbox; the **RM 300K conference budget** referenced in the engagement brief is **not corroborated by public sources** and may refer to a forward Aras Integrasi-facing event or the Nov 12 Serdang launch. The next PQC conferences are **Munich (Nov 2026)** and **Amsterdam (Dec 2026)**.

### Finding 8: NIST PQC Standards — Global Baseline for Malaysia's Sandbox
- **Source:** https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography ; https://csrc.nist.gov/News/2024/postquantum-cryptography-fips-approved
- **Date:** Standards released 13 Aug 2024 (current page accessed 26 Jul 2026)
- **Summary:** NIST released the three principal PQC standards in Aug 2024 as FIPS: **FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation)**, **FIPS 204 (ML-DSA, Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature)**, **FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA, Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature)**. NIST states these "can and should be put into use now." Per **NIST IR 8547**, NIST will **deprecate quantum-vulnerable algorithms and remove them from standards by 2035**; high-risk systems must transition much earlier. Additional standardization in progress: **Falcon** (digital signature, selected 2022) and **HQC** (4th-round KEM selection, 2025); soliciting additional digital signature schemes (backup to ML-DSA + niche use cases). Technical lead: **Dr. Dustin Moody**. Malaysia's Sandbox and roadmap explicitly align to these NIST standards (no Malaysia-specific algorithm requirements).
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP005-001, PIR-OPP005-006, PIR-OPP005-008
