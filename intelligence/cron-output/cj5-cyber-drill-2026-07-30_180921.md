### Finding 8: CEPA Framework — Kirkpatrick Model Applied to X-Maya Drill Assessment
- **Source:** https://theses.gla.ac.uk/7553/ (University of Glasgow PhD thesis, Arniyati Ahmad, 2016) ; https://zenodo.org/records/1107786/files/10002012.pdf ; https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Arniyati-Ahmad/publication/283715366
- **Date:** PhD 2016; published chapters 2014 (Malacca) and 2015 (Istanbul)
- **Summary:** Arniyati Ahmad's doctoral research (University of Glasgow, supervised by Prof. Christopher Johnson and Dr. Timothy Storer) developed the CEPA (Cyber Exercise Post Assessment) framework and applied it to Malaysia's X-Maya cyber exercise. The framework has two parts: (1) Lessons Learnt Investigation using the **Kirkpatrick Training Model** with four levels — Reaction, Learning, Behaviour, Results — coding X-Maya 5 participant interviews; (2) Organisation Cyber Resilience (OCR) Investigation using the WEF C-Suite Executive Checklist (2012) distributed to all 10 CNII sectors, producing an OCR Maturity Model. Reliability: Cronbach's alpha 0.974 (19 items) / 0.975 (17 items) — excellent. Sector resilience scores (1-5 scale): Banking & Finance highest (4.640), then Energy (4.509), Information & Communications (4.486); lowest: Water (2.798), Emergency Services (3.116), Health Services (3.149) — statistically significant sector differences (p<0.05, eta squared 0.6). X-Maya exercise history: X-Maya 1 (2008, 11 agencies) → X-Maya 2 (2009, 28) → X-Maya 3 (2010, 34) → X-Maya 4 (2011, 51) → X-Maya 5 (2013, 96). The research identifies a key challenge: multi-sector cyber exercises face diversity of participant backgrounds, different working environments, and varying incident response policies — and the open question of how well lessons learned transfer to participating organisations. **Strategic significance:** This is the only documented academic framework for measuring Malaysian national cyber drill effectiveness. It directly answers PIR-OPP007-005 (drill success metrics) with a tested 4-level model (Reaction/Learning/Behaviour/Results) and PIR-OPP007-010 (post-drill improvement) with a structured lessons-learnt methodology. The OCR Maturity Model provides a sector-by-sector resilience baseline (Banking/Finance strong; Water/Emergency/Health weak) for targeting drill participation. The X-Maya agency growth (11→96) documents the inter-agency participation trajectory for PIR-OPP007-004.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP007-005, PIR-OPP007-010, PIR-OPP007-004, PIR-OPP007-001
