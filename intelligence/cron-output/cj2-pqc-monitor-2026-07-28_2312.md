### Finding 8: NIST PQC Standards Finalised — FIPS 203, 204, 205 (Aug 13, 2024)
- **Source:** https://www.nist.gov/pqc ; https://entangledfuture.com/guides/nist-pqc-standards/
- **Date:** August 13, 2024 (finalised); guide updated April 2026
- **Summary:** NIST finalised three PQC standards on August 13, 2024: **FIPS 203 (ML-KEM)** — key encapsulation, formerly CRYSTALS-Kyber, replaces RSA/ECDH/DH, NIST's primary recommendation is ML-KEM-768; **FIPS 204 (ML-DSA)** — digital signatures, formerly CRYSTALS-Dilithium, replaces RSA-PSS/ECDSA; **FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA)** — digital signatures, based on SPHINCS+, uses only hash functions (conservative, for root certs/firmware/code signing). **FIPS 206 (FN-DSA/FALCON)** was in progress as of April 2026. NSA's CNSA 2.0 Suite mandates all three finalised standards with no classical asymmetric algorithms for new systems. NIST IR 8547 proposes deprecating quantum-vulnerable public-key crypto after 2030, disallowing after 2035. Malaysia's roadmap explicitly aligns with these NIST standards.
