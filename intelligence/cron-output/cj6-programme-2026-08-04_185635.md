- **Source:** https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/malaysia-senate-passes-cyber-security-bill-2026/

- **Summary:** Malaysia's Dewan Negara (Senate) passed the **Cyber Security Bill 2026** (8 parts, 61 clauses) on 20 July 2026, designed to repeal the Computer Crimes Act 1997. ## Analytical Notes & Recommendations for Aras Integrasi Positioning

1. **Perwira Cyber IS the Community Champions programme.** The NADI x Cybersecurity Perwira Cyber 2026 programme is the operational vehicle for the 1,000 Community Champions target. The "1 NADI, 1 Perwira Cyber" model yields ~932-1,097 champions. Aras Integrasi should align any curriculum/programme-design offering to the NADI Cybersecurity LMS and the Perwira Cyber delivery structure (online, weekends, 3 sessions + community missions, beginner-level, 18+).

2. **Curriculum is integrated, not standalone.** No separately published "Community Champions curriculum draft" exists publicly. The curriculum lives inside the NADI Cybersecurity LMS. The Asia Foundation ACF curriculum (phishing, passwords, devices, data) is the closest ready-made, locally-curated comparator (yearlong effort to localise). Aras Integrasi's Academy solution and its experience testing 8 LLM training methods position it well for LMS-integrated curriculum design.

3. **Accreditation pathway is NACSA/MCSS.** Budget 2026's 50% tax deduction requires NACSA accreditation. Any CSCDC curriculum should seek NACSA accreditation (via MCSC framework) to unlock the funding incentive. CyberGURU and ISCB (ISO/IEC 17024) provide the certification infrastructure.

4. **KPM collaboration is confirmed via My Cyber Hero** (KPM as support partner for the school-age programme), while KPT collaboration runs through the 6 university MoUs (UKM, UiTM, UTeM, UTHM, MSU, UCTATI) + City University MoA. For adult/community champions (Perwira Cyber), the MCMC/NADI infrastructure is the primary inter-agency vehicle (Ministry of Communications), not KPM/KPT.

5. **ASEAN diplomacy is mature and accelerating.** Malaysia has moved from "intention to implementation" — the ASEAN strategy is published, the Regional CERT is operational, and ASEAN My Cyber Hero provides regional soft-power outreach. CSCDC (as NACSA's technical arm) is the institutional home for G2G briefings.

6. **International benchmarking:** UK TechFirst (£187M, tiered, measurable) is the strongest model for a tiered talent pipeline with KPIs. Australia Stay Smart Online (80,000 community) is the strongest volunteer-network benchmark. Malaysia's Perwira Cyber is distinctive in ASEAN for its grassroots community-reference-person model anchored in physical NADI centres.
