## Executive Summary

This cycle produced **one major new VERIFIED intelligence finding** that materially advances resolution of the leadership PIRs: the identity of the CSCDC Board Chairman. On **4 June 2026**, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim formally launched CSCDC at PTPKM and witnessed the appointment of **Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar — the Chief Secretary to the Government (KSN)** — as **Chairman of the CSCDC Board of Directors**. ### Finding: CSCDC Formally Launched — KSN Shamsul Azri Appointed Board Chairman
- **Source:** https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2564763 (corroborated by https://media-perpaduan.com/2026/06/04/pm-tinjau-pusat-teknologi-dan-pengurusan-kriptologi-malaysia/ and https://harapanmadani.com/pusat-teknologi-dan-pengurusan-kriptologi-malaysia-terima-kunjungan-pm-anwar/)
- **Date:** 4 June 2026
- **Summary:** PM Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim conducted a working visit to PTPKM (Pusat Teknologi dan Pengurusan Kriptologi Malaysia) and witnessed the formal establishment/launch of the Cyber Security Cryptology Development Centre (CSCDC). At the same event, he witnessed the appointment of **Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar — Chief Secretary to the Government (Ketua Setiausaha Negara / KSN)** — as **Chairman of the CSCDC Board of Directors (Pengerusi Lembaga Pengarah CSCDC)**. The visit also showcased PTPKM's Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) initiative via the PQC Sandbox programme led by young talents. Three independent outlets (Bernama, Media Perpaduan, Harapan Madani) report identical facts.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-CSCDC-001 (CEO / shepherds identification), PIR-INIT-CSCDC-002 (credible introduction path)
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED] (triple-corroborated, government wire Bernama)

### Finding: CSCDC Governance Architecture — Cabinet-Approved, Under PM's Department, Regulated by NACSA + MKN
- **Source:** https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/02/13/anwar-national-cybersecurity-agenda-to-be-boosted-amid-rising-global-threats/209038 (corroborated by https://vom24.com/malaysia-strengthens-cybersecurity-framework-advances-national-cybercrime-bill-pm-anwar/)
- **Date:** 12-13 February 2026
- **Summary:** At the National Cyber Security Committee (JKSN) Meeting No. 1/2026 chaired by PM Anwar, the Cabinet approved the establishment of CSCDC as a centralised entity. Per PM's statement: *"PTPKM will be given a permanent institutional placement, while the functions and resources of CSM will be centralised under the CSCDC, which will be placed under the Prime Minister's Department and regulated by NACSA and the National Security Council as its technical and operational arms."* This definitively confirms the supervisory chain and that NACSA + MKN are the technical/operational regulators — directly mapping to the "MKN/NACSA shepherds" in PIR-CSCDC-001.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-CSCDC-001 (MKN/NACSA shepherds), PIR-INIT-CSCDC-002 (introduction path — JPM/MKN/NACSA all in scope), PIR-OPP010-001 (PTPKM-CSM integration status)
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED] (Cabinet-level announcement, double-corroborated)
