### Finding 1: NC4 Advisory 13 — Full Alert Format Revealed (CVE-2026-48908, Joomla SP Pagebuilder RCE)
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/advisory13.php
- **Date:** 30 June 2026
- **Summary:** Full extraction of NC4 Advisory 13 reveals the structured format NACSA's National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre uses for vulnerability alerts: (1) Introduction (threat description, CVE reference), (2) Impact (RCE, persistent backdoor, full server compromise, CIA triad risk), (3) Affected Product (specific versions, CVSS score), (4) Recommendation with three sub-sections — Immediate (Patch), Hardening/Mitigation (access restrictions, WAF rules), Detection/Post-Compromise Forensics (log analysis, IOC hunting), (5) Reporting ("Malaysian NCII entities affected by this advisory are advised to report indicators or incidents to NC4 as per required under Act 854 for National coordination and intelligence sharing"), (6) References (NVD, OpenCVE, PoC GitHub links). The advisory references NC4-ALR numbering. **This is the definitive technical specification for the alert format the encrypted portal must support** — structured fields, CVSS scoring, multi-section recommendations, bidirectional reporting mandate. The Act 854 reporting requirement confirms the portal must support both outbound alert distribution AND inbound incident reporting from NCII entities.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-001 (technical requirements — alert format, audit trail, APIs), PIR-OPP003-004 (new build vs upgrade — NC4 existing alert infrastructure), PIR-OPP003-002 (CNII reporting obligations)
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED]

- **Summary:** Enhanced extraction of MCMC's digital signature regulatory framework confirms: the **Digital Signature Act 1997 [Act 562]** (in force 1 Oct 1998) governs PKI-based digital signatures fulfilling four requirements — **confidentiality, identity authentication, non-repudiation, and integrity**. **5 licensed Certification Authorities** operate, each holding all three licence categories: (1) Certification Authority, (2) Recognised Repository, (3) Recognised Date/Time Stamp Service. All have published disclosure records. MCMC also maintains a list of **Qualified Auditors** for CA audits. **For PIR-OPP003-009:** this is the legally recognised PKI vendor pool — the encrypted alert portal's certificate-based authentication should draw from these 5 CAs. Pos Digicert and MSC Trustgate are the most established (5th renewal), while Vista Kencana is the newest. The DSA 1997 legal note allows continued operation during pending renewals, ensuring service continuity.

### Finding 12: Cross-Ref CJ2 — NACSA Arahan KE No. 9: Legally Binding PQC Migration Directive for NCII Entities
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/doc/Arahan%20KE%20NACSA%20No.%209.pdf (via CJ2 PQC Monitor, 28 Jul 2026 cycle)
- **Date:** 22 September 2025 (effective date)
- **Summary:** Cross-referenced from CJ2 PQC Monitor intelligence: NACSA CEO Ir. Dr. Megat Zuhairy issued **Directive (Arahan) No. 9** under Section 13 of the Cyber Security Act 2024 [Act 854], legally mandating all NCII entities and sector heads to prepare PQC migration data. Required data includes: (1) cryptographic inventory (Table 0), (2) Software Bill of Materials (SBOM — Table 1), (3) Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM). **Submission deadline: within 3 months of written notice.** PTPKM is designated coordinator under NACSA supervision. **This is the single most important finding for PIR-OPP003-005:** PQC-readiness is not a design choice — it is a **legally binding obligation** for all NCII entities under Act 854. The encrypted alert portal, serving NCII operators, must be PQC-ready from launch to comply with this directive. The SBOM/CBOM requirement also means the portal itself must maintain and provide these artifacts. **This finding elevates PIR-OPP003-005 from RESOLVED to RESOLVED with legal authority.**
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-005 (PQC-ready from launch — legally mandated), PIR-OPP003-001 (technical requirements — SBOM/CBOM), PIR-OPP003-002 (CNII operator obligations)
