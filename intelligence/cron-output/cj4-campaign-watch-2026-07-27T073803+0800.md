### Finding 3: Cybercrimes Bill 2026 — Safeguard Architecture and Oversight Requirements (EXPANDED)
- **Source:** https://malaysiapulse.com/article/parliament-passes-cybercrimes-bill-2026-with-safeguards-against-deepfakes-and
- **Date:** 1 July 2026 (passed Dewan Rakyat); article dated 8 July 2026
- **Summary:** Cybercrimes Bill 2026 — **61 distinct clauses**, passed 1 Jul 2026 by majority voice vote after debate by 48 MPs (government + opposition). Primary targets: creation/distribution of deepfakes and non-consensual intimate imagery via computer manipulation. Defended by **DPM Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Zahid Hamidi**. Key **safeguards and procedural requirements** (useful for CSCDC compliance posture): (a) **no unlimited authority** for enforcement agencies; bill does **not supersede the Official Secrets Act 1972**; (b) computer-system-access powers must follow prescribed legal procedures (no arbitrary exercise); (c) **data preservation notices** only issued when investigating officers genuinely believe (i) the information is necessary for the investigation and (ii) it faces genuine risk of deletion/alteration/destruction without intervention; (d) **data disclosure** requires formal written notice to the data owner/controller and demonstration of lawful investigative purpose — creating documentary trails for judicial review and transparency. Identified **implementation challenges**: digital literacy gap (urban demographics understand rights; rural/less-connected communities vulnerable), regional trends (Thailand, Indonesia addressing deepfakes but enforcement inconsistent), enforcement-vs-text distinction (Civil society/legal experts will scrutinise how the Cybercrime Investigation Department applies the law), oversight needs (training for police/prosecutors, transparent complaint mechanisms for rights violations, regular parliamentary oversight). Future requirements for effectiveness: **public education campaigns** explaining citizens' rights and responsibilities, **international coordination** (cross-border cybercrime), **tech-company responsibility** (improved reporting + faster removals = shared-responsibility model). For CSCDC: the bill explicitly calls out **public-education campaigns** as a future effectiveness requirement — direct whitespace validation for the RM 80K Anti-Deepfake Campaign.
