### Finding 10: NIST PQC Standards — FIPS 203/204/205 Finalised; FIPS 206 (Falcon) In Development
- **Source:** https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography ; https://quantumsecuritydefence.com/insights/nist-fips-standards/
- **Date:** August 13, 2024 (publication); updated context 2026
- **Summary:** NIST published FIPS 203 (ML-KEM, key encapsulation, from Kyber), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA, digital signatures, from Dilithium), and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA, hash-based signatures, from SPHINCS+) on August 13, 2024, concluding an 8-year evaluation of 69 submissions. FIPS 206 (FN-DSA, from Falcon) is in development. Enterprise defaults: ML-KEM-768, ML-DSA-65, SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s. ML-DSA-65 signature is 3,293 bytes (~50× larger than P-256 ECDSA's 64 bytes) — protocol testing and capacity planning mandatory. Hybrid constructions (e.g., ML-KEM-768 + X25519) covered in NIST IR 8547, IETF drafts, ETSI TS 103 744. Malaysia's roadmap aligns with these NIST standards.
