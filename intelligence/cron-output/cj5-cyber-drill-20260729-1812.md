### Finding 4: National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre (NC4) — Inter-Agency Architecture
- **Source:** https://www.mcmc.gov.my/en/resources/guidelines/security-trust-governance
- **Date:** Current as of July 2026
- **Summary:** Malaysia's national cyber security architecture centres on NC4 (National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre) under NACSA, which serves as the National Computer Emergency Response Team. NC4 is supported by three operational pillars: (1) MyCERT under CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM); (2) CDOC (Cyber Defence Operations Centre) under Ministry of Defence; (3) MCMC NSC (MCMC Network Security Centre) under MCMC. Each of the 10 CNII sectors is guided by its sectoral CSIRT/CERT responsible for anticipating, monitoring, identifying, and addressing threats. MCMC has published 9 technical codes governing information/network security including incident management (TC-G015), business continuity management (TC-G014), and security posture assessment (TC-G016). NC4 portal (nc4.gov.my) actively publishes threat alerts — e.g., NC4-ALR-2026-000005 for CVE-2026-48908 (Joomla RCE).

### Benchmarking Insights for CSCDC War Room
- **Locked Shields model** (NATO CCDCOE): Integrated scope (technical + legal + communication + disinformation + strategic decision-making) with scoring system rewarding collaboration — the gold standard for national cyber drill design. RM 200K budget suggests a focused national exercise rather than Locked Shields-scale, but methodology is transferable.
- **ACID model** (ASEAN): Technical drill + tabletop exercise + APT workshop, with cross-border ISM — directly participable by Malaysia and benchmarkable for CSCDC's ASEAN coordination role.
- **CyberDrill model** (CSM/MyCERT): Existing national capability — methodology-based, CSM tools/infrastructure, evaluates detection/response against IR procedures. This is the foundation CSCDC's RM 200K drill should build upon.

### DAF Political War Room Transferability Assessment
The institutional architecture now formally supports political-to-cyber war room methodology transfer:
- **CSCDC under PM's Dept** provides the political-operational bridge (same institutional tier as political war rooms)
- **MCSS crisis communication framework** provides the strategic mandate for holding statements and coordinated messaging
- **NCCMP + Arahan MKN No. 24** provide the procedural framework for activation and coordination
- **NC4 + inter-agency architecture** provides the operational nodes (NACSA, CSM, MINDEF, MCMC, PDRM)
- **Recent real-world cases** (KLIA/MAHB ransomware Mar 2025, Prasarana Aug 2024) provide scenario material for drill injects
- DAF's experience (N17 Semerah, N16 Sungai Balang, N41 Puteri Wangsa) in rapid response, holding statements, and coordinated messaging is directly transferable to the 30-minute holding statement KPI
