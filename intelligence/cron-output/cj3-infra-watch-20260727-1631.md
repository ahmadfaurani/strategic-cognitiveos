### Finding 3: PKTN — Five Information Classification Tiers (Public to Top Secret)
- **Source:** https://mykripto.cybersecurity.my/index.php/services/pktn
- **Date:** Current as of Jul 2026 (page live)
- **Summary:** The **Produk Kriptografi Terpercaya Negara (PKTN)** framework operates within **five distinct product classifications: Public, Restricted, Confidential, Secret, and Top Secret**, each mapped to varying sensitivity degrees for CNII sectors. Products are evaluated by the **SPPPKTN Working Committee** with final status granted by the **SPPPKTN Steering Committee**. This is the authoritative mapping for the SULIT/Rahsia question: the encrypted alert portal will handle a **mixed classification environment** spanning at least Confidential and Secret tiers (analogous to SULIT/Rahsia), and any cryptographic product used must carry PKTN status at the relevant classification. This is the certification path for PIR-OPP003-003.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-003 (classification levels / certification), PIR-OPP003-001 (encryption standards)
- **Confidence:** HIGH

### Finding 4: Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854) — NCII Entities, Sector Leads, Service Provider Licensing
- **Source:** https://www.nacsa.gov.my/act854.php ; https://www.nacsa.gov.my/NCII.php ; https://www.nacsa.gov.my/application-licensing.php
- **Date:** Gazetted 26 Jun 2024; in force 26 Aug 2024; NCII sector leads appointed 11 Sep 2024 (national) and 28 Jan 2025 (state SUK offices)
- **Summary:** Act 854 establishes the **National Cyber Security Committee**, defines the powers of the NACSA Chief Executive, and sets out the **functions and duties of NCII sector leads and NCII entities**, including management of cyber security threats/incidents. It regulates **cyber security service providers through licensing** — specifically **managed security operation center (SOC) monitoring services** and **penetration testing services**, applied for via licence.nacsa.gov.my since 1 Oct 2024. The Act supersedes NSC Directive No.26. The **11 NCII sectors** are: (1) Government, (2) Banking and finance, (3) Transportation, (4) Defence and national security, (5) Information/communication/digital, (6) Healthcare services, (7) Water/sewerage/waste, (8) Energy, (9) Agriculture/plantation, (10) Trade/industry/economy, (11) Science/technology/innovation. **Implication for portal scope (PIR-OPP003-002):** the encrypted alert portal must serve CNII operators across all 11 sectors — these are the entities Act 854 designates as having mandatory cyber-incident duties. Technical capability across these sectors varies widely (from sophisticated banks/telecoms to less-mature agriculture/plantation operators), implying the portal must support a spectrum from API-integrated to low-tech/manual ingestion modes.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-002 (CNII operator count/capabilities), PIR-OPP003-003 (classification/certification), PIR-OPP003-001 (encryption standards/audit trail)
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED]

### Finding 5: MCMC Digital Signature Act 1997 — 5 Licensed Certification Authorities (PKI Vendor Pool)
- **Source:** https://www.mcmc.gov.my/en/sectors/digital-signature ; https://www.mcmc.gov.my/en/sectors/digital-signature/list-of-licensees
- **Date:** Licences current as of Jul 2026 (Pos Digicert licence to Dec 2030; others to 2027-2028)
- **Summary:** MCMC administers the **Digital Signature Act 1997 [Act 562]** (in force 1 Oct 1998), which governs PKI-based digital signatures fulfilling four requirements: **confidentiality, identity authentication, non-repudiation, and integrity**. As of this cycle, **5 Certification Authorities are licensed and active**: (1) **Pos Digicert Sdn Bhd** (LPBP-1/2025, to Dec 2030), (2) **MSC Trustgate.Com Sdn Bhd** (to Jul 2028), (3) **Raffcomm Technologies Sdn Bhd** (to Apr 2027), (4) **TM Technology Services Sdn Bhd** (to Jul 2027), (5) **Vista Kencana Sdn Bhd** (to Oct 2028). All five also hold recognised **Repository** and **Date/Time Stamp** service certifications. This is the vetted vendor pool for PKI authentication under the DSA 1997 regime — the legally recognised path for digital-signature-grade authentication on a government portal. MyGPKI (the government-specific PKI operated under MAMPU/Kementerian Digital) draws on this CA ecosystem.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-009 (authentication model — PKI/FIDO2/MyGPKI/custom)
