## Executive Summary

This cycle produced substantial intelligence confirming the **institutional identity and timeline of the CSCDC** and mapping the surrounding community-awareness partner ecosystem — but found **no public announcement of the specific "1,000 Community Champions programme"** or its RM 100K curriculum allocation. The CSCDC is the newly established **Cyber Security and Cryptology Development Centre**, approved by Cabinet and announced by PM Anwar on 13 Feb 2026, consolidating PTPKM and CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM) under the Prime Minister's Department as NACSA's technical/operational arm. The Malaysia Cyber Security Strategy (MCSS) 2025–2030 explicitly reframes communities as "active collaborators" under a whole-of-nation approach — the policy hook for a Community Champions programme. Adjacent community-awareness programmes (My Cyber Hero, CyberSAFE, the Asia Foundation/Google.org APAC Cybersecurity Fund, MCCO/rawSEC, SherpaSec) provide ready partners, curricula, delivery models, and KPI templates the CSCDC can draw from. ASEAN positioning is strong: Malaysia led the ASEAN Cybersecurity Cooperation Strategy 2026–2030 (as 2025 ASEAN Chair), chairs the ASEAN Regional CERT Taskforce, and ran 12 bilateral meetings + MOUs with 6 universities at CYDES 2025. **The absence of any public "Community Champions" launch strongly indicates the programme is in scoping/design phase — corroborating the working assumption that curriculum is starting largely from scratch and validating Aras Integrasi's positioning as a curriculum/programme-design partner.**

**KEY GAP:** A targeted search for "Malaysia community champions cybersecurity launch 2026" returned zero relevant results — the 1,000-champions figure, RM 100K budget, and curriculum draft appear to be internal/non-public. This is itself an intelligence signal: the programme is pre-launch.
