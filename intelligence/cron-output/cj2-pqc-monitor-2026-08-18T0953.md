## PIR Findings

### PIR-OPP005-001: PQC Sandbox Scope & Architecture
- **Priority:** Critical
- **Previous Status:** RESOLVED (Aug 4, 2026)
- **Current Status:** UPDATED — RESOLVED with material scope impact
- **Finding (NEW):** KAZ-SIGN — Malaysia's first locally-developed quantum-resistant digital signature — has been **cryptanalytically broken**. IACR ePrint paper 2026/064 (published January 16, 2026, by researchers at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and China Telecom Quantum Information Technology Group) demonstrates practical key recovery attacks that completely break ALL four KAZ schemes: KAZ-KA, KAZ-KEM, KAZ-SIGN v1.6.4, and KAZ-SIGN v2.0. Attacks execute in **under 1 second on a consumer MacBook** across all security levels (128, 192, 256 bits). The paper concludes: "These results conclusively prove that the analyzed schemes are fundamentally insecure and unsuitable for any deployment or migration." A follow-up paper (IACR ePrint 2026/188) further refines the attack. This directly impacts the PQC Sandbox scope — KAZ-SIGN was previously identified as the national algorithm under test alongside NIST FIPS 203/204/205.
- **Source:** https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/064 (IACR ePrint, Jan 16, 2026; rev. Feb 2, 2026) ; https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/188 (follow-up paper)
- **Confidence:** HIGH (2+ independent sources: IACR ePrint primary paper + follow-up paper + Google Groups PQC Forum discussions showing forgery attacks)
- **Analysis:** KAZ-SIGN's elimination from NIST competition and cryptographic breakage means Malaysia's sovereign PQC strategy must pivot to NIST FIPS standards (ML-KEM/ML-DSA/SLH-DSA) as the primary path, with KAZ-SIGN relegated to research/development only. The prior cycle's assessment that "NACSA requires government tenders to include KAZ-SIGN support" now represents a security risk if implemented. The PQC Sandbox 2026 cohort's scope (testing "calon algoritma PQC standard antarabangsa dan kebangsaan" — international and national PQC algorithm candidates) must now account for KAZ-SIGN's broken status.
