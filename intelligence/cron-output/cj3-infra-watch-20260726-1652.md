- **Summary:** The Cyber Security Act 2024 establishes the National Cyber Security Committee (NCSC, chaired by the PM) and grants NACSA's Chief Executive enforcement authority. ### Finding 6: Official Secrets Act 1972 [Act 88] — Classification Regime (SULIT/Rahsia)
- **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Secrets_Act_1972
- **Date:** Act commenced 1 Oct 1972; amended 1986; current form as of 1 Jan 2006.
- **Summary:** The OSA 1972 governs official-secret classification in Malaysia, modelled on the UK OSA. Classifications are: **Top Secret, Secret, Confidential, Restricted**. (In Malay: *Rahsia Besar* / *SULIT* / *Rahsia* / *Terhad* — "SULIT" maps to "Secret" and "Rahsia" to "Confidential".) A Minister, Menteri Besar/Chief Minister, or designated public officer may classify. The Schedule covers Cabinet documents and national security/defence/international-relations material. Critically, for the portal: a "mixed" SULIT/Rahsia classification is governed by OSA, and any portal handling such data must comply with OSA handling/penalty provisions plus the Cyber Security Act NCII obligations. Combined with PKTN's "Public to Top Secret" coverage, the portal must be designed for Top-Secret-grade handling even if initial traffic is lower-classified.
- **PIR(s) Addressed:** PIR-OPP003-003 (classification levels & certification)
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Tag:** [VERIFIED]

## ANALYST NOTES & RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Classification ceiling is Top Secret.** Portal design must assume Top-Secret-grade handling from day one (PKTN "Public to Top Secret"), even if initial traffic is SULIT/Rahsia. This has major cost implications and may stress the RM 180K cap (PIR-OPP003-006) — recommend early confirmation of budget flexibility.
2. **Authentication is decided, not open.** MyGPKI (5 licensed CAs) + MyDigital ID is the government direction; FIDO2 is not evidenced. Portal should integrate MyGPKI for agency/NCII-operator auth and MyDigital ID where citizen-facing. Vendor lock-in to Pos Digicert or MSC Trustgate is likely (most established, 5th renewal cycle).
3. **PQC-readiness is strategically expected.** MyKriptografi (Nov 2025) + quantum-research funding + Digital Trust Strategy 2026-2030 form a coherent signal. Recommend designing PQC-ready crypto-agility from launch even without an explicit mandate — it aligns Aras Integrasi as a capability builder with national direction.
4. **Sovereign cloud is the default for classified tiers.** National Cloud Computing Policy + Sovereign AI Cloud steer SULIT/Top-Secret workloads to sovereign government cloud; certified third-party acceptable only for lower tiers.
5. **Social-listening budget realism.** RM 120K likely insufficient for full Meltwater enterprise. Recommend Brand24 (budget-aligned SaaS, multi-seat) as primary, with CARMA managed-service as the analytical-depth option; negotiate onshore data-residency clauses with whichever vendor.
6. **Portal is an upgrade, not greenfield.** NACSA NC4/NC4S infrastructure exists; CSCDC portal extends it. Position Aras Integrasi's offering as capability extension to NACSA's existing stack, consistent with the "capability builder, not vendor" framing.
7. **Procurement path = ePerolehan tender.** No standing social-listening panel; expect competitive tender via ePerolehan with MOF certificate prerequisite. Aras Integrasi should ensure MOF registration currency.
