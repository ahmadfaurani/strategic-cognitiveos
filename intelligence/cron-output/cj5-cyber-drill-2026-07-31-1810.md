## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This cycle surfaced a dense, mutually-reinforcing set of findings centred on Malaysia's rapidly consolidating national cyber crisis architecture. Three developments dominate:

1. **CSCDC formal establishment (3 June 2026):** The Cyber Security and Cryptology Development Centre was officially created, consolidating PTPKM and CyberSecurity Malaysia under the Prime Minister's Department, regulated by NACSA/MKN as technical-operational arms. This directly answers the RM 150K Playbook allocation question (PIR-OPP002-001) — the budget sits inside an inter-agency, internally-led architecture rather than external consultancy.

2. **NCCMP + Arahan MKN No. 24 as the crisis protocol backbone:** The National Cyber Crisis Management Plan (NCCMP), operationalised under the Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854) and rooted in MKN's Arahan MKN No. 24, is Malaysia's existing crisis communication protocol. NC4 (the National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre) is the standing crisis-coordination hub and de facto "War Room" operator under statute.

3. **Benchmark drills confirm the integrated (communication + technical) standard:** X-MAYA (Malaysia's national drill, joint NSC/CSM, annual since 2008), ASEAN ACID (Malaysia's NACSA CEO is ASEAN Regional CERT Task Force overall coordinator), and NATO Locked Shields (41 nations, live-fire, explicitly includes crisis communications + legal + strategic tracks) collectively establish that modern national drills are integrated — not purely technical. This is the benchmark CSCDC's RM 200K National Cyber Drill should target.

The MAHB/KLIA cyberattack (March 2025, US$10M ransom rejected) is the most consequential live incident of the period, exposing communication-protocol gaps (10+ hour outage disputed, whiteboard fallback) that the Playbook is presumably designed to close.

### Finding 3: NC4 — The Statutory National Cyber Crisis Coordination Hub (de facto War Room operator)
- **Source:** https://www.nc4.gov.my/about-us
- **Date:** Current (Cyber Security Act 2024 [Act 854] basis)
- **Summary:** Under Act 854, the National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre (NC4) is a centre established for cyber crisis management, including monitoring cyber threats to critical systems, ensuring readiness/response/mitigation at strategic and tactical levels. **During crisis**, NC4: (1) coordinates/manages national cyber crisis; (2) reports to stakeholders consistently until threat returns to Level 1 (Low); (3) declares the Medium Threat Level; (4) provides information-security experts; (5) acts as technical advisor to the National Cyber Crisis Management Committee and National Cyber Crisis Management Working Group. NC4 is therefore the standing, statute-backed crisis-coordination hub — functionally the national "War Room" operator. Its physical existence (nc4.gov.my portal, Putrajaya-based under NACSA/PM's Dept) indicates a physical operations centre already exists, though CSCDC-specific War Room space is a separate question.

### Finding 4: NC4 National Cyber Threat Level Scale — Activation Threshold Framework
- **Source:** https://www.nc4.gov.my/ ; https://www.nc4.gov.my/about-us
- **Date:** Last updated 05 April 2024 (threat level); framework current

## ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENTS (Synthesis Across Findings)

### A. RM 150K Cyber Crisis Communication Playbook Allocation (PIR-OPP002-001 — CRITICAL)
**Assessment:** The Playbook is an **internal-development / inter-agency collaboration** deliverable, not external consultancy. Evidence: (1) MCSS 2025-2030 mandates "Establish a cyber security crisis communication framework to streamline crisis communication" as a stated strategy objective — a government-owned deliverable. (2) CSCDC (3 June 2026) consolidates PTPKM + CSM under PM's Dept, regulated by NACSA/MKN — the technical-operational capacity is internal. (3) CYDES 2025 dedicated a session to "Malaysia's National Cyber Crisis Playbook" — an NACSA/NSC-led workstream. (4) NC4 (Act 854) is the statutory crisis-communication coordinator. RM 150K is consistent with an internal playbook/holding-statement-bank development effort, not a large external-consultancy contract (which would typically run higher). **Confidence: HIGH.**

### B. Existing MKN Crisis Communication Protocols (PIR-OPP002-002 — HIGH)
**Assessment:** MKN already has a layered protocol set: (1) **Arahan MKN No. 24** — the National Cyber Crisis Management Plan directive; (2) **NCCMP** — the operational plan covering detection/response/communication/coordination across 10 CNII domains; (3) **NC4** (Act 854) — the standing coordination hub with a 5-level threat scale and crisis-mode reporting obligations; (4) **NACSA Chief Executive's Directive Number 1** on cyber security incident notification; (5) Section 23(1) Act 854 + Notification Regulations 2024. This is not a blank slate — CSCDC's Playbook would build on/refresh these, not invent them. **Confidence: HIGH.**

### C. War Room Physical Space (PIR-OPP002-003 — HIGH)
**Assessment:** A physical national cyber crisis operations centre already exists — **NC4** (National Cyber Coordination and Command Centre), Putrajaya-based under NACSA/PM's Dept, with a public portal (nc4.gov.my) and defined crisis-mode functions. NC4 is functionally the standing national "War Room."
