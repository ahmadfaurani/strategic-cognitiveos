## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Open-source intelligence confirms that **CSCDC (Cyber Security and Cryptology Development Centre / Pusat Pembangunan Keselamatan Siber dan Kriptologi) was formally established on 4 June 2026** by PM Anwar Ibrahim, with **Chief Secretary to the Government Tan Sri Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar appointed as Chairman of the CSCDC Board of Directors**. The centre is placed under the Prime Minister's Department (JPM) and regulated by NACSA + MKN as technical/operational arms. NACSA's Chief Executive (DG-equivalent) **Ir. Dr. Megat Zuhairy Megat Tajudin** is the confirmed NACSA shepherd. CyberSecurity Malaysia is led by **Acting CEO Roshdi bin Haji Ahmad** (since 14 Jan 2026, following Dr. Amirudin Abdul Wahab's retirement). No public OSINT was found on: a distinct operational CSCDC CEO, the CCO role, framework v2.0 approval date, the 90-day mobilisation milestones, or external PR/communications vendors — these appear to be internal planning artefacts not yet surfaced in the public domain.
