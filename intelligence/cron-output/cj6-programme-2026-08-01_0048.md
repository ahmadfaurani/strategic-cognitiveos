## Analytical Synthesis (for Aras Integrasi positioning)

1. **Curriculum draft status (PIR-OPP006-001 / PIR-INIT-CSCDC-008):** No Community Champions curriculum draft is public. The programme appears to be **starting from scratch** at the curriculum level, but it is **not** starting from zero — there is a rich baseline of reusable awareness assets (CyberSAFE, My Cyber Hero 2026, NICTSeD, Jelajah Anti-Scam, Sebenarnya.my, Cyber Parenting Guidebook, Makchic "Online Superheroes" book). Aras Integrasi's curriculum-design opportunity is real and open; the value-add is **synthesis/accreditation of existing fragments into a coherent 1,000-champion pathway**, not greenfield authoring of all content.

2. **Accreditation framework (PIR-OPP006-007):** Global ACE Certification (Student/Associate/Professional tiers, international mutual recognition, government-private-academia partnership programme) is the natural accreditation backbone and is already operated by CSM/ISCB. A "Community Champion" credential would most plausibly sit at Global ACE's Student/Associate tier. This is a strong, concrete Aras Integrasi talking point.

3. **Partner ecosystem (PIR-OPP006-005):** The CSM-CP (relaunched Sept 2025) is the formal partnership vehicle; Makchic is a confirmed content partner. Specific KPM/KPT institutional partners were not enumerable from public pages (gap for human/relationship intel). The Malaysia Cyber Security Academy (ops 2025) is the likely training-delivery host.

4. **ASEAN/G2G (PIR-OPP009 series):** Malaysia's positioning is active and credible — "Vision For ASEAN" (Jan 2025), CyberDSA regional convening, MTCP Digital Security G2G channel, and the ASEAN-Japan mobile-security e-booklet. The ASEAN 2026 Philippines chairship + ACV 2045 first-year implementation is a near-term policy window. CSCDC's G2G briefing capability is underpinned by real, ongoing MTCP delivery.
