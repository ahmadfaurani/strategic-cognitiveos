## Analytical Assessment: PQC Sandbox Architecture & Sovereign AI Intersection

### PQC Sandbox Technical Scope (PIR-OPP005-001, PIR-CSCDC-006)
The PQC Sandbox Proof-of-Concept Programme tests quantum-safe implementations in **government systems**, aligned with **NIST-standardised algorithms** (FIPS 203 ML-KEM/Kyber, FIPS 204 ML-DSA/Dilithium, FIPS 205 SLH-DSA/SPHINCS+). The roadmap explicitly references **hybrid cryptography** (classical + PQC) as an early-adoption approach and **cryptographic agility** as a framework goal. The five-phase migration methodology (Assess → Select → Validate → Deploy → Monitor) defines the sandbox as primarily in the "Validate" (pilot/interoperability) phase. 30 companies presented sandbox solutions at ICE-PQC 2025, spanning PKI/CA (MSC Trustgate, POS Digicert, TM One), HSM/security hardware (Utimaco), endpoint (Blackberry), and consulting (Baker Tilly). The **MySEAL** National Trusted Cryptographic Algorithm List indicates Malaysia is building a sovereign algorithm approval mechanism alongside NIST alignment. Confidence that specific algorithms under test are disclosed: MEDIUM (NIST alignment confirmed; exact sandbox test algorithms not individually published).

### Industry Participation Model (PIR-OPP005-003, PIR-INIT-CSCDC-007)
The engagement model is a **curated/invited consortium with formal MoC/MoU exchange** — not a pure open call. NACSA exchanged MoC and MoU with strategic industry partners at the ICE-PQC launch. 30 companies presented PQC Sandbox solutions, with 30+ exhibiting. Participation appears to be by invitation/strategic partnership rather than open competitive bid. Key participating companies: TM One/TM Technology Services, Blackberry Malaysia, Utimaco, Kaspersky (via Aswant Solution), MSC Trustgate.com, POS Digicert, SIRIM Berhad, CyberSecurity Malaysia, Awantec Systems (Google Cloud partner), Securemetric Technology. This is a **pilot cohort + invited consortium** hybrid model.

### Sovereign AI Intersection (PIR-OPP005-005)
The PQC Sandbox is directly connected to Malaysia's Sovereign AI agenda through: (1) the **NQAIC** (National Quantum and AI Centre of Excellence) being established via the NACSA/MCMC/IBM MoU (9 July 2026, witnessed by PM); (2) PTPKM (the cryptology centre at UPM, RM25M Budget 2025) being tasked with both quantum computing **and AI readiness**; (3) MOSTI's national quantum hub linking PQC to the AI/quantum strategy; (4) IBM as both the Diamond sponsor of the PQC conference and the Sovereign AI MoU partner. The PQC Sandbox is the security foundation upon which Sovereign AI infrastructure (data lakes, sovereign compute) must rest to be quantum-safe.

### ASEAN Regional Positioning (PIR-OPP005-010)
Malaysia is **explicitly and deliberately** positioning the PQC Sandbox as an ASEAN regional initiative. The Ministry of Digital's opening remarks at the PQC Conference outlined a roadmap for a "quantum-secure ASEAN." Malaysia is the first Southeast Asian country with a structured national PQC framework. ICE-PQC featured a dedicated ASEAN-perspective keynote from Thailand's NCSA. The Human Readiness pillar includes collaboration with ASEAN counterparts. However, no formal ASEAN-level PQC agreement or coordinated ASEAN migration timeline has been announced — the regional dimension remains aspirational at this stage.

### The RM 300K Strategic Conference Question (PIR-OPP005-004)
The RM 300K allocated by CSCDC for a "PQC Sandbox Strategic Conference and launch" most likely corresponds to participation in or a dedicated track within the **ICE-PQC 2025** conference (12–13 Nov 2025, UPM Serdang) and/or the **PKI Consortium PQC Conference** (28–30 Oct 2025, KL). Both events served as the PQC Sandbox launch venues. The NCSS 2026 (7–9 July 2026, Putrajaya) is the subsequent major venue where the Sovereign AI/quantum MoU was signed. A distinct standalone "Strategic Conference" matching the exact RM 300K budget line was not identified as a separate event — the launch appears to have been integrated into ICE-PQC 2025.
