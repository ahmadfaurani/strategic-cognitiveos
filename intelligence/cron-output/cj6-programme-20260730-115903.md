## Analyst Assessment & Recommendations for Aras Integrasi / Capability Partner

1. **Curriculum is build-on-existing, not scratch.** CyberSAFE (awareness tier), My Cyber Hero (engagement tier), and JURUS (competency tier) form a ready three-tier scaffold. A Community Champions curriculum can assemble these into a branded pathway. Recommend a "tiered champion" architecture: Awareness Champion (CyberSAFE-based) → Advocacy Champion (My Cyber Hero/advocacy training) → Technical Champion (JURUS-style).
2. **KPM engagement already exists** via My Cyber Hero — this is the strongest existing Ministry of Education foothold. KPT (higher education) engagement is the gap; USM/UM academic links (CYRES, UMCyberSafe) are the entry points.
3. **AIS/Alpine Integrated Solution** is the confirmed co-organiser of the national convening platforms (CYDES/NCSS) with 20+ ministry relationships — well-positioned as capability partner for programme design and stakeholder mobilisation.
4. **The "1,000 Community Champions" target** aligns with MCSS's community-stakeholder mandate but is not yet publicly branded. Aras Integrasi has a window to shape this programme by packaging existing assets + proposing a structured champion pathway with certification (JURUS model) and KPIs (reach + advocacy).
5. **International models** (UK Cyber Champions especially) provide a proven volunteer-role-model template adaptable to Malaysia's MADANI/community-centric framing.
6. **G2G/ASEAN positioning** is strong and advancing — CSCDC's placement under PM's Dept + NACSA/NSC operational arms is optimal for G2G briefings. ASEAN Regional CERT coordination is a concrete bilateral/multilateral asset.
