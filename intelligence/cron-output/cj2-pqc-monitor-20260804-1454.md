## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This cycle produced **substantial, high-confidence intelligence**. Since the last collection, Malaysia's PQC posture has crystallised from a roadmap into an operational, internationally-connected programme. Key developments:

1. **PQC Sandbox is live and accepting applications.** The POC PQC Sandbox 2026 open call is active at `pqcsandbox-ptpkm.upm.edu.my`, operated by UPM's Pusat Teknologi dan Pengurusan Kriptologi Malaysia (PTPKM). It is an **open call** (not invited consortium) for SSM-registered firms with TRL 4+ commercial PQC products.
2. **National Cryptography Policy (MyKriptografi) Cabinet-approved (28 Nov 2025) and publicly launched (9 Jul 2026)** alongside the MyKriptografi Action Plan 2026-2030 (4 pillars, 12 strategies, 32 programmes, 80 activities) — and simultaneously with the **Artificial Intelligence Systems Cybersecurity Framework (AISCF)**, confirming the PQC↔Sovereign AI intersection is a deliberate national strategy.
3. **Sovereign AI infrastructure is now formally tethered to PQC.** NACSA-MCMC-IBM MoU (10 Jul 2026) establishes the National Quantum and AI Centre of Excellence (NQAIC); GovSec "Sovereign Threat Intelligence Platform" briefed to the PM is built with CyberSecurity Malaysia for "secure and sovereign AI."
4. **Malaysia explicitly positions PQC Sandbox as an ASEAN regional initiative** ("building a quantum-secure ASEAN"), with Thailand's NCSA Director keynoteing at ICE-PQC 2025.
5. **KAZ-SIGN** — Malaysia's first locally-developed quantum-resistant digital signature (UPM/Phison/MaiStorage, based on Second-Order Discrete Logarithm Problem) — is submitted to NIST's PQC Digital Signature Competition (Round 1) and NACSA requires government tenders to include KAZ-SIGN support. Financial sector resists, preferring NIST standards.
6. **No binding migration deadline exists** — Arahan KE NACSA No. 9 mandates NCII *assessment and reporting* only; the PQC Migration Plan is explicitly non-binding (per NACSA CEO Megat Zuhairy). Malaysia lags the US (Dec 2030/2031), UK (2028/2031/2035), Canada, and Australia on enforceable deadlines.
7. **International partners confirmed:** NIST (algorithm alignment + KAZ-SIGN submission), Singapore CSA (Quantum-Safe Handbook/QRI + bilateral roundtable), Thailand NCSA (ASEAN perspective), IBM (NQAIC MoU), PKI Consortium (PQCMM), Utimaco, Kaspersky. No direct evidence of NSA/BSI/ANSSI formal involvement in the Sandbox specifically.

### Finding 6: KAZ-SIGN — Malaysia's First Locally-Developed Quantum-Resistant Digital Signature
- **Source:** https://upm.edu.my/news/upm_develops_kaz_sign_pioneers_malaysias_post_quantum_digital_security-90027 ; https://csrc.nist.gov/csrc/media/Projects/pqc-dig-sig/documents/round-1/spec-files/kaz-sign-spec-web.pdf
- **Date:** 11 November 2025 (announcement); NIST submission in PQC Digital Signature Competition Round 1
- **Summary:** UPM (via PTPKM) developed KAZ-SIGN (Kriptografi Atasi Zarah Digital Signature) with Phison Electronics Corporation and MaiStorage — Malaysia's first locally developed quantum-resistant digital signature, built on UPM-developed mathematics (Second-Order Discrete Logarithm Problem, 2-DLP). The financial sector resists, preferring tried-and-tested NIST standards (ML-DSA/SLH-DSA), so KAZ-SIGN remains "an afterthought for ecosystem-wide banking solutions" until proven.

## ANALYST ASSESSMENT — IMPLICATIONS FOR ARAS INTEGRASI / CSCDC ENGAGEMENT

1. **The PQC Sandbox is real, open, and operational.** Engagement is via open-call POC submission through PTPKM/UPM — not via closed consortium. Aras Integrasi's engagement strategy should target the POC Sandbox 2026 call with a TRL 4+ commercial PQC offering.

2. **The strategic conference vehicle is CYDES/NCSS (July, PICC Putrajaya), not a standalone event.** The RM300K conference budget should be framed within the ICE-PQC (November, UPM) and/or NCSS (July, PICC) formats, both of which already host PQC Sandbox tracks and exhibitions.

3. **PQC and Sovereign AI are now officially fused at the policy level** (MyKriptografi + AISCF co-launch; NQAIC MoU). Any PQC Sandbox pitch should explicitly articulate the sovereign AI intersection — this is the current national strategic language ("#StrengtheningSovereignResilience").

4. **KAZ-SIGN is the sovereign-crypto political priority** — NACSA requires it in government tenders — but the financial sector prefers NIST standards. Aras Integrasi should support both (NIST ML-KEM/ML-DSA/SLH-DSA AND KAZ-SIGN) to maximise addressability.

5. **No binding migration deadline exists** — Malaysia is in the awareness/assessment/pilot phase. This means the Sandbox is the *only* structured pathway for industry to gain practical PQC experience before mandates arrive. Early participants gain asymmetric advantage.

6. **The encrypted alert portal (PIR-OPP003-005) should be PQC-ready or crypto-agile from launch** — the HNDL threat makes retroactive protection meaningless for already-intercepted data, and the national direction is unambiguous even without a mandate.
