# Summary

CSCDC has allocated RM 200,000 for a National Cyber Drill Simulation. This is likely the primary rehearsal vehicle for the War Room and the crisis communication playbook. The drill requires scenario design, facilitation, real-time evaluation, and after-action reporting — all areas where war room methodology experience applies.

# Strategic Significance

A National Cyber Drill is a complex multi-agency exercise involving CSCDC, MKN, NACSA, CNII operators, and potentially PDRM and MCMC. The scenario design determines whether the drill tests communication, technical response, or both. DAF's war room experience (scenario planning, rapid response, after-action analysis) is directly applicable to drill design and facilitation.

# Intelligence Type

Market — exercise design and facilitation opportunity

# Evidence

- RM 200K allocated (Page 17, Sector 3)
- War Room rehearsal requirement (implied by Unit 4 function)
- Playbook must align with MKN directives (Page 9)
- Multi-agency scope: CSCDC, MKN, NACSA, CNII operators

# Assessment

**[MEDIUM]** RM 200K for a national-level multi-agency cyber drill is modest but workable if scoped as a communication-focused exercise rather than a full technical simulation. The drill is a natural extension of the War Room methodology opportunity (OPP-002).

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — The budget is allocated, but whether external facilitation is sought or MKN runs the drill internally is unknown.

# Implications

- Drill design reveals the real crisis scenarios CSCDC is preparing for
- Participation in drill design = deep engagement with CSCDC's operational thinking
- After-action report = intelligence product (what CSCDC learned about its own gaps)
- Drill is a visible, high-profile event — success builds reputation

# Recommended Actions

- Include drill design support in the War Room methodology positioning
- Prepare a communication-focused cyber drill scenario framework
- Identify whether MKN has existing drill protocols that must be followed
