# Summary

CSCDC has allocated RM 150,000 for a content studio (4K cameras, microphones, studio lighting, video editing, green screen). Unit 3 must produce graphics, infographics, multimedia clips, and explanatory videos. Unit 7 needs content for anti-deepfake and anti-scam campaigns on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. The studio is a one-time capital expense, but content production is an ongoing operational need — creating a potential managed services opportunity.

# Strategic Significance

Content production is the engine of both the Corporate Operations division (Unit 3) and the Community Resilience pillar (Unit 7). If the studio is built but content production capacity is insufficient, the KPI targets (25% quarterly engagement growth, 30% literacy improvement) become unattainable. A managed content production service could fill the ongoing production gap.

# Intelligence Type

Market — infrastructure and managed services opportunity

# Evidence

- RM 150K studio fit-out (Page 17, Sector 2)
- Unit 3 function 3: graphics, infographics, multimedia, video (Page 8)
- Unit 7: anti-deepfake, anti-scam content for TikTok/YouTube (Page 10)
- RM 35K for creative software licenses (Adobe CC 5-user, Canva Pro Enterprise) (Page 17)
- KPI: 25% quarterly social media engagement growth (Page 16)

# Assessment

**[MEDIUM]** The studio budget covers equipment but not ongoing production labour. With only 8 executives across all units, content production bandwidth will be constrained. A managed content service (scriptwriting, production, editing, distribution) could address the ongoing need.

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — The equipment gap is clear, but CSCDC may intend to produce content entirely in-house with the 8 executives.

# Implications

- Studio without production capacity = expensive equipment sitting idle
- TikTok/YouTube content requires consistent high-volume output (weekly minimum)
- PQC animation and deepfake awareness content require specialised expertise
- This could be a retainer-based ongoing service, not a one-time project

# Recommended Actions

- Prepare a managed content production service proposal
- Include sample content concepts (PQC explainer animation, deepfake awareness series)
- Position as augmenting in-house capability, not replacing it
