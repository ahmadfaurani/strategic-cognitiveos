## STEP 8 — LEARN

### Learning Extraction Framework

#### Learning 1: Regulatory Intelligence Creates Commercial Urgency — But Only When Converted to Product Evidence

**Observation:** The RSWG paper (L1 source) and ITSS directive (L1 source) create a regulatory-driven market for VoronCitadel. But regulatory intelligence is not product evidence. The capability mapping (ACT-001/003) is the conversion step — it transforms "VoronCitadel aligns with RSWG" (assertion) into "VoronCitadel covers §10.1 natively, §10.2 with extension, §10.3 out of scope" (demonstrated). Without this conversion, the regulatory tailwind is strategic narrative, not commercial leverage.

**Lesson:** Regulatory intelligence must be converted to product evidence before it has commercial value. The conversion step (capability mapping) is not optional — it is the bridge between compliance intelligence and product positioning.

**Institutionalisation:** For every regulatory intake (new standard, directive, recommendation), the Cognitive Loop must include a "Product Evidence Conversion" step: map regulatory requirements to product capabilities with coverage assessment. Intake without conversion is analysis, not intelligence.

#### Learning 2: Two-Actions-Same-Deadline = Merge Decision Required

**Observation:** ACT-20260827-001 (RSWG mapping) and ACT-20260827-003 (ITSS mapping) were created the same day, both due Aug 29, both owned by DAF, with overlapping scope. The merge opportunity was identified in this loop (P3) but not in the original action creation.

**Lesson:** When two actions have the same deadline, same owner, and overlapping scope, the merge decision should be made at creation time, not discovered in a later cognitive loop. Action creation without merge assessment creates duplicate work and timeline pressure.

**Institutionalisation:** Add to Intake SOP: before creating a new action, check for existing actions with same deadline + same owner + overlapping scope. If found, merge or explicitly justify why separate.

#### Learning 3: Gate 0 Pattern — Flagging Without Resolution Is Decision Debt, Not Risk Management

**Observation:** Gate 0 (Roshdi) has been flagged as UNVERIFIED for 4 consecutive cycles. Each cycle, it's noted as "highest risk" but no escalation or resolution occurs. The parallel-gates correction (Aug 27) may have inadvertently reduced urgency by decoupling Gate 0 from the POC critical path.

**Lesson:** Flagging a risk without resolving it for 4+ cycles is not risk management — it's decision debt. After 3 cycles of flagging without action, the system should force a binary decision: confirm, escalate, or decouple. "UNVERIFIED" is not a status — it's an absence of status.

**Institutionalisation:** Add to Cognitive Loop Step 5 (Prioritise): if a risk has been flagged in 3+ consecutive cycles without resolution, flag as DECISION DEBT CRITICAL and force a binary resolution: (a) confirm with date, (b) escalate with owner, (c) decouple with rationale. No 4th cycle of "UNVERIFIED."

#### Learning 4: The Regulatory Compliance Play Changes the POC's Strategic Role

**Observation:** Before the RSWG/ITSS intake (Aug 27), the Bursa POC was framed as a product demonstration — showing VoronCitadel's capabilities to Bursa. After the intake and DEC-20260827-001 (ITSS §10 focus), the POC is reframed as a compliance enabler — helping Bursa meet existing regulatory obligations. This is not a subtle distinction. A product demo can be deferred ("interesting, we'll consider it"). A compliance enabler cannot ("we must comply with ITSS §10").

**Lesson:** When regulatory intelligence aligns with product capability, the POC's strategic role changes from "nice-to-have evaluation" to "must-have compliance." This reframing should propagate through all downstream artefacts: POC document, sales kit, CSM briefings, CyberDSA narrative.

**Institutionalisation:** For every POC, assess: is this a product demonstration or a compliance enabler? The distinction determines the urgency framing, the objection handling, the pricing model, and the competitive positioning. Build this assessment into the Engineered Success Framework (Doctrine §9) as a mandatory field.
