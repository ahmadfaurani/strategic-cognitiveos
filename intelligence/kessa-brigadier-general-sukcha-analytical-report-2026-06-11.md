### 2.3 Inspektorat Jeneral Function

The **Inspektorat Jeneral** (Inspector General) within MDIO serves as the internal oversight, audit, and integrity assurance mechanism:

- **Primary Role:** Conduct management audits, inspections, and compliance reviews across MDIO units
- **Authority:** Reports directly to the Director-General (KSPP)
- **Scope:** All MDIO directorates, field units, and classified operations
- **Significance:** Critical for maintaining operational security, preventing leaks, and ensuring adherence to intelligence protocols
