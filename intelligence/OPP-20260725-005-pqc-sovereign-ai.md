# Summary

CSCDC's PQC Sandbox is Malaysia's first serious post-quantum cryptography initiative. RM 300K is allocated for a strategic conference and launch. Unit 6 is tasked with promoting private sector participation. This sits at the exact intersection of DAF's Sovereign AI workstream and National Cybersecurity workstream — the PQC Sandbox is both a cybersecurity capability and a sovereign infrastructure asset that depends on AI-aware threat modelling.

# Strategic Significance

The PQC Sandbox is not just a CSCDC project — it is a national infrastructure initiative that touches:
- **Sovereign AI:** PQC protects AI infrastructure from future quantum decryption
- **CNII Security:** All critical national infrastructure will need PQC migration
- **International Positioning:** Malaysia's PQC readiness is an ASEAN leadership signal
- **Industry Engagement:** Private sector participation determines scale of PQC adoption

DAF's Sovereign AI narrative + CSCDC's PQC technical mandate = a unified national story. This is the highest-value strategic intersection in the framework.

# Intelligence Type

Market — strategic positioning and cross-workstream opportunity

# Evidence

- PQC Sandbox explicitly mentioned in framework introduction (Page 4)
- RM 300K for PQC Sandbox Strategic Conference & Launch (Page 17, Sector 3)
- Unit 6 function 3: promote private sector participation in PQC Sandbox (Page 9)
- Unit 5 function 2: international MoU/MoA publicity for cyber agreements (Page 9)
- Framework mentions "senjata siber berasaskan Kecerdasan Buatan" and "era pengkomputeran kuantum" as primary threat drivers (Page 4)

# Assessment

**[HIGH]** The PQC Sandbox is the single most strategically significant element in the CSCDC framework for DAF's portfolio. It connects cybersecurity, sovereign AI, international positioning, and industry engagement in one initiative. The RM 300K conference budget is an entry point — the broader PQC Sandbox programme will require significantly more investment.

# Confidence Level

**MEDIUM** — The PQC Sandbox is clearly a priority, but its scope, timeline, and engagement model are not defined in the framework. The opportunity depends on how the Sandbox is structured.

# Implications

- PQC Sandbox could become a flagship national programme beyond CSCDC's communication division
- DAF can bridge CSCDC's technical mandate with the Sovereign AI narrative — few others can do this
- The PQC conference (RM 300K) is an entry point to a multi-year programme
- International positioning (ASEAN, ITU) creates diplomatic and commercial dimensions
- PQC migration will eventually affect every CNII operator — a market far larger than CSCDC

# Recommended Actions

- Draft a PQC Sandbox — Sovereign AI intersection positioning brief
- Identify PQC Sandbox's current scope, timeline, and industry engagement model
- Position Aras Integrasi as the bridge between CSCDC's technical mandate and sovereign AI strategy
- Prepare concept for PQC Sandbox as ASEAN regional leadership initiative
