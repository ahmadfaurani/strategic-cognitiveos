## 🎯 PLATFORM-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES

### Facebook: The Mass Reach Platform
**Primary Demographic:** 25-65, all ethnicities, urban + rural  
**Content Type:** Long-form posts, event photos, live streams  
**Posting Frequency:** 2-3 times daily per candidate

#### Best Practices
```
✓ Post times: 07:00-09:00 (morning commute), 12:00-14:00 (lunch), 20:00-22:00 (evening)
✓ Video content gets 3x engagement vs. images
✓ Live streams for ceramah (announce 2 hours before)
✓ Use BM for mass appeal, English for policy posts, Chinese for community-specific
✓ Tag PH leaders, PKR Johor, AMK for amplification
✓ Respond to comments within 2 hours (shows engagement)
```

#### Content Mix (Weekly)
| Content Type | Frequency | Example | Expected Engagement |
|--------------|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| **Policy Thread** | 2x/week | "3 Reasons Why [Issue] Matters" | 200-500 reactions |
| **Ground Work** | 3x/week | Ceramah photos, house visits | 300-800 reactions |
| **Live Stream** | 1-2x/week | Ceramah, Q&A sessions | 500-2K viewers |
| **Personal** | 1x/week | Family, hobbies, behind-scenes | 400-1K reactions |
| **News Response** | As needed | Media statement, fact-check | 100-300 reactions |
| **Event Promo** | 2x/week | Ceramah announcement, rally | 200-500 reactions |

#### Facebook Ads Strategy
```
Budget Allocation (per candidate, per week):
- Awareness (reach): RM 200 (broad targeting, 18-65, constituency)
- Engagement (video views): RM 150 (25-45, interested in politics)
- Conversion (event RSVP): RM 100 (high-intent, past engagers)

Targeting Parameters:
✓ Location: [Constituency] + 5km radius
✓ Age: 18-65 (adjust per candidate)
✓ Interests: Anwar Ibrahim, PKR, PH, local news, community issues
✓ Behaviors: Engaged shoppers, frequent travelers (urban), mobile users
✓ Custom Audiences: Website visitors, video viewers (retargeting)

Ad Creative Best Practices:
✓ First 3 seconds must hook (text overlay, candidate face)
✓ Subtitles required (85% watch without sound)
✓ Clear CTA: "Join Us", "Learn More", "RSVP Now"
✓ A/B test headlines weekly
```

### Instagram: The Youth & Visual Platform
**Primary Demographic:** 18-35, urban, English + BM  
**Content Type:** Reels, Stories, carousel posts  
**Posting Frequency:** 1-2 times daily + Stories throughout day

#### Best Practices
```
✓ Reels get 10x reach vs. regular posts (prioritize)
✓ Stories for real-time updates (ceramah prep, behind-scenes)
✓ Use trending audio for Reels (check TikTok trends)
✓ Hashtags: 5-10 per post (mix of popular + niche)
✓ Tag locations (constituency landmarks, event venues)
✓ Collaborate with local influencers (micro-influencers, 5-50K followers)
```

#### Content Mix (Weekly)
| Content Type | Format | Frequency | Example |
|--------------|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Policy Explainer** | Reel (60 sec) | 2x/week | "60 Seconds on [Issue]" |
| **Ground Work** | Carousel | 3x/week | "Day in the Life" photo series |

| **Collaboration** | Reel (duet) | 1x/week | With other candidates, influencers |

```
✓ Cross-post Reels to Facebook (saves production time)
```

### TikTok: The Viral Youth Engine
**Primary Demographic:** 16-30, first-time voters, Gen Z  
**Content Type:** Short-form video (15-60 seconds)  
**Posting Frequency:** 3-5 times weekly (consistency critical)

#### Best Practices
```
✓ First 2 seconds MUST hook (text overlay, surprising statement)
✓ Use trending sounds (check TikTok Creative Center weekly)
✓ Text overlay required (many watch without sound)
✓ Vertical format only (9:16 aspect ratio)
✓ Post at 18:00-22:00 (after school/work)
✓ Engage with comments (algorithm favors interaction)
✓ Duet/stitch with trending content (relevant to politics)
```

#### Content Strategy by Candidate Type

**Youth Candidates (Danish, Jalex, Faezuddin):**
```
Content Pillars:
1. "Day in the Life" (campaign trail, ground work)
2. "Policy Explained" (60-second issue breakdowns)
3. "Response to Attacks" (quick, witty rebuttals)
4. "Youth Issues" (unemployment, housing, education costs)
5. "Behind-the-Scenes" (campaign prep, team bonding)

Posting Schedule:
- Monday: Policy Explainer (serious but accessible)
- Tuesday: Day in the Life (ground work)
- Wednesday: Response/Trend (react to news or trending sound)
- Thursday: Youth Issue Deep-Dive
- Friday: Behind-the-Scenes (lighter, personal)
- Saturday: Ceramah Highlights (energetic, crowd shots)
- Sunday: Q&A (answer follower questions)
```

**Older Candidates (Maszlee, Onn, Md Ysahrudin):**
```
Content Pillars:
1. "Experience Matters" (career highlights, achievements)
2. "Why I'm Running" (personal story, emotional connection)
3. "My Constituency" (showcase local landmarks, businesses)
4. "Response to Young Critics" (respectful, bridge-generational)
5. "Legacy" (what they've built, what's next)

Tone: Wise, experienced, but not out-of-touch
Avoid: Trying too hard to be "cool", using slang incorrectly
```

#### TikTok Viral Formula
```
Hook (0-2 sec): Surprising statement, question, or visual
Setup (2-10 sec): Context, problem statement
Payoff (10-45 sec): Solution, candidate position, call-to-action
CTA (45-60 sec): "Follow for more", "Comment your thoughts", "Share if you agree"

Example Script (Danish Rahman - Age Attack Response):
Hook: "They say I'm too young at 24. Here's why that's wrong."
Setup: "When I graduated, I couldn't find a job for 8 months. I know 
       what youth unemployment feels like."
Payoff: "Older politicians forgot that struggle. I'm living it. That's 
       why I'm running - to make sure no graduate waits 8 months like I did."
CTA: "If you've struggled to find work, comment below. Let's fix this together."
```

```

Objective: Video views → Profile follows → Event RSVP
Targeting: 18-25, [constituency + 10km], interests: politics, news, education

Ad Creative:
```

### Twitter/X: The Media & Influencer Platform
**Primary Demographic:** 25-50, journalists, activists, policymakers  
**Content Type:** Threads, real-time commentary, media engagement  
**Posting Frequency:** 5-10 times daily (high frequency, lower production)

#### Best Practices
```
✓ Threads get 10x engagement vs. single tweets
✓ Tag journalists, media outlets (increases pickup chance)
✓ Respond to trending topics (political hashtags)
✓ Use polls for engagement (simple questions)
✓ Retweet PH leaders, PKR official (show unity)
✓ Quote tweet opposition with fact-checks
```

#### Content Mix (Daily)
| Time | Content Type | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| 08:00 | Morning news commentary | "Reading about [issue]. Here's what's wrong..." |
| 10:00 | Policy thread (part 1) | "🧵 1/7: Let's talk about [constituency] housing crisis" |
| 12:00 | Media engagement | Reply to journalist's article with candidate perspective |
| 14:00 | Policy thread (part 2) | Continue morning thread |
| 16:00 | Ground work update | "Just visited [location]. Here's what I saw..." |
| 18:00 | Opposition response | Fact-check or rebuttal to opposition statement |
| 20:00 | Community engagement | Poll, Q&A, or retweet supporter content |
| 22:00 | Reflection/night thought | Personal reflection on day's events |

#### Thread Structure (High Engagement)
```
Tweet 1/7: Hook + overview
"🧵 Let's talk about [issue] in [constituency]. This affects [X families]. 
Here's what's happening and what we can do: 1/7"

Tweet 2/7: Problem definition
"[Statistic or anecdote]. This isn't isolated - it's systemic. Here's why: 2/7"

Tweet 3/7: Root cause analysis
"[Policy decision or historical context]. This led to current situation: 3/7"

Tweet 4/7: Human impact
"Meet [name]. [He/She] [personal story]. This is the real cost: 4/7"

Tweet 5/7: Candidate position
"I believe [policy position]. Here's my plan: 5/7"

Tweet 6/7: Call-to-action
"You can help by: [1] attending [event], [2] sharing this thread, [3] volunteering: 6/7"

Tweet 7/7: Summary + hashtags
"To recap: [3 key points]. Join us at [event]. #[Hashtag1] #[Hashtag2] 7/7"
```

### WhatsApp: The Ground Game Multiplier
**Primary Demographic:** 25-65, all demographics, especially rural  
**Content Type:** Broadcast messages, group coordination, rumor control  
**Posting Frequency:** 2-3 times weekly per group (avoid spam)

#### Best Practices
```
✓ Segment groups by constituency, interest, demographic
✓ Keep messages under 500 characters (higher read rate)
✓ Include images (4x engagement vs. text-only)
✓ Forward carefully (avoid "forwarded" tag, reduces credibility)
✓ Use for: event invites, rapid response, volunteer coordination
✓ NEVER use for: attacks, negativity, unverified claims
```

## 🚨 CRISIS COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL

### Social Media Crisis Levels

**Level 1: Minor Criticism (<100 negative comments)**
- Response: Digital Lead handles
- Timeline: 4-6 hours
- Action: Standard response template, monitor

**Level 2: Elevated Attack (100-1K negative, media pickup)**
- Response: War Room Commander + Digital Lead
- Timeline: 2-4 hours
- Action: Custom response, media statement, amplify supporters

**Level 3: Viral Crisis (1K-10K negative, national media)**
- Response: Election Director + Full Team
- Timeline: 1-2 hours
- Action: Press conference, coordinated response across all candidates, legal review

**Level 4: Existential Threat (10K+, candidate scandal)**
- Response: Full Command Activation
- Timeline: Immediate
- Action: Emergency meeting, candidate briefing, potential withdrawal consideration

### Crisis Response Steps
```
Step 1: Assess (15 minutes)
- Verify facts
- Determine severity level
- Identify origin (opposition, media, organic)

Step 2: Convene (30 minutes)
- Alert appropriate response team (per severity)
- Share verified information
- Decide response strategy

Step 3: Respond (1-4 hours)
- Draft response (per template or custom)
- Get approvals
- Deploy across platforms

Step 4: Monitor (24-48 hours)
- Track sentiment
- Escalate if worsening
- Adjust strategy as needed

Step 5: Debrief (Post-crisis)
- Document lessons learned
- Update templates
- Train team on improvements
```
