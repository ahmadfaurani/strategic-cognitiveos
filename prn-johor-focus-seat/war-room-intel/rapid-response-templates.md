## 📋 USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

### When to Use
- Opposition attack on social media
- Negative media article
- Viral misinformation
- Candidate scandal (real or fabricated)
- Policy criticism requiring response

### Response Time Targets
| Priority | Response Time | Approval | Examples |
|----------|--------------|----------|----------|
| 🔴 CRITICAL | 2 hours | War Room Commander | Viral attacks, scandals |
| 🟡 HIGH | 4 hours | Digital Lead | Opposition statements |
| 🟢 MEDIUM | 24 hours | Candidate | Local criticism |

### Customization Protocol
1. Select relevant template
2. Insert candidate-specific details (bracketed placeholders)
3. Add evidence links (if available)
4. Get required approval
5. Deploy across platforms (FB, Twitter, WhatsApp)
6. Monitor engagement, adjust if needed

### Template RR-001: "Too Young/Inexperienced" Attack
**For:** Danish Rahman, Jalex Lee, Faezuddin Puad  
**Trigger:** Media/opposition questions about age, experience

```

[youth/seniors/families] with [concrete examples]. Age isn't experience - 
impact is. And [Candidate Name] has already made an impact. #ReadyToServe 
#[CandidateHashtag] #[ConstituencyBangkit]"

"Umur muda bukan bermakna kurang pengalaman. [Candidate] sudah anjurkan 
[program], bantu [komuniti]. Yang penting bukan umur - yang penting hasil. 
#[CandidateHashtag]"

track record of getting things done."

□ Program photos with attendance numbers
□ Testimonials from beneficiaries
```

## 📊 DEPLOYMENT CHECKLIST

### Before Posting
- [ ] Verify facts in response (no misinformation)
- [ ] Insert candidate-specific details (replace all bracketed placeholders)
- [ ] Attach evidence (photos, documents, links)
- [ ] Get required approval (per priority level)
- [ ] Check timing (avoid posting during major news events)
- [ ] Prepare for follow-up (Q&A, additional evidence)

### During Deployment
- [ ] Post across all platforms simultaneously (FB, Twitter, WhatsApp)
- [ ] Tag relevant accounts (opposition, media, influencers)
- [ ] Use approved hashtags (2-3 per post)
- [ ] Monitor engagement (likes, shares, comments)
- [ ] Respond to constructive questions (ignore trolls)

### After Deployment (2-4 hours later)
- [ ] Assess engagement metrics (reach, sentiment)
- [ ] Document opposition response (if any)
- [ ] Escalate if viral (10K+ engagement)
- [ ] Archive for post-campaign analysis
- [ ] Update template if needed (lessons learned)
