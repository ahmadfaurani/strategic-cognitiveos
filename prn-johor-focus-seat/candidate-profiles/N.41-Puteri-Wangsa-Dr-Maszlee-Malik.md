### Detailed Vulnerability Analysis

#### 1. Ministerial Record (2018-2020)
**Attack Vector:** Opposition will scrutinize Education Minister tenure
- Jawi script controversy
- Free laptop program delays
- UEC recognition stance shifts
- Teacher union relations
- Budget management during tenure

**Defensive Posture:**
- Document all policy decisions with rationale
- Highlight achievements (teacher housing, digital initiatives)
- Frame controversies as "reform resistance from bureaucracy"
- Emphasize systemic constraints (coalition politics, budget limits)

**Talking Points:**
> "As Minister, I fought for systemic reform against entrenched interests. Some battles we won, some we're still fighting. But I never stopped fighting for our children's future."

#### 2. 2022 Electoral Loss (Simpang Renggam)
**Attack Vector:** "Lost before, why win now?"
- Need clear analysis of 2022 loss factors
- National vs. local factors breakdown
- Demonstrate lessons learned

**Defensive Posture:**
- Prepare comprehensive 2022 post-mortem
- Highlight changed circumstances (PH state govt potential)
- Emphasize grassroots rebuilding since 2022
- Contrast with opposition incumbent's record

**Talking Points:**
> "2022 was a national wave. Puteri Wangsa is different - this is about state issues, local leadership. I've been here, working with the community, not just parachuting in for elections."

#### 3. "Elitist/Academic" Attacks
**Attack Vector:** "Too intellectual, out of touch with ordinary people"
- PhD = "elitist"
- Professor = "ivory tower"
- International travel = "always overseas"

**Defensive Posture:**
- Emphasize Johor roots, humble beginnings
- Highlight grassroots work (ceramah, constituency visits)
- Show practical policy impact (not just theory)
- Use relatable language in ceramah

**Talking Points:**
> "I may have a PhD, but I grew up in Johor. I know the cost of living, the struggle of parents, the dreams of our youth. Education isn't about being elitist - it's about empowering everyone."

#### 4. Religious Credential Scrutiny
**Attack Vector:** PAS may challenge Islamic credentials
- Syariah background vs. "liberal" positions
- International education vs. "Western influence"
- Coalition with DAP (anti-Islam narrative)

**Defensive Posture:**
- Emphasize Syariah academic background
- Highlight moderate Islam positions
- Document mosque attendance, religious activities
- Frame PH as protecting Muslim interests better than PN

**Talking Points:**
> "I studied Islam at university level. I know our faith teaches justice, compassion, excellence. That's what I bring to politics - not hate, not division, but Islamic values of service."
