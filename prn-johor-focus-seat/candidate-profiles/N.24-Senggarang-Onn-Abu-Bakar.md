**Attack Vector:** "He was MP, why now DUN? Failed at federal level?"
- Implicit failure narrative (couldn't retain/advance at federal)
- "Step down" framing (DUN is lesser role)
- Questions about commitment to state issues
- "Using DUN as stepping stone back to Parliament"

**Defensive Posture:**
- Frame as "bringing federal experience to state level"
- Emphasize state-federal coordination importance
- Highlight specific Johor issues requiring federal connection
- Position as "service at every level" not "stepping down"

**Talking Points:**
> "Eight years in Parliament taught me how federal and state must work together. Johor needs someone who knows both systems. I'm not 'stepping down' - I'm bringing experience where it's needed most. State issues affect daily life: water, roads, schools, clinics. I can get things done because I know how to navigate both levels."

**Evidence to Gather:**
- List of parliamentary achievements (bills, debates, committee work)
- Federal funding secured for Batu Pahat/Johor
- Ministerial relationships (PH administration)
- State-level initiatives during MP tenure

#### 2. Parliamentary Record Scrutiny
**Attack Vector:** Opposition research on 8-year voting record
- Attendance record (Parliament sittings)
- Voting patterns (controversial bills)
- Committee participation (or lack thereof)
- Speeches/transcripts (gaffes, contradictions)
- Question time performance

**Defensive Posture:**
- Complete parliamentary record audit (Hansard review)
- Prepare defence for any controversial votes
- Highlight key speeches, interventions
- Document constituency work during MP tenure

**Talking Points:**

#### 3. "Career Politician" Attacks
**Attack Vector:** "Only knows politics, never worked real job"
- Pre-politics career unclear (need to verify)

**Defensive Posture:**

- Frame political experience as "public service" not "career"

**Talking Points:**

#### 4. Constituency Service Gaps
