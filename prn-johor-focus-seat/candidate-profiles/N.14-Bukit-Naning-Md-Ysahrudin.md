#### 3. FELCRA Board Scrutiny
**Attack Vector:** "Political appointment, using FELCRA for campaign"

**Defensive Posture:**
- Clarify FELCRA board role (governance, not operations)

- Emphasize agricultural expertise (agropreneur background)
- Commit to separating board work from campaign

**Talking Points:**

This isn't a 'political reward' - it's using my expertise for national development. And I will never mix board responsibilities with campaign work."

#### 4. National Wave Victim

- Anti-Hibah sentiment
- Youth swing to PN

**Defensive Posture:**

- Highlight changed circumstances (PH learned, reformed)
- Frame as "survivor" not "victim" (still standing, fighting)

**Talking Points:**
