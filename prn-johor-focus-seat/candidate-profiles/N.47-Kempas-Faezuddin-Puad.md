### Detailed Vulnerability Analysis

#### 1. "Political Operator" Narrative (CRITICAL)
**Attack Vector:** "Career party man, never worked real job"
- Only knows party politics
- Living off party/payroll positions
- Connected to "elite" political circles
- Out of touch with ordinary people's struggles

**Defensive Posture:**
- Frame political work as "public service" not "career"
- Highlight IT/Information Management background (can return to private sector)
- Emphasize grassroots AMK work (youth outreach, not just leadership)
- Document community service beyond party politics

**Talking Points:**
> "Yes, I work in politics. But I'm not a 'career politician' - I'm a public servant. My job is to serve the MP, serve the constituency, serve AMK members. And I studied Information Systems - I have skills I can use anywhere. I chose politics because I believe in service, not because it's my only option."

**Evidence to Gather:**
- List of AMK community programs (non-partisan if possible)
- IT project portfolio (university, freelance)
- Testimonials from constituents (MP office work)
- AMK member growth under leadership

#### 2. AMK "Radical" Label
**Attack Vector:** "AMK = protest politics, instability"
- Street protests = irresponsible
- Anti-establishment rhetoric = can't govern
- Youth wing = impulsive, inexperienced
- Will bring "chaos" to DUN

**Defensive Posture:**
- Reframe AMK as "youth empowerment" not "protest"
- Highlight policy-focused AMK initiatives
- Document transition from protest to governance
- Emphasize AMK's role in PH government (not opposition)

**Talking Points:**
> "AMK isn't about protest - it's about participation. We're not shouting from outside anymore; we're building from inside. As AMK Johor Chief, I've focused on voter education, skills training, job placement. That's not 'radical' - that's responsible."

#### 3. Political Secretary Role Scrutiny
**Attack Vector:** "Insider, privileged access"
- Taxpayer-funded position
- Proximity to power = corruption risk
- "Yes man" to MP, no independence
- Using public office for campaign advantage

**Defensive Posture:**
- Document job responsibilities (public service)
- Emphasize transparency in role
- Highlight constituent service work
- Clarify salary/source of income (if appropriate)

**Talking Points:**
> "As Political Secretary, I serve the people of Tebrau. I help constituents navigate bureaucracy, access services, solve problems. That's not 'privilege' - that's service. And I'm accountable to the MP, the party, and most importantly, the voters."

#### 4. Youth Wing Credibility
**Attack Vector:** "35 is too old for 'youth' leader"
- AMK age limit is 40, but 35 = not "young" anymore
- Out of touch with Gen Z issues
- Using AMK as career stepping stone

**Defensive Posture:**
- AMK age range is 18-40 (still within)
- Bridge generation (Millennial connecting to Gen Z)
- Document youth-focused initiatives
- Highlight Danish Rahman mentorship (24-year-old candidate)

**Talking Points:**
> "I'm 35, which means I remember struggling as a fresh grad, entering the job market, starting a career. I'm close enough to youth issues to understand, experienced enough to solve them. And I'm proud to mentor leaders like Danish Rahman - 24, running for DUN. That's AMK's future."
