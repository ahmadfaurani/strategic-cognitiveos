### Detailed Vulnerability Analysis

#### 1. Age/Experience Attacks (CRITICAL)
**Attack Vector:** "24 years old, what does he know about governance?"
- Too young to understand real-world problems
- Never worked full-time, no adult responsibilities
- Student bubble, disconnected from reality
- Can't relate to parents, workers, business owners

**Defensive Posture:**
- Reframe: "Young doesn't mean inexperienced"
- Highlight AMK leadership responsibilities (managing people, events, budgets)
- Emphasize IT freelance work (real client experience)
- Point to youth issues only youth can represent
- Compare to opposition candidates' children (many same age)

**Talking Points:**
> "Yes, I'm 24. I'm the youngest candidate in Johor. But I'm not here to represent 50-year-olds - I'm here to represent 20-year-olds, first-time voters, students, young workers. We have different problems: student debt, job scarcity, housing unaffordability, climate anxiety. Who better to fight for us than someone living these issues?"

**Evidence to Gather:**
- List of AMK events organized (attendance, budget, impact)
- IT freelance projects (clients, deliverables, testimonials)
- Community service record (pontian initiatives)
- Academic achievements (scholarships, dean's list, etc.)

#### 2. "No Real Job" Narrative
**Attack Vector:** "Never held a real job, living off parents/party"
- No corporate experience
- No understanding of business/economy
- Student = privileged, not struggling
- AMK position = party paycheck

**Defensive Posture:**
- Document IT freelance work (invoices, clients, income)
- Highlight financial independence (if applicable)
- Emphasize student loan/debt (if applicable - relatable)
- Frame AMK role as volunteer/service (if unpaid)

**Talking Points:**
> "I work as an IT consultant while studying. I've built websites for small businesses, managed social media for local shops, taught coding to kids. That's real work. And yes, I'm a student - which means I know the struggle of balancing bills, studies, and family expectations."

#### 3. Student Leader vs. Political Operator
**Attack Vector:** "AMK activist, not community leader"
- Only knows protest politics
- Radical youth wing mentality
- Will bring instability, not governance
- Puppet for senior party leaders

**Defensive Posture:**
- Document community service (not just political events)
- Highlight collaborative work (NGOs, non-partisan initiatives)
- Emphasize policy knowledge (not just activism)
- Show independence (times disagreed with party line)

**Talking Points:**
> "AMK taught me leadership, but Johor taught me service. I've organized blood drives, tuition programs for B40 kids, tech literacy for seniors. Politics isn't just about rallies - it's about making life better. That's what I've done, that's what I'll continue to do."

#### 4. Academic Credential Scrutiny
**Attack Vector:** "Still studying, hasn't even graduated"
- Incomplete degree = incomplete candidate
- Prioritizing politics over education
- Will abandon studies if elected

**Defensive Posture:**
- Clarify enrollment status (full-time? part-time? final semester?)

- Emphasize ability to balance (studies + AMK + campaign)
- Frame as "learning while serving"

**Talking Points:**
> "I'm completing my IT degree while serving the community. Time management? I've been doing it for years. Responsibility? Ask my professors, my clients, my AMK team. I don't see education and service as either/or - both make me a better leader."
