## 📝 CUSTOMIZATION EXAMPLES

### Example 1: Youth Candidate (Danish Rahman, age 24)
**Emphasis:** Energy, fresh perspective, youth issues
**Tone:** Dynamic, forward-looking, relatable
**Key Themes:** Unemployment, education costs, housing affordability
**Quote:** "I'm not too young – I'm exactly the right age to represent Johor Lama's youth."

### Example 2: Former Minister (Dr. Maszlee Malik)
**Emphasis:** National experience, policy expertise, reform track record
**Tone:** Statesmanlike, accomplished, visionary
**Key Themes:** Education reform, national unity, institutional reform
**Quote:** "As Education Minister, I fought for quality education for all. Now I bring that experience to Johor."

### Example 3: Incumbent (Md Ysahrudin)
**Emphasis:** Track record, continued commitment, unfinished business
**Tone:** Experienced, dedicated, resilient
**Key Themes:** Rural development, agricultural support, constituency service
**Quote:** "I never left Bukit Naning. Now I'm back to finish the job."

### Example 4: Business Candidate (Arthur Chiong)
**Emphasis:** Private sector experience, job creation, economic expertise
**Tone:** Professional, pragmatic, results-oriented
**Key Themes:** SME support, employment creation, economic growth
**Quote:** "I've created jobs in the private sector. Now I'll create opportunities in government."

## 🔄 UPDATE PROTOCOL

**Update Triggers:**
- New party position
- Educational achievement
- Major policy announcement
- Media appearance (significant)
- Community program milestone
- Family milestone (marriage, children)
- Election result (win/loss)

**Update Frequency:**
- **Active Campaign:** Weekly review
- **Between Elections:** Quarterly review
- **Major Events:** Within 48 hours

**Version Control:**
- Save as: `biography-[candidate-name]-v[number]-[date].md`
- Archive old versions (do not delete)
- Maintain changelog (what changed, when, why)
