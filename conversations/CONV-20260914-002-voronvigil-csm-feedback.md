# Summary

CSM responds to the VoronVigil OMS TPRM proposal with an 8-area review table. Tone: appreciative ("technical and data-sovereignty design is genuinely strong") but firm on scope. No Phase 0–1 approval as framed; conditions defined; alignment meeting proposed.

## Area-by-Area Position (CSM feedback)

| Area | CSM Position |
|------|--------------|
| Problem statement | Fragmentation framing fair (reflects why the Thematic review of OMS providers is raised); retained — but solution must be scoped to the Phase 1 objective: 24 independent PO assessment reports + 1 consolidated report against ITSS/GTRM |
| Core solution | Registry/evidence-repository/workflow/audit-trail well conceived but designed as a PO-adopted operational system — not the Phase 1 function. Conflict of interest: CSM/Bursa cannot be both assessor and vendor to the assessed POs. Need: secure replacement for PO-emails-PDF-to-Bursa process. Reposition as document upload-and-evaluation tool for CSM/Bursa; POs see only "submission received" — no scoring, compliance status, dashboard, workflow visibility on PO side; all evaluation/clause mapping/findings/determination CSM-side |
| Regulatory alignment | ITSS 10.3/10.4 mapping solid, retain largely as-is. GTRM 8.03–8.15 clause-level treatment required — built into the proposal itself, not deferred to later clarification (cross-mapped unified checklist requirement) |
| Sovereign AI | No change required — AI-assists/deterministic-decides framing, Malaysia hosting, audit logging of AI actions meet the requirement, PROVIDED AI assistance is applied only on the Bursa/CSM evaluation side. AI support must NOT extend to the PO-facing upload interface |
| CTDM | Separation of compliance status / residual risks / CTDM trust score well designed, consistent with best practice in transparency. But CTDM is proprietary methodology outside the assessment framework applied to this engagement — make it a separate OPTIONAL annex, not a dependency for Phase 1 approval |
| Assessment unit | PO × OMS Provider × OMS/Service Arrangement is the right granularity — retained. Must be extended to explicitly address the 11 banking-group-affiliated POs where the OMS may be group-owned or shared rather than PO-owned, with RMiT overlay considered alongside ITSS/GTRM |
| Outputs | Compliance packs + audit evidence generated on CSM/Bursa side from submitted documents align with Section D requirements. Board-level reporting beyond the consolidated report, and any continuous or PO-facing dashboards, fall outside the current deliverable → future-phase annex |
| Implementation | Six-phase roadmap does not map to the milestones of 24 reports by Nov 2026 + consolidated report by Q4 2026. Once Phase 1 is reframed as the upload-and-evaluate tool, a revised delivery plan must show specifically how it lands within the proposed timeframe. Phases 2–5 = clearly marked separate future-phase proposal, not part of the current ask |
| Immediate decision | "We are not in a position to approve Phase 0-1 as currently framed as it extends beyond the Phase 1 scope. We would like to resolve the feedback above before any approval is sought by CSM/Bursa." |
