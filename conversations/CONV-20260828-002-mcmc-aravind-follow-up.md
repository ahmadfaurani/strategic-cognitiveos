# MCMC Social Media Intelligence Capability Alignment

## Overview

DAF sent a formal follow-up email to Tuan Aravind at MCMC following their meeting on August 28, 2026. The email contains two formal requests and positions Aras as a capability-enhancement partner, not a replacement.

## Key Content

### Meeting Context
- DAF met Aravind in person on Aug 28, 2026
- Discussed MCMC's current social media intelligence capabilities and intended future-state alignment
- DAF expressed appreciation for the meeting and interest in further engagement

### Request 1: Telemetry Data Schema Alignment
DAF requests visibility of MCMC's telemetry data schema and data structure. Purpose:
- Develop analytical overview of current-state capability
- Understand existing data flows and analytical structure
- Identify potential integration points with broader operational intelligence capabilities within the parent organisation

### Request 2: MCMC AI Capability Development Workshop
DAF proposes a structured working session with 5 objectives:
1. Establish existing monitoring, analytics and AI capability baseline
2. Identify genuine operational and technical gaps
3. Review telemetry, integration and architecture requirements
4. Explore potential AI-driven capability enhancements
5. Prioritise practical development roadmap and potential POC opportunity

### Positioning
- "Build upon and enhance what MCMC already has, rather than duplicate existing capability"
- Identify where new AI-enabled capabilities can provide meaningful operational value
- Asks MCMC to identify how to coordinate workshop and relevant technical stakeholders

## Strategic Significance

This email converts a meeting discussion into formal written requests, creating two parallel tracks:
1. **Telemetry visibility track** — analytical assessment of MCMC's current state
2. **Workshop track** — structured capability development engagement

Both advance INIT-20260826-001 from "framed" to "active engagement". The workshop agenda artifact already exists (MCMC-AI-Capability-Development-Workshop-Agenda-v1.docx), created during the Aug 26 initiative framing.
