**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-18 | **TLP:** AMBER

---

## Bring Your IP. Build With Aras. Own What We Create Together.

This agreement governs intellectual property created during your participation in the Aras Cohort Programme. It is designed to protect what you already own while ensuring fair ownership of what we build together.

---

### 1. What You Keep

**Your Core IP remains 100% yours.**

Everything you bring into the programme — existing frameworks, methodologies, algorithms, domain models, research, taxonomies, processes — remains your intellectual property. Participation in this programme does not transfer ownership of your pre-existing IP to Aras or any other party.

### 2. What Aras Keeps

**Aras Background IP remains 100% Aras's.**

Aras's pre-existing infrastructure, platform architecture, AI models, engineering frameworks, security components, and deployment tooling remain Aras's intellectual property. Your use of Aras technology during the programme does not give you ownership of the underlying platform.

### 3. What We Co-Own

**Joint IP is owned 50:50.**

New software, AI capabilities, algorithms, workflows, models, tools, or deployable solutions created through our joint development during the programme are co-owned on a 50:50 basis between you (or your institution) and Aras Integrasi.

### 4. Commercialisation

- **Joint technology licence revenue:** 50:50, subject to agreed deductions
- **Aras infrastructure/compute fees:** Aras commercial revenue
- **Implementation services:** Revenue retained by performing party
- **Participant domain consultancy:** Participant commercial revenue
- **IP sale, exclusive licensing, sublicensing:** Mutual consent required
- **Product roadmap:** Joint Steering Committee decision

50:50 IP ownership does not mean every ringgit is split 50:50. Revenue is separated by component. See the full Commercialisation Framework in the Cohort IP Framework document.

### 5. IP Governance Process

Each project passes through a formal IP Gate process:

- **Gate 1:** You declare your Core IP (what existed before the programme)
- **Gate 2:** Aras declares its Background Technology
- **Gate 3:** We jointly define what we intend to create together
- **Gate 4:** Contributions are recorded throughout development
- **Gate 5:** Outputs are classified as Joint IP
- **Gate 6:** IP protection (copyright, patent, trade-secret) is determined
- **Gate 7:** Commercialisation decision (R&D → Pilot → Productise → Licence → Deploy)

### 6. Protection Boundary (Non-Negotiable)

Participation in this programme does not transfer ownership of either party's Background IP. This is a non-negotiable principle of the Aras Cohort Programme.

### 7. Legal Basis

This agreement follows WIPO collaborative development IP principles (Background IP / Foreground IP distinction). Malaysian patent law provides for joint patent ownership where two or more persons jointly make an invention (MyIPO). Copyright and licensing arrangements are expressly documented.

### 8. Alumni Community

Upon completing the programme, you join the Aras Cohort Alumni Community — a persistent network of practitioners, technologists, and institutional champions who co-own sovereign technology on the Aras platform. Your joint IP ownership ensures ongoing platform access to use, maintain, and extend what you have built.
