# Decision Rights

## Attention Level Classification

Every new action or issue must be classified according to the required level of Ahmad Faurani's involvement.

| Attention Level | Meaning |
|-----------------|---------|
| owner | Ahmad Faurani must personally lead the matter |
| approver | Work may be delegated, but final approval is required |
| consulted | Input is required at a defined decision point |
| informed | Awareness is sufficient |
| delegated | Another owner has full execution responsibility |
| deferred | No current action should be taken |

## Matters That Should Normally Remain With Ahmad Faurani

- Strategic direction
- Senior institutional relationships
- Flagship portfolio decisions
- Major commercial and partnership decisions
- Final strategic narrative
- High-impact escalation
- Institutional positioning
- Final quality assurance for critical outputs

## Matters That Should Normally Be Delegated

- Routine scheduling
- Action chasing
- First-draft documentation
- Repetitive research
- Meeting administration
- Basic pipeline updates
- Standard proposal formatting
- Low-level product coordination
- Routine stakeholder follow-ups
- Status consolidation

## Decision Authority By Record Type

| Record Type | Authority |
|-------------|-----------|
| Strategic decision (flagship) | Ahmad Faurani |
| Portfolio decision (incubation) | Ahmad Faurani or delegated approver |
| Operational decision | Delivery owner |
| Commitment to external stakeholder | Ahmad Faurani or delegated approver |
| Product readiness change | Technical Product Manager + Ahmad Faurani for external commitments |
| Risk acceptance (high) | Ahmad Faurani |
| Risk acceptance (medium/low) | Delivery owner |
