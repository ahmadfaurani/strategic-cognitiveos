# Strategic Operating Principles

## 1. Mission Before Tool

The system is designed around the leadership mission, not around the capabilities of Notion, Obsidian, GitHub or any individual AI model. Tools may change. The strategic operating standard must remain stable.

## 2. The Standard Is the System

The permanent foundation consists of:
- Canonical record structures
- Permanent record identifiers
- Controlled tags
- Relationship models
- Decision history
- Ownership rules
- Evidence references
- Information classifications
- Portfolio and readiness gates

## 3. Capture Strategic Value, Not Noise

A record should be created when the information affects:
- Strategy
- Commitments
- Stakeholders
- Product direction
- Commercial opportunity
- Institutional relationships
- Delivery
- Risk
- Intelligence requirements
- Organisational capability
- Future decisions

## 4. Every Strategic Record Must Support Action or Understanding

A record must clarify at least one of:
- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What was decided?
- What must happen next?
- Who owns the next step?
- What evidence supports the position?
- What has changed?
- What risk requires attention?

## 5. Human Authority, AI Assistance

Ember may extract, summarise, classify, recommend, connect, detect gaps, identify contradictions, and surface priorities. Final strategic authority remains with Ahmad Faurani Jaafar or a formally delegated decision owner.

## 6. Strategic Bandwidth Must Be Protected

The system continuously distinguishes between:
- Matters requiring Ahmad Faurani's judgement
- Matters requiring his awareness
- Matters suitable for delegation
- Matters that should be deferred or removed from the active portfolio

## 7. GitHub Is Authoritative

Where information differs between systems, the validated GitHub record takes precedence.

## 8. No Orphan Records

Every record must have an owner. Actions without owners are classified as unresolved, not active.

## 9. External Commitments Respect Readiness

External commitments should not exceed the verified readiness level of the product or programme.

## 10. Supersession Is Explicit

When a record replaces an earlier one, the `supersedes` and `superseded_by` fields must be populated on both records.
