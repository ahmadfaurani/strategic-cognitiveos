## 🎯 Operational Assessment

### Strengths (Observed)

1. **High Technical Expertise**
   - 50% hold Ts. title (Professional Technologist)
   - KPP has dual credentials (Ts. + Dr.)
   - Suggests deep technical knowledge for CII assessment

2. **Senior-Heavy Structure**
   - All staff are leadership-level (Pengarah/KPP/PPK)
   - Enables high-level sector engagement
   - Appropriate for policy/standards role

3. **Lean Management**
   - Clear, simple reporting chain
   - No middle management layers
   - Efficient decision-making

4. **Academic Credibility**
   - KPP holds doctorate
   - Supports research and standards development
   - Enables academic/industry partnerships

### Potential Gaps (Inferred)

1. **No Execution Staff**
   - Zero PPTM officers
   - Cannot conduct audits/assessments directly
   - Must rely on contractors or BAI support

2. **Small Team Size**
   - Only 4 officers for national CII portfolio
   - May limit sector coverage depth
   - Risk of capacity constraints

3. **No NC4 Integration**
   - No @nc4.gov.my email addresses
   - May be disconnected from operational crisis response
   - Potential coordination gap during incidents

4. **Limited Network Diversity**
   - Only 3 telco prefixes (vs. 6-7 in other divisions)
   - Lower communication resilience
