## 🎯 Mandate & Mission

### Core Functions (Inferred from Structure)

Based on divisional structure and personnel distribution:

1. **Cyber Threat Intelligence & Response (BAI)**
   - National-level threat monitoring
   - Incident response coordination
   - Threat actor analysis
   - Vulnerability assessment

2. **Critical Infrastructure Protection (BPIT)**
   - National Critical Information Infrastructure (NCII) oversight
   - Sectoral security standards
   - Compliance monitoring
   - Risk assessment frameworks

3. **Crisis Management & Coordination (NC4)**
   - National Computer Crisis Centre operations
   - 24/7 Security Operations Centre (SOC)
   - Cross-agency incident coordination
   - Emergency response protocols
