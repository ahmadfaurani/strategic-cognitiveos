# Executive Brief Template

## Purpose
Standardized template for executive-level research outputs across all modes.

---

## Structure

```markdown

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Prepared By:** [Name/Team]
**Classification:** [Internal/Confidential/Public]
**Review Status:** [Auto-approved/Human-reviewed]

---

[1-3 sentences summarizing the key takeaway and required action]

**Key Message:** [One sentence]
**Required Action:** [What decision/action is needed]
```

---

## Mode-Specific Adaptations

### Cyber Threat Intelligence

```markdown
## BLUF
- **Threat Level:** [Elevated/High/Critical]
- **Active Exploitation:** [Yes/No/Unknown]
- **Action Required:** [Patch/Monitor/Investigate]

## Key Findings
### CVE-2024-XXXX: Critical RCE in [Product]
- **CVSS:** 9.8
- **Exploit Status:** Public/In-the-Wild
- **Affected:** [Versions]
- **Remediation:** [Patch version]

## Recommended Actions
### Immediate (24h)
- [ ] Patch [product] to version X.X.X
- [ ] Monitor for IOCs: [list]
```

## Writing Guidelines

### Tone & Style
- **Concise:** Get to the point quickly
- **Action-oriented:** Focus on what needs to be done
- **Evidence-based:** Back claims with sources
- **Confidence-marked:** Indicate certainty levels
- **Jargon-free:** Use plain language

### Length Guidelines
- **BLUF:** 1-3 sentences
- **Context:** 2-3 short paragraphs
- **Each Finding:** 2-3 sentences + evidence
- **Total Brief:** 1-2 pages maximum (excluding appendix)

### Visual Elements
Use tables for:
- Risk/opportunity assessments
- Comparison matrices
- Timeline summaries
- Source summaries

Use bullet points for:
- Action items
- Key findings
- Decision options
