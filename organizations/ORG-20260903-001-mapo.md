# Majlis Antipemerdagangan Orang dan Antipenyeludupan Migran (MAPO)

## Organisation Type

government-agency — National coordinating council for anti-human-trafficking and anti-migrant-smuggling enforcement, under Kementerian Dalam Negeri (KDN).

## Sector

government — Law enforcement coordination, national security

## Strategic Relevance

MAPO operates under KDN — same institutional ecosystem as:
- NACSA (ORG-20260815-003, STK-20260819-001) — existing Workstream C relationship
- JDN (ORG-20260815-005) — existing Workstream C relationship
- MCMC (ORG-20260815-004) — existing engagement (Workstream D, OPP-20260826-001)

The Enforcement Committee Meeting Bil. 1/2026 articulated three strategic directives:
1. Result-based enforcement (not output metrics)
2. Intelligence-driven, proactive, high-impact enforcement
3. Strengthened inter-agency information sharing and strategic cooperation

This creates a demand space for analytical/intelligence capability products and inter-agency coordination platforms.
