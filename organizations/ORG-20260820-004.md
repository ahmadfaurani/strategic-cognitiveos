# Data Research Acquisition & Development (RADAR)

## Organisation Type

Internal agency — CognitiveOS architecture

## Sector

Intelligence operations / Data acquisition

## Mandate

- **Data Research:** Systematic acquisition of OSINT from news, media, and public records
- **Acquisition:** Intake SOP execution for all incoming news/media intelligence
- **Development:** Development of intelligence products from acquired data
- **Cronjob Governance (DEC-20260820-005):** Oversight of all cronjobs — collection, monitoring, maintenance
- **RMR Enforcement (DEC-20260820-005):** Mandatory RMR marking on all intakes with standard taxonomy compliance

## Radar Mark Registry (RMR)

The RMR is the registry namespace under RADAR. All intelligence intake events from news/media sources receive a Radar Mark — a permanent registry entry.
