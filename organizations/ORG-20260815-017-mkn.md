# Organisation Type

government-agency — National Security Council under the Prime Minister's Department.

# Strategic Relevance

National Security Council — operational authority for CSCDC. MKN serves as the technical/operational arm for national security coordination including cybersecurity. The MKN DG sits in the CSCDC four-signature approval chain (CCO → CEO → KSN/MKN → DG NACSA).

Position Aras Integrasi within MKN's operational framework for CSCDC deployment and national security coordination.
