# Commitment

DAF commits to a development freeze on GovSec TIP, effective immediately (10 Aug 2026). New major feature development is suspended unless a feature is critical for the CyberDSA launch. The team's focus shifts entirely to:

1. Stabilising and hardening the current v3.0 build
2. Closing critical technical and security gaps
3. Validating key end-to-end workflows and integrations
4. Completing development handover to Hadri
5. Preparing platform and demonstration flow for CyberDSA October 2026

This commitment is made to the GovSec team (Fuad, Hadri) and to the CSM partnership (joint launch dependency).

# Commitment Owner

DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice) — authority who declared the freeze and owns the commitment to maintaining it.

# Receiving Stakeholder

STK-20260804-003 — Ahmad Fuad (current technical owner, must comply with and execute within the freeze constraints)

Also applies to: STK-20260803-007 (Hadri, incoming delivery owner must operate within the freeze)

# Risk of Non-Delivery

Medium — the freeze may be breached if:
- A critical CyberDSA demo feature is identified as missing from v3.0
- CSM integration tracks require GovSec platform modifications
- Stabilisation reveals issues that require feature-level fixes rather than patching

Mitigation: DEC-20260810-002 includes an exception clause ("unless it is critical for launch") to allow targeted development when justified.

# Escalation Date

2026-09-15 — if the freeze has not produced sufficient launch readiness by this date, escalation is required to reassess scope and timeline.
