# Commitment

DAF formally commits to pausing all further feature development across all three Cybersecurity Flagship Products (VoronCitadel, GovSec TIP, ChainSentry). Development is restricted to:
1. Critical bug fixes and platform stability
2. Security remediation
3. Issues that materially affect demos, POCs, or customer engagements
4. Previously committed integration requirements necessary for commercialisation

This commitment extends COM-20260810-002 (GovSec-only freeze) to all three flagship products.

Additionally, DAF commits to establishing a centralised product repository and compiling a 6-category productisation framework for each product, with clear separation between current capabilities and future plans.
