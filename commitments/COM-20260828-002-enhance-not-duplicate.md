# Commitment

DAF explicitly commits that the MCMC engagement intention is to **build upon and enhance what MCMC already has, rather than duplicate existing capability**, while identifying where new AI-enabled capabilities can provide meaningful operational value.

## Commitment Owner

Faurani Jaafar (DAF)

## Receiving Stakeholder

Tuan Aravind (MCMC)

## Nature of Commitment

This is a **standing positioning commitment** — it applies to all engagement activities under INIT-20260826-001:
- Workshop design must start from MCMC's existing baseline
- Proposals must reference and integrate with existing systems
- AI enhancements must be positioned as augmenting, not replacing
- Capability recommendations must acknowledge what already works

## Risk of Non-Delivery

Low as a positioning statement. The real risk is in **execution**: if workshop outputs or proposals are perceived as duplicative or dismissive of existing MCMC capability, the commitment is violated in spirit.

Mitigated by:
- ACT-20260828-003 (telemetry data visibility — understanding current state before proposing)
- Workshop Objective 1 (establish existing baseline as first step)
