# Commitment Statement

DAF commits to the broader alignment vision: SiberSUITE → Telemetry → GovSec Analytics → CBOM Agent → Cybersecurity Score Card. This pipeline represents the integrated architecture for the GovSec × CSM partnership, where each layer builds on the previous to deliver CNII sector cybersecurity posture visibility.

# Committed By

DAF (Director, Cyber Security Practice, Aras Integrasi)

# Committed To

Mohamad Hafiz Bin Rahman (CSM) and the CSM team — as a shared vision communicated in the post-meeting follow-up email.

# Context

Following the GovSec × CSM SiberSUITE integration session on 10 August 2026, DAF articulated the broader alignment vision that connects the three immediate collaboration areas into a coherent integration pipeline. This is a strategic commitment to the architectural direction, not a technical delivery commitment.

# Scope of Commitment

- Strategic alignment on the integration pipeline architecture
- Progressive alignment of the three workstreams toward the pipeline vision
- Joint development approach with CSM as complementary partner (not duplicating SiberSUITE)

# Evidence

- DAF's post-meeting follow-up email (CONV-20260810-001) explicitly states: "From our perspective, the broader opportunity is to progressively align: SiberSUITE → Telemetry → GovSec Analytics → CBOM Agent → Cybersecurity Score Card"

# Deadline

Implicit deadline: CyberDSA October 2026 joint launch — pipeline should be demonstrable by early October 2026.

# Status

Active — communicated to CSM, awaiting CSM's implicit or explicit alignment.
