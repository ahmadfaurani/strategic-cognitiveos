# Lesson: Practice-Level Deliverables Not in CognitiveOS

**Gap:** 9 collateral documents + MCMC narrative + talent intake SOP + engineering hiring model costing exist as practice-level deliverables but are not in CognitiveOS records.

**Root cause:** Intake SOP triggers on DAF-forwarded emails and "that's it" session-level triggers. Practice work created in Athena sessions or team sessions doesn't trigger intake.

**Fix options:**
1. DAF forwards practice deliverables for intake (manual, adds burden)
2. Athena (or practice team) triggers intake on completed deliverables (requires SOP awareness)
3. Weekly review comparison becomes the retroactive intake trigger — any items in Athena's review not in CognitiveOS get ingested (systematic, this session's approach)

**Option 3 is what we're doing now** (ACT-20260821-001). But it's reactive, not proactive. Consider a practice-level intake trigger for future sessions.
