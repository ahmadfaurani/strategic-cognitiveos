# Lesson

Always verify which product/workstream a risk belongs to before importing it as a blocker for a different workstream's analysis. Product conflation is a CVS category error.

## Source

Cognitive Loop review (SOP-CL-001) of Zulfeka working group pitch, Aug 23 2026

## Date

2026-08-23

## Category

analytical

## Applies To

- All Cognitive Loop reviews (SOP-CL-001)
- Any cross-workstream risk analysis
- Risk import/escalation across products or gates

## Evidence

1. Cognitive Loop Step 2 flagged ACT-20260810-001 (GovSec, overdue) as the largest gap blocking the VoronCitadel pitch
2. DAF corrected: VoronCitadel is POC-ready (training delivered Jul 29, product operational). ACT-20260810-001 is GovSec (Gate 4 track)
3. Two different products, two different gates, two different readiness states
4. CVS-CBO-006 logged in Evidence Register as T3/L5, score 5 (Rule 6 — AI cap)
