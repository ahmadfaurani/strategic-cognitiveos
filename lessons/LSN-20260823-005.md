# Lesson

Never assume stakeholder hierarchy from incomplete or ambiguous records. When records say "under" or "covers domain," verify the exact reporting line before designing engagement artefacts.

2. Strategy 3 assumed "brief up to Fahdzli" — incorrect
3. DAF corrected: Fahdzli reports TO Zulfeka — tasking document, not briefing deck
4. Three stakeholder records updated to explicitly reflect the confirmed reporting line
