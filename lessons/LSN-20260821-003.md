# Lesson: Dual-Review Convergence

**Pattern:** Two independent reviews using different data sources, different units of count, and different analytical lenses reached the same strategic conclusion.

**Ember:** 6 workstream clusters, 105 commits, ~70+ records → "infrastructure came online, phase transition"
**Athena:** 32 completed items, 12 pending, 10 new workstreams → "product documentation solved, bottleneck moved downstream"

**Same conclusion, different vocabulary, different data.** That's epistemic triangulation.

**Application:** Consider dual-review cadence as standard weekly practice. Ember (governance) + Athena (operational). Not for redundancy — for confidence.
