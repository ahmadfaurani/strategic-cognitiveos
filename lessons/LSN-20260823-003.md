# Lesson

## Observation

The analytical review of TBH-001 identified that the strongest justification for the PM hire is not the workload analysis (69 actions, 37 delegatable, 54% reduction). It is the structural argument: the approved operating model already identifies this role as a P1 vacancy, with explicit consequences for leaving it unfilled.

The workload analysis is supporting evidence. But leading with numbers invites debate about methodology (are there really 69? is 2hrs/week realistic? is 54% workload or ownership?). Leading with structure invites agreement on the problem before debating the numbers.

## Pattern

**Argument sequence matters.** Structure → Quantity → Boundary produces management-defensible cases. Quantity → Structure → Boundary produces persuasive arguments that fail fact-checks.

## Corrective Action

For future hiring cases:
1. **Structure first:** Does the approved operating model identify this role as a vacancy? What are the stated consequences of leaving it unfilled?
2. **Quantity second:** Add workload analysis as supporting evidence — with corrected numbers, no uniform assumptions, proper margin calculations
3. **Boundary third:** Scope the role against other P1 roles to prevent SPOF displacement

This sequence produces cases that survive fact-checks and management scrutiny.
