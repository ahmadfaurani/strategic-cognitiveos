## 🎯 MANIFESTO THEME

**"JOHOR UNTUK SEMUA"**  
*Kemajuan Yang Dikongsi. Kemakmuran Yang Dirasai.*

**Core Narrative:** Johor's economic transformation (RM110B+ investment, JS-SEZ, RTS, data centers) must benefit ALL Johor rakyat — not just investors. Cost of living, housing affordability, wage growth, and balanced development are the central challenges.

**Median Wage Context:** RM2,982/month (Johor) vs rising cross-border economic pressure

## 📋 10 JANJI UTAMA — PUTERI WANGSA RELEVANCE MAPPING

### Promise 1: 100,000 Pekerjaan Bergaji Tinggi dan Bermaruah
**State Target:** 100,000 new jobs, 30% median wage increase, 20,000/year  
**Upskilling:** 10,000 workers in AI, data center, automation, smart logistics, modern agriculture

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL)
- **Youth vote (37% = 47,688 voters)** — directly targets 18-29 demographic
- **Urban, educated electorate** — AI, digital economy, tech jobs highly relevant
- **Mount Austin/Setia Enau** — young professionals, tech workers, digital economy participants
- **Message:** "Maszlee bawa ekonomi digital ke Johor — anak muda tak perlu ke KL atau Singapura"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **House-to-house script:** "Manifesto PH janji 100,000 pekerjaan bergaji tinggi. Anak Johor boleh kerja dekat rumah, tak perlu berhijrah."
- **Social media:** Infographic on AI/data center jobs in Johor (link to JS-SEZ)
- **Ceramah talking point:** "Gaji median Johor RM2,982 — PH nak naikkan 30% jadi RM3,877. MUDA/BN ada pelan apa?"

---

### Promise 2: 80,000 Rumah Mampu Milik Dalam 5 Tahun
**State Target:** 16,000 units/year, prioritize young families + local workers, enhance Felda Gen-2 housing

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL)
- **Urban seat** — housing affordability is TOP issue for Chinese/Malay/Indian families
- **Young voters (37% aged 18-29)** — first-time homebuyers
- **Setia Enau, Mount Austin, Pelangi Gaya** — property prices inflated, young families priced out
- **Message:** "16,000 rumah setahun — PH faham anak muda nak rumah sendiri, bukan sewa seumur hidup"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **House-to-house script:** "PH janji 80,000 rumah mampu milik. Keluarga muda dapat keutamaan. Tak perlu tunggu 10 tahun lagi."
- **Target PDs:** Tier 1 (Setia Enau, Mount Austin, Pelangi Gaya, Desa Tebrau) — highest concentration of young renters
- **Attack line:** "BN dah 70 tahun — kenapa rumah masih mahal? MUDA muda tapi takde pelan konkrit."

---

### Promise 3: RM8,000 Bantuan Rumah Pertama Anak Johor
**State Target:** RM8,000 deposit assistance, easier financing, rent-to-own schemes, 50,000 young families benefit

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL)
- **Direct cash benefit** — tangible, easy-to-communicate promise
- **50,000 families statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 2,000-3,000 beneficiaries (4% of state)
- **Chinese/Indian middle-class** — strong resonance with M40 struggling with down payments
- **Message:** "RM8,000 deposit — PH bagi duit betul, bukan janji kosong"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Leaflet highlight:** RM8,000 figure in BIG FONT
- **Testimonial strategy:** Find young couple who received BR1M/BSH under PH federal govt (2018-2020)
- **Calculator tool:** "With RM8,000, your monthly payment drops from RM1,800 to RM1,500"

---

### Promise 4: Pemerkasaan Johor Sebagai Pengeluar Utama Agromakanan
**State Target:** Modernize durian/pineapple value chains, maintain/expand permanent agricultural land

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐ (LOW)
- **Urban seat** — minimal direct agricultural activity
- **Indirect benefit:** Food security, lower food prices
- **Felda Ulu Tebrau (94.3% Malay)** — some relevance for Felda Gen-2 housing link
- **Message:** "Johor kurangkan import makanan — harga sayur, ikan, daging lebih stabil"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Minimal focus** — not a priority for urban voters
- **Felda outreach only:** Link to Felda Gen-2 housing promise (Promise 2)
- **Food price angle:** "Import makanan mahal — PH nak Johor keluar sendiri"

---

### Promise 5: Skim Kesihatan Johor Sehingga RM100,000 Setahun
**State Target:** RM100,000 annual health coverage, B40/M40/OKU eligible, revive Kad Sihat Johor, 500,000 beneficiaries

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (HIGH)
- **Aging population (12.4% aged 60+)** — healthcare is priority for seniors
- **M40 squeeze** — many earn too much for subsidies, too little for private insurance
- **Indian community** — strong resonance (healthcare costs frequently cited as burden)
- **500,000 beneficiaries statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 20,000-25,000 (4% of state)
- **Message:** "RM100,000 perlindungan — tak perlu risau mak ayah sakit, PH tanggung"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Senior outreach:** Focus on Tier 1 PDs with higher 60+ population (Pekan Pandan, Desa Tebrau)
- **Indian community:** Temple visits, Indian Bureau leaders highlight healthcare promise
- **OKU advocacy:** Disability groups — RM100,000 coverage includes OKU explicitly
- **Attack line:** "BN hapus Kad Sihat Johor — PH hidupkan semula dengan perlindungan lebih baik"

---

### Promise 6: RM500 Juta Dana Anak Muda Johor
**State Target:** TVET Premium, AI/robotics/digital training, young entrepreneur fund, first job guarantee, free SPM/STPM tuition for B40, improve education institutions, English/digital skills, 200,000 youth beneficiaries

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL)
- **47,688 youth voters (18-29)** — this is THE youth manifesto promise
- **UTM proximity** — university students, TVET participants, tech training beneficiaries
- **Chinese/Indian parents** — free SPM/STPM tuition for B40 resonates strongly
- **200,000 beneficiaries statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 8,000-10,000 (4-5% of state)
- **Message:** "RM500 juta untuk anak muda — TVET, AI, robotik, tuisyen percuma, kerja pertama"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Campus blitz:** UTM, Kolej Komuniti, private colleges in Mount Austin/Setia Enau
- **Social media:** TikTok/Instagram campaign — "Apa PH buat untuk aku?" → RM500 juta answer
- **Parent outreach:** "Anak SPM tahun depan? PH bagi tuisyen percuma kalau keluarga B40"
- **Contrast with MUDA:** "MUDA janji reformasi, PH bagi RM500 juta + program konkrit"

### Promise 7: Internet Gigabit dan AI Untuk Semua Sekolah Menjelang 2030
**State Target:** 100% high-speed internet, AI/Robotics labs in all districts, 50,000 students/year, digital libraries, school-TVET-IPT-industry partnerships

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (HIGH)
- **Educated electorate** — parents prioritize children's education, digital skills
- **Chinese/Indian families** — strong emphasis on education, tuition, STEM
- **50,000 students statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 2,000-2,500 students (4-5% of state)
- **Message:** "Sekolah anak dapat internet laju, makmal AI, robotik — PH invest masa depan"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **School visits:** PTA meetings, parent briefings (weekend mornings)
- **Teacher outreach:** Teacher unions, education NGOs
- **Comparison:** "Singapore schools have AI labs — why not Johor? PH will deliver"
- **Long-term frame:** "2030 target — anak kita darjah 1 hari ini akan dapat manfaat"

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (HIGH)

- **Chinese business community:** Strong SME base (F&B, retail, services, tech startups)
- **Indian business community:** Shops, restaurants, professional services
- **50,000 SMEs statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 2,000-2,500 SMEs (4-5% of state)

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Business roundtable:** Maszlee appearance with SME owners (Mount Austin cafes, shops)
- **Chinese chamber:** Engagement with Johor Bahru Chinese Chamber of Commerce
- **Digitalization pitch:** "Kedai takde online? PH bagi bantuan digital — grab, foodpanda, TikTok shop"
- **Export angle:** "PKS Johor boleh eksport ke Singapore, Indonesia — PH buka pintu"

### Promise 9: Masa Menunggu Rentas Sempadan Dikurangkan 50%
**State Target:** RTS/ETS/Bas.My integration, special lanes for cross-border workers, smart mobility system, RTS feeder buses from Skudai, Kulai, Kempas, Pasir Gudang, 300,000 workers benefit

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL)
- **Johor Bahru urban seat** — many residents work in Singapore
- **Tebrau corridor** — daily congestion for Singapore commuters
- **300,000 workers statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 15,000-20,000 (5-7% of state, higher than average)
- **RTS Link** — directly connects to Bukit Chagar (15 mins from Puteri Wangsa)
- **Feeder buses:** Skudai, Kulai, Kempas routes pass near/through Puteri Wangsa
- **Message:** "Kerja Singapore, tinggal Johor — PH kurangkan tunggu 50%, ada bas terus ke RTS"

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Singapore commuter outreach:** Morning/evening blitz at customs bus stops
- **WhatsApp groups:** Singapore worker networks (very active in JB)
- **Testimonial:** "Saya kerja Singapore, sehari 3 jam kat jalan. PH janji kurangkan — saya percaya"
- **Attack line:** "BN dah 70 tahun — kenapa causeway masih sesak? MUDA ada pelan apa?"

**State Target:** Furniture manufacturing, high-tech industry, tourism, creative economy, oil & gas, modern agriculture/fisheries/food production

- **Tourism:** Mount Austin, Setia Enau — F&B, hotels, entertainment venues benefit
- **Creative economy:** Young Chinese/Indian professionals in digital content, design, media
- **Oil & gas:** Pasir Gudang proximity — some residents work in O&G sector

**Campaign Integration:**
- **Tourism businesses:** F&B owners, hotel operators in Mount Austin/Setia Enau
- **Creative economy:** Digital content creators, designers, media professionals
- **Indirect jobs:** Most voters won't work in these industries directly, but benefit from multiplier effect

## 🚀 5 INISIATIF GAME CHANGER — PUTERI WANGSA RELEVANCE

### Game Changer 1: 250 Taska Madani dan Taska Tempat Kerja
**Target:** 250 new nurseries in industrial areas, government offices, communities  
**Benefit:** Help women return to workforce

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (HIGH)
- **Working mothers:** Large demographic in urban seat (Setia Enau, Mount Austin, Pelangi Gaya)
- **Female voters (49.6% = 63,897)** — direct benefit for young mothers
- **Message:** "Taska dekat tempat kerja — ibu boleh kerja balik, tak perlu pilih antara kerjaya dan anak"

---

### Game Changer 2: Pas Pengangkutan Awam Johor RM50 Sebulan
**Target:** Students, seniors (warga emas), OKU

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (HIGH)
- **Students:** Large student population (UTM, colleges, SPM/STPM students)
- **Seniors (12.4% = 15,966 voters):** Elderly need affordable transport for medical visits, social activities
- **OKU:** Explicit inclusion — disability community support
- **Message:** "RM50 sebulan — pelajar, warga emas, OKU boleh naik bas tak had"

---

### Game Changer 3: Program Balik Johor
**Target:** Bring back 20,000 Johor professionals from Singapore/overseas by 2031, job/entrepreneur incentives

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL)
- **Singapore commuters:** Many Puteri Wangsa residents already work in Singapore
- **"Brain drain" narrative:** Families separated, children raised in Singapore, Johor loses talent
- **20,000 professionals statewide** — Puteri Wangsa should get 1,000-1,500 (5-7% of state)
- **Message:** "Anak Johor kerja Singapore, tinggal jauh dari keluarga — PH bawa balik, insentif kerja dekat rumah"

---

### Game Changer 4: Memudaha cara Proses Tanah Pajakan yang Layak
**Target:** Establish Johor Land Rights Tribunal to expedite pending cases

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐ (LOW)
- **Urban seat** — minimal agricultural/leasehold land issues
- **Felda Ulu Tebrau (94.3% Malay):** Some relevance for Felda land inheritance issues
- **Message:** "Kes tanah tertunggak bertahun — PH ada tribunal, selesaikan cepat"

---

### Game Changer 5: RM1,000 Bonus Anak Johor
**Target:** RM1,000 for every baby born in Johor, starter savings for child's future

**Puteri Wangsa Relevance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (HIGH)
- **Young families:** Large demographic (18-35 age group with newborns)
- **Chinese/Indian families:** Strong cultural emphasis on children's education savings
- **Direct cash benefit:** Tangible, easy-to-communicate
- **Message:** "Baby lahir dapat RM1,000 — PH invest masa depan anak Johor dari hari pertama"
