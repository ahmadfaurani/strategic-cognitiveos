## 📊 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Strategic Classification:** Tier-1 Urban Battleground (Five-cornered fight)

**Core Reality:** Puteri Wangsa is a **Chinese-majority urban seat (51.4%)** with significant Malay minority (35.7%) and decisive youth bloc (37.0% aged 18-29). This is PH's natural terrain, but MUDA's incumbency (won 2022 with 7,114 majority) complicates the landscape.

**2026 Context:** Incumbent Amira Aisya (MUDA) NOT defending. PH deploying **Dr Maszlee Malik** (former Education Minister, PKR) — signals serious intent to reclaim. MUDA fielding **Rashifa Aljunied** (26, Chief of Staff to MUDA President) — youth appeal play. **NO PN/PAS candidate** — Malay vote splits 3 ways (BN/MUDA/Bersama).

**CM Narrative:** Maszlee is **widely discussed as a potential Chief Minister candidate** if PH wins Johor, though Anwar had not formally named PH's CM candidate as of 23 June 2026 (CNA). Maszlee himself has dismissed direct speculation.
