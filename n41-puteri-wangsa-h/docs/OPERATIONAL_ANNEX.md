### Rapid Response Protocol

**Trigger:** Opponent attack, scandal, fake news, controversy

**Response Time:**
- **Social Media:** 30 minutes (acknowledgment) → 2 hours (full response)
- **Press Statement:** 4 hours
- **Ceramah Rebuttal:** Same evening (if attack before 2pm)

**Escalation Ladder:**
1. **Level 1 (Minor):** Social media post + WhatsApp blast to volunteers
2. **Level 2 (Medium):** Press statement + flyer distribution
3. **Level 3 (Major):** Maszlee personal response + mega-ceramah rebuttal
