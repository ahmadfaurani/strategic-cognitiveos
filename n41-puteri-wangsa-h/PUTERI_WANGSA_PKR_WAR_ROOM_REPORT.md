## 3. VOTER SEGMENT ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Chinese Vote (51.4% — 66,196 voters)

**Profile:**
- Urban, middle-class, educated
- Concentrated in Mount Austin, Pelangi Gaya, Setia Enau, Desa Tebrau
- Key issues: Cost of living, education, corruption, governance
- **Vulnerability:** Disaffected by PH economic performance, MUDA inroads

**Activation Strategy:**
- **Messaging:** "PH delivered stability, PN brings uncertainty"
- **Tactics:** Town halls with economic experts, WeChat/WhatsApp blitz
- **Risk:** Low turnout = PN opportunity

### 3.2 Indian Vote (10.0% — 12,930 voters) — **KINGMAKER**

**Profile:**
- Concentrated in Puteri Wangsa 1 (1,991), Bukit Jaya (1,634), Puteri Wangsa 2 (1,068)
- Working-class to middle-class, small business owners
- Key issues: Education quotas, temple rights, economic opportunities
- **Historical loyalty:** PH, but erosion risk if taken for granted

**Activation Strategy:**
- **Messaging:** "PH defended Indian interests, PN marginalizes minorities"
- **Tactics:** MIC/PH Indian leaders roadshow, temple visits, tuition programs
- **Target:** 75%+ turnout, 80%+ PH vote share

### 3.3 Malay Vote (35.9% — 46,169 voters)

**Profile:**
- Concentrated in FELDA Ulu Tebrau (5,297), Maju Jaya (4,353), Ladang Tebrau (5,080)
- Rural/semi-urban, FELDA settlers, civil servants
- Key issues: Subsidies, rural development, religious affairs
- **Vulnerability:** PN religious messaging, PAS appeal

**Activation Strategy:**
- **Messaging:** "PH protects FELDA, PN privatizes"
- **Tactics:** FELDA-specific outreach, asnaf programs, surau visits
- **Realistic target:** 30-35% of Malay vote (not majority needed)

### 3.4 Youth Vote (21-45: 57.0% — 73,292 voters)

**Profile:**
- Digital-native, first-time voters (Undi18 impact)
- Progressive, reform-oriented, skeptical of old politics
- Key issues: Jobs, housing, climate, governance
- **Opportunity:** PH reform agenda resonates

**Activation Strategy:**
- **Messaging:** TikTok/Instagram-first, policy explainers
- **Tactics:** Influencer partnerships, university roadshows, digital AMAs
- **Target:** 70%+ turnout, 65%+ PH vote share
