## 🔥 Campaign Priorities

### Week 1 (Days 1-7)
- House-to-house in Tier 1 PDs (Setia Enau, Mount Austin, Pelangi Gaya, Desa Tebrau, Pekan Pandan)
- Malay outreach in Bukit Mutiara (key swing PD)
- Indian voter consolidation in Bukit Jaya, Puteri Wangsa 2

### Week 2 (Days 8-14)
- Tier 2 PD blitz (Pinang Sagu, Rumbia Daya, Nibong Daya, etc.)
- Follow-up rounds for undecided households
- Youth mobilization (campus outreach, social media)

### GOTV (Final 72 Hours)
- Transport logistics (376 vans for 15,000 voters)
- Polling agent deployment (250 agents across 100 stations)
- Real-time turnout monitoring (hourly updates)
