## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Seat Character:** Malay-majority (73.6%) coastal-rural constituency with significant Chinese minority (25.1%). Structurally BN-leaning but highly contestable under high-turnout scenarios.

**Key Strategic Insight:** Chinese vote concentration in 3 urban PDs (12.5% of total voters) provides PH with mobilisation anchor. Malay vote split (BN vs PN) is decisive variable. Youth 18-29 (28.1%) represents critical swing bloc.

**PH Ceiling:** 35-40% under normal turnout; 45%+ achievable with Chinese turnout surge + Malay progressive penetration in mixed PDs.

**BN Safety Margin:** Vulnerable if turnout exceeds 75% and PN siphons >15% Malay vote.

## 🔍 DEMOGRAPHIC VULNERABILITIES

### BN Vulnerabilities

1. **Youth Disaffection (18-29 = 28.1%)**
   - 8,736 youth voters; low economic mobility in rural setting
   - Vulnerable to PH reform narrative, PN religious-conservative appeal
   - **Risk:** 20-25% youth defection to opposition

2. **Chinese Vote Consolidation**
   - 7,780 Chinese voters concentrated in 3 PDs (20.3% of seat)
   - If PH achieves 80%+ turnout in Tier 1 PDs = ~5,000+ votes
   - **Risk:** Chinese turnout surge compresses BN margin

3. **Cost of Living Pressure**
   - Fishing, agriculture, small-town economy
   - B40/M40 households sensitive to fuel, food prices
   - **Risk:** Economic dissatisfaction → PN/PH defection

4. **Incumbent Performance Scrutiny**
   - Selamat Takim (BN/UMNO) re-fielded
   - Local service delivery record will be tested
   - **Risk:** Poor accessibility perception → Malay leakage to PN

---

### PH Challenges

1. **Malay Vote Ceiling**
   - 73.6% Malay majority; PH historically struggles >25%
   - Requires breakthrough in Tier 2 mixed PDs
   - **Mitigation:** Focus on 21-30 age bracket, women voters

2. **2022 Collapse Recovery**
   - PH performed poorly in 2022 (low turnout favoured BN)
   - Must rebuild from collapsed base
   - **Mitigation:** Re-engage 2018 voters who sat out 2022

3. **Resource Stretch**
   - 19 PDs, 51.7% are Malay >80% (low ROI for PH)
   - Must prioritise Tier 1 + Tier 2 (48.3% of voters)
   - **Mitigation:** Surgical resource allocation, avoid over-extension
