## 👤 VOLUNTEER PROFILES

### Profile A: Community Leader (Age 40-65)

**Characteristics:**
- Respected local figure (temple committee, mosque leader, business owner)
- Existing social network (100+ contacts)
- Available evenings/weekends
- Motivates: Community representation, service delivery

**Best Roles:**
- PD Coordinator
- Town Hall Speaker
- Endorsement Ambassador
- Elder Outreach Lead

**Recruitment Approach:**
- Personal visit from candidate/senior leader
- Emphasise community impact, legacy
- Provide leadership title, visible role

---

### Profile B: Youth Activist (Age 18-30)

**Characteristics:**
- Social media native
- High energy, flexible schedule
- Motivates: Reform, employment, housing, climate
- Network: University peers, online communities

**Best Roles:**
- Social Media Content Creator
- First-Time Voter Outreach
- Campus Organiser
- TikTok/Instagram Live Host
- Rapid Response Team

**Recruitment Approach:**
- Social media DM, peer referral
- Emphasise movement-building, change
- Provide creative autonomy, public recognition

---

### Profile C: Professional Volunteer (Age 30-50)

**Characteristics:**
- Skills: Law, data, communications, logistics
- Limited weekday availability
- Motivates: Good governance, anti-corruption
- Network: Professional associations

**Best Roles:**
- Legal/Agent Coordinator
- Data Analyst
- Communications Strategist
- Transport Logistics Manager

**Recruitment Approach:**
- Skills-based ask ("We need your expertise")
- Flexible remote options
- Emphasise strategic impact

---

### Profile D: Grassroots Organiser (Age 25-55)

**Characteristics:**
- Door-knock experience (2018/2022 veteran)
- Local knowledge (knows every street)
- Motivates: Party loyalty, community service
- Network: Neighborhood contacts

**Best Roles:**
- Canvass Team Leader
- GOTV Captain
- Transport Coordinator (local routes)

**Recruitment Approach:**
- Party branch referral
- Emphasise team camaraderie, duty
- Provide training, clear protocols

### Step 2: Orientation Session (Week 1)

**Format:** 90-minute group session (in-person or Zoom)

**Agenda:**
| Time | Topic | Presenter |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 0-10 min | Welcome + Icebreaker | War Room Director |
| 10-25 min | Seat Overview (demographics, history) | Data Team Lead |
| 25-40 min | PH Victory Path (math, strategy) | Tier Commander |
| 40-60 min | Volunteer Roles + Expectations | Volunteer Coordinator |
| 60-75 min | Safety + Security Protocols | Legal Coordinator |
| 75-90 min | Q&A + Group Photo | All |

**Materials Provided:**
- Volunteer handbook (PDF)
- Seat fact sheet (1-pager)
- Contact list (PD Coordinator, Tier Commander)
- T-shirt/badge (if budget allows)

---

### Step 3: Role-Specific Training (Week 2)

**Canvassing Training (60 min):**
- Door-knock script + rebuttals
- Data collection (voter intention coding)
- Safety protocols (dogs, hostile voters)
- When to escalate (incidents, threats)

**Phone Banking Training (45 min):**
- Call script + tone guidance
- Handling objections
- Data entry (Google Form)
- Call time scheduling

**Polling Agent Training (120 min):**
- Legal rights + responsibilities
- Form 14 procedures
- Incident reporting
- Counting room protocols

**Transport Coordinator Training (60 min):**
- Route planning + timing
- Vehicle safety checks
- Voter pickup protocols
- Emergency contacts

## 📊 VOLUNTEER RETENTION STRATEGY

### Recognition Program

**Weekly Shout-Outs:**
- Top Canvasser (most doors knocked)
- Top Caller (most calls completed)
- Top Recruiter (most new volunteers)
- Spotlight Saturday (volunteer story on social media)

**Milestone Badges:**
```
🥉 Bronze Volunteer: 20 hours completed
🥈 Silver Volunteer: 50 hours completed
🥇 Gold Volunteer: 100 hours completed
💎 Diamond Volunteer: GOTV week + Polling Day
```

**End-of-Campaign Recognition:**
- Certificate of Appreciation (all volunteers)
- Dinner/Gathering (post-election, all hands)
- Reference letters (upon request, for Gold+ volunteers)
- Priority consideration for future campaigns

---

### Morale Maintenance

**Weekly Check-Ins:**
- PD Coordinators check on each volunteer (10-min call)
- Identify burnout signs (missed shifts, low energy)
- Offer support, adjust workload if needed

**War Room Atmosphere:**
- Food/snacks always available
- Music playlist (volunteer requests)
- Whiteboard with progress trackers (visual momentum)
- Daily huddle (10 min, energy boost)

**Stress Management:**
- Quiet room available (for decompression)
- Counselling referral (if serious distress)
- Rotation system (no one works 7 days straight)

## 🚫 VOLUNTEER DISCIPLINE PROTOCOLS

### Code of Conduct

**All volunteers must agree to:**
1. No unauthorised media statements (refer to Communications Lead)
2. No sharing of voter data outside campaign systems
3. No harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behaviour
4. No alcohol/drugs during campaign activities
5. Respect all voters (including opponents)
6. Follow safety protocols at all times

---

### Violation Response

| Severity | Examples | Response |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Level 1 (Minor)** | Late to shift, minor data error | Verbal warning, retraining |
| **Level 2 (Moderate)** | Unauthorised social media post, repeated lateness | Written warning, 1-week suspension |
| **Level 3 (Serious)** | Data breach, voter harassment, intoxication | Immediate dismissal, ban from campaign |
| **Level 4 (Critical)** | Violence, criminal activity, treason | Immediate dismissal, legal action if warranted |

**Appeal Process:**
- Volunteer may appeal to War Room Director within 48 hours
- Director decision is final
- Documentation retained in volunteer file
