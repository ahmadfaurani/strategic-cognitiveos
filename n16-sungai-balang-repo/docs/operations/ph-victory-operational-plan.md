## 🛡️ RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Low Chinese Turnout (<75%)

**Triggers:**
- Week 6 polling shows <60% commitment in Tier 1
- Early voting turnout lower than expected
- Weather forecast poor (rain on polling day)

**Mitigation:**
- Escalate community leader outreach (double down on temple/business visits)
- Increase transport capacity (more vans, more pickup points)
- Weather contingency: Umbrellas, raincoats for volunteers and voters
- Social media urgency: "Setiap Undi Berarti" campaign

---

### Risk 2: Malay Vote Leakage to PN (>25%)

**Triggers:**
- PN rallies drawing large crowds in Tier 2/3 PDs
- Religious sentiment messaging gaining traction
- PH Malay support polling <20% in Week 5

**Mitigation:**
- Deploy Muslim PH surrogates (ADUNs, religious figures)
- Emphasise economic message over cultural/religious debates
- Targeted outreach to PAS-leaning voters (anti-corruption, cost of living)
- Accept 15-20% PN vote share as baseline; focus on BN defections

---

### Risk 3: BN Incumbent Machinery Overwhelms

**Triggers:**
- BN door knock coverage exceeds PH (intelligence reports)
- BN transport network larger than expected
- Government announcement of local projects (last-minute "goodies")

**Mitigation:**
- Expose "project announcement" as election bribery (file complaints)
- Out-organise: More volunteers, longer hours, better data
- Highlight incumbent's track record gaps (specific unfulfilled promises)
- Appeal to "change" sentiment among disaffected BN voters

---

### Risk 4: Operational Breakdown (Volunteer No-Show, Transport Failure)

**Triggers:**
- Week 8 volunteer attrition >20%
- Vehicle breakdowns on polling day
- Communication system failure (WhatsApp down, phones dead)

**Mitigation:**
- Recruit 30% volunteer buffer (213 target → recruit 280)
- Vehicle inspection 1 week before; backup drivers on standby
- Redundant communication: WhatsApp + phone tree + radio (if available)
- Command centre backup power (generator, UPS)

---

### Risk 5: Disinformation/Black Ops

**Triggers:**
- Viral WhatsApp messages attacking PH candidate
- Fake news about PH policies (anti-Islam, anti-Malay)
- Candidate scandal (real or fabricated)

**Mitigation:**
- 24/7 monitoring team (social media, WhatsApp groups)
- Pre-prepared rebuttal templates (rapid deployment)
- Legal team ready for defamation complaints
- Candidate transparency (press conference to address allegations)
