### Press Conference Protocol

**Pre-Conference Checklist:**
```
[ ] Venue booked (War Room or neutral location)
[ ] Podium + backdrop prepared (PH branding)
[ ] Microphones tested (TV + radio)
[ ] Seating arranged (front row: major outlets)
[ ] Press kits printed (release, fact sheets, candidate bio)
[ ] Water + light refreshments
[ ] Sign-in sheet (track attendance)
```

**Running Order:**
| Time | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| 0-5 min | Arrival + sign-in | 5 min |
| 5-10 min | Informal mingling | 5 min |
| 10-15 min | Opening remarks (candidate) | 5 min |
| 15-25 min | Policy announcement (candidate) | 10 min |
| 25-40 min | Q&A session | 15 min |
| 40-45 min | Closing remarks | 5 min |

- Anticipate 10 tough questions (BN/PN attacks, vulnerabilities)

### Fact-Checking Protocol

**Before Any Response:**
```
[ ] Verify claim against source documents (SPR data, official records)
[ ] Check date/context (old story recycled? taken out of context?)
[ ] Identify origin (who posted first? credible source?)
[ ] Assess reach (shares, comments, media pickup)
[ ] Determine intent (mistake, trolling, coordinated attack?)
[ ] Consult legal (if criminal allegations, defamation risk)
```

**Response Decision Tree:**
```
Is the claim TRUE?
├── Yes → Acknowledge, contextualize, pivot to solutions
└── No → Is it worth responding to?
    ├── No (low reach, trivial) → Ignore, monitor
    └── Yes (high reach, damaging) → Factual rebuttal + escalate
```

**Golden Rule:** Never amplify false claims unnecessarily. Sometimes silence is better than engagement.

## 🎭 CRISIS COMMUNICATIONS

### Pre-Approved Crisis Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Candidate Scandal (Real or Fabricated)**

**Immediate Actions (First 60 Minutes):**
1. War Room Director convenes crisis team (Comms Director, Legal, Spokesperson)
2. Candidate briefed (get their account, evidence if available)
3. Legal assessment (defamation, criminal exposure)
4. Decision: Fight (false) vs. Apology (true, minor) vs. Step aside (true, serious)
5. Draft holding statement ("We are aware, investigating, will respond soon")

**Fight Strategy (If False):**
- Issue categorical denial (candidate + party leadership)
- Provide evidence if available (documents, witnesses)
- Announce legal action (police report, defamation suit)
- Frame as "election dirty tricks" (sympathy wave)
- Surrogate speakers (respected community leaders vouch for character)

**Apology Strategy (If True, Minor):**
- Candidate apologizes (sincere, specific, no excuses)
- Takes responsibility ("I made a mistake")
- Commits to improvement ("I will do better")
- Asks for second chance ("Let me serve you")
- Surrogates: Focus on track record, not personality

**Step Aside Strategy (If True, Serious):**
- Party leadership announces replacement (quick, decisive)
- Thank outgoing candidate (graceful, no details)
- Introduce new candidate (positive, forward-looking)
- Minimize media cycle (one press conference, then move on)

---

**Scenario 2: Volunteer Misconduct (Racist Remark, Violence, Corruption)**

**Immediate Actions:**
1. Suspend volunteer pending investigation (public statement)
2. Investigate within 24 hours (interview witnesses, review evidence)
3. Decision: Reinstate (false accusation) vs. Dismiss (verified)
4. If dismissed: Public statement ("Zero tolerance, dismissed, cooperating with authorities")
5. If criminal: Report to police (demonstrate seriousness)

**Messaging:**
```
PH Sungai Balang mengambil serius sebarang salah laku oleh sukarelawan kami.

Setelah siasatan, [Name] telah diberhentikan serta-merta. Kami tidak bertolak ansur
dengan [racism/violence/corruption].

Kami bekerjasama dengan pihak berkuasa jika perlu. Integriti campaign kami adalah keutamaan.
```

---

**Scenario 3: Opponent Dirty Tricks (Bribes, Threats, Vote Buying)**

**Documentation Protocol:**
- Photo/video evidence (if safe to obtain)
- Witness statements (name, phone, IC number)
- Date/time/location stamps
- Submit to SPR + BERSIH + MACC (official channels)
- Media release (after official submission, not before)

**Messaging:**
```
PH Sungai Balang merakamkan kebimbangan terhadap laporan [bribery/threats] di [location].

Kami telah mengemukakan aduan rasmi kepada SPR/MACC. Semua pihak patut beri ruang
untuk pilihan raya yang adil dan bebas.

Kami menggesa pengundi: Jangan jual undi anda. Masa depan Sungai Balang lebih berharga.
```

**Escalation:**
- If pattern emerges (multiple incidents): Press conference
- If opponent-linked: Name them (carefully, with evidence)
- If criminal: Police report + protection for witnesses
