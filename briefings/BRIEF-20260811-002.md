# VoronCitadel GTM Working Documents — CMO Review Package & 193-Org Stakeholder Mapping

DAF shared two working documents from the Aug 11 Sales/Marketing alignment meeting for team reference and operational execution.

## Document 1: VoronDRQ Lead Generation Outreach Information Package (CMO Review)

**File:** `products/voroncitadel/GTM_OUTREACH_PACKAGE.md`
**Prepared:** August 8, 2026 by DAF for CMO (Azzatullina Pawanchik)
**Status:** Approval required before activation

### Key Recommendations

1. **Account-based outreach, not mass campaign** — 193 institutional rows, but ~146 directional buying centres after deduplication
2. **15 group-level pilot accounts** in two lanes:
   - **Lighthouse (8):** Maybank, CIMB, RHB, Hong Leong, Public Bank, Affin, Bank Islam/BIMB, Alliance — executive awareness and category validation
   - **Conversion (7):** Bank Muamalat, Kuwait Finance House, MIDF, Kenanga, BPMB, EXIM Bank, Etiqa — diagnostics, demos, POC qualification
3. **Three-week campaign architecture:**
   - Week 1: Establish relevance (email + LinkedIn + role-specific openers)
   - Week 2: Offer useful engagement (readiness diagnostic or closed-door roundtable)
   - Week 3: Convert interest into evaluation (tailored demo, executive sponsor escalation, close-the-loop)

### Campaign Objectives (3-Week Targets)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Buying centres activated | 15 |
| Verified stakeholders engaged | 45–60 |
| Engaged accounts | 10 |
| Discovery/diagnostic sessions | ≥5 |
| Demonstration commitments | 5–6 |
| POC candidates | 2–3 |
| Accounts without next action | 0 |

### CMO Approval Items (6)
1. Positioning and campaign theme ("From periodic compliance to continuous assurance")
2. Initial 15-account, two-lane portfolio
3. Three-week email, LinkedIn and executive-engagement cadence
4. Readiness diagnostic and closed-door roundtable as primary conversion offers
5. Claims governance (only verified capabilities, credentials and outcomes)
6. Pilot success targets and weekly Marketing-Sales review cadence

### Operating Model (RACI)

| Function | Responsibility |
|----------|---------------|
| CMO | Campaign approval, message governance, assets, channel coordination, performance review |
| Cyber Security Practice | Product truth, technical messaging, diagnostics, demonstrations, POC qualification |
| Sales / Account Owners | Personalisation, outreach execution, CRM hygiene, meeting conversion |
| Executive Sponsors / Partners | Warm introductions and senior-stakeholder access |
| Legal / Governance | Claims, privacy, consent and external-distribution review |
| Marketing Operations | Tracking, attribution, reporting, sequence administration |

### Non-Negotiable Guardrails
- Do not contact all seven stakeholders simultaneously
- Do not use unverified claims (customer, certification, cost-reduction, deployment)
- Do not position VoronDRQ as replacement for entire security/GRC estate during awareness
- Stop automated follow-ups after substantive response — transfer to human-led engagement
- Record activity against parent buying centre, with subsidiaries mapped underneath
- Every meeting must end with a named owner, action and date
